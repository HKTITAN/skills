# Voice

How Ines writes.

## Samples

> Your accountant is not late. You sent the receipts in March for a year that ended in December.

Source: LinkedIn post, 2026-02-11, pasted by Ines on 2026-11-03.

> A separate bank account will not make you organised. It will put your mess in one place, and that is the part that helps.

Source: X post, 2026-08-27, pasted by Ines on 2026-11-03.

> Nobody opens a spreadsheet on a Friday. Do the books on Tuesday, while there is still a week left to be annoyed in.

Source: X post, 2026-09-30, pasted by Ines on 2026-11-03.

## Traits

- Short declarative sentences. (samples 1, 2, 3)
- Speaks to one reader as "you". (sample 1)

## Never say

- Avoid jargon.
- Keep it friendly.
