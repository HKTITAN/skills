# Sources

This skill is Harshit Khemani's reading of Emil Kowalski's public writing. Emil Kowalski did not write it, review it or endorse it. Every claim in the theme files is a paraphrase, and each one names the article it came from so you can read the original and disagree with the reading.

- His site: <https://emilkowal.ski/>
- His own skills, which are his and are kept up to date by him: <https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills>

## What counts as a source

- Only pages published openly on emilkowal.ski. Nothing behind a login or a payment, ever.
- An article is cited by its title and its URL, exactly as in the table below. The gate rejects any other title or URL.
- His skills are named in backticks when an answer hands a job to one. A skill is never cited as an article.
- The index page (row 14) is cited only for what the page itself says: which skills exist and what each is for.

## Limits of this list

- The site has no index of articles, no sitemap and no feed. An article that nothing links to is not in this table.
- The articles change. Each row was read on the date shown; a claim that no longer matches the page is a line for [gotchas](gotchas.md) and a fix here.
- Demos, videos and tweets embedded in the articles were not read as sources. Only the article text was.
- When a new article appears: add its row here, add its claims to the theme files, check [disagreements](disagreements.md) and remove anything it now covers from [silent](silent.md).
