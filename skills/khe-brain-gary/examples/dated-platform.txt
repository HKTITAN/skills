His Snapchat calls moved with the year, and he says himself that he changed twice: on Stories in 2014, and on its age in January 2016 (I Made a Bad Call on Snapchat but Here's Why I'm Totally Cool With It; Deploying Against the Snap: Takeaways from Snapchatting in Vegas & Ces). In 2013 he called Snapchat Stories high-friction and a bad fit for an app built on one-to-one messages, and in 2014 he said he was "100% wrong" (I Made a Bad Call on Snapchat but Here's Why I'm Totally Cool With It). By 2024 he said he was very bullish on Snapchat Spotlight (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 42:45).
Changed: in February 2016 he said Snapchat was the next big social network, with 24 to 36 months of importance ahead (Marketing In the Year 2016).
Changed: in March 2016 he said Snapchat filters were underpriced and would stop working within four to seven months (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 9:00).
Changed: in February 2017 he said Snapchat had been about to become a monster with 40 and 50 year olds, but Instagram's story features gave them no reason to download it, so it lost momentum (Joe Rogan Experience #910 - Gary Vaynerchuk at 1:15:45).
