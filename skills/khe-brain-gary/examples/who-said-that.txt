Gary Vaynerchuk gave the 79/21 rule in an article on his site published 21 March 2019 (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business). In 2019 he defined it as about 79 percent of your time on the platform bringing the most value and about 21 percent tasting others, so you are ready when one moves up (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business). He says he named it "to be a little different" (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business; Strike While It's Hot: It Doesn't Matter If A Platform Is Around In A Year).
