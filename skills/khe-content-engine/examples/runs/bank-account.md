From Ines, 2026-11-10. An opinion, with her reasons and no figures.

- New freelancers ask her which bookkeeping app to buy. She tells them to open a separate bank account first.
- Her reason: with one account for the business, every business payment is in one statement. An app can only sort what it is given.
- She is clear that the account does not make anyone tidy. It makes the mess readable.
- She has no numbers for this and does not want any invented.

Claim: the separate account does more for a new freelancer's books than any app chosen before it.
