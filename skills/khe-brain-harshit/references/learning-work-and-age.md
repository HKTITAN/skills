# Learning, work and age

He learns by building, breaking and finding out, and tells others to do the same instead of waiting to feel ready. Since mid-2025 his settled view is that people learn by doing, that age is no filter, that AI is an extension of the mind as long as it keeps you active, and that the economy pays for leverage and judgment rather than effort. He is a college student who started building around 9th grade and writes often about being young among older builders. His 2022-24 posts give the same advice in the form of LinkedIn maxims about knowledge, habits and quitting.

## How does he say people learn?

- He says he learned design mostly through pure FAFO: building, breaking, and working out why something works. "i’ve mostly learned through pure FAFO." (2026-05-19-thank-ve-learned-ton, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056637882065141844)
- He says people learn by doing, so a young builder who is motivated by building in public should keep at it. "people learn by doing." (2026-08-31-dont-agree-showing-himself, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2094352639882604840)
- He tells people to learn by FAFO and be tinkerers, and to keep experimenting. "learn by FAFO, be a tinkerer!" (2026-09-19-learn-by-fafo-tinkerer, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2101350604270706740; 2026-10-19-keep-experimenting-keep-tinkering, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112113623778951249)
- He says building with agents has been one of his best ways to learn, and that information velocity in recent months has sped up his own learning. (2026-07-29-hadikhantech-mean-has-been, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2082511015942758474; 2026-10-21-true-information-velocity-has, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112713351893750055)
- From Vivek Sridhar's habit of giving talks to learn, he took that you learn by teaching; his own line was that you do not need a roadmap to start, because clarity comes through action. "clarity doesn’t come before action." (2025-10-31-didn-t-expect-friday, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7390040536491077632)
- His takeaways from a Dan Shipper talk: experiment a lot and face the frontier. (2026-09-25-rule-no-1-never, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2103493731169263816)
- Using Varun Mayya's framework, he wrote that intelligence is trainable: go wide before specializing, build judgment, use tools, turn lessons into principles, run premortems, unlearn old identities. "Intelligence is not fixed." (2026-05-17-varun-mayya-s-point, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056052441397989843)
- He says nothing is truly original, everything is a remix, and urges people to create instead of only consume. (2025-12-04-thejayalto-were-all-patchworks, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1996609315994812768; 2025-12-05-coolest-thing-ive-seen, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1996948070186340566)
- (2024) He wrote that knowing is not doing: learn one thing, do one thing, document the result. "Implementation beats information." (2024-12-01-most-expensive-advice-one, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7268907331264884736)
- (2024) He wrote that knowledge only sticks when it has somewhere to land, such as use, a framework or notes, and that what you feed your mind decides what you create. (2024-12-07-imagine-all-knowledge-your, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7271075418722463745; 2024-12-13-mindful-content-consume-shapes, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7273394668090912768)
- (2022-23) He told beginners to build basic skills before going advanced, and warned that leaning too hard on a second brain can weaken memory. "BALANCE IS KEY!" (2022-05-16-simsays00-anav-chawla-notionhq, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1526176512860631040; 2023-06-28-thoughts-second-brain-technique, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1674091179737055232)

## Is anyone too young to start?

- He says the urge to build does not come with an age filter, and clarity comes from iteration. (2025-12-14-walking-through-indian-institute, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7405960147660992512)
- He says kids are far more capable with tech than adults assume and learn by exploring until it clicks; his dad says he taught him Android at age 4 by FAFO. (2026-05-19-dad-still-tells-basically, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056644516778791170)
- He says he was driven by curiosity as a kid and turned out fine, and he values people who let you experiment and find out. (2026-08-29-44latte-depends-driven-by, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2093735048591954330; 2026-10-26-people-who-let-experiment, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2114761325817815068)
- Replying about a younger builder, he said it makes him feel old even though he started around the same age. (2026-07-13-sonofalli-sharavarora-makes-feel, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2076484543771718012)
- (2025) Before his first hackathon he wrote that you should not wait to feel skilled, since skills come from doing. "skills are built through doing, not waiting." (2025-01-01-don-t-have-skills, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7280082192557350912)
- (2024) He wrote that age is no barrier, pointing to his first startup in 9th grade. "Start small, start messy, but START." (2024-12-07-am-too-young-start, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7271081968077799424)

- He welcomed a cheaper student plan for AI coding tools, saying the $20 a month entry price kept many students from trying Cursor or Claude. (2026-07-28-pricing-sits-perfectly-college, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2081989470275227921)

- (2023) On choosing a major he advised trying options and accepting that interests change. (2023-01-18-choosing-college-major-field, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1615655641359740928)

## Does AI make people think less?

- In his X article on the water bottle as a tool he says AI is an extension of the mind; the better question is not dependence but what it frees you to do, since every tool raised the floor. (2026-05-07-water-bottle-tool-something, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/article/2052412506002644992)
- He says a screen is not bad by itself: what matters is whether a tool increases passive dependency or active engagement, as with books and toys. (2026-05-19-don-t-think-usefulness, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056632120337141908)
- He also warns that people will be tempted to outsource their thinking as never before. (2026-05-09-yacinemtb-youll-tempted-ways, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2052933477595828462)

## What counts as work, and what does the economy pay for?

- After a talk with his mom about house help pay, he wrote that labor demands more effort while cerebral work commands more money, because the economy pays for leverage: decisions and software multiply, physical work does not. (2026-08-09-ve-built-economy-where, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2086447174704091432; 2026-08-09-why-do-pay-thinking, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/article/2086442247239925760)
- He says sitting at a screen counts as work, because the brain is doing it. "my brain is doing the work." (2026-09-16-ve-been-using-computers, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2100228344478666780)
- He says intelligence is commoditized but the benefit goes to people who put it to work, a rich-get-richer pattern; capital also buys more access to the tech. (2026-10-08-intelligence-has-commoditized-sure, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2108193786535059687; 2026-08-24-speakjava-intelligence-may-have, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2091687137498607807; 2026-10-18-mean-floor-has-raised, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2111720743059050783)
- He says AI compresses execution friction rather than simply replacing jobs: costs drop and new markets and job types appear. (2026-05-19-most-people-think-ai, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056751441918955620)
- He says that as agents make execution cheap, value moves to judgment inside a company and trust outside it, so he would spend scarce time on skills, judgment, trust and relationships. "cheap cognition makes judgment more valuable." (2026-08-18-shift-toward-one-person, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7495549226785898498)
- He says generalists see problems that do not look like problems yet; go wide enough to see the game, then deep where it moves the needle. (2026-04-04-what-knowledge-actually-looks, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7446192714871918592)
- He says only time, money and knowledge compound, and time is the only non-renewable one that converts into both others. (2026-08-15-funny-thing-being-trillionaire, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2088623868382691789)
- He says busyness is not learning: many projects work only if they compound, and getting better at choosing what to work on is the sign you are learning. (2026-07-28-if-youre-getting-better, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2082040821805875272; 2026-01-09-if-youre-getting-better, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2009588808988450908)
- He says staying in motion across many projects compounds learning, and momentum is a superpower. (2026-05-25-ve-been-tinkering-lot, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2058984826578088192)
- He says not every build has to be a startup: he built a hand-gesture window switcher in a weekend just to remove an annoyance. "not everything has to be a startup." (2025-07-26-can-make-something-lets, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7354769866282684417)
- He objects to unpaid design assignments that crowdsource product decisions, and to interns paying for internships. (2026-10-15-aleksksaa-yourpersona-think-they, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2110677077603262642; 2026-07-16-sidposting-deyritoja-ive-seen, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2077716825492922614)
- He says pricing is a demand-curve problem, with purchasing power parity as one way to find the best point; in one reply about one product he said people would not value it enough if it went free. (2026-07-28-no-does-revenue-simply, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2082035252458143894; 2026-07-30-uraviva-people-wouldnt-value, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2082762621124808825)
- He explains a stock as a fractional claim on a company's economics. (2026-08-17-think-your-stock-fractional, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2089344066035257406; 2026-08-17-investing-your-own-business, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2089347546716217772)
- He expects compute to split: local for what must be instant, private and always on, rented for frontier scale, with AI compute getting cheaper. (2026-09-28-think-there-only-two, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2104587767506460874; 2026-06-14-meet-sijariya-shubhxho-wouldnt, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2066157669082451980)
- Ideas he has floated: idle PS5s as cloud-gaming nodes, "an Airbnb for PlayStations", and an AI agent built from user surveys that takes Amazon's empty customer chair in product meetings. (2026-07-16-startup-idea-what-if, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2077581887338373368; 2026-07-04-amazon-kept-empty-chair, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2073349208212681111)
- (2024-25) He wrote that preparation, not pressure, creates good work, that knowing when to quit is a skill, and that quick-commerce speed may reach emergency healthcare. (2024-12-01-biggest-lie-productivity-work, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7268905849153044481; 2024-12-01-most-powerful-skill-knowing, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7268911974007074817; 2025-01-02-quick-commerce-changed-how, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7280581758981435394)
