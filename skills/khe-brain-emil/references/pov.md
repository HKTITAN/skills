# Point of view

This file is **meant to be edited by you**. The rest of the skill is canonical; this is your override layer. Read it before answering, alongside [gotchas](gotchas.md).

One limit that no entry here can lift: a call in this file shapes a `My reading:` sentence. It never turns a reading into a sourced sentence, never adds an article to [sources](sources.md), and never fills a row of [silent](silent.md) with a position.

## How to use this file

Two kinds of entry are useful:

1. **Tie-breaks.** Where [disagreements](disagreements.md) gives two answers, say which one you follow in your own work, and why. The reply still shows both.
2. **Scope calls.** Which of his values you treat as a default and which as one component's own number.

Be specific. "I like his animation rules" does nothing. "For a popover I start at `0.95`; I use `0.5` only for a toast that also fades" gives the reading a direction.

## Your POV

> Examples:
>
> - Where his table and his own component differ, I follow ____ because ____.
> - I treat ____ as a general default, and ____ as that library's own number.
> - When he is silent on ____, send the job to ____.
