# Leadership, hiring and culture

This file collects what he said about hiring, firing, a leader working for the team, and his change of mind about candor. The sources include 2015 to 2019 articles, 2017 to 2019 keynotes and 2021 to 2026 videos. Figures about VaynerMedia and VaynerX are his own and carry the year he gave them. A few articles are team-written, and those are reported as what the article says.

## Whom does he hire and how does he interview?

- In 2013 he said a common strain among his favorite candidates for partners and employees was retail experience, including fast food order taking. (The Secret to Working in Retail, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-secret-to-working-in-retail/)
- In 2015 he wrote that in an interview his main question is where the candidate wants their career to go, and that any honest ambition is fine because it lets him help them get there. (5 Things You Need To Do When Hiring Someone, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-things-you-need-to-do-when-hiring-someone/)
- In 2015 he said he spends the first 15 minutes of an interview making the candidate cozy, asking about siblings, ambition and history, because it is his job to make it good for them. (Gary Vaynerchuk Interview on the Startup Grind LA 2015 at 1:26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3C4cuCc7jU&t=5175s)
- In 2015 he preferred the jack of all trades, balanced with specialists who are curious and can see past their one skill; in 2019 he repeated the same view. (5 Things You Need To Do When Hiring Someone, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-things-you-need-to-do-when-hiring-someone/; What Most People Don't Think About When Running a Business in 2019, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/running-a-business/)
- In 2015 he said to make the call fast: nothing is worse than hesitating, so choose between two candidates or hire both. (5 Things You Need To Do When Hiring Someone, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-things-you-need-to-do-when-hiring-someone/)
- In 2015 he called hiring friends a great idea because friends bring the culture you want, provided you are ready to enforce a meritocracy; he said he has fired friends and it can hurt. (Hiring Friends: Disastrous or Smartest Decision You'll Ever Make, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/hiring-friends-disastrous-or-smartest-decision-youll-ever-make/)
- In 2016 he said emotional intelligence trumps IQ in his building every day of the week, and that he can teach platform tactics but cannot teach someone not to be a jerk. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=1350s)
- In 2016 he said a 700 person company cannot be 700 A players, and early on he hired almost anyone because they were A players next to a market that did not understand social media. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=2115s)
- In 2017 he said he hires young, unproven creative talent instead of competing for famous creative directors, and cannot name one star creative director in the agency world. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=2565s)
- In 2017 he sorted people by EQ, meaning getting along, and IQ, meaning hard skills; those with both win and those with neither are in trouble. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=2295s)
- In 2017 he said VaynerMedia had favored EQ in its early culture and that in the previous six months he had let go of people who lacked skill, because a meritocracy matters. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=2295s)
- In 2017 he said empathy is about 80 percent of what he looks for in future leaders, and work ethic and gratitude are the other 20 percent. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=3060s)
- In 2019 he said emotional intelligence matters above everything else in hiring, and he will not hire a jerk with phenomenal numbers. (Management Strategies from the CEO of a $200 Million Company, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/management-strategies-and-tips/)
- In 2021 he said pure talent is secondary to people skills; he spent six months finding a first chief creative officer with emotional intelligence and tells people to "act like a normal person or leave". (3 Tips For Hiring Top Talent In 2021, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-hiring-top-talent-in-2021/)
- In 2021 he said he hires for intent and a lack of insecurity, plus a communication style that suits him because he hates micromanaging. (3 Tips For Hiring Top Talent In 2021, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-hiring-top-talent-in-2021/)
- In 2023 he said that when hiring for social media you should ask what they think, that you need to know what you believe yourself, and that a candidate who says they can make your grid great is out for him. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=1575s)
- In 2023 he said the first 7 to 15 employees should be able to do everything, which builds infrastructure for scale. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=1845s)
- In 2025 he said the quickest way to a big business is a number three, four and five and not only a number two, and most businesses stay at one to two million in revenue because the owner takes money out instead of hiring. (From Posts to Profits A Step-by-Step Social Media Guide For Businesses at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RJ3ujlMYlus&t=945s)
- In 2026 he gave his hiring order as insecurity versus confidence first, then the ability to communicate and be communicated to, and said everything else is trainable. (Social Media Is Dead at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWqpfx2iM2g&t=1530s)

## How fast does he fire, and who goes even if they perform?

- In 2014 he said the game is not hiring smarter but firing faster, and to treat someone as paid for six months even if you fire at two, with a four-month package. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tells You How to Rock SXSW Full Session Interactive 2014 SXSW at 1:24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aSOS9XZL48&t=5085s)
- In 2016 he said hiring is easy and firing is the hard part, and you must cut a top earner who does the wrong thing. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 57:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=3420s)
- In 2016 he said the skill is in the firing, so if someone in your inner circle is full of it and you can taste it, get them out. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 34:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=2070s)
- In 2017 he said you cannot spot self-awareness in a 15-minute interview; managers tie their ego to a hire and keep a bad one to avoid admitting error, and he once fired a hire after one day. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 1:09:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=4140s)
- In 2017 he said most people sit on the wrong person for a year or two because they do not want to be exposed as having made a wrong decision, and that he would feel accomplished if one person in the room fired the person they should be firing. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=2385s)
- In 2017 he said people fail to fire because of ego or because the person is financially important, and that people are fired faster for being not nice than for being bad at the job. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 57:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=3465s)
- In 2018 he told CEOs to audit every employee for who makes others miserable, whether top salesperson, best developer or co-founder, and said "Cancer spreads.". (How to Lead a Team With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-lead-a-team-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2019 he told a mortgage audience that a toxic salesperson costs more than they bring in, that a $300,000 producer may cost $500,000 on the back end, and that he had fired a top employee two weeks earlier. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=1710s)
- In 2019 he wrote that a CEO who looks the other way on an employee who keeps making others miserable because they bring in revenue cannot say they value good culture. (Why You Might Need to Fire Employees Who 'Perform' the Best, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/firing-employees-who-are-talented-but-toxic/)
- In 2019 he said he helps a person he lets go land another job, and that keeping those relationships led some former staff to recommend VaynerMedia to a new employer. (How to Fire Employees With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-fire-employees-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2019 he said he no longer does the firing at VaynerMedia because of its size, and that when he did he would spend a month working out how to feel better about it. (How to Fire Employees With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-fire-employees-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2023 he said he personally signs off on every firing at VaynerMedia globally, and that about 20 percent of the firings others wanted do not happen because he sees something that feels corporate. (GaryVee wants your attention w/ VaynerX's Gary Vaynerchuk Masters of Scale Complete at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbBO9tjw9bc&t=1530s)
- In 2019 he said we need to be thoughtful not to put human beings in a level of entitlement. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 54:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=3285s)
- In 2021 he said to admit hiring mistakes fast, since most people sit on them for a year or two, and that he has made many horrible hires in 20 years. (3 Tips For Hiring Top Talent In 2021, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-hiring-top-talent-in-2021/)
- In 2023 he put it as hire fast, fire faster, promote fastest: hiring is guessing, firing is knowing, and he does not think he has ever checked a reference. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=180s)
- In 2024 he said that keeping many employees for a long time cost him lost profit and reputational damage, and that when the person eventually went it was always sloppy and he was the bad guy. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=1080s)
- In 2026 he said hiring is guessing and the skill is knowing when to fire, and he suggested a 30-day exit clause in agency contracts and a couple of weeks plus severance scaled to tenure for employees. (The Safe Job Is Officially Dead at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TjdPqUI6Vjs&t=1080s)

## How does he manage, delegate and give trust?

- In 2015 he said a leader swallows pride, empowers people and eats it once in a while, because it all falls on the leader and nobody likes a boss who passes the buck. (These 5 Steps to Being a Better Boss Are So Obvious, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/these-5-steps-to-being-a-better-boss-are-so-obvious/)
- In 2015 he said delegation is about ego, not offloading weaknesses, and that 99.9 percent of things do not matter once you see it. (The Biggest Failure of My Career, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-biggest-failure-of-my-career/)
- In 2015 he described a 51/49 split: give 51 percent of the relationship and make the other person good enough to deliver the other 49. (The Best Way to Hold Employees Accountable, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-way-to-hold-employees-accountable/)
- In 2016 he said he cannot do everything, so he needs trusted people who do the work while he adds magic on top; early on he hunted clients and his brother AJ farmed them. (The Single Best Episode in AskGaryVee History AskGaryVee Episode 229 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LomXO4Snqw&t=405s)
- In 2017 he said he considered a **Gary tax**, which subtracts the lift from his own halo from a division's numbers to see whether the leader runs a real business. (Gary Vaynerchuk on Why Perspective Will Make or Break You Impact Theory at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lITalyctN4&t=2340s)
- In 2017 he said a manager works for the people and that the common mistake of new managers is thinking people work for them. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=1080s)
- In 2019 he said that as CEO of a company of 1,000 he has 1,000 bosses and his job is to listen and put each in a position to succeed. (The Entrepreneur's Mindset Gary Vaynerchuk USC Talk 2019 at 1:04:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVc0Qw1LSm4&t=3870s)
- In 2023 he said every morning he tells himself he works for his roughly 2,000 employees, and the hard part is caring on an individual basis. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=450s)
- In 2019 he said that when owners ask how to make employees care as much as they do, the answer is "Give them half the company". (What Most People Don't Think About When Running a Business in 2019, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/running-a-business/)
- In 2019 he said you cannot make employees care like an owner without equal equity, and he believes he works for his 1,000 employees. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=720s)
- In 2018 he said not to ask people to earn your trust, that he gives trust more than most CEOs would, that giving trust is offense, and that people say no too often because they fear ramifications. (How to Lead a Team With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-lead-a-team-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2019 he said he starts employees with free rein and adds restrictions only when they lose his trust, the reverse of most managers. (5 Common Company Problems and Solutions That Demand Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-common-company-problems-and-solutions-that-demand-attention/)
- In 2019 he said that full buy-in from employees puts you in a dangerous spot because a CEO or founder sees everything that is ahead and some friction with the people in the trenches is healthy, and that as a parent type of leader he gives people room to try, fail and learn. (4 Leadership Strategies of Great CEOs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-leadership-strategies-of-great-ceos/)
- In 2019 he described a **bat phone** style: he does not micromanage until there is an issue, then puts a finger in the biggest hole in the boat. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=855s)
- In 2023 he said micromanaging has never worked, that monitoring remote workers brings tension and fear, and that he delegates what is not the single most important thing. (are you making this leadership mistake, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/are-you-making-this-leadership-mistake/)
- In 2023 he said he delegates tremendously, and that the lack of ego of thinking you do everything the best is what he most looks at when working out why a friend, family member or investment is not scaling. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=405s)
- In 2023 he said he decides whether he really knows a new leader or is guessing, goes in optimistic, and makes the call at 12 to 36 months. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=450s)
- In 2023 he said he **judges the judgers**: he weighs what his reports say by how far he can trust their word, because some undermine people below them to protect their spot. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=270s)
- In 2025 he said to spend three months in one-on-ones with every employee whatever the company size, and not play in the middle between marketing and retention. (From Posts to Profits A Step-by-Step Social Media Guide For Businesses at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RJ3ujlMYlus&t=1080s)

## Why did he change his mind about candor?

- In 2015 he said that at Wine Library, which grew from 10 to 150 people with no training or onboarding, he fixed the problem of employees judging each new hire by sitting down with everyone individually, telling them to their face they were part of the solution, and then firing a couple of them. (AskGaryVee Episode 100 The Live Show at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUFbOa-8dPk&t=315s)
- In 2017 he described **honey before vinegar** as a frame: say you will do anything for the person, then say the hard thing and the insecurity behind it. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 2:11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=7875s)
- In 2017 he called radical candor the next frontier and said too much EQ leaves money on the table, while admitting he is not good at radical candor. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 2:12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=7920s)
- In 2019 he said his shortcoming as a manager was holding conflict in and then firing people without context, and that VaynerMedia is healthier after five years of work on it. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=2340s)
- In 2019 he said that on a scale of one to ten he used to be a one or two at recognizing and addressing a shortcoming of his own, and was on his way to an eight or nine but would never be a ten. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=2115s)
- In 2021 he said his biggest weakness was fear of confrontation, that for 25 years he spiraled over firing people, and that he is working on **kind candor**. (The Ultimate Life Advice For Success and Happiness Edmonton Canada Keynote 2021 at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz679oTO7Oo&t=2115s)
- In 2021 he wrote that his lack of candor made him withhold critical feedback, build resentment toward several employees and surprise-fire people once he was fed up. (Kind Candor and Why How You Deliver the Medicine Matters: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/kind-candor-and-why-how-you-deliver-the-medicine-matters-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2021 he wrote that too much kindness creates delusion and entitlement, and too much candor with no context or empathy is cruel. (Kind Candor and Why How You Deliver the Medicine Matters: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/kind-candor-and-why-how-you-deliver-the-medicine-matters-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2022 he said that for his first 20 years he thought candor created fear, and now he delivers the medicine with a lot of sugar. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=3060s)
- In 2022 he told the story of telling an employee on Thursday to keep up the great work and firing him on Tuesday, after two and a half years of frustration he never voiced, and said he did not have candor as a weapon then. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=2340s)
- In 2023 he defined kind candor as hard news with honey and real intent for the person to win, neither too red nor too blue but purple. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=1440s)
- In 2023 he described the conversation he now has: ask for 15 minutes, say you love them and that they do a lot of things right, name what they are not doing well, then offer how both sides can help. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=90s)
- In 2023 he said silence breeds entitlement and resentment: in his 20s and 30s he never told underperformers the truth and fired them after two years. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=1800s)
- In 2023 he said that if your boss rules by fear, you should first have one real kind candor conversation and see if it does anything, and that people who sit buffering a fear-driven boss need to quit their job. (How To Deal With The Fear of Making The Wrong Decisions l VeeCon 2023 Closing Keynote at 34:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09Pf5sIBNqk&t=2070s)
- In 2025 he said candor has been his kryptonite, that on a good day he is still a five or six out of ten on it, and described liking employees so much that he waited to fire them and then surprised them with a large severance. (What Happens When ChatGPT Replaces Google GaryVee Q&A For ActionCoach UK at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlNAM0M4WNE&t=765s)
- In 2026 he said he branded the fix kind candor and scales it in his companies so people hear the problem humanely and can fix it. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=2385s)

## How does he build culture and avoid politics?

- In 2015 he said he rejects the Steve Jobs style of cruelty and urges sticking to your own DNA; he repeated this in 2016 and 2021. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=630s; The Single Best Episode in AskGaryVee History AskGaryVee Episode 229 at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LomXO4Snqw&t=720s; The best way to be successful in 2022 Compass Real Estate Keynote at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjNq7jMUyWc&t=2385s)
- In 2016 he said that going from 20 to 640 employees in 48 months, only EQ kept the culture alive, not a two-week vacation policy. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=540s)
- In 2016 he called himself an HR-driven CEO, the head of HR as much as CEO of VaynerMedia, and said snacks, espresso bars and open floor plans do not create culture. (The Ambition of a Human Based Company, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-ambition-of-a-human-based-company/)
- In 2016 he described the **Rickey Henderson Effect**: a small act of acknowledgment returns a lot, so he meets every new hire for about five minutes in their first few months. (The Ambition of a Human Based Company, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-ambition-of-a-human-based-company/)
- In 2017 he said the Chief Heart Officer sits above the head of HR as the most important person in the company besides him, and that he did not want her called head of HR, so heart just seemed right. (Gary Vaynerchuk on Why Perspective Will Make or Break You Impact Theory at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lITalyctN4&t=2205s)
- In 2017 he said speed is what great culture is: when you are not worrying about being undermined you are executing. (The Importance of Speed in Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/importance-speed-business/)
- In 2017 he said companies say culture is first but act on it third because they do not want to spend the money. (The Ultimate Father And Son Duo And How Wine Library Got It's Name AskGaryVee 254 at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KIfUI94_5qU&t=1035s)
- In 2018 he said the biggest incumbents have never been quicker to copy features from startups, so you have to move quicker than ever to penetrate your market, and that speed is no question the variable of success. (How to Lead a Team With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-lead-a-team-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2018 he said "we've built a dictatorship around culture. We force it." (How to Lead a Team With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-lead-a-team-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2018 he said building culture takes financial commitment, that most companies play a short term game, and that if your investors measure you on 90-day returns you have to find a way to justify spending on your team. (How to Lead a Team With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-lead-a-team-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2018 he said the Chief Heart Officer sits second from the top and wins a debate between people and the bottom line nine times out of ten. (The Best Business Strategy No One Talks About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-business-strategy-no-one-talks-about/)
- In 2019 he said making employees feel safe is his number one aim, because safety creates speed. (4 Leadership Strategies of Great CEOs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-leadership-strategies-of-great-ceos/)
- In 2019 he said he looks at voluntary retention ten times more than profit margin, and can because he owns his businesses and answers to no board. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=450s)
- In 2019 he said short-term finance, with two-year exits and 90-day reporting, blocks long-term investment in employees. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 45:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=2745s)
- In 2019 he described safety with responsibility: the fact that he could fire someone creates accountability, and he creates very safe environments that he believes lead to good behavior. (30 Years of Leadership Advice in One Video Brilliant Minds Podcast at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sch1yA_xFZs&t=1530s)
- In 2020 he said that to scale you need culture, and the order is employees first, customers second, himself third. (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=1755s)
- In 2020 he said employee-first businesses would win in the next decade, and in 2023 he said they have won big for many years. (Why You Should Put Your Employees First: A Conversation With Arthur Blank, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-should-put-your-employees-first-a-conversation-with-arthur-blank/; are you making this leadership mistake, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/are-you-making-this-leadership-mistake/)
- In 2021 he said kindness, empathy and compassion, the things your grandma tried to teach you, are becoming the core things you need in your business to succeed.
