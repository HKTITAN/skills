- Breaks: it animates height; he animates `transform` and `opacity`, which skip layout and paint [2].
- Breaks: the keyboard shortcut plays the animation; keyboard-initiated actions never animate [3].
- Open: 600ms is longer than his table allows [4] and longer than his own drawer [5].
- My reading: a drawer that imitates the native sheet takes his drawer's value, and any other drawer takes the table's.
