# Glossary

These are the words a reader meets in his posts that mean something specific to him: names of his projects and features, phrases he coined, and common words he bends to his own use. Most of the coined phrases date from 2026 and cover agents, interfaces and Smash&Clash; the 2022-24 entries are mostly names from Homework Bros, Luna and his Notion communities. FAFO is the word he uses most, always as praise for learning by trying. Generic tech terms are left out.
