# Verification and rehearsal

One claim per line, each with its piece. The filming method is written for faux product UI. The stress tests are for a live chart. The removal test comes from a shipped app. Say which when you use one.

## Look at the real thing

- Agents usually verify by rereading the code or by taking one settled screenshot. Neither shows a fault that happens in motion. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- Stageshow films the demo, lays the frames on a contact sheet and reports what can be measured. The agent still watches playback for what a script cannot detect. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- Three scripts do the measuring: one films and flags, one catches one-frame snaps the sheet can miss, one checks that text and controls fit their boxes. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- Every answer in the last pass comes from a frame that was looked at, never from the code. (Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A dress rehearsal runs the whole demo at real speed and watches it as the audience will, before anyone else does. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Read every image you make, and confirm the page you filmed runs the code you wrote. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Measure, never eyeball: compare computed styles, and sample a morph on every animation frame. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A still cannot show a pop. Film the loop, then step through every press, menu, morph and the loop seam. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Matching endpoint screenshots prove nothing about the frames between them. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A contact sheet cannot prove that a line stayed attached or that text stayed steady: the gaps between samples are unchecked. (Stageshow, references/annotations.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Put a still of the demo next to a screenshot of the real product at the same scale. If a control looks foreign, read its real computed styles again. (Stageshow, references/looks.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## Order of work

- Most of the quality is decided before any code. Five of the seven steps (medium, look, story, examples, screen) come before choreography and rehearsal. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- Prove the still before any choreography: the opening and the payoff at both shipping widths, beside the gold example. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Clear the framing faults on the still before motion work. (Stageshow, references/framing.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Fix the largest visible fault first: framing, then hierarchy, then motion, then polish. Then re-film. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Film at the shown width and at 390, and at the brief's own width when it names one. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Film every look that ships, and check reduced motion: the poster is still and complete. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/craft.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A light pass does not prove the dark theme. (Stageshow, references/craft.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## The gate

- The film gate ends on a pass only when every line that is never intentional is at zero. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Every line the gate prints is a fault a viewer will see, even when the contact sheet looks smooth. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Passing is necessary, not sufficient. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Clearing a gate line by hiding, removing or marking the thing it points at is a bug. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A gate line the builder believes is intentional is looked at again, written into the handover with its frame, and judged again by the reviewer. (Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- An empty, late or gapped capture is inconclusive, not a pass. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A clean tool report does not close a composition question. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A checker can flag a jump and still miss a wrong path, an implausible gesture, a bad story or an unreadable crop. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Never cut good motion to quiet a detector: fix the real findings and name the intentional ones. (Stageshow, references/motion.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- A smooth virtual-clock export says nothing about live playback; check pacing on the real page. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/choreography.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## The bar

- The gold examples are the bar, not a reference. If yours is the emptier, flatter or smaller one beside the closest gold, that is the first fix. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Press like a product designer building a launch page they will be judged on. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Picture the most exacting designer you know screenshotting the one wrong thing and sending it back. If you can picture that screenshot, you have found the next fix. (Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Hold the scene beside the closest gold and say the one difference. (Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Ten film runs with the gate green and the frame unchanged means stop and bring in a fresh eye. (Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## The fresh eye

- A reviewer gets the frames, the gold examples, the checklist and a one-line brief. They never get the builder's plan, reasons or handover file. (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- The reviewer's first question comes before any checklist: does this composition answer this product and this page, or could it be any product in its category? (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- The bar for a finding is a frame, quoted by file. A predicted failure is not a finding, and neither is a predicted pass. (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Findings are ranked by how much of the scene they spoil, never by how easy they are to fix. (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- The reviewer fixes nothing and does not soften a finding because the build is nearly there. (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- With no findings the reviewer says "nothing" and lists the frames they looked at. (Stageshow, references/review.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## Handing over

- The handover file sits beside the component and lists the gold that was copied, what was captured, the gate's last line, the assumptions, what is unresolved and what was not checked. (Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Say which browser and device you checked, and say what you did not watch. (Stageshow, references/rehearse.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, references/verify.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Say what the tools covered and what you watched yourself. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## Stress and removal

- Looking good on calm data is not enough. Liveline's article tests wild volatility, near-flat lines with isolated spikes, and irregular arrival with random gaps. (Liveline, https://benji.org/liveline)
- Sharp reversals are the classic breaking point for a live chart. (Liveline, https://benji.org/liveline)
- Recognisable products are the hardest test of a faux UI skill, because errors are obvious. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- A removal test proves value: the same screens with the motion taken out, side by side with the real ones. (Family Values, https://benji.org/family-values)
- The same test proves a skill: the same short prompt given to a fresh agent with the skill and to one without it. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- He publishes what is still wrong: some icon morphs pass through awkward shapes and some rotations overshoot. (Morphing icons with Claude, https://benji.org/morphing-icons-with-claude)
- Agentation is described as a v1 that has already changed how he works. (Agentation, https://benji.org/agentation)

## What a check cannot judge

- Checks catch mistakes, not dull ideas. A demo can pass every check and still not be worth watching, so a person still has to look at it. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
- The model could not tell when a morph looked wrong; that judgement came from him watching it. (Morphing icons with Claude, https://benji.org/morphing-icons-with-claude)
- Beauty decides: the calmer, fuller, better-framed version beats the one with more story. (Stageshow, references/motion.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)

## Teaching by comparison

- A teaching pair is the same scene twice with one thing wrong. It is used to diagnose a fault, and the bad half is never a starting point. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills; Stageshow, SKILL.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- To make a comparison, change one thing: same content, layout, endpoints and input timing. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- If the lesson only shows at slow speed, the pair needs another go. (Stageshow, references/teaching.md, https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills)
- Side-by-side pairs make a rule such as not shrinking the app into a card harder to ignore. (Stageshow, https://benji.org/stageshow)
