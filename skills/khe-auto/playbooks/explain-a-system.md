# Explain a system

**You own this from "can you diagram this?" to a picture someone can read, or a clear no.** A diagram is the costliest way to say something.

1. **Decide whether to draw** with `khe-diagram`. A sentence or a table often wins. Exit: a yes with the reason, or a no with what replaces it.
2. **Pick the surface** with `khe-diagram`, by who touches the picture next: a finished figure, or a live canvas someone will keep editing.
3. **Draw it** with `khe-diagram`. Delete first, one or two accents, connectors before boxes.
4. **Skin it from the contract** with `khe-design-tokens` when the project has one.
5. **Review it** by the connector and budget rules in `khe-diagram`.

**Reply:** the file or the board, what the figure leaves out on purpose, and the assumption you made about who edits it next.
