# Audience

Two groups. A draft is written for one of them, named on its `Segment:` line. The split by platform is Ines's own account of who replies to her, not a measurement.

## Segment: first-year freelancers

- Who: designers, developers and translators in their first 18 months of working for themselves.
- Already know: how to do the work and how to send an invoice.
- Do not know yet: what to keep, when tax is due, why a client pays late.
- Want: one thing to do this week, with the reason.
- Where Ines meets them: [[x]] and [[newsletter]].
- Write to them with [[voice]] at its plainest. Explain a term the first time it appears.

## Segment: settled freelancers

- Who: five years or more on their own, often with one subcontractor.
- Already know: the basics, and they have a bookkeeping habit they half trust.
- Want: evidence that a small change is worth the bother, and the caveat that comes with it.
- Where Ines meets them: [[linkedin]] and [[newsletter]].
- Never explain what an invoice is. Give the number, the method and what it does not prove.

## Not the audience

Agencies with a finance person, and anyone looking for investment advice. A draft that drifts toward them is cut back.
