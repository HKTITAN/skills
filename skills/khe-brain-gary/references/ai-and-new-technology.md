# AI and new technology

This file collects what he said about voice, wearables, VR, NFTs, AI and the early internet. Most of this comes from keynotes, interviews and videos, with articles on his site adding the 2015 to 2017 wearables, voice and automation views. Every prediction here is dated by the year he made it, and some are timing calls, so read each bullet with its year. Several 2021 articles on wallets and buying are team-written and are left out, and figures about his own companies are his own.

## How does he reason about any new technology?

- In 2008 he said that at 17 or 18 he had to go to the library for Hugh Johnson wine books, and that with the internet he would have become a wine master, because technology changed the game. (Gary Vaynerchuk WineLibrary com Talks at Google at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2c_L60H-cpY&t=1935s)
- In 2011 he said his skill is understanding what people say they are not going to do but are going to do, and he asked a room who had once said they would never get a Facebook account to show it. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=585s)
- In 2014 he wrote that the mass arrival of the internet in 1995 and 1996 was the most substantial cultural shift for humankind, and that he meant the internet as a whole, not Facebook, Twitter or Tumblr. (This Is the Most Substantial Cultural Shift of Our Time, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-most-substantial-cultural-shift-of-our-time/)
- In 2016 he said technology exposes people and does not change them, because it gives more pathways to behaviors that always existed. (This Generation Will Be Fine: Why Social Media Won't Ruin Us, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-generation-will-be-fine-why-social-media-wont-ruin-us/)

(MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=1845s)
- In 2017 he said smart technologists fail by ignoring consumer behavior, because the tech may work years before people are ready, and he gave online dating that worked in 1996 but needed about a decade for the stigma to go. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=1485s)
- In 2017 he said panic about kids and technology has been wrong every time, with Elvis and the Beatles as earlier cases, and that he does not bet against humans. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 1:39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=5940s)
- In 2017 he said he will be obsessed with VR, AR or AI at 53 and will mock people still doing Facebook then, so his platform advice is tied to the day he gives it. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=1170s)
- In 2019 he said technology always wins and does not care that you have been crushing it for 20 years, with pager and BlackBerry users as the people who resisted. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=990s)
- In 2019 he said the internet is one of the one to five biggest inventions in human history, that 2005 to 2009 was nirvana, and that the backlash since is chapter two. (How to Build Confidence and Self-esteem Aubrey Marcus Podcast w/ GaryVee at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzEkg9aM4Hk&t=765s)

- In 2022 he said hoping for regulation when a technology arrives is always a bad strategy, citing the taxi and limo convention he addressed after investing in Uber. (Why You Need to Make Even More Content in 2022 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yemqSyqKqpY&t=495s)

(Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=45s)

(How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=1035s)
- In 2019 he said that if he were starting over he would call his company Vayner Voice, sell voice consulting and aim to be the best dev shop on Alexa skills and Google Home, though he did not yet see the scale. - In 2026 he said he has receipts from 2016 and 2017 on voice, because he became infatuated when he started seeing early AI and thought Alexa was a beast. (AI is Ending the Social Media Era and What Comes Next Gary Vee - Sinead Bovell at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUvWVF28CNM&t=405s)
