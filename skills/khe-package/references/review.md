# Review

Give the reviewer the video, the contact sheet, the three ideas' stills and the first line. Never the plan's reasons.

Ask: "Watch it once at phone size with the sound off, as someone scrolling. What did the first second show? Where did your eye go? Which frame would you pause on and send back? Would the first line make you stop?"

The reply: what the first second showed, the frame to send back with its time, the first line's verdict (stops or scrolls), and `Not verified:` what the reviewer could not see. Fix the worst finding, re-render only what it touches, and ask again with a fresh reviewer.
