# Point of view

This file is **meant to be edited by you**. The theme files record what he said and stay as they are. This file is where you say what you do with it. Read it before answering, alongside [gotchas](gotchas.md).

## How to use this file

Two kinds of entry are useful:

1. **Where you part ways.** For example: he says to pick one thing and go all in, and you run two products on purpose, for a reason you state. The skill still reports his position with its video, then adds one line, `Yours:`, with what you wrote here.
2. **What to press on.** For example: when testing a business plan, always check the distribution question first. The skill orders a pressure-test by it.

Be specific. "I like bootstrapping" changes nothing. "I will not raise money before the product earns 5 lakh a month" changes the answer.

An entry here is never reported as his. A reply keeps the two apart: his line with its `(Title at m:ss)`, yours on a `Yours:` line with no citation.
