# Family and parenting

This file collects what he said about what his mother and father gave him, how to praise, punish and raise a child, eighth-place trophies and entitlement, and his own family time and privacy. The bullets run from 2012 to 2026. Figures about the family store are his own.

## What he says his mother gave him

- In 2016 he says his success is entirely due to how he was raised, and that this is why he finds it easy to stay grounded. (For Tamara and All The Other Amazing Mothers Out There, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/for-tamara-and-all-the-other-moms/)
- He says his mother taught him to believe in himself while valuing others: she cheered for the right things, never gave "8th place trophies", played down school while teaching him to respect it, and gave fitting consequences. (For Tamara and All The Other Amazing Mothers Out There, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/for-tamara-and-all-the-other-moms/; Social Media's Negative Effects on Kids: A Platform or Parenting Problem, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/debating-the-negative-effects-of-social-media-on-kids/)
- He says she punished him for bad grades and made him face the result, but never made him feel stupid or unsuccessful. (Be Accountable But Don't Judge Yourself, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/be-accountable-but-dont-judge-yourself/)
- He says she praised character far above grades: at nine he held a door for an elderly woman at a McDonald's and she reacted as if he had won the Nobel Peace Prize. (3 Tips for Building Confidence in Kids, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-building-confidence-in-kids/; How To Build Your Child'S Self Esteem, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-build-your-childs-self-esteem/)
- He says she parented each child differently, and could read him, his sister Liz and his brother AJ despite their different personalities. (For Tamara and All The Other Amazing Mothers Out There, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/for-tamara-and-all-the-other-moms/)
- In 2014 he says the person he looks up to most is his mother, because she cannot complain. (The One Thing You'll Never Get Any Value From, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-one-thing-youll-never-get-any-value-from/)
- In 2018 he told the story of his mother losing her own mother at five and being raised in the Soviet Union while her father went to jail for a decade for being Jewish, and said she never complained once. (42 Years of My Life in 42 Minutes Interview with Drama at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MQ-4mr6q4Ak&t=1935s)
- In 2019 he says she only cares that he has slept, eaten and is healthy, and that this is what he listens to her for. He says to take parents' advice on what they are expert in. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 48:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=2880s)
- In 2016 he says he has thought of writing a book called Perfectly Parented. In 2025 he says he was parented uncomfortably well and that there is no such thing as perfectly parented. (For Tamara and All The Other Amazing Mothers Out There, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/for-tamara-and-all-the-other-moms/; GaryVee Your AI & Social Media Must-Knows + 7 Life-Changing Keys to Success Pt 1 - Jamie Kern Lima at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Df8QmoT6G8&t=495s)

## What he says his father gave him

- In 2012 he says his father told him, when he became the store's buyer at 22, that if you commit to a purchase you eat the wine. He says it has kept egg off his face. (Big Think Interview with Gary Vaynerchuk Big Think at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjYDTnCIIVM&t=1395s)
- In 2016 he says his best advice was his father telling him that his word is the one thing they have. He says it came when he was 14 and would say anything to sell, and that it turned him from a huckster into someone with real potential. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=810s)
- In 2018 he says his father, who hates lying, took the embellishing out of him over about ten years, starting when he worked the store at 14 for two dollars an hour. (Gary Vee on 6ix9ine's Trolling Millennials Fortnite and Dressing The Part - The Cruz Show at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqvIbndsoxw&t=180s)
- In 2025 he says his father treats a slight embellishment as the great lie of a lifetime and corrected him at 14 to say he sold 17.3 cases, not 20. (GaryVee Your AI & Social Media Must-Knows + 7 Life-Changing Keys to Success Pt 1 - Jamie Kern Lima at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Df8QmoT6G8&t=2025s)
- He says his father set the example for work ethic, and that without it he might not have thought anyone could work that hard. (This Is the Straightest Road to Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-straightest-road-to-success/)
- In 2018 he said his father left at 7:00 and got home at 11:00 p.m., the family took one vacation, and his mother did everything at home. He said he now has the internet and travel to outwork his father. (Why the Internet & the Blockchain Will Kill Middlemen Blackrock Keynote 2018 at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CoHpVwlF4YU&t=540s)
- In 2022 he says his parents' work ethic was too extreme: 80 or 100 would have been fine and they lived at 130. (Why You Need to Make Even More Content in 2022 at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yemqSyqKqpY&t=1260s)
- In 2016 he said his father never talked to him much, yet the night before his first card show in eighth grade said it would be a great learning experience. He says he never wavered after that night. (Eric Thomas Motivation Success & Public Speaking AskGaryVee Episode 223 at 36:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6katuaR5l4&t=2160s)
- In 2017 he says it scares him how little he appreciated the air cover his father gave him, and that a parent giving a child that cover lets the child stand on their shoulders. (The Ultimate Father And Son Duo And How Wine Library Got It's Name AskGaryVee 254 at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KIfUI94_5qU&t=1125s)
- In 2016 he writes a Father's Day letter saying that in his 20s and 30s he thought he was 90 percent his mother and 10 percent his father, and now sees it as 50/50. (A Letter to Dads Like Ours, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/letter-dads-like/)
- He says that focusing on his differences with his father was his own ego and fear that his father would be seen as the only reason he succeeded. He says he is no longer afraid of that shadow. (A Letter to Dads Like Ours, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/letter-dads-like/)

## What he took from it, and compassion for his parents

- In 2014 he says immigrants classically escape poverty through education, yet his parents watched him be a D and F student and let him sell baseball cards. He says he is grateful they did not follow their contemporaries' conversation. (A Note on Being Grateful, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/a-note-on-being-grateful/)
- In 2017 he credited his parents and his childhood, capitalism, coming from the Soviet Union as an immigrant, and never being taught to complain or expect anything from anyone else. (Confidence & Humility, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/confidence-humility/)
- In 2018 he says the real lottery is who your mother is, because parents put you on defense or on offense. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=270s)
- In 2018 he says he lived his first 18 years as a loser, which made the rest of life easy because he was not scared of losing; he lost a lot as a kid, did not speak the language, had no money and was a terrible student. (Why Kindness is the Key to Winning in Business at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7j8SRRqQxss&t=810s)
- In 2022 he says he has a fear of his parents dying that he lived in for his first 15 years, since both lost a parent young, and that he feels deep gratitude that he still has them. (Gary Vee's Emotional Confession About His Success & Family E207 - The Diary of a CEO at 1:12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ji_N6orURuw&t=4365s)

- In 2023 he said many people are angry at their parents while giving a grandparent a pass when the grandparent started it, and he told people who have not talked to a relative to leave and call them and say I'm sorry. (How To Deal With The Fear of Making The Wrong Decisions l VeeCon 2023 Closing Keynote at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09Pf5sIBNqk&t=1485s)
- In 2024 he says people judge their parents too much because there is no should. He uses his mother's loss of her mother at five and her father's jailing to explain why he does not dwell on what parents should have done. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=1350s)
- In 2026 he asked when it is no longer your parents' fault, and asked at what age you can stand on your own two feet and choose who you surround yourself with and what you believe. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=855s)
- In 2026 he says that on your 25th birthday you stop saying it is mom's, dad's or Instagram's fault, and he gives 18, 22 and 25 as his ages for being grown. (Gary Vee isn't afraid to lose it all betting on himself and reveals one thing - The Pivot Podcast at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ChdO5O6llw&t=1530s)

## How he says to praise, punish and raise a child

- He says he is wary of advice on parenting, and in 2015 said he is fine advising as a child or sibling but not as a parent. (AskGaryVee Episode 100 The Live Show at 53:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUFbOa-8dPk&t=3195s)
- In 2018 he says most parents parent from their own insecurities instead of what is best for the child's future. His example is sending kids to fancy colleges to brag that Ricky went to Princeton. (3 Tips for Building Confidence in Kids, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-building-confidence-in-kids/)
- His framework is **react instead of force**: give a child permission to explore and rope around what they are already drawn to, and build up steam around it, rather than forcing them down a path. (3 Tips for Building Confidence in Kids, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-building-confidence-in-kids/; 4 Parenting Ideas No One's Talking About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-parenting-ideas-no-ones-talking-about/)
- In 2019 he described his parents' mix of freedom and accountability: they let him play and win on his own terms and still made him respect the alternative, so he had to go to school and was punished for bad grades. (Why You Should Let Your Kids Lose, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-should-let-your-kids-lose/)
- In 2019 he says that a parent creating permission by saying yes, you can do this, is a big deal and makes a huge difference in how much kids believe in themselves. (4 Parenting Ideas No One's Talking About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-parenting-ideas-no-ones-talking-about/)
- In 2022 he says self-esteem is not delusion. He says his mother would not let him blame the sun in his eyes for a strikeout. (Gary Vee's Emotional Confession About His Success & Family E207 - The Diary of a CEO at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ji_N6orURuw&t=540s)
- In 2023 he said his mother reacted enthusiastically to the good stuff, which taught him that you can be bad at a ton of things like everybody else, so try as many things as possible until you find what you are great at and like doing. (How To Build Your Child'S Self Esteem, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-build-your-childs-self-esteem/)
- In 2026 he says telling kids they can be anything backfires, since kids think the parent is full of it. He says parents overcorrected into disproportionate love when the answer was the middle. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 52:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=3150s)
- In 2026 he says raising a child with 100 percent self-love and confidence while being 100 percent accountable and self-aware is a tight rope. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 51:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=3105s)
- In 2022 he says whatever you cheer for becomes the child's framework: looks make them vulnerable later, sports leave them lost at 22, and grades teach conforming to a system that judges every 90 days. (How To Say No Communication Techniques & Branding AskGaryVee 335 at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xo1mGiM86JU&t=1800s)
- In 2018 he said it is ridiculous that so many parents raise each of their children the exact same way, and that his parents raised him and his siblings differently based on who they were. (3 Tips for Building Confidence in Kids, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-tips-for-building-confidence-in-kids/)
- In 2025 he says being a good parent has no link to having a good student. He says to praise work ethic, not put honor-student stickers on cars. (Stop Fearing AI Start Using It Like This GaryVee w/ The Liz Moody Podcast at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCdX4pebCb4&t=810s)
- In 2025 he said to parent your kid based on your kid and not on what you think people think of them, and that the number one thing you can do for a kid is give them exposure. He said kids who can smell that you are making them do things to show them off will leave you. (AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 58:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=3510s)

## Eighth-place trophies, losing and entitlement

- In 2016 he says America is in the eighth-place trophy business, and that he lets his son Xander never score on him in basketball until 15 or 16. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=2520s)
- In 2017 he says the market punches a kid in the mouth later, so he did not let his brother AJ score on him until AJ was about 15. He says AJ became the better player. (Joe Rogan Experience #910 - Gary Vaynerchuk at 48:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OLHdzLpMg-s&t=2925s; Joe Rogan Experience #910 - Gary Vaynerchuk at 49:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OLHdzLpMg-s&t=2970s)
- In 2017 he calls his wish not to let his kids beat him a flaw he is not proud of. In 2022 he says he plays Xander less hard than he played AJ and is already adjusting. (Gary Vaynerchuk on Why Perspective Will Make or Break You Impact Theory at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lITalyctN4&t=1170s; Gary Vee's Emotional Confession About His Success & Family E207 - The Diary of a CEO at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ji_N6orURuw&t=810s)
- In 2022 he says for about 30 years it was popular to demonize losing and shield kids, which raised **zoo animals** who get eaten in the wild. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=1755s)
- In 2026 he says telling a crying child it is just a game starts teaching them indifference, and that sixth place trophies have destroyed our society. He says a kid who cries after a loss is a winner, and tells of getting in front of a crying boy after a 3 to 2 baseball loss. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=2700s; Inside a Warehouse Where Real Money Is Made at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiJvQBWJwvQ&t=1305s)
- In 2019 he says parents who build barriers against losing do kids harm, since whether adversity makes or breaks you is mindset. (Why You Should Let Your Kids Lose, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-should-let-your-kids-lose/)
- In 2025 he says entitlement and cynicism both come from how people grew up: some had parents give them everything, others heard we will never get ahead. (I'm Risking Everything To Build The Next Pokémon DailyVee ep 670 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7fgjfuiBF4&t=675s)

- In 2016 he says teenage selfies are the evolution of old attention-seeking, comparing his daughter in her room to a teenager on three-way calls in 1984. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 58:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=3510s)

(MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 1:01:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=3690s)

- In 2025 he says parents should delete the TikTok app from a child's phone rather than complain, and to be a parent instead of citing their friends have it. (AI & Social Media Playbook For The Next 5 Years You Can't Hide From This GaryVee Q&A at 48:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_RiVvFwZ2sw&t=2880s)
- In 2021 he told a dad to start a seven-year-old influencer with a closed network of about 12 family members, and to ask whether she loved the dance if it got six likes. (13 Ways to Build a Brand on Social Media in 2021 Clubhouse Q&A at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYilnGQ7Ub0&t=765s)

## His own family time and privacy

- In 2015 he says weekdays are intense, weekends are all in on his kids, and he does not play four hours of golf. (Before You Question My Work/Life Balance Read This, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/before-you-question-my-worklife-balance-read-this/)
- In 2015 he says he and his wife keep their kids out of public view until the kids can decide, and that others being open is fine too. (Before You Question My Work/Life Balance Read This, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/before-you-question-my-worklife-balance-read-this/)

In 2020 he says he never shares his kids and that a pitch made while he holds their hands has a 100 percent chance of him hating the pitcher.
