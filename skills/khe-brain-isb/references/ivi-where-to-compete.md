# Where to compete, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025

- Professor Siddharth says targeting has three steps: judge which segments are attractive on your own criteria, rank them, then ask what it takes for any company to win in each and match that against your own strengths. (ivi: Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-20-instructor-market-definition-growth-hacking-and-value.md)
- Professor Siddharth says what it takes to win is specific to a segment and the same for every entrant, and a startup that lacks it should prepare before entering. (ivi: Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-20-instructor-market-definition-growth-hacking-and-value.md)
- Professor Siddharth says Nintendo's Wii served an existing need for game-based entertainment while Sony and Microsoft were building a home entertainment hub, a latent need, so the two sides were not truly competing. (ivi: Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-20-instructor-market-definition-growth-hacking-and-value.md)
- Professor Siddharth says competitors sit in rings, with close ones to focus on and distant ones to watch, and a founder who does not watch the outer rings will be blindsided. (ivi: Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-20-instructor-market-definition-growth-hacking-and-value.md)
- Professor Siddharth says digital technology made niches viable because demand for an unusual item can be added up from across the world, which a physical shop with a limited catchment could not do. (ivi: Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-20-instructor-market-definition-growth-hacking-and-value.md)

## Operations, value indicators and process thinking, 29 May 2025

- The instructor names three routes to market leadership within a segment: product leadership, customer intimacy and operational excellence. (ivi: Operations, value indicators and process thinking, 29 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-29-instructor-operations-value-indicators-and-process-thinking.md)

## Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025

- The instructor defines a value indicator as the reason customers in my market segment come to me and not to my competitors. He says a premium experience does not qualify for a premium brand, because rivals in that segment offer it too. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says quality is too broad to be a value indicator, since safety, on-time delivery and taste are all quality. He asks founders to name the specific thing and to separate what they offer from how they deliver it. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor compares three ice cream chains: Cream Stone mixes signature seasonal real-fruit ice creams to order, Ibaco lets customers pick from about 40 flavours and pay by weight, and Baskin Robbins sells standard tubs. He says each gives customers a different reason to come. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says being first to enter a market gives no advantage by itself. The advantage goes to whoever is first to scale. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says a firm with no strong value indicator and no time to build one can ally with a successful player in another industry. His example is the co-branded card from Citibank and Indian Oil aimed at car-owning customers. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)

## Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025

- The instructor says all dissatisfiers green means survival, dissatisfiers and satisfiers green means competitive parity, and all three baskets green is when a firm gets competitive advantage. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor says process improvement is not only an internal operations matter, since it can produce competitive advantage, and he calls connecting operations to strategy operational excellence. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor says the real target of a delighter is the people the delighted customer talks to, who then ask why their own provider gives nothing like it and consider switching.
