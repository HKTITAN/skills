# Money and investing

This file collects what he said about spending, debt, housing, angel investing, and what he said about markets and assets in particular years. It comes mostly from 2015 to 2022 articles and keynotes, with Bitcoin and collectibles talk again in 2026. Every call about an asset or the economy is what he said in that year, not advice from this skill, and figures about his own funds and deals are his own. Where he holds or held a stake, the bullet says so.

## How does he think about spending and living below your means?

- In 2013 he wrote that he hates stuff and does not want planes, cars or watches, because that is not why he works. (13 Thoughts on being an Entrepreneur, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/13-thoughts-on-being-an-entrepreneur/)
- In 2017 he said he kept expenses low in his twenties, owned no fancy car, watch or suit, and rented an apartment at 33 so cash was ready when a chance came. (How Much Money I Made During My Twenties, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/much-money-made-twenties/)
- In 2018 he described the immigrant formula as make money, buy nothing dumb, save it for 13 years, then buy an asset, and said his dad worked hard and saved every dollar. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=900s)
- In 2018 he said he made $35,000 to $55,000 a year from 22 to 28 with no expenses and kept saving so he could later put in hundreds of thousands that returned millions. (The 6 Videos You Need to Start Your New Year, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/6-videos-need-start-new-year-veecap/)
- In 2018 he said he worked 15 hours a day in a liquor store from 22 to 34, bought nothing, and then put everything into Facebook, Tumblr and Twitter. (NOW is your time - Speech to Alabama Football Team Gary Vaynerchuk 2018 Keynote at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqlz3Lngh1U&t=630s)
- In 2019 he said that if you claim you have bills to pay, you should change your bills, because many people hold jobs they dislike to pay for brand-name clothes, cars and homes. (Stop Living For The Weekend, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stop-living-for-the-weekend/)
- In 2019 he said his mother lost everything in Russia and has always felt it is not secure yet, and that he saves money, which nobody talks about anymore. (ABtalks with Gary Vee - Chapter 12 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxgQRJ1SwZ8&t=270s)
- In 2022 he said he overspends on convenience and experiences, such as assistants, drivers, vacations and food, and does not care about stuff or cars. (Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=1710s)
- In 2026 he said his relationship with money has been the same all his life, living within his means, and that his best year from age 20 to 28 was $54,000. (Social Media Is Dead at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWqpfx2iM2g&t=900s)
- In 2026 he said 98 percent of people who buy a Mercedes or BMW like what it says to others, and that the monthly payment is why they stay in a job they hate. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=495s)

## What does he say about debt and student loans?

- In 2017 he told a woman with debt to take every job and skip leisure for a year, because one year goes by fast and debt compounds. (Eating Shit for 24 Months Doing What You Love & Monetizing Your Strengths AskGaryVee 237 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o34pWhhzrZM&t=270s)
- In 2017 he said kids who go to college to become founders end up starting over at 25 with 100,000 dollars of debt, and that you could take the same four years, save the same $100,000 and go work for free for the person you most admire. (The New School For Entrepreneurship, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/new-school-entrepreneurship/)
- In 2017 he called student debt a fraud, saying 23-year-olds leave college with $280,000 in debt, the piece of paper is not getting them a good job anymore and bankruptcy cannot clear it. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 1:09:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=4185s)
- In 2018 he predicted student loan debt would tear down the economy and that colleges would be blamed in the next recession as banks were in 2008. (5 Things To Watch For the Next Economic Recession, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-things-to-watch-for-the-next-economic-recession/)
- In 2021 he said debt is no reason to skip the years after college, because the loan will be there either way and you can still pay it off after waking up at 26 or 27. (What To Do After College? 12 Great Ideas Post College, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-to-do-after-you-graduate-college/)
- In 2025 he said it is fine to live in debt and be happy, as long as you do not complain. (Stop Fearing AI Start Using It Like This GaryVee w/ The Liz Moody Podcast at 27:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCdX4pebCb4&t=1620s)

## What does he say about buying a house?

- In 2018 he said the great mistake of his career was buying an apartment six months before he could have invested in Uber and doubled down on Facebook, and that he was getting weirder about ever owning a home. (How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=2430s)
- In 2019 he said he passed on Uber twice in the angel round because he bought a new apartment, and that one more year of staying put would likely have meant $400 million more. (4 Keys to Make Decisions Faster, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-keys-to-make-decisions-faster/)
- In 2019 he wrote that entrepreneurial people in their 20s to 40s should not buy a home, because it means liquidating most of their net worth for a down payment, and they should rent longer and keep that capital on offense. (One Man's Point of View: Buying a House in Your 20s – 40s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/one-mans-point-of-view-buying-a-house-in-your-20s-40s/)
- In 2019 he said a down payment of $20,000 to $200,000 does more in ads or the business, and a nicer home can be bought later at 39, 42 or 52. (One Man's Point of View: Buying a House in Your 20s – 40s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/one-mans-point-of-view-buying-a-house-in-your-20s-40s/)
- In 2019 he said to sell your home and rent to pursue happiness, and that he was destroyed in the comments for saying it. (Why Everything Is Scary Until You Do It DailyVee 593 at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fB5lqkTFtns&t=180s)
- In 2020 he said first-time buyers max their down payment, kill their cash flow and buy more house than they use, so entrepreneurs should rent and maybe buy at 39, 57 or never. (The Best Business to Start in 2020 Can Be Anything Interview with Sam Parr at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9Ap2JNr9QE&t=2430s)
- In 2022 and again in 2023 he said that if money is tight you should sell a nice home and live in a smaller one or move back with parents, and that pride and judgment stop most people. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 27:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=1620s; Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=2340s)
- In 2025 he said he is not against owning a home but dislikes big down payments and prefers renting for optionality. (Stop Fearing AI Start Using It Like This GaryVee w/ The Liz Moody Podcast at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCdX4pebCb4&t=1845s)

## Should you save, or put money into yourself and your business?

- In 2015 he said not to rush spending savings: talk to 50 to 100 people about real estate, startups and Wall Street first, and he cited a friend who put $150,000 into each of six startups that all failed. (Gary Vaynerchuk Interview on the Startup Grind LA 2015 at 1:44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3C4cuCc7jU&t=6255s)
- In 2020 he said a whole generation thinks about raising capital and appeasing venture capitalists, while his own approach was always to make money and reinvest it. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=225s)
- In 2018 he said he has never borrowed money because he wants things slower, and that he started VaynerMedia in a borrowed conference room to avoid rent. (NOW is your time - Speech to Alabama Football Team Gary Vaynerchuk 2018 Keynote at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqlz3Lngh1U&t=1890s)
- In 2017 he said many people raised capital they did not need and it destroyed their business, while others would have failed without a strategic investment, and that anything on your mind should be raised before you sign the contract. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 1:06:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=4005s)
- In 2020 he said to avoid VCs where you can and take as much as you can from a very high net worth individual, because a VC takes control, and on valuation he said "As much as they'll give you." (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 1:00:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=3600s)
- In 2017 he said to build enough attention that an influencer post becomes a deal for equity, because ownership is worth a whole lot more than 6 photos a year if you play your cards right. (When Patience Goes Platinum, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/patience-goes-platinum/)
- In 2017 he gave a **projection haircut**: subtract 20 percent from your projections and expand only if the math still works. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 59:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=3555s)
- In 2021 he said he stays within a good sense of what is coming in and going out, and that his dad ran the wine business with no line of credit, which trained him to play within himself and not spend what he did not have. (How Gary Vee Thinks about NFTs My First Million #209 with Gary Vaynerchuk at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uw6Zpq1U1A&t=450s)
