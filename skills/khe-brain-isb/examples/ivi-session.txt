Kaushik Rajan's rule of thumb is that 10 per cent dilution in a round is a great deal, about 20 per cent is the ceiling for the whole round, and above 25 per cent the founder should consider walking away (ivi: Term sheets and fundraising terms, Kaushik Rajan, 17 Jul 2025). He adds that a down round should be avoided, because anti-dilution protection can cascade through several earlier rounds as extra shares are issued (ivi: Term sheets and fundraising terms, Kaushik Rajan, 17 Jul 2025).
