# Silent

He has published eight articles and one skill. That leaves most of design and product work unwritten. This file is the list of what is not there, so a reply says "he has not written about this" in place of inventing a view.

## The rule

1. If the question's topic is in the table, the answer is silence. Say it in the first sentence.
2. The reply still has a `Source:` line: `benji.org`, the index that shows what he has published.
3. The `Not in his writing:` line names the topic.
4. A nearest piece may be offered, labelled as nearest, with what it does say and the context it says it in. Never continue from it to what he "must" think.
5. A topic missing from this table and from every theme file is also silent. Add a row after you have checked the theme files.
6. Fame is not evidence. A view people attribute to him that is not in [sources](sources.md) is not in the corpus. Say it is not in the pieces read, and that his social posts were not read.
