# Point of view

This file is **meant to be edited by you**. The theme files record what was published and stay as they are. This file is where you say what you do with it. Read it before answering, alongside [gotchas](gotchas.md).

## How to use this file

Three kinds of entry are useful:

1. **Where you part ways.** For example: Geoff Ralston's guide says it is an advantage at seed to use standard documents such as the safe, and in your market rounds are priced from the start, for a reason you state. The skill still reports the position with its person and piece, then adds one line, `Yours:`, with what you wrote here.
2. **Your stage and kind of company.** For example: "We sell to businesses and have 14 months of runway." The skill then leads with lines said about that case and labels the rest by the stage they were said about.
3. **What to press on.** For example: when testing a fundraising plan, always check default alive first. The skill orders a pressure-test by it.

Be specific. "I like growth" changes nothing. "I never take advice about consumer apps; we sell to hospitals" changes the answer.

An entry here is never reported as anyone else's. A reply keeps the two apart: the partner's line with its `(Title)`, yours on a `Yours:` line with no citation.
