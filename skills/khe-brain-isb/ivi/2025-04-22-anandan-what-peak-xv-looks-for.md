# What Peak XV looks for in early founders, Rajan Anandan

Sparse typed notes from a session with Rajan Anandan of Peak XV. The notes mostly record his answer to a question from the women founders in the cohort about what Peak XV looks for when admitting someone to its Spark programme, and a few remarks made while founders practised their pitches with him.

## What was said

### What the Spark programme looks for

- Rajan Anandan gives three questions for admission to Peak XV's Spark programme, and says the answer to all three must be yes.
- The first question is whether you have built a product or service that customers really love.
- The second is whether your unit economics work. He adds that you will not be profitable yet, and points to marketing payback.
- The third is whether you have found a growth channel that works.
- He says the programme comes with a grant of 100,000 dollars.
- His advice to founders is not to die and to keep pivoting.

### What he looks for in a founder

- He looks for whether the founder has deep insight about the problem they are trying to solve, the kind that makes him say that is interesting.
- He looks for founder-market fit.
- He asks what the founder has done so far, and whether they have been doing interesting things.
- He says there is no playbook to being a great founder.
- He says the obsession has to be with users.
- He asks whether the founder is solving a real problem and whether it can become a big thing.
- He says storytelling is key.

### Costs and customers

- He says inference costs are only going to go down, and that costs to the customer will go down with them.
- He says to go and sell to people who will give you money easily.
- He says the best form of funding is your customer's capital.
- He tells a founder to go deeper and deeper into the problem.

## Numbers given

- Peak XV Spark: a 100,000 dollar grant, as stated by Rajan Anandan.
