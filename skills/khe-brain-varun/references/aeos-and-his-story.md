# Aeos and his story

This file is the record, not the advice: what he says happened, when, and with what numbers. Every figure is his own account in a video, so each one carries the video date, and where two videos give different figures both are listed. Names and numbers the captions garbled are left out or described generically.

## What did he do before Aeos?

- He is a software engineer by training who studied computer science and engineering, took AI in his last or second-last semester, freelanced in Ruby on Rails, and then built software companies. (Kata 1 at 16:59, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1019s; PGX Ideas 2 at 1:34:57, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=5697s)
- He says he has had design skills since 17 or 18 and has blogged or talked about running businesses since 19. He was a software engineer for the first 10 years, and content is only a few years old for him. (Masters Union at 5:33, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=333s; Masters Union at 3:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=192s)
- He started Jobspire very young, in his teens as the note gives it, to help companies with recruitment. He describes it as a job platform that helped hundreds of thousands of freshers. (Kata 8 at 6:26, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=386s; PGX Ideas 2 at 1:42:55, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=6175s)
- During Jobspire he lived on about 15,000 rupees a month, sharing a small flat in Delhi with two others. They had a little funding and barely used it, treating it like fixed deposit money. (Kata 9 at 41:29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2489s)
- In 2015, just out of college and running his first startup, he emailed Sam Altman while emailing about 70 investors for money. He got a generic reply and counts it as one more rejection. (Kata 1 at 26:40, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1600s)
- Ten years before January 2026 he was, in his words, part of an unknown second tier of Indian startups, raising from tier 2 and tier 3 VCs with no way into the top circle of names like Lenskart, Zomato and Cred. (Kata 4 at 4:51, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=291s)
- He wrote a book about freelancing in 2017, and names a 2017 book of his as Pajama Profit. (Kata 7 at 12:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=725s; PGX Ideas 2 at 24:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=1486s)
- He ran a community called Avalon on Discord, which he counts among the largest Discord communities in India. He says he is still reaping the rewards of gathering a few ambitious people there. (Kata 1 at 1:12:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=4366s; Kata 2 at 52:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=3142s)
- Scenes, his company before Aeos, was a Discord-like product that a brand could run on its own app or web platform. It did okay on revenue, had weak product-market fit, and he sold it to Unacademy, though it did not end where he wanted. (Kata 11 at 14:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=867s)
- Before Aeos he raised venture money, and investors kept telling him to grow instead of being profitable. He says he followed that venture path for 10 years. (Kata 13 at 0:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=53s; KK Create at 20:21, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1221s)
- After his last company he spent a year as only a content creator, which he calls a fall from grace that became protection. In October 2026 he says he has been a creator for 9 years and had 100K followers at 26 or 27. (PGX Ideas 2 at 1:50:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=6653s; KK Create at 41:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=2477s)
- He counts Aeos as his third company, and says he has run, shut, failed, been acquired and had small outcomes many times. (KK Create at 1:42:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=6139s; Kata 9 at 37:29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2249s)
- His count of years running companies moves with the videos: his 13th year in December 2025, 14 years of hiring in April 2026, 14 to 15 years as an entrepreneur in July 2026, and 14 years in October and November 2026. (Kata 2 at 54:49, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=3289s; Kata 8 at 0:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=50s; Kata 10 at 0:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=56s; Masters Union at 3:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=192s; Why Belief Matters at 0:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=47s)
- In December 2025 he gives his age as 32 and says he has about 14,000 hours on Dota. (Kata 1 at 1:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=104s; Kata 1 at 56:25, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=3385s)

## How did Aeos start?

- About three years before December 2025 he made a deck in a house for a small core team, saying he wanted to be the best company in video and later in video games, 100% bootstrapped. (Kata 1 at 4:54, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=294s)
- He modelled the plan on Walt Disney's mind map of his company, and says in April 2026 that it went about 60 to 70% according to plan. (Masters Union at 7:10, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=430s; Kata 9 at 47:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2820s)
- Aeos started with zero money. In August 2026 he says he has raised no investor money for four years and Aeos is entirely bootstrapped. (Kata 6 at 35:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEnxvZXzo0E&t=2153s; Kata 13 at 0:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=53s)
- AVTV began by mixing skits into long-form informational business case studies, with early videos on Jio and Physics Wallah. (KK Create at 2:23, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=143s)
- He gives two accounts of where the editing school came from. In December 2025 he says it was Achina's unplanned suggestion in a chat after they struggled to hire editors. (Kata 1 at 21:52, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1312s)
- In October 2026 he says that when AVTV was running out of money he invited an editor to Bangalore who proposed teaching editing, and that his own first reaction was no. (KK Create at 3:10, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=190s)
- The services company began when Zoho emailed Achina after an RCB video. It became a channel called Builder Central, and clients later included Zerodha, Vodafone and Cleartrip. (KK Create at 7:11, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=431s)
- Rohit and Lavina, Achina's sister and her husband, were brought in to run the services company when its revenue was barely anything. (KK Create at 7:58, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=478s)
- Labs was started with Tejas, his CTO and co-founder from the Scenes and Avalon days, and Harsha, after what he calls an existential crisis about AI. (KK Create at 8:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=526s)
- The early offices were a house in Prestige Westwoods, then one and later two houses in Embassy Grove, then the first Hebbal office, a double office, and a current era where he says they will have multiple offices. (Kata 1 at 1:02:49, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=3769s; Kata 1 at 1:03:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=3819s; The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 48:59, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2939s; The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 49:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2986s)
- He gives two accounts of the name. In October 2026 he says the original word Aeos was very random and is a Dragon Ball character, agrees when another speaker says the name was changed about five times, and says he wanted a name that did not have its SEO taken. (KK Create at 1:39:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=5993s; KK Create at 1:40:43, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=6043s)
- Eleven days later he says the name was picked at random with no full form, and someone inside later came up with Attention Economy Operating System. (Masters Union at 7:10, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=430s)

## What are the Aeos companies and what does each do?

- AVTV is the group's first channel and its video editing school. As of December 2025 the school had run nine or maybe 10 cohorts of 300 to 400 students each, with 3,000 plus editors trained, and he calls it the world's largest. (Kata 1 at 22:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1359s)
- The editing cohort is 3 months long. By October 2026 editors were leaving him for 24 to 25 lakhs a year, some touching 30. (Kata 8 at 31:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=1877s; Masters Union at 53:07, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=3187s)
- YAS, short for YouTube as a service, runs YouTube end to end for clients. It is run by Rohit, Lavina and Ronit with Aeos as a co-founder. (Kata 1 at 43:37, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=2617s; Kata 9 at 1:02:51, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3771s)
- His channel counts for YAS rise over time: close to 40 client channels in December 2025, the ability to spawn three new channels a week by April 2026, and 70 to 80 channels done by August 2026. (Kata 1 at 29:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1746s; Kata 9 at 1:03:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3819s; Kata 12 at 40:14, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=2414s)
- Clients he names for the services side include Zoho, Zerodha, Vodafone, one of the banks and Coca-Cola. In July 2026 he says Aeos works with hundreds of enterprises in India. (Kata 1 at 27:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1647s; Kata 1 at 28:16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1696s; Kata 9 at 31:58, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=1918s; Kata 10 at 0:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=56s)
- Aeos Labs is the experimentation hub, a team focused on content but using technology, and it also has a commercial entity. Its work has included Indian Police Force on Amazon Prime, the technical implementation of a Mirzapur WhatsApp face-swap campaign, RCB, and Bangalore police avatars and a calling project. (Kata 1 at 37:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=2232s; Kata 1 at 38:01, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=2281s; Kata 1 at 38:48, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=2328s)
- Labs later did commercial software work for Amazon, dubbing for RCB, and started an ad films division, which he was very against. (KK Create at 18:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1127s; Masters Union at 11:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=665s)
- Video Vault is the group's internal video management software, also sold to customers and run by two engineers. (KK Create at 30:57, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1857s)
- 100x Engineers is an investment, not a company Aeos manages. It is run by his former team from Scenes; he put in cash and later more for a larger stake. (Masters Union at 12:38, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=758s)
- On owned channels, on 29 October 2026 he says Aeos owns about 11 channels internally, including AVTV, AVTV Daily, his own, Full Disclosure and 100x Engineers. On 3 November 2026 he uses having already built 10 channels as an example of why building the 11th is not a big jump. (Masters Union at 22:57, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1377s; Why Belief Matters at 16:48, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=1008s)
- He built a gaming channel from zero to about 150k on Instagram in 3 or 4 months by March 2026, and puts it at almost 200K followers in October 2026. (PGX Ideas 2 at 1:00:33, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=3633s; Masters Union at 22:57, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1377s)
- The influencer marketing company is run by Shreyansh, who was managing him, with his own channel as its primary client. He caps it at four or five people. (Masters Union at 29:18, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1758s; KK Create at 42:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=2525s; Masters Union at 30:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1805s)
- Aeos runs a fully offline school for creators, with a cohort of about 30 in a classroom, and it forces people to put out 90 videos. (Why Belief Matters at 20:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=1246s; Why Belief Matters at 22:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=1342s)
- His count of businesses moves with the videos: five profitable units plus game dev in January 2026, six or seven all profitable except the game team in April 2026, seven fairly profitable plus the game unit in August 2026, and every company profitable in October 2026. (Kata 3 at 24:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=1446s; Kata 9 at 1:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=99s; Kata 12 at 4:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=245s; KK Create at 29:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1757s)

## What does he say about God in a Box and the AI avatars?

- The first Labs experiment was God in a Box, which he says had millions of users and was shut down because Meta stopped all bots. AlphaCTR came next, and AI avatars were the fourth experiment. (Masters Union at 10:18, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=618s)
- On launch day in 2022 God in a Box was just ChatGPT's API on WhatsApp. He calls it the dumbest, simplest thing he built. (Kata 11 at 12:02, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=722s; Kata 11 at 12:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=770s; Kata 9 at 10:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=627s)

- On Feb 18, 2023 Labs tried script-to-video using open-source audio and Wav2Lip on a Telugu clip from YouTube. People laughed at the output, and a year later the avatar workflow was doing very well. (Kata 1 at 32:21, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1941s)

(Kata 1 at 34:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=2087s; Masters Union at 26:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1566s; PGX Ideas 2 at 1:02:11, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=3731s)

## What is the record on Unleash the Avatar and the game studio?

- The first significant money came at the end of 2024, and that is when he called his brother Rohan, who was at Y Combinator, and told him to leave to build a game. Rohan laughed for a few seconds and asked him to explain. (Masters Union at 14:16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=856s)
- The game is called Unleash the Avatar. The main character is guided by Vishnu and lent some of his powers for a few days, written that way so they do not get cancelled. (Masters Union at 15:49, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=949s)
- The first game team was seven people, one per department, and he wanted to know within 3 months of putting the core team together whether the project should live. (Kata 12 at 8:08, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=488s; Kata 12 at 5:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=344s)
