# Sources

This skill is Harshit Khemani's reading of Benji Taylor's public writing and public skills. Benji Taylor did not write it, review it or endorse it. His own words are at [benji.org](https://benji.org/) and his own skills are at [github.com/benjitaylor/skills](https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills). When this file and a piece disagree, the piece is right.

Every claim in the theme files ends with `(Title, URL)`, and both come from the table below. A reply cites a piece as `(Title)` inside the sentence and as `Source: Title, URL` under the answer. The gate (`scripts/brain-check.mjs`) reads the first two columns of this table, so a title that is not here cannot be cited.

## How it was read

- The eight articles were read as text, twice, on 2026-11-10. The demos, videos and images on those pages were not watched. Anything that lives only in a demo (the exact curve of an example, a pixel value on screen) is not in the corpus.
- The Stageshow files were read from a clone of the public repository at commit `299140f`. A citation to one of them reads `Stageshow, <file>` and links to the repository, never to a line number, because the files change.
- Every claim is a paraphrase. The few direct quotes in this skill are under 15 words each and were checked against the page text on 2026-11-10.
- `agentation.com` is a product page with three makers. A claim from it is the product's stated practice and is never reported as his sentence alone.

## Refresh

The corpus is a dated reading. Before answering a question about a piece newer than 2026-10-31, or when the user links a piece that is not in the table:

1. Open `https://benji.org/` and compare its list with the table. Open `https://github.com/benjitaylor/skills` and compare the commit.
2. For each new or changed piece: read it in full, add its row here with today's date, then add its claims to the theme files, one line each, in your own words.
3. Check [disagreements](disagreements.md) and [silent](silent.md): a new piece can end a silence or start a disagreement.
4. Run `node scripts/brain-check.mjs --self-test`. It fails when a claim cites a title that is not in the table.

Use only what is published openly. Nothing behind a login or a payment goes into this file or into any reply.
