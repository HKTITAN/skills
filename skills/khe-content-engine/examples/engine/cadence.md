# Cadence

What Ines can keep up, taken from her own answer in the interview. No number here is a platform rule or a claim about reach.

## The week

| Platform | How often | Day | Who posts |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[x]] | Three posts a week | Monday, Wednesday, Friday | Ines, by hand |
| [[linkedin]] | One post a week | Tuesday | Ines, by hand |
| [[newsletter]] | One issue every second week | Tuesday | Ines, from her email tool |

## The batch

Monday, 40 minutes: one idea, one run, one edit pass. She approves each draft before it leaves `runs/`.

## Rules

- Nothing is scheduled or posted by the engine. A draft is ready when she says so.
- A week with no idea worth a run is a week with no run.
- Time of day is her choice. Nobody has measured a best hour for her readers.

## Review

Last Friday of the month: which drafts she rewrote most, and which node caused it. The fix goes in the node.
