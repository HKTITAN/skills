# Hooks

The opening line of a draft, by type. A hook is a promise, and the body pays it in full. Pick the type the brief can fund, write it in [[voice]], and use a different type on each platform in one run, as [[repurpose]] requires.

## The correction

Name a belief the reader holds about themselves and say it is wrong, in one sentence. Needs: nothing from the brief beyond the claim. Pays off when the next sentence gives the real cause.

## The number

Lead with one figure from the brief, with its unit. Needs: that figure in the brief. Pays off when the body says how it was measured and what it does not prove.

## The instruction

Tell the reader the one thing to do, then why. Needs: a step small enough to do today. Pays off when the body is the step and nothing else.

## The scene

One moment, with the object in it: the invoice, the bank statement, the Tuesday. Needs: a moment in the brief that happened. Pays off when the scene turns into the point within three sentences.

## The admission

Something Ines got wrong, stated flat. Needs: the mistake in the brief, in her words. Pays off when the body says what she does now.

## Never open with

- A question the reader did not ask.
- A greeting, the date, or the word "So".
- A promise of a number of tips.
