In 2015 he said everyone has one job, to grab attention, tell the story and then sell (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 15:45). In 2023 he said attention is the most important commodity, but it only buys the chance to tell people about your product, and people err by expecting a purchase after they earn it (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 42:00). In 2026 he said attention can be very overpriced and very underpriced, that there is no general rule, and that what matters is attention per behavior in the world we live in today (If You're Not Posting You're Falling Behind at 9:45).
Also: in 2014 he told small owners with no staff or budget to take one, two or three hours from staffing, strategizing and selling and put them into content, because lack of resources is no excuse (How Small Businesses Can Compete With Fortune 500s).
Also: in 2021 he said you do not need to target accounts with 100,000 followers, because people with smaller followings but tremendous engagement who live nearby are enough to win with (Instagram DM: Direct Messaging to Network).
