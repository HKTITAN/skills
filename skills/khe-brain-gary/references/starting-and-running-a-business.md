# Starting and running a business

This file collects what he said about who should start a business, how to start, selling, pricing, cash, competition, raising money, partners, scaling and quitting. The sources are articles from 2013 to 2021 and keynotes and interviews from 2015 to 2026. Read each claim with its year, since his advice on tools and markets is dated. Figures about VaynerMedia and Wine Library are his own, and some posts on his site are team-assisted.

## Who should and should not be an entrepreneur

- In 2013 he said entrepreneurship is mostly DNA, a skill like singing or basketball that some people are born with, though outliers can be forced into it and become great. (Entrepreneurial Dna. Do You Have It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/entrepreneurial-dna-do-you-have-it/)
- In that same 2013 post he adds that some people work their way up to a high skill set through sheer determination, that he is an extreme version of the DNA view, and that there are many places in between. (Entrepreneurial Dna. Do You Have It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/entrepreneurial-dna-do-you-have-it/)
- In 2015 he said you cannot decide to want to be an entrepreneur, you either are one or you are not, and you only become one by doing the work and selling. (So You Wanna Be an Entrepreneur…, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/so-you-wanna-be-an-entrepreneur/)
- In that 2015 post he also says a full-time job rules out being an entrepreneur, then allows that entrepreneurial tendencies are fine as long as you are happy. (So You Wanna Be an Entrepreneur…, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/so-you-wanna-be-an-entrepreneur/)
- In 2016 he said entrepreneurship is not a developable skill. He compares it to the NBA, where players combine a massive amount of born athleticism and talent with years of daily practice. (The Entrepreneurial Spirit: Are Entrepreneurs Born or Made, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-entrepreneurial-spirit-are-entrepreneurs-born-or-made-2/)
- His 2016 list of five entrepreneur traits begins with salesmanship, a chip on your shoulder and an independent spirit that trusts its own final call. (The Entrepreneurial Spirit: Are Entrepreneurs Born or Made, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-entrepreneurial-spirit-are-entrepreneurs-born-or-made-2/)
- In that 2016 article he says everybody is born with some capability to run a business but 90 percent are born with the capability to run it into the ground, and that sure, anyone can start a business, but only the top 25 percent will grow into multi-million dollar companies with more than one employee. (The Entrepreneurial Spirit: Are Entrepreneurs Born or Made, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-entrepreneurial-spirit-are-entrepreneurs-born-or-made-2/)
- In 2016 he told Lewis Howes that entrepreneurship can be taught only up to a point, and that 18 hours a day might only produce a business making $100,000 a year. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=270s)
- In 2016 he said hard work moves you within your range: it can take an $83,000 business to $337,000 but cannot turn you into a Lebron or a Beyonce, and some people would be great number threes and sevens. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 59:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=3555s)
- In 2016 he defined a pure-bred entrepreneur as someone who cannot breathe at the thought of working for someone, and would live on a couch with roommates and cockroaches to build something. He allows others "entrepreneurial tendencies". (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=1755s)
- In 2013 he said many people trying to be number one are really number twos and threes. In 2015 he told a USC room it was full of future twos, threes and fours, and that the difference is having a stomach. (Coding Vs. Selling, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-cant-code-and-you-cant-sell-crap/; Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=360s)
- In 2017 he said being number one is firefighting with no one to blame, that many people are really artists or number threes, and that the downside of entrepreneurship is not discussed. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=675s)
- In 2016 he said it was the greatest generation of **fake entrepreneurship** in a long time and that many people would be hurt financially and emotionally. (My Advice for First-Time Entrepreneurs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advice-first-time-entrepreneurs/)
- In 2018 he said 99 percent of startups will fail in a bubble made by cool status, near-zero cost and incubator money, and that many would do better as number 17 or 94 at a startup than number one of a weak idea. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=450s)
- In 2019 he told USC that failing 18 to 24 months in, with capital plentiful, entry cheap and the internet at full scale, means you are not an A at entrepreneurship, then added that he is bad at piano. (The Entrepreneur's Mindset Gary Vaynerchuk USC Talk 2019 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVc0Qw1LSm4&t=495s)
- In 2017 he said entrepreneurship has been put on a rockstar pedestal, which is great for him, but that the pendulum has swung too far. In 2019 he called himself among the loudest voices in entrepreneurship telling people they are not entrepreneurs. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=2295s; Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=2115s)

## How to start and what to start with

- In 2014 he said he built Wine Library with no money by joining every wine forum and acting as a user, not a pitcher, with patience from 1997 to 2003. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tells You How to Rock SXSW Full Session Interactive 2014 SXSW at 1:12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aSOS9XZL48&t=4320s)
- In 2016 he gave three things for starting out: cash is oxygen, know your strength and surround yourself with the other pillars, and find the white space where nobody else is marketing or storytelling. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=765s)
- In 2016 he told first-time entrepreneurs to be practical about money, saying cash is oxygen, and asked if you have one year's worth of rent and overhead. (My Advice for First-Time Entrepreneurs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advice-first-time-entrepreneurs/)
- In 2017 he said he persevered through the dot-com crash and 9/11 because he was practical, had little overhead and was not romantic, so he kept buying and selling wine, liquor and beer and kept his expenses below the money coming in. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=180s)
- In 2017 he told a caller to take a night job at a restaurant, start a club there every Wednesday night, test it, then get investors or save money for his own space. His answer was to do something. (Eating Shit for 24 Months Doing What You Love & Monetizing Your Strengths AskGaryVee 237 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o34pWhhzrZM&t=810s)
- In 2017 he said that young people can taste many ideas to avoid being crippled by what-if, but the better formula for fast wealth is to triple down on one thing. (The Ultimate Advice For Every 20 Year Old at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qRj144Gd7EA&t=90s)
- In 2018 he said the practical default for someone who must make money within 24 months is retail arbitrage on Craigslist, eBay, Facebook Marketplace and Letgo, and described the flip: scan items at a dollar store, check the eBay price, and buy if a $2 item sells for $9. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=585s; Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=405s)
- In 2018 he said the quickest way to learn how to sell is to buy or get something and sell it, on places like Facebook marketplace, eBay and Amazon. (Your Problems Don't Matter Here's Why Talk at the Precious Dreams Foundation at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALZTZFMeWXU&t=720s)
- In 2019 he said that for the first time in his career he had eliminated the excuse of having no money, and that the Craigslist free section is how. (The Ultimate Flip: How to Find Free Stuff to Sell on Facebook Marketplace Craigslist and eBay, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-ultimate-flip-how-to-find-free-stuff-to-sell-on-facebook-marketplace-craigslist-and-ebay/)
- In 2018 he said that if you build a community first you will not make money for a couple of years, so decide whether you will live at home or keep another job, and plan to sell nothing for a couple of years. (How One Video Can Change Your Life at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e8COwvlFUc&t=1305s)
- In 2019 he said that at a young age people should taste things, thinking of starting a business the way you find your favorite food, and that creative and helping people are too broad, so you have to go narrower. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 52:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=3150s)
- In 2024 he said one test before launching a first product is whether you brought a lot of value to your audience before you sold to them, in years and not days, and that you should make sure you believe in what you are selling. (2 things I'm focused on for 2024: Pbcpg & Live-Streaming, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-things-im-focused-on-for-2024/)
- In 2025 he said buying a business can cost $10,000, and that someone earning $80,000 or more who gets humble and strategic can save toward buying a place they love as a customer. (The Secret To Building Wealth That Anyone Can Apply at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0OuXdCXxOl4&t=495s)

## Spotting opportunities, ideas and execution

- In 2014 he said that anytime he is frustrated because something is not happening as fast as he would like he treats it as inefficiency, and that people should think about what current technology can solve that was once accepted as unsolvable. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tells You How to Rock SXSW Full Session Interactive 2014 SXSW at 59:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aSOS9XZL48&t=3555s)
- In 2015 he said time is the asset to build around because people massively overpay for it, and that Uber, Airbnb and Instacart were previews, not anomalies. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=855s)
- In 2017 he said he passed on Uber twice at a $4 million valuation and invested later at $100 million, and that he missed that Uber sold the perception of time. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=1260s)
- In 2024 he said the best businesses fix something everyone complains about that has not been fixed, and that pain points are bigger than wants. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=540s)
- In 2012 he predicted that tea would be monstrous in the United States in five to nine years. In 2016 he predicted meditation would be a big business within five years, the counterpart to what SoulCycle and Bikram Yoga were in fitness. (Big Think Interview with Gary Vaynerchuk Big Think at 33:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xjYDTnCIIVM&t=1980s; Meditation… The Next Big Business Wave, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/meditation-the-next-big-business-wave/)
- In 2015 he said ideas are not the game and execution is. He said time spent on NDAs, trademarks and patents is time not executing, and that there is no original idea. (3 Ways To Go Out Of Business…Fast, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-ways-to-go-out-of-business-fast/)
- In 2017 he said he would give away every idea for AI, VR and AR because 99.9 percent of listeners will not act. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=1035s)
- In 2017 he said strategy comes out of execution: we glorify strategy and underestimate execution, and hindsight makes people look like geniuses. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 37:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=2250s)
- In 2021 he said he rarely loves an idea but loves execution, and that he has lost bets when the executor could not deliver, as with Yo Bongo. (How Gary Vee Thinks about NFTs My First Million #209 with Gary Vaynerchuk at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uw6Zpq1U1A&t=1485s)
- In 2025 he said everyone shoots ideas around at dinner and does nothing, and that the idea person can still make the most by finding operators. He tells the Uber story, where the originator of the idea never ran the company. (Future of Online Business Community AI & Monetizing Your Passion at 1:12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RF2AD_kk7Qs&t=4320s; Future of Online Business Community AI & Monetizing Your Passion at 1:17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RF2AD_kk7Qs&t=4635s)
- In 2015 he said some businesses he sat on for years to let them marinate and refine, while after a dinner with Jerome Jarre a talent agency for Vine stars existed the next day. (Ok You Decided to Take the Leap…What Now, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/ok-you-decided-to-take-the-leapwhat-now/)
- In 2014 he said that if the product is bad you are dead whatever the marketing, and in 2015 that no marketing budget need stop a strong product because people spread it. (Build the Steak not the Sizzle, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/build-the-steak-not-the-sizzle/; Great Advertising Won't Save Your Shit Product, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/great-advertising-wont-save-your-shit-product/)
- In 2015 he said his strategy is mostly intuition because there was no data at the start of each new thing, but that running a business needs data to make payroll. (#AskGaryVee Episode 69: Monetizing Media Sites Sad Super Bowl Ads & Solid Data, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-69-monetizing-media-sites-sad-super-bowl-ads-solid-data/)

## Selling and customers

- In 2015 he said sales is the DNA of a business from day one, because the greatest TV show or cup of coffee earns nothing if you cannot sell it. (Entrepreneurial Oxygen: The Cash Cycle and My Business Dna, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/entrepreneurial-oxygen-the-cash-cycle-and-my-business-dna/)
- In 2016 he said many people will fail in business because they underestimate salesmanship, and that artists starve because they cannot sell. (The Law of Attraction Importance of Sales Skills & Working Smarter AskGaryVee 232 at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R9GEnEFJtXE&t=135s)
- In 2008 he said staff should ask the customer what they have liked in the past before recommending a wine, and in 2014 that he sold wine as a teenager by listening and asking questions. (Gary Vaynerchuk WineLibrary com Talks at Google at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2c_L60H-cpY&t=2565s; How to Sell Wine as a 15-Year-Old Kid, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-sell-wine-as-a-15-year-old-kid/)
- In 2011 he said to stop hunting and start farming, because lifetime value comes from humans and you cannot automate real feelings. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=2790s)
- In 2015 he told readers to go right in for the kill and ask for the sale plainly: "Just. Freaking. Ask." (To Make the Sale You Need to Go In for the Kill, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/to-make-the-sale-you-need-to-go-in-for-the-kill/)
- In 2015 he also wrote that in sales you never go straight for the kill and should take your time. (Stop Pretending That Fundraising Is Different From Sales, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stop-pretending-that-fundraising-is-different-from-sales/)
- In 2011 and 2017 he warned against closing on the first transaction, calling it the move of a 19-year-old, and told a founder who asked a stranger for a tweet to give value first. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 59:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=3555s; What Every 19 Year Old Gets Wrong, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/every-19-year-old-gets-wrong/)
- In 2015 he said to spend zero time convincing people, and to reach everyone in your industry, find **the eighteen** who are intrigued and work with them. "Don't try to convert the unconvertable." (5 Core Values of Business from GaryVee, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-core-values-of-business-from-garyvee/)
- He repeated the point in 2017, 2019 and 2023: do not try to convince the unconvinced, and move to the next door, even ending an hour-long pitch after 15 minutes when he senses it is over. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 1:22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=4950s; 13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=1935s; How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=1440s)
- In 2019 he said there are a million reasons a company will not work with you even if you have the better product and a cheaper price, such as a brother-in-law married to the CMO of the agency they use or investors with a different agenda. (The Most Important Concept for Sales Success in 2019, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/concept-for-sales-success/)
- In 2016 and 2017 he said the best sellers understand the other side without being told, and that you check your own wants at the door. (Empathy: One of the Keys to My Business Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/empathy-one-of-the-keys-to-my-business-success/; Put Yourself in Your Customers Shoes, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/put-customers-shoes/)
- In 2017 and 2019 he said automation is not the answer in sales: people love bots, auto-replies and CRMs, but sales is about people, and you have to build relationships first and empathize with the customer. (5 Best Tips For Salespeople, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-best-tips-salespeople/; How to Sell, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-sell/)
- In 2019 he said B2B sales work the same way as B2C and that it is a lot easier to sell B2B because on LinkedIn you can filter by industry, job title, location or company. He says he operates a 150 million dollar revenue business where 90 percent of his time is spent selling B2B. (How to Sell, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-sell/)
- In 2019 he said selling is not scary but selling something you do not believe in is, and in 2024 that nothing is better than selling what you believe in. (I Love Selling. Here's Why, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-love-sales/; How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 1:26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=5175s)
- In 2019 he told small-business sellers to put a dollar amount on hidden costs and make it an ROI conversation, since cash and short-term sales matter more to them than to big brands. (How to Sell to Small Businesses, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-sell-to-small-businesses-smb/)
- In 2015 he said that if a pissed-off customer is right you fix it whatever the cost, and if wrong you empathize and then protect your business by explaining the rules. (The Surefire Steps for Dealing With a Pissed-Off Customer, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-surefire-steps-for-dealing-with-a-pissed-off-customer/)
- In 2016 he said the customer is always right as long as you want their money, and that whoever pays has the leverage but you can still fire a customer. (The Customer is Always Right as Long as You Want Their Money, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-customer-is-always-right-as-long-as-you-want-their-money/)
- In 2021 he said that if you get attention but no conversions, fix the offer rather than the platform, by changing the price, a day or a word, because the problem is at the back. (The Ultimate Life Advice For Success and Happiness Edmonton Canada Keynote 2021 at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz679oTO7Oo&t=1485s)
- In 2015 he said the best-selling stuff is usually unscalable, because if it scaled everyone would do it, and in 2018 that the magic is in the unscalable. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1935s; How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=2295s)
- In 2015 he said not to market a pricier product to your existing audience. (How You Should Market Your New Product to Existing Customers, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-you-should-market-your-new-product-to-existing-customers/)

## Pricing, negotiation, free work and cash

- In 2015 he wrote that customer satisfaction, company culture, budget, marketing and hiring all matter, but that one thing always transcends everything else, and that is cash. (Entrepreneurial Oxygen: The Cash Cycle and My Business Dna, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/entrepreneurial-oxygen-the-cash-cycle-and-my-business-dna/)
- In 2015 he listed thinking over executing and a unique idea among the top three ways to go out of business fast, and said execution is the name of the game. (3 Ways To Go Out Of Business…Fast, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-ways-to-go-out-of-business-fast/)
- In 2016 he said under-funded founders run out of cash in six months and over-funded ones never build revenue muscle, because they focus on the next round. (My Advice for First-Time Entrepreneurs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advice-first-time-entrepreneurs/)
- In 2013 he said he has never worried about the bottom line and that growing quickly was always worth more to him than profit. In 2015 he said he ran an early business at $40,000 a year to grow it. (13 Thoughts on being an Entrepreneur, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/13-thoughts-on-being-an-entrepreneur/; The Two Strategies Needed to Maintain Corporate Culture During Business Growth, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-two-strategies-needed-to-maintain-corporate-culture-during-business-growth/)
- In 2015 he said that for sponsors you should ask for as much as possible because you do not know your ceiling, and double or triple what you actually want. (Don't Short-Change Your Podcast's Ad Inventory, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/dont-short-change-your-podcasts-ad-inventory/)
- In 2016 he said never do the next job for what you did the last one for until the market says no, and that the $500 customer never becomes the $50,000 customer. (Gary Vaynerchuk on CreativeLive Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrJSjSy8Ymo&t=2115s; Gary Vaynerchuk on CreativeLive Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LrJSjSy8Ymo&t=2025s)
- In 2019 he said to test your price ceiling by asking a little more on the next scope, since one no does not mean the market does not want it. (50 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Digital Agency Expo Keynote at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Wcg15FaUnU&t=2610s)
- In 2021 he told an agency owner his price is an arbitrary call, and that to scale you either go lower-price and stack many, as at VaynerMedia, or go higher, and neither style is better. (13 Ways to Build a Brand on Social Media in 2021 Clubhouse Q&A at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYilnGQ7Ub0&t=1125s)
- In 2025 he said his first speaking fee, about 2006, was $5,000 and he raised it until the market said no at $25,000 and above. He says to expect seven to ten nos before you know your price. (What Happens When ChatGPT Replaces Google GaryVee Q&A For ActionCoach UK at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlNAM0M4WNE&t=450s; What Happens When ChatGPT Replaces Google GaryVee Q&A For ActionCoach UK at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlNAM0M4WNE&t=540s)
- In 2016 he said to negotiate for a deal both sides are satisfied with, to take their number if it matches yours, and to draw a line in the sand before you start and hold it. (The Nuances of Negotiating, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-nuances-of-negotiating/)
- In 2019 he said that going silent after a seller says no gave the seller time to rethink, and turned a no on a $10 item into a yes at $8. (6 Garage Sale Flipping Strategies to Make Extra Money, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/garage-sale-flipping/)
- In 2014 he said exposure counts as payment and that he is all in on spec work. He accepts critics say he had an easier start, but does not see why people do not treat free work as part of a larger strategy. (Working for Free: The Debate, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/working-for-free-the-debate/)
- In 2015 he said free work should be strategic, creating leverage for paid work, and to work out who needs to see it for that to happen. (Why You Shouldn't Charge For Your Best Work, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-shouldnt-charge-for-your-best-work/)
- In 2019 he said free work is worth doing because work nobody pays for is worth zero anyway, and later that it is a personal call where you choose to be taken advantage of. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 57:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=3420s; 50 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Digital Agency Expo Keynote at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Wcg15FaUnU&t=2565s)

## Competition and the market

- In 2013 he said entrepreneurship is war and that he wanted everyone he competed with to succeed as long as he succeeded a little more. (13 Thoughts on being an Entrepreneur, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/13-thoughts-on-being-an-entrepreneur/)
- In 2014 he wrote that he tears competitors down as quickly as possible when clients raise them. In 2015 he said the biggest mistake is looking around to see who else is in the running, and that ignoring competitors is an advantage. (#AskGaryVee Episode 50: White Lies Klout Scores & Musical Chairs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-50-white-lies-klout-scores-musical-chairs/; 5 Negative Business Situations and How to Turn Them Around, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-negative-business-situations-and-how-to-turn-them-around/)
- In 2017 and 2019 he said he ignores competitors because he is obsessed with the end consumer, that competition is a storm you cannot pay attention to, and that being copied is good. (Don't Take Attention For Granted, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/taking-attention-granted/; 13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 1:07:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=4050s)
- In 2015 he said that when you are David you do not play Goliath's game, and he made sure bigger agencies did not know how large VaynerMedia was getting. In 2021 he said a small business's biggest vulnerability is believing its size is one.
