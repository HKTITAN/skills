# Technology and data, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025

- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK chose not to collect repayments by QR code, the easiest option for customers, because a QR code is only a payment solution and would not let SubK source loans, appraise or engage customers in the same place. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK's repayment app had only 10% adoption in its first year and took two and a half to three years, until 2023, to pass 50% of customers paying through it. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK's risk tool includes the profile of the field officer who made the first visit, because who that person is affects the quality of the loan, alongside macro data, geography data and the customer's financial behaviour. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK uses satellite night light data as a sign of how developed an area is, and treats a customer surrounded by dark spots as carrying seasonal risk because the area may have many migrants. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK's risk tool does not make the lending decision. It sorts customers so that the 80% in the green band get only a remote appraisal and field effort goes to the other 20%. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says SubK runs loan appraisals as live recorded video calls inside its app and analyses them with AI across 22 parameters, including face match, visible household assets and whether a third person is prompting the customer. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)
- Sasidhar Thumuluri says founders do not have to change radically how a business is done. They can keep the existing process and see what more they can get out of it with available technology. (ivi: Digitising rural microfinance, Sasidhar Thumuluri, 26 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-26-thumuluri-digitising-rural-microfinance.md)

## Bitcoin from numbers to blockchain, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry

- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says Bitcoin is nothing but an agreement among enough people that certain numbers, kept in limited supply, are special. (ivi: Bitcoin from numbers to blockchain, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-bitcoin-hashes-and-blockchain.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry puts the space of 256-digit binary numbers at about 10 to the power 77, using the shortcut that 2 to the power 10 is about 10 to the power 3. (ivi: Bitcoin from numbers to blockchain, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-bitcoin-hashes-and-blockchain.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry describes SHA-256 as an algorithm that turns any message into a 256-digit binary number, and calls that number a fingerprint of the document. - Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says the same message always gives the same hash, a change of one capital letter changes the hash completely, and the function cannot be inverted to recover the message.
