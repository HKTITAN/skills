# Brand, content and culture at Zepto, Chandan Mendiratta

Typed notes from an orientation talk by Chandan Mendiratta, chief marketing officer of Zepto and an ISB alumnus. He spoke about how Zepto uses LinkedIn and Instagram, what makes content travel, and how to start a brand from nothing.

## What was said

### LinkedIn

- Chandan Mendiratta says Zepto is big on LinkedIn and reads the comments there.
- He says the propensity to pay is higher on LinkedIn, because the cream of the country is on it and is not creating content.
- He says influence flows top down.
- He says LinkedIn shows a post only to your followers and to the followers of people who engage with it.
- He says text with a picture does better on LinkedIn at present, and he expects LinkedIn to bring its own video algorithm in the coming months, after which videos will do better.

### What makes content work

- He says viral social media campaigns come from ongoing chatter and small insights, happen about three or four times a year and can rarely be planned.
- He says a founder or chief executive has to create content, so start now and go through the grind.
- He says content should entertain, educate and engage.
- He says marketing channels have changed drastically, and offline marketing does little unless it sparks online conversation.
- He says people sharing your content is a win because it is earned media, and asks what will make people notice, click a picture and send it.
- He says relatable content does well on Instagram, in genres such as relationships and nostalgia, and that it is also about being noticeable.
- He says push notifications are a great marketing channel.
- He says marketing should be kept classy, because awareness without respect is worth little.

### Starting a brand

- He says to cold start a brand you first finalise your brand archetype.
- He gives Zepto's archetype as magician with a hint of jester, and Shaadi.com's as leader.
- He says to start with the why, and if the why is reaching more people then create on both channels.
- He says people do not follow brands, they follow stories, and they come for engagement and entertainment, not to learn about a startup.
- He says your future employees come after seeing your content.
- He says the answer is authenticity, rooted in your own skill set and where you come from.
- He says the last 10 to 20% of effort gives massive returns, so go the extra mile.

## Examples and cases used

- Zepto: brand archetype of magician with a hint of jester, and heavy use of LinkedIn.
- Shaadi.com: brand archetype of leader.
