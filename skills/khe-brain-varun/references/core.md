# Core

Read this for a broad question, then open one theme file for the detail. Every line is a paraphrase of what he says in the video cited. Most of these talks were given to his own employees at Aeos, a bootstrapped media group in Bengaluru, and three are podcasts. Check [disagreements](disagreements.md) before giving any of these as his settled view: he says he learns something every two months that shows his earlier thinking was wrong. (PGX Ideas 2 at 2:05:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=7506s)

## The thesis

- The only thing he says he would boil all his lessons from 14 years of business down to: the world is extremely responsive to supply and demand. What people say or think does not matter, and all that matters is what people are doing. (Masters Union at 3:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=192s; Masters Union at 4:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=240s)

Everything else here is that idea applied to a company, a career, a brand or a bet.

## The fourteen ideas

- Build in a gap. He does not think of a business as hard or easy, he looks for a hole between supply and demand and seals it. (Masters Union at 2:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=144s)
- Distribution is about 10 times more valuable than product, because it brings talent, capital and customers, and those then build the better product. (Kata 10 at 0:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=56s)
- All success is luck, and luck can be engineered by increasing your surface area, which for him means content. (Kata 10 at 4:58, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=298s; Kata 10 at 6:37, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=397s)
- Converting someone takes many touch points: he cites a cross-industry figure of about 28.9, so he reckons a person will reliably join Aeos or buy from it if they watch 30 videos, ideally 30 different ones. He says he does not think he is there yet. (Kata 10 at 25:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=1506s; Kata 10 at 25:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=1556s)
- An entrepreneur's job is to reduce risk, not to take it: do what looks obvious to you, very cheaply, until it wins. (Kata 9 at 1:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=99s)
- Before you take a risk, build shock absorbers, which he defines as cash and broad skills; he calls taking risk without them suicidal. His suggested strategy is to keep 80 to 90% ultra safe and go crazy on 10%, trying a small amount and testing and staging it. (Kata 9 at 41:29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2489s; Kata 9 at 42:16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2536s; Kata 9 at 53:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3199s; Kata 9 at 54:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3246s)
- His starting premise for a project is that you do not actually know what you are building. For Unleash the Avatar he wanted to know within 3 months of putting the core team together whether to continue, stop, or call it unclear. (Kata 12 at 1:42, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=102s; Kata 12 at 5:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=344s)
- Every person in a team has a cost of coordination, and the team produces good output when everybody is below the threshold of effective coordination. He says money is not the issue, and a leader's job is to drop the cost of coordination. (Kata 2 at 12:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=773s; Kata 2 at 13:42, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=822s)
- He says most AI and human employees are like a literal genie: they take things literally to the extent of their own context and fill in the rest themselves. Relaying Andy Grove's pancake example, he says the lesson is that the bottleneck is a missing spec, not missing skill. (Kata 3 at 6:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=390s; Kata 3 at 12:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=776s)
- Hire for attitude first and skill last, because skills can be learned, and judge a candidate on proof and not on a CV. (Kata 8 at 7:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=435s; Kata 8 at 6:26, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=386s; Kata 8 at 13:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=819s)
- Competence is proven by an attempt. Deep belief without an attempt is the biggest trap, and pulling off one real swing raises your level for good. (Kata 4 at 14:28, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=868s; Kata 4 at 12:52, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=772s)
- Decide. Nobody knows the right answer, so pick one thing, go all in, and the rest becomes possible later. (Kata 7 at 24:02, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1442s; Kata 7 at 20:52, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1252s)
- He calls himself a doomer about jobs but says jobs are an anomaly, and that he has said for 3 years that software engineers will be automated. He believes the future of jobs is jobs with bounded rules, and that status ranking is a bounded game AI cannot play. (PGX Ideas 2 at 1:35, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=95s; PGX Ideas 2 at 1:31:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=5460s; PGX Ideas 2 at 41:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=2487s; PGX Ideas 2 at 42:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=2537s)
- When AI automates everything digital, he says world building is your only moat: world building is the brand, or you become the cheap knockoff. A brand is a story that enough people believe. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 0:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=47s; The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 1:12:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=4373s)

## His method, in the order someone would live it

- Pick one thing and commit to it. Keeping every option open gives the least impact. (Kata 7 at 8:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=485s)
- Start with a belief slightly crazier than your social circle. If you have a crazy belief, he says it is sometimes valuable to shut up about it and downgrade it to an easier, much smaller, multi-staged plan. (Why Belief Matters at 14:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=864s; Why Belief Matters at 15:12, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=912s)
- Predicting or believing something without taking the swing earns only a little competence, and he calls deep belief that is never attempted the biggest trap. Even if the outcome is bad, you still gain competence if you took a clean, high integrity attempt. (Kata 4 at 14:28, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=868s; Kata 4 at 16:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=966s)
- Build distribution before you need it. He says any founder who wants to do something really ambitious in India must first build media. (Masters Union at 49:09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=2949s)
- Find a gap, enter a market with little competition, and start as cheaply as possible even when you have money. (Masters Union at 2:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=144s; Kata 9 at 1:03:39, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3819s; KK Create at 1:18:07, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=4687s)
- Find the one feature that works, then stop building and sell. (Kata 11 at 21:38, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=1298s)
- Spend in stages as the odds are proven, and shut down what does not work without ego. (Kata 9 at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=1395s)
- Hire people who cannot be swapped out, each with a superpower, and aim for zero C players. (Kata 8 at 4:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=290s; Kata 8 at 38:32, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=2312s)
- Write clearly, because clear writing lowers the cost of coordination. (Kata 2 at 44:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=2662s)
- Motivate with mastery before cash. He calls cash the least powerful incentive. (Kata 2 at 28:09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=1689s; Kata 2 at 27:20, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=1640s)
- He says the point of a leader, or of a manager in any pod at his company, is to be the "serotonergic balancer": the job is to make sure everyone is in a good enough mood to collaborate. The wider goal is a positive sum ecosystem, which includes creating psychological safety by reducing social defeat. (Secret Kata 1 at 26:34, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wM7jyLJBi_g&t=1594s; Secret Kata 1 at 27:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wM7jyLJBi_g&t=1642s)
- Aeos has a rule that a co-founder comes in at zero salary and lives on equity and profit share, offset with salary only in rare cases. He calls it a double win when you produce your own resources and consume them, as when editors trained at AV can be placed at his services company. (KK Create at 29:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1757s; Masters Union at 11:51, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=711s; Masters Union at 12:38, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=758s)
- Attribute results honestly. He says the only real way to attribute is qualitative, and that chasing measurable ad returns makes a business worse. (Kata 6 at 25:25, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEnxvZXzo0E&t=1525s; Kata 6 at 10:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEnxvZXzo0E&t=615s)
- Build the brand as a world, since that is what he says survives AI. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 1:12:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=4373s)
- Change your beliefs when the data changes. (Why Belief Matters at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=2340s)
