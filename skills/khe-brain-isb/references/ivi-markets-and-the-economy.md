# Markets and the economy, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025

- The instructor gives four rules of mental accounting: separate out gains, combine losses, integrate smaller losses with larger gains, and separate a small gain from a larger loss. ## Behavioural pricing and reference prices

- The instructor says that when you understand the systematic manner in which people behave, their behaviour becomes predictable, and that this is essentially what a bias is. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and reference prices, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-behavioural-pricing-and-reference-prices.md)
- The instructor says people go for certainty and are very risk averse when a choice is framed as a gain, and take riskier gambles when the same outcomes are framed as a loss. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and reference prices, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-behavioural-pricing-and-reference-prices.md)
- The instructor reports a choice experiment in which 16 chose the gamble against 84 in the gain frame and 69 chose it against 31 in the loss frame, with the same final outcomes on offer. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and reference prices, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-behavioural-pricing-and-reference-prices.md)
- The instructor says platforms use virtual coins for the same reason credit cards work: once money is virtual the pain of paying is lower, and the loss is felt only when the statement arrives. He calls this inter-temporal bias. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and reference prices, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-behavioural-pricing-and-reference-prices.md)
