# Careers, college and your twenties

This is his advice to people choosing or changing a working life. Most of it is in articles from 2015 to 2021 and in keynotes and interviews from 2015 to 2026. Platform tactics such as LinkedIn and Instagram DMs carry their year, and the college predictions are his calls from those years, not outcomes. Some pieces on his site are team-written guides under his byline, and they are marked as guides below.

## What he tells teens, graduates and people in their early twenties

- In 2015 he said that graduating that year he would start a business and ask for at-bats, using what he called dumb money from people hunting the next big founder. (AskGaryVee Episode 122 What I Would Do If I Was Graduating College Today at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thExIE-X4Ag&t=180s)
- He tells 22 to 24 year olds not to settle for the first paying job and to rank dream jobs first, then second, third and fourth best. His phrase is "start with the moon and go backwards". (AskGaryVee Episode 122 What I Would Do If I Was Graduating College Today at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thExIE-X4Ag&t=315s)
- In 2026 he repeated it: aim for the dream job first, be patient, and if you cannot get into VaynerMedia go for the second and third best. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=990s)
- In 2015 he said to live humbly in those years with roommates and 99-cent meals, and to flip things from Goodwill on eBay to cover rent. He calls it the price of what you want. (AskGaryVee Episode 122 What I Would Do If I Was Graduating College Today at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thExIE-X4Ag&t=360s)
- He says passion plus one or two skills is the combination, and that skills come either from training or naturally. (AskGaryVee Episode 100 The Live Show at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUFbOa-8dPk&t=3060s)
- He says to bet on your strengths. Going from an 8 to a 12 in what you are great at beats going from a 0 to a 2 in a weakness. (AskGaryVee Episode 123 How Creatives Can Start Thinking Like an Entrepreneur at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CF5M6i4cUMs&t=855s)
- In 2018 he said you cannot find your passion without trying a vast array of things, so evaluate your life in ten year terms and try, taste, test and learn. (The Best Advice to Anyone About to Start Their First Job, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/first-job/)
- In 2018 he said school success does not map to work success. Being a lacrosse captain or a good student does not make you a good manager, so treat each chapter as new. (The Best Advice to Anyone About to Start Their First Job, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/first-job/)
- In 2020 he said that at 19 you start from what you consume in your leisure time, decide whether you want to create, sell or flip, and then cold-contact hundreds of people in that world. (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=1890s)
- In 2018 he warned against calling yourself a digital expert at 22 because you have Instagram. Hold four jobs if needed and show what you have sold. (How One Video Can Change Your Life at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0e8COwvlFUc&t=855s)
- In 2017 he said 23-year-olds take shortcuts because they are insecure and want to prove someone wrong, and that success feels twice as good at 30. (Gary V Talks Mindset K-Swiss + Why He Will Never Do Business With Michael - The Breakfast Club at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-EjUI-lkrs&t=1890s)
- In 2017 he said the best advice for a young person starting out is to experience as much as possible, such as being an artist, writing a blog or selling cards. (Don't Be Afraid to Take Your Next Bite, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/dont-afraid-take-next-bite/)
- A 2023 guide on his site says under-18s should learn to flip instead of taking an after-school job, because the life lessons matter more than the money. (The One Skill Every Young Person Needs to Learn, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-one-skill-every-young-person-needs-to-learn/)
- In 2024 he told a rich kid there are two choices: take the parents' money and fake it, or go work hard with roommates. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=2295s)

## Your twenties: risk, time and not having it figured out

- In 2018 he told readers to take the biggest risks in their twenties, arguing people become unhappy by playing life in reverse: a safe job, expensive stuff, then chains. (A Message For Those Feeling Lost In Their 20s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/if-youre-feeling-lost-in-your-20s-read-this/)
- In 2019 he said it is never more practical to be risky than from 22 to 30, on your own dime, yet people turn conservative to prove themselves. (The Entrepreneur's Mindset Gary Vaynerchuk USC Talk 2019 at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVc0Qw1LSm4&t=1440s)
- In 2021 he said that in your early twenties, whether or not you are graduating from college, you are entering some of the greatest years of your life and this is your time to be massively risk oriented. (What To Do After College? 12 Great Ideas Post College, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-to-do-after-you-graduate-college/)
- In 2024 at an NYU graduation he said to take high-risk bets on what you like at 22, and if it fails by 30 you can still take the job you had lined up. (The Ultimate College Graduation Speech 2024 l NYU Stern Business School at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGEScShDdvs&t=900s)
- In 2026 he said that if you are 22, the next five years are the window to not go practical and safe, and to be massively risk oriented, by traveling, learning and starting the business or band you want. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=270s)
- His decades model runs: 20 to 30 taste a lot of things, 30 to 40 refine, 40 to 50 a more polished version of that refinement, and 50 to 60 feels like insanity, far bigger than anything he has done so far. (Gary Vee's Emotional Confession About His Success & Family E207 - The Diary of a CEO at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ji_N6orURuw&t=1710s)
- In 2018 he said a person under 40 is not lost, they have not started, and that insecurity about showing parents you are successful drives the angst. (7 Mindset Shifts That Bring Happiness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/7-mindset-shifts-that-bring-happiness/)
- In 2019 he said age 30 as a deadline is a made-up norm, since 98 percent of the 59-year-olds he knows do not have it figured out. (57 Minutes of Advice For People Early In Their Career Chase Fireside Chat 2019 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0doo_v0EPOQ&t=990s)
- In 2026 he said nobody has life figured out at 18, 22 or 30, and that parents who pressure you do not have theirs figured out. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=0s)
- In 2017 he said that under 30 you are not behind, you are early. He says he made nothing until about 34. (Gary V Talks Mindset K-Swiss + Why He Will Never Do Business With Michael - The Breakfast Club at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-EjUI-lkrs&t=1800s)
- In 2022 he said his one piece of advice to people under 30 is to enjoy the next ten years more, since there is so much time. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=585s)
- In 2025 he said not having found your purpose yet is something to be happy about, because he worked 100 hours a week for his father from 22 to 34 and started his own career at 34. (One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=2565s)
- In 2026 he said wanting fast success in your twenties is doing it for others, and doing it for yourself is slower and more certain. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=630s)
- In 2024 he told graduates that a tough job market is good, because adversity is what leads to happiness and success. (The Ultimate College Graduation Speech 2024 l NYU Stern Business School at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGEScShDdvs&t=135s)

## College, student debt and what a degree is worth

- In 2015 he said college is a racket for business students, that loans cannot be bankrupted away, and that if you are collecting debt to be a business person you should quit now. He exempted lawyers and doctors. (AskGaryVee Episode 122 What I Would Do If I Was Graduating College Today at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thExIE-X4Ag&t=540s)
- In 2017 he narrowed it: college can make sense for a lawyer, a doctor, someone with no debt, or someone who needs the move to a city, but not for someone who lives and breathes starting a business. (The New School For Entrepreneurship, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/new-school-entrepreneurship/)
- In 2017 he predicted that colleges trade on brand, that top brands would dilute, and that higher education would be vulnerable over the next decade as professors go direct to consumer. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 2:00:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=7245s)
- In 2017 in Oslo he said a degree still matters if you want Bain or McKinsey within 24 to 36 months, and that the education sits with the professor. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 1:07:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=4050s)
- In 2018 he predicted that college debt would take colleges' brand down the way banks fell in 2009, with people blaming colleges instead of themselves. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=900s)
- In 2018 he said that if you go to college and collect debt at 11 percent you will be in bad shape until you are 40, and that college is broken in America. He also said school is exactly right for some who understand the system. (Your Problems Don't Matter Here's Why Talk at the Precious Dreams Foundation at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ALZTZFMeWXU&t=1530s)
- In 2019 he said kids should have a hard conversation about whether a degree puts them in the best position, and that he is not telling everyone to skip college. (The Debate: Is College Really Worth It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/is-college-really-worth-it/)
- In 2019 he said only seven or eight colleges will keep enough brand equity in 15 years. (57 Minutes of Advice For People Early In Their Career Chase Fireside Chat 2019 at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0doo_v0EPOQ&t=2295s)
- In 2019 in Auckland he called university a self-awareness decision. Some kids should do the first year, some should travel or start a side hustle, and many should work and avoid debt. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=2700s)
- In 2020 he said he would not pay 40,000 dollars for Zoom classes, and that enrollment would have kept falling without Covid. (The 18 to 28 Debate: Is College Worth It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-18-to-28-debate-is-college-worth-it/)
- In 2020 he said he takes issue with the one size fits all education model, especially in the United States. In poor countries where the internet is not yet strong enough, he says schools are the real gateway. (The 18 to 28 Debate: Is College Worth It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-18-to-28-debate-is-college-worth-it/)
- In 2022 he said he is not against college, he is against a lack of self-awareness and against the last 30 years where everyone should go. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 1:49:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=6570s)
- In 2023 he said education will be torn down and repackaged over the next 50 years, and that he has never been more bullish on education. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=2025s)
- In 2025 he said the idea that everyone should go to college is insanity, because in America kids go into massive debt. (What Happens When ChatGPT Replaces Google GaryVee Q&A For ActionCoach UK at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlNAM0M4WNE&t=990s)
- In 2026 he said that if parents are paying, a creative or entrepreneurial kid can go have a four-year vacation, but collecting debt is out of your mind. He exempted people aiming at a legal career. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=2610s)
- In 2026 he said he argued college is a waste for a lot of people from 2005 on, with the nuance that a creative or entrepreneurial kid whose parents cannot afford it should avoid 120,000 dollars of debt. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 1:02:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=3735s)

## Working for someone, internships and working for free

- In 2014 he said that to become good at a job you should work a year for the person you want to be like, at half pay if needed, and learn their nuances. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tells You How to Rock SXSW Full Session Interactive 2014 SXSW at 1:20:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aSOS9XZL48&t=4815s)
- In 2018 he described working for someone you admire for as little money as you possibly can as a new way of entering the workforce, and said that looking back the only option he would have considered besides his family business was working for top entrepreneurs, even if it meant being poor with a job at midnight on the side. (3 Ways To Start Accomplishing Your Goals, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/accomplish-goals/)
- In 2018 he said getting close to an alpha operator and over-delivering is the single best thing you can do, and that getting coffee for a superstar for years beats being a mid-level manager somewhere you hate. (3 Ways To Start Accomplishing Your Goals, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/accomplish-goals/)
- In 2018 he said the three ways are going to school, doing it yourself, or being a number 7 or number 17 behind an A player who will help you win, and that you choose by deploying self-awareness. (3 Ways To Start Accomplishing Your Goals, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/accomplish-goals/)
- In 2018 he said that if you take a 12 dollar an hour job near someone you admire you are "closest to the sun", and that beats a 52,000 dollar job elsewhere. (A Message For Those Feeling Lost In Their 20s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/if-youre-feeling-lost-in-your-20s-read-this/)
- In 2017 he said an internship is about who you meet, not what you learn, and that 98 percent of interns will take jobs where they are not taught anything beyond what they already know. (Listen Up Interns, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/listen-up-interns/)
- In 2018 he told interns that skills are commoditized, that who you meet matters most, and that many of the people they work with now will become CMOs of the biggest companies and CEOs of agencies or brands. (#1 Thing for an Intern or College Student to Do 2018 Summer Interns Fireside Chat at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BJiPvnfdbg&t=90s)
- In 2021 he said unpaid internships are highly underrated, that early on it is about stacking opportunities, and that you must handle being left on read because it takes one yes. (What To Do After College? 12 Great Ideas Post College, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-to-do-after-you-graduate-college/)
- In 2014 he said he does not think there is a single person with substantial success who did not do something for free as a strategic move. (Working for Free: The Debate, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/working-for-free-the-debate/)
- In 2015 he said giving away work for free has been his "gateway drug" to many opportunities, and that there is a risk. (#AskGaryVee Episode 73: Podcasting Building Legacy & The Chamber of Commerce, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-73-podcasting-building-legacy-the-chamber-of-commerce/)
- In 2019 he said free work is an opportunity issue, not a privilege issue, and that many could afford it but do not value the exposure and relationships. (Like They Say Nothing In Life Is Free, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nothing-in-life-is-free/)
- In 2019 he said free work to reach the top 100 people in your craft is your foot in the door, and that you should highly debate an unpaid project that gets you there. (Like They Say Nothing In Life Is Free, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nothing-in-life-is-free/)
- In 2019 he said you get more value working free for a star than at a 41,000 dollar entry-level music job, and that this is not about letting anyone take advantage of you. (Like They Say Nothing In Life Is Free, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nothing-in-life-is-free/)
- In 2019 he said to say your honest view at work politely, and called it "die on your own sword". People who stay silent to please managers get hurt when the company resets. (57 Minutes of Advice For People Early In Their Career Chase Fireside Chat 2019 at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0doo_v0EPOQ&t=1665s)
- In 2015 he said there is no risk in giving your boss feedback if you keep your cool and are diplomatic, and that you should not stick around if the boss will not take you seriously. In 2019 he said a great boss will welcome performance feedback and listen. (How To Tell Your Boss He's F**king It All Up, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-tell-your-boss-hes-fking-it-all-up/; The Biggest Problem With 360 Performance Reviews, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-biggest-problem-with-360-performance-reviews/)

## Getting hired: outreach, resumes and LinkedIn

- In 2018 he said to cold-message 50 people a day and offer free work. (3 Ways To Start Accomplishing Your Goals, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/accomplish-goals/)
- In 2019 he said to do the outreach 100 to 500 times a day because you only need one yes to a DM, and to deliver once you get an opportunity. (5 Principles to Find Your Dream Job Without Experience, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/landing-your-dream-job-without-experience/)
- In 2020 he said finding a job is a game of no's, so spray, do not pray: if he had no job right then he would apply to a thousand places in a day and DM 500 people. (The Solo Show Is Back AskGaryVee 332 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yI047XWUh7Y&t=810s)
- In 2020 he said persistence on the internet beats credentials, since 4,000 may say no and the 4,001st says yes. (How the Top 1% of Entrepreneurs Operate That Separates Them From the Rest at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y06OTbH8mVQ&t=45s)
- In 2017 he said that without a degree you can email people at businesses you want to work for, and after three months one will say yes. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 2:03:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=7380s)
- In 2020 he said that if your dream is far away you should take a boring job for a year, save every dollar, buy a ticket and a week in a hotel, then DM everyone there to find a couch. (Life After High School: The Apprentice, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/life-after-high-school-the-apprentice/)
- In 2020 he said to reply thoughtfully to executives' LinkedIn posts consistently for months, and that you do not need to be an expert to give perspective. (Life After High School: All Of The Above, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/life-after-high-school-all-of-the-above/)
- In 2020 he said that after a layoff you should turn it into opportunity, and that someone who lost a job they did not like could write a LinkedIn letter explaining why they want to go into a new industry. He argued that 50 percent of the people laid off in the pandemic disliked their field. (Career Advice In A Pandemic, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/career-advice-in-a-pandemic/)
- A 2023 guide on his site says one LinkedIn post, even with no followers, can bring inbound job offers. (how to start creating content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-start-creating-content/)
- In 2026 he said the number one skill for a job seeker is making content on LinkedIn, such as an executive assistant making a five-things video. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 1:00:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=3600s)
- In 2023 he said he never looked at a resume, and that companies look at resumes and colleges less and less. A gap taken for something you love or for a family will not hurt. (6 Pieces of Advice From VeeCon 2023, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/6-pieces-of-advice-from-veecon-2023/)

## Side hustles, passion and quitting a job

- In 2020 he said to use 7:30 to 10:30 each night to try things you like, try nine things for about four months, and keep the ones that pull. (Are You the Type of Person That Needs to Start Investing in Pokemon Cards Tea With GaryVee at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FHShGCwpxmY&t=2340s)
- In 2021 he said to fix your well, not just your sink, by using 7 pm to 10 pm for five years to build a vehicle out of a career you dislike. (Stress In The Workplace: Three takes for you to consider, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stress-in-the-workplace-three-takes-for-you-to-consider/)
- In 2017 he said happiness beats a bit more money, and he was stunned by people who accept being unhappy for an extra 8,000 dollars. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 49:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=2970s)
- In 2019 he said to do something you are passionate about and actually good at, and if your skill is only okay, settle into knowing you will make about 130,000 dollars and aim for happiness. He said he wants the fashion designer making 130,000 dollars to be happy, not trying to be the next Off-White. (ABtalks with Gary Vee - Chapter 12 at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxgQRJ1SwZ8&t=720s)
- In 2021 he said that if you quit you should be prepared for things to get a lot harder before they get better, should not expect mom and dad to fund your life, and need the humility to go backwards financially. (How to Quit a Job Professionally, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-quit-a-job-professionally/)
- In 2021 he said to always give advance notice, make sure your boss hears it first, give two weeks at minimum, and have a face to face meeting if possible. (How to Quit a Job Professionally, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-quit-a-job-professionally/)
- In 2023 he said that if you are unhappy at work you should quit or stop complaining, and that someone paid 200,000 dollars can downsize the lifestyle. If you are staying to hit a pension, eat it quietly. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=2610s)
- In 2023 he said it is fine to quit a toxic job after six months. (How To Deal With The Fear of Making The Wrong Decisions l VeeCon 2023 Closing Keynote at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09Pf5sIBNqk&t=585s)
- In 2024 he qualified it: quitting your job is not necessarily the right thing 80 percent of the time, and he said he was being serious. (These 5 Traits Are The Secret To Success In Business & Life l GaryVee's VeeCon 2024 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0lw3YiniDc&t=270s)
- In 2024 he said passion is always the bet but must be paired with practicality, or you usually go back to a job. (These 5 Traits Are The Secret To Success In Business & Life l GaryVee's VeeCon 2024 at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0lw3YiniDc&t=1305s)
- In 2026 he said being fired is often the best thing that happens, because jobs will always exist and the next one is likely more fun and pays more. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=360s)

- In 2016 he said you should switch paths if you want to, and not be half pregnant on what you want to be. (Nobody Can Define Who You Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nobody-can-define-who-you-are/)
- In 2023 he said that when an athlete's dream ends, the person must reset and find another competitive outlet. (Discovering New Passions When Dreams Fade l AskGaryVee 336 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJzCLL0jv0Q&t=405s)
- In 2025 he told someone unsure where to go in their career to keep talking to the world, keep trying things, sign up for free classes, go to events and give themselves that hour a day. (How to Actually Get Ahead with AI Before It's Too Late DailyVee ep 677 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jZ2V1IeObiE&t=495s)

## Employee or founder, and the number two

- In 2013 he said to be honest with yourself, because many could make far more money joining a number one than starting their own thing. (Coding Vs. Selling, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-cant-code-and-you-cant-sell-crap/)
- In 2015 he said that if you have a full-time job you are not an entrepreneur, and that someone with entrepreneurial tendencies who is happy is fine. (So You Wanna Be an Entrepreneur…, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/so-you-wanna-be-an-entrepreneur/)
