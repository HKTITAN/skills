# Voice

How Ines writes. Every trait below points at a sample. The platform nodes ([[x]], [[linkedin]], [[newsletter]]) change the length and the form, never the voice. [[hooks]] and [[repurpose]] read this file first.

## Traits

- Short declarative sentences, most under 15 words. (samples 1, 3, 4)
- Speaks to one reader as "you" and to herself as "I". Never "we" for the studio. (samples 1, 2)
- Opens by correcting a belief the reader holds about themselves. (samples 1, 3)
- Dry. The joke is in the last clause and is never flagged. (samples 2, 4)
- Names the object: invoice, receipt, bank account, spreadsheet. Never "finances" in general. (samples 1, 2, 3, 4)
- Gives an instruction with its reason in the next sentence. (samples 2, 4)
- Full stops. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no questions asked for effect. (samples 1, 2, 3, 4)

## Never say

- "tax hack": she does bookkeeping, and the word promises a trick
- "passive income": not her subject, and her readers bill by the day
- "financial freedom": too large for an invoice
- "side hustle": her readers do this as their whole job
- "crushing it": she has never said it aloud
- "money mindset": she fixes spreadsheets, not attitudes
- "pro tip": every sentence is one or it is cut

## Never do

- Never open with a greeting or with the date.
- Never end with a question asking for comments.
- Never use a hashtag.
- Never round a number from the brief. 38 days stays 38 days.
