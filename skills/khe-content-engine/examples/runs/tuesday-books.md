# Run: the Tuesday books

Engine: ../engine
Idea: 20 minutes of bookkeeping every Tuesday replaces the scramble at the end of the quarter
Platforms: x, linkedin

## Brief

From Ines, 2026-11-09.

- Her Tuesday checklist has 4 steps: match every bank line to an invoice or a receipt; photograph the paper receipts; flag any spend over 250 euros that has no receipt; write one line saying who owes you money and how much.
- For a client with under 40 transactions a week it takes about 20 minutes. She has timed it.
- She has done it herself every Tuesday since 2023.
- Before 2023 she did her own books once a quarter. In her words, she once lost a whole Saturday to one missing receipt.
- Why Tuesday: by Friday nobody opens a spreadsheet, and on Monday the weekend's bank lines have not all landed.
- An unmatched line is asked about the same day, while someone still remembers what it was.

Claim: a short weekly pass costs less time in a year than a quarterly one, because nothing has to be remembered.

```text
Do your books on Tuesday. Every Tuesday, for 20 minutes.

By Friday nobody opens a spreadsheet. On Monday the weekend has not landed in your bank yet.

The whole of it is below.
---
First, open the bank statement and match every line to an invoice or a receipt.

Anything you cannot match, ask about today. Somebody still remembers what it was.
---
Second, photograph the paper receipts. Every one, including the one in your coat.
---
Third, flag any spend over 250 euros that has no receipt.

That is the one your accountant asks about in the spring.
---
Last, write one line: who owes you money, and how much.

Read it aloud. Then send the reminder you have been putting off.
```

```text
Until 2023 I did my own books once a quarter. I am a bookkeeper, and I did them badly.

I once lost a whole Saturday to one missing receipt. The receipt was for something small. The Saturday was not.

The trouble with a quarter is that it asks you to remember. What was this payment for, who was that transfer to, why is there a taxi in March. Nobody can answer those in July.

So I moved to a weekly pass. Tuesday, because by Friday I will not open a spreadsheet and on Monday the bank has not caught up.

For a client with under 40 transactions a week it takes about 20 minutes. I have timed it. Mine takes the same.

The method is dull and I will spare you. The point is the interval. A week is short enough that the answer to every question is still in somebody's head.
```
