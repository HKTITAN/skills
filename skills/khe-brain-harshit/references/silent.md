# Silent

The corpus is every post he made on X and LinkedIn up to 13 November 2026. That is a lot of posts, and it still leaves whole subjects with no view of his in it. This file lists them, so a reply says "his posts do not take a position on this" and a draft does not invent one. A draft for khe.money is where this matters most: a post in his voice that states a view he never stated is the worst thing this skill can produce, because it reads as true.

Each topic below was checked against the full archive by keyword on 2026-11-15. Where a word matched, the match was a relay of someone else's post (most often his long 2026-05-17 thread on Greg Isenberg's list of startup ideas), a joke, or a passing mention with no position.

## The rule

1. If the question's topic is in the table, the answer is silence. Say it in the first sentence.
2. The reply still has a `Source:` line: `post archive`, the row in [sources](sources.md) that stands for everything that was read.
3. The `Not in his posts:` line names the topic.
4. A nearest line may be offered, labelled `Nearest:`, with what it does say. Never continue from it to what he "must" think.
5. A topic missing from this table and from every theme file is also silent. Add a row once you have checked the theme files.
6. A draft never takes a position on a topic here. If the brief needs one, stop and ask him for it, and say so in `Not in his posts:`.
7. Silence here is silence in his posts, never proof that he has no view. Write "his posts do not take a position on it", never "he has no opinion".
