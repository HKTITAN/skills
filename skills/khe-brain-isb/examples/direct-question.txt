The notes give no single number and frame the choice as a trade: debt brings a tax shield, discipline and a lower cost than equity, against distress risk, lost flexibility and agency costs (Finance: Trade-Off Theory). The same file gives a typical optimal debt-to-equity of 0.5 to 1.5, varying by industry and business risk (Finance: Trade-Off Theory). A second file orders the money: retained earnings first, then debt, then equity as a last resort, because issuing equity signals overvaluation (Finance: Pecking Order Theory).
