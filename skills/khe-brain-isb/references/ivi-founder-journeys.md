# How founders built their companies, in their own telling at ivi

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

- Akshay Jalan says his company began as a toy company on the insight that the early childhood market was not very price sensitive, then moved to distributing baby product brands. (ivi: Building Allthingsbaby and raising late, Akshay Jalan, 4 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-04-jalan-allthingsbaby-raising-late.md)

## Orientation day 2, Harish Manwani with Deepesh Agarwal, Aakriti Ahuja and Rhitiman Majumder, 4 Apr 2025

- Rhitiman Majumder says Pickrr was drowning in consumer legal cases, asked its competitor Shiprocket to buy it for 10 crore rupees and was offered zero. (ivi: Orientation day 2, Harish Manwani with Deepesh Agarwal, Aakriti Ahuja and Rhitiman Majumder, 4 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-04-manwani-orientation-day-2.md)
- Rhitiman Majumder says Pickrr existed from 2015, found success in 2020 with 36 crore rupees of revenue at 10% EBITDA, and was rejected five times by Sequoia. (ivi: Orientation day 2, Harish Manwani with Deepesh Agarwal, Aakriti Ahuja and Rhitiman Majumder, 4 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-04-manwani-orientation-day-2.md)
- Rhitiman Majumder says Pickrr was sold for 200 million dollars in cash, and that people who invested 30 lakh rupees went back with 3 crore. (ivi: Orientation day 2, Harish Manwani with Deepesh Agarwal, Aakriti Ahuja and Rhitiman Majumder, 4 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-04-manwani-orientation-day-2.md)
- Rhitiman Majumder says the founders took a 25,000 rupee salary for three years and that if you show up every day for seven to eight years it works out. (ivi: Orientation day 2, Harish Manwani with Deepesh Agarwal, Aakriti Ahuja and Rhitiman Majumder, 4 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-04-manwani-orientation-day-2.md)

He describes it as the first and largest campus placement programme run by a coaching company in India. (ivi: Personalised tutoring and how Aakash hired teachers, Aakash Chaudhry, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-chaudhry-personalised-tutoring-and-aakash.md)
