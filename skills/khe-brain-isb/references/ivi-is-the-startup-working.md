# Is the startup working, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025

- Vamsi Krishna names three things to watch when looking for product-market fit: margins, week on week growth, and retention or renewals. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says early stage founders fall into choosing a narrow, niche market, and that Vedantu spent seven to eight months on international board students before realising this. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says to optimise for speed of discovery, and to keep the product open to anyone, because product founders tend to be guarded and say it is not ready yet. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says a paid customer is the key validation for 99.9% of companies, with the exception of companies that thrive on network effects. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says to beg, borrow or steal to get your first customer fast, and not to think about elaborate customer acquisition strategies. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna cites Paul Graham's weekly growth rate of 5 to 10% on the right core metric, and says below 4% something is off and 6 to 7% is great. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says an NPS of 40% is critical before you think of blitzscaling, and that the NPS question should be asked on the plain 1 to 10 scale without changes. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says the margins you are born with are the margins you die with, so a founder should ask whether contribution margin is positive. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says you achieve product-market fit, then plateau or hit a wall, then iterate on everything and find it again. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)
- Vamsi Krishna says that when there is no fit, either the product is wrong or the customer is wrong, so look at both ends. (ivi: How to figure out product-market fit, Vamsi Krishna, 5 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-05-krishna-vedantu-figuring-out-pmf.md)

## Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram frames an MVP around three limits: the features you need, the time you have and the money you have. For her cancer care venture the features were people, infrastructure and processes. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says her MVP was not a full cancer hospital. She kept medical oncology fully in-house, rented operating theatres for surgery and outsourced radiation, because 80% of patients need medical oncology and it was the cheapest to build. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says a small startup facing a large problem has to pick one part of it. She chose access to cancer treatment over affordability because much of a patient's spend goes on travel, overnight stays and an attendant. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says her chemotherapy patients rejected the recliners that premium hospitals use because they had travelled for hours and wanted to lie down, so she replaced the recliners with beds at extra cost. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says a founder should know whether the business is repeat or one-time. Cancer care has no repeat purchase, and a business that has to sell constantly must invest in its sales process. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)

## Lean startup workshop 1, building an MVP for distributed cancer care, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram says founders always love their own product, so it has to be put in front of customers, however scary, measured, and then built up. (ivi: Lean startup workshop 1, building an MVP for distributed cancer care, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-lean-startup-mvp-cancer-care-case.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says a small startup facing a large problem must pick one part of it: of oncologist shortage, access, affordability, screening and allied services, she chose access, because bringing treatment closer also cuts the patient's travel cost. (ivi: Lean startup workshop 1, building an MVP for distributed cancer care, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-lean-startup-mvp-cancer-care-case.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says her MVP was not a full cancer hospital but a treatment centre offering what 80% of patients need: medical oncology built in-house, surgery in rented operating theatres, and radiation fully outsourced. (ivi: Lean startup workshop 1, building an MVP for distributed cancer care, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-lean-startup-mvp-cancer-care-case.md)
