Against the Finance files this model breaks four stated positions and holds one. The largest is the terminal value: the pitfalls list says that at 80% or more of the total, small changes in assumptions cause large swings (Finance: Pitfalls to Avoid).
Breaks: terminal value at 88% of the total; the pitfalls list sets its warning at 80% or more and says to check the result against comparable multiples (Finance: Pitfalls to Avoid).
Breaks: terminal growth of 5%; the same list calls perpetual high growth unrealistic and gives GDP-like rates of 2 to 3% (Finance: Pitfalls to Avoid).
Breaks: WACC weighted on book values; the WACC file says the weights use market values (Finance: WACC, Weighted Average Cost of Capital).
Breaks: one method only; the triangulation file says to run DCF, multiples and an asset-based value and report a range (Finance: Valuation Triangulation).
Holds: discounting free cash flows at WACC and adding a terminal value (Finance: DCF, Discounted Cash Flow Valuation).
