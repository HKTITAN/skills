# Timing

Holds end up shorter than the first draft, never longer. Start at these numbers. When the user says "shorter" with no number, cut the holds by a quarter to a half. Cut a repeat before you cut a pause.

## Lengths

| Format | Length |
|---|---|
| Explainer | 20 to 26 s: fit a lot in, then add pauses so the viewer catches up |
| Click-through | 6 to 9 s |
| Hover reel | about 2.6 s per item |

## Openings and cuts

- The first action starts 0.3 to 0.4 s into each scene. No dead opening.
- The first scene is the hook: it reads in under a second, with no explanation.
- Between scenes, hard cuts only. A transition between examples is dead time.

## Comparisons

- Each comparison scene runs 5 to 7 s as cycles of open, close and slow-down: twice at normal speed, then once at 4 times slower, left open until the cut. In the gold explainer: `[[0.4, 1.4, 1], [2.3, 3.3, 1], [4.2, null, 4]]`.
- When the comparison is about duration (400 ms against 180 ms), run the third cycle at normal speed too; slowing it hides the difference.
- Leave 0.6 to 0.9 s of stillness between runs.
- Graphs: draw the curve once at real speed, then again twice as slow.

## Motion you author

- A press before an open: scale to 0.97 over 90 ms and back over 160 ms, the deepest point on the open frame.
- An exit you author runs at least 0.8 of its entrance (0.3 s in, about 0.25 s out). A flat short exit after a long entrance feels abrupt. Components copied from source keep their own timing.

## Per item

| Format | Beat |
|---|---|
| Hover reel | rest 0.2 s, glide in 0.67 s, hold 0.65 s, glide out 0.6 s, rest 0.5 s, hard cut |
| Click-through, per switch | glide 0.55 s, settle 0.15 s, click, hold 0.6 s |

A longer hold drags by the third item.

## Loops

End on the same state as the first frame so the post loops cleanly.
