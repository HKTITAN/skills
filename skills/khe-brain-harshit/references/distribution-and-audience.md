# Distribution and audience

He treats distribution as part of the product: the best growth loop is the product itself, and the best channel is wherever people already are. Since mid-2025 his settled view is that marketing is trust and clarity built in public one post at a time, that products (and his game Smash&Clash) should live across many surfaces instead of one website, and that X beats LinkedIn for thinking in public. He also says social apps drifted from friends to "interest media". His older posts (2022-24) show him building Notion and no-code communities on WhatsApp and Telegram and growing an X account with threads.

## What is marketing for?

- He says content is trust, not branding, and trust is built in public one post at a time. "content isn’t branding. it’s trust." (2025-07-06-marketing-isn-t-department, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7347652046616961024)
- Recapping Chandan Mendiratta's class, he wrote that if a product needs explaining, teaching people is how you acquire them. "then education is your acquisition." (2025-07-06-marketing-isn-t-department, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7347652046616961024)
- He says marketing is about clarity and the discipline to keep returning to it, not clever ads. "marketing isn’t about clever." (2025-07-06-marketing-isn-t-department, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7347652046616961024)
- In one reply he said a founder should be the face of the product. "be the face of your product." (2026-07-23-soham-nayak04-not-really, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2080302717797625986)
- (2024) He wrote that marketing should show a product working where its audience already is, using Sony's waterproof Walkman in gym vending machines as the example. "Create Experiences, Not Ads." (2024-12-07-innovation-isnt-about-product, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7271050136451973120)
- (2024) He wrote that Spotify Wrapped works because it turns user data into personal, emotional storytelling built on personalization, gamification and social currency. (2024-12-04-spotify-wrapped-2024-has, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7270076489117642755)
- (2022) He listed free channels for finding customers (TikTok, LinkedIn, Reddit, Instagram, YouTube, Product Hunt, Discord and more). "Finding customers doesn't have to be expensive." (2022-11-30-finding-customers-doesnt-have, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1597886883492933634)

## How does a product grow itself?

- He says product-led growth means making the product itself the growth loop, not just building a great product. (2026-08-30-plg-isn-t-about, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2094061078514950597)
- He calls Poke recipes one of the best PLG mechanics he has seen: a repeatable, shareable set of instructions others copy for their own workflow. He ranks it second only to Notion. "best PLG I've seen in a while (after notion ofc)" (2026-06-25-poke-has-one-best, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2070021680383328271; 2026-07-12-thvdnta-interaction-samyok-bahah, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2076283282061439387)
- He reported that over 1.4k people used Poke recipes he made, in under a minute. (2026-06-06-over-1-4k-people, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2063276611001339951)
- Discussing a game's spread, he says distribution comes from embedding it in platforms and games people already use and sharing in their monetization. (2026-11-11-mattworkman-by-integrating-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2120429822967251226)
- He agrees that shipping on an existing platform such as Roblox gives easy distribution. "true, easy distribution" (2026-09-13-jawadmakes-true-easy-distribution, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099078609684381813)

## Should a product live in one place?

- He says a product no longer lives in one place but across many surfaces shaped by context, user and intent. "composable software. infinite surfaces. one underlying product." (2026-10-20-your-product-wont-live, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112453994643870174)
- In the same post he says discovery becomes less about getting people to your website and more about making the product available wherever intent exists, through MCP and on-demand UI. (2026-10-20-your-product-wont-live, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112453994643870174)
- For Smash&Clash he runs one shared match pool across the browser, Snapchat, Telegram, Minecraft, GTA V and more, and treats each client as a distribution channel. "one game. infinite clients." (2026-10-20-gaming-also-going-get, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112457428868776278)
- He pitches the Smash&Clash SDK so anyone can build their own game client. (2026-10-08-pranvtwt-could-integrate-smash, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2108107082520272987)
- He says Snapchat gave Smash&Clash the most users of the platforms it launched on (November 2026); he had announced 100K+ matches hosted on Snapchat in October 2026. (2026-11-13-fnionic-true-launched-across, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2121062435536908683; 2026-10-24-100k-matches-hosted-snapchat, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2114054062157312155)
- He points out that X articles can only be distributed on X. (2026-07-07-arnesh-11-nikitabier-like, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2074337759637151841)
- He says launching XChat as a standalone app lets people without X join the ecosystem, and jokes that its hour of early outage spread the news. (2026-06-29-ykw-if-think-about, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2071625274668859549)
