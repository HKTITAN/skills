# Reading and building the numbers, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Orientation day 3 on how a VC fund's maths works, Sarthak Ahuja and Piyush Shah, 5 Apr 2025

## Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025

- The instructor says two principles govern cash flow estimates for an investment decision: count only what is actually cash, and count only what is incremental to that decision. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says incremental cash flow is the cash flow with the project minus the cash flow without it, so cannibalisation of an existing product or a lift to it belongs in the estimate. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says free cash flow starts from NOPAT, adds back depreciation and amortisation, subtracts any increase in net working capital and subtracts capital expenditure. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says depreciation is subtracted before tax only because it lowers the tax bill, and is then added back because no cash leaves the business for it. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says that when net working capital goes up the business needs that much more money and it is a cash outflow, and when it goes down it is a cash inflow. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says negative working capital is not a bad thing, because customers pay at once while vendors give time, and as such a business grows its vendors finance part of the growth. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says content or IP that can be licensed for years is a long-term intangible asset and not inventory, because inventory stops being yours once it is sold. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says a positive NPV means a project gives back more than it takes after covering all spending and the minimum return investors need, so never take a negative NPV project and pick the highest NPV when only one can be chosen. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says the discount rate is the investors' opportunity cost, not the fixed deposit rate or nominal GDP growth, because it carries a risk premium that is always higher for startups. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says profit gives a period-by-period view, while cash flow shows how much money has to be locked into the business and when, which is the time over which investors must earn their return. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says that in a forecast the sum of the negative cash flows is the total funding the business needs over that period. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says IRR is the percentage return per year a project generates, found as the discount rate at which NPV is zero, and a project is accepted when its IRR is above the cost of capital. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says ROE and ROCE are worked out year by year from profit and ignore cash timing and working capital, while IRR is one number for the whole life of a project. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says IRR ignores the scale of a project, so a small project with a 100% IRR can add less value than a campaign ten times its size with a 50% IRR. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says NPV suits startups focused on scale and large corporations, while IRR suits bootstrapped or capital-constrained companies ranking projects and is what investment funds focus on. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)
- The instructor says an NPV profile plots a project's NPV at different discount rates, shows the IRR where the line crosses zero, and works as a sensitivity check when only a range for the investors' required return is known. (ivi: Capital budgeting with NPV and IRR, 15 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-15-instructor-capital-budgeting-npv-irr.md)

## Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025

- Siddharth Shekar says that in every transaction the company gets something from the customer and gives something back, and the difference is the net benefit, which founders should use in place of gross margin. (ivi: Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-28-shekar-customer-lifetime-value.md)
- Siddharth Shekar says customer lifetime value is the net present value of the future net benefits from a customer, with acquisition cost added as an outflow when the customer is still a prospect. (ivi: Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-28-shekar-customer-lifetime-value.md)
- Siddharth Shekar defines the retention rate as the customers left at the end of a period divided by those at the start, within the same cohort and not counting new acquisitions. (ivi: Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-28-shekar-customer-lifetime-value.md)
- Siddharth Shekar says retention has the biggest effect on customer lifetime value because it enters the simplified formula in both the numerator and the denominator. (ivi: Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-28-shekar-customer-lifetime-value.md)
- Siddharth Shekar says a lifetime value estimate gets more accurate as you account for more costs at the customer transaction level, such as technical support and returns. (ivi: Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-28-shekar-customer-lifetime-value.md)

## Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1

- The instructor says evaluating a project needs three inputs: the upfront expenditure, the cash flows expected in each period, and a discount rate that stands for the opportunity cost. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor gives two principles for estimating a project's cash flows: count only cash, and count only what is incremental, meaning cash flows with the project minus cash flows without it. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says a new product's cash flows must include both the positive spillover onto older products and any cannibalisation of them. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says cannibalisation is estimated from market research plus judgement about overlap: the more the new product resembles the old, such as a premium version, the more customers will switch. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor builds free cash flow by taking EBIT less tax on EBIT, adding back depreciation, subtracting any increase in net working capital and subtracting capital expenditure. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says depreciation is added back because no cash leaves the bank, and that its only cash effect is the tax it saves, which is why it is subtracted before tax and added back after. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says the adjustments from profit to cash follow one idea: subtract cash outflows that accounting does not treat as expenses, and add back expenses that are not cash. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor's rule is that an increase in net working capital is a cash outflow and a decrease is a cash inflow. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says a rise in inventory or receivables makes cash flow smaller than profit, because the inventory has been paid for but not expensed and the receivable has been booked as a sale but not collected. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says a business paid upfront by customers and given time by vendors has negative working capital, which is a good thing, and that as it grows its vendors in effect finance part of the growth. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says net working capital typically rises as a business grows, though not always, because of economies of scale. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says content or IP that can be licensed for years is a long term intangible asset and not inventory, because inventory stops being yours once sold and IP does not. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor says negative free cash flow means you are still investing in the business and positive free cash flow means the business has started paying money back. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)
- The instructor's classroom example of a drinks company launching a smoothie turns an EBIT of 7 lakh rupees into free cash flow of 5 lakh: tax of 1.4 lakh comes off, 2 lakh of depreciation is added back and a 2.6 lakh rise in working capital is subtracted. (ivi: Estimating free cash flow for an investment decision, part 1, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-free-cash-flow-estimation.md)

## NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2

- The instructor says NPV discounts every cash flow over a project's life back to the present and adds them up, and it is called net because some of those cash flows are outflows. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says the discount rate is the investors' opportunity cost, that it is neither the fixed deposit rate nor nominal GDP growth, and that it carries a risk premium which is always higher for startups. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says a positive NPV means the project gives back more than it takes after investors have already received their required return, and it is a rupee amount, not a percentage. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor's NPV rule is never to take a negative NPV project because it destroys value, and to pick the highest NPV when projects are mutually exclusive. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor suggests rerunning NPV with a pessimistic sales forecast to check that it stays positive. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says NPV uses cash flows and not profits because cash flows capture when money must go into the business and how long it stays locked in, while profit only gives a period to period view. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says that in a projection the sum of the negative cash flows is the total funding the business needs. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor defines IRR as the return per year a project generates, calculated as the discount rate at which NPV becomes zero, and illustrates it with 80 invested and 100 returned a year later giving 25%. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says the IRR rule is to accept a project when IRR is greater than the cost of capital, which is equivalent to a positive NPV. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says IRR ignores scale: a tiny process improvement can show 100% IRR while a marketing campaign ten times larger shows 15%, and the small project cannot be scaled up to match. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says IRR assumes every extra rupee earns the same return, which is reasonable for VCs investing across many startups, and that VCs use IRR extensively. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says NPV is better for startups focused on achieving scale and is used by large corporations for internal decisions, while IRR can be better for small, high growth companies that are short of capital. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says an NPV profile, which plots NPV against a range of discount rates, works as a sensitivity analysis when you only know the range of return investors want, and the rate where the line crosses zero is the IRR. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says that when capital is cheap a project with larger but later cash flows beats one with smaller but earlier cash flows, because you do not mind waiting. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
