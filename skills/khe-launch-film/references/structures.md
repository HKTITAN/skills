# Structures

A structure is the order in which a film presents its evidence. Pick it by the question the brief's viewer is asking ([brief](brief.md)), never by the look of a film you admire: two films can share real UI, the same type and the same music and still answer different questions. Structure and register are separate choices. The structure says what comes first and what the film ends on; the register ([launch-video-registers](launch-video-registers.md)) gives the cut rate, the still share and the loudness, and the beat timings still come from [launch-video-structure](launch-video-structure.md).

The eight below come from Hiten Shah's study of eight 2026 launch films. His scene times were read from sampled frames and are approximate, so none of them is a number this skill plans from.

## Pick by the viewer's question

| The viewer asks | Structure | What the film does | Needs | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What useful result do I get? | Inputs become one output (Granola Briefs) | Brings a few scattered sources on screen one at a time, then turns them into a single artifact shown large enough to recognise | Two or three real inputs and the real output | A viewer can name what went in and what came out |
| How does it work? | One guided task (Linear Diffs) | Stays inside the product from a recognisable starting state, through the one place the user acts, to the changed state | A true capture of the whole task | The changed state is readable at feed size |
| How fast can I get there? | Short path to success (Cursor Origin) | Cuts the route to a handful of real states and ends on a finished state the audience already values | Four or five real states and the exact end state, confirmed by the product owner | The shortened path still happened in that order |
| What does it improve in what I already have? | Visible change (CleanShot Studio Mode) | Shows a raw artifact with a real problem, the control applied, then the improved version at the same speed | One source, untreated and treated | The improved version is easier to follow, not just busier |
| How does the whole process connect? | End-to-end workflow (Notion Ship OS) | A person states the job, then one piece of work moves through every stage to a finished result | A short real speaker clip and one real artifact at each handoff | A viewer can retell what moved through the system and where it ended |
| How different are the two outcomes? | Side-by-side comparison (OpenAI Ultrafast) | Starts two versions from the same state and lets them diverge on screen | Two comparable runs, or a metaphor labelled as one | A literal comparison is fair; a metaphor claims no measurement |
| What happens while the work is going on? | Live process to artifact (Claude Meetings) | The product joins work already in progress, changes an intermediate artifact, and the finished artifact appears at the end | The human task, the product's visible contribution, the final output | A viewer can separate what the people did from what the product did |
| Which capabilities matter? | One outcome per card (Firecrawl Job Tools) | A run of product cards, each with one highlighted result that is new | Real or approved cards with correct labels and examples | A viewer can name what each card showed; five cards saying one thing is a montage |

## Rules

- **Choose the structure whose question matches the promise.** Borrow the decision a film made, never its surface: its typography, music, transitions and colour are secondary to the job, and its footage and branding are its maker's.
- **Write the real footage the structure needs before you choose it.** If your sources cannot supply a row's "Needs" column, the structure is not yours to borrow ([brief](brief.md), The source inventory).
- **A glimpse of the result may open any structure.** One of the most common revisions Hiten Shah proposes is a 1 to 2 s preview of the result in the first 3 s, then the real path to it. It is a hypothesis to test on cold viewers, not a measured gain; inside a problem-first film it moves the name, so check the name window again.
- **Some films have another job.** A film made to establish a belief or make an object desirable can carry no product UI at all, and is judged against that job: an abstract announcement or a physical product reveal fails no rule by showing no screen. Its brief still names one moment, and its register comes from the picking list as usual ([launch-video-registers](launch-video-registers.md)).
- **Dense UI is not legible UI.** The guided-task and card structures fail most often at phone size. Crop to the changed region, keep enough context to show the workflow is real, and check at the destination size ([launch-video-formats](launch-video-formats.md)).

## Gotcha

Two of the eight look alike and are not: inputs-become-output prepares something before the task, and live-process-to-artifact helps during it. Pick by when the product does its work in the viewer's day. A preparation product told as a live process implies a capability it may not have.
