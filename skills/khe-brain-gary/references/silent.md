# Silent

The corpus is 584 articles from garyvaynerchuk.com, published from 2013 to 2026, and 145 long videos from 2008 to 2026: 101 from his own YouTube channel, 25 guest appearances on other shows, 8 episodes of his podcast and 11 more talks. Sources were fetched on 2026-11-21. His short videos, tweets, other posts, about 2,000 audio-only episodes, paid sessions and four books were not read. This file is the list of what the corpus does not hold, or holds only in a passing remark, so a reply says "not in the corpus" in place of inventing a view in his voice.

## The rule

1. If the question's topic is in the table, the answer is silence. Say it in the first sentence.
2. The reply still has a `Source:` line: the list that shows which pieces were read.
3. The last line of the reply is `Not in the corpus:` and then the topic.
4. A nearest line may be offered, labelled as nearest, with what it does say and the context it says it in. Never continue from it to what he must think.
5. A topic marked "Passing remark only" is thin, not empty. Report the remark, its year when the row gives one, its source, and any theme file the row names. Say the row holds only this, not that he said nothing more.
6. A topic missing from this table and from every theme file is also silent. Add a row after you have checked the theme files.
7. Fame is not evidence. However often a view is attributed to him, a view that is not in the sources is not in the corpus. Say it is not in the pieces read, and that his short videos, tweets, other posts and paid material were not read.
8. Only his own lines count. A host's, a guest's or a team writer's statement does not make a topic covered.
9. Silence here is silence in this corpus, never proof that he has no view. Write "the corpus does not cover it", never "he has no opinion". For personal topics say only that the corpus does not cover them, and do not guess.

## Not covered

The gate reads the first two columns. When a sentence marked as his contains a watched word and cites a piece, the gate prints the candidate line `silent-topic:` so the sentence is looked at again. The third column starts with why the topic is silent: out of corpus by rule, searched and not found, or a passing remark only.

## What to say when a topic is silent

1. Open with the answer: the corpus does not cover the topic. Name the topic in his-sources words, for example "The corpus does not cover how to value a company."
2. If the row has a nearest line, offer it as nearest, with its source and what it does say. Do not carry it forward to what he would say about the question.
3. For a row marked "Passing remark only", give the remark and its source, and name any theme file the row points to. Say this is the line the list holds on it.
4. Keep the `Source:` lines for the pieces you cite.
5. The last line of the reply is `Not in the corpus:` and then what is missing, for example `Not in the corpus: how to value a company.`

The books are represented only by what he says about them in these sources. Their text was not read, so no reply describes what a book contains.
