# How Astrotalk found product-market fit, Puneet Gupta

Typed notes from an orientation talk by Puneet Gupta, founder and chief executive of Astrotalk. He described the product changes that customers asked for, one after another, until the business scaled.

## What was said

### Start narrow and listen

- Puneet Gupta says that in services, people who launched an app with a hundred things failed and people who were very particular about one thing succeeded.
- He says a founder does not know what the customer wants until the product hits the market.
- He says Astrotalk began as let us start something, see how it works and take it from there.

### Changes customers asked for

- He says customers wanted an audio call instead of a video call, and when Astrotalk offered it 90% of customers shifted to audio.
- He says fixed 30 minute sessions were a problem, because a customer who spoke for 20 minutes still paid for 30 and one who needed more had to buy another 30.
- He says moving to per-minute pricing was a game changer, and customers stopped feeling the app was cheating them.
- He says Astrotalk let customers recharge during a chat, and focused on the app experience and on keeping customers in the app.
- He says customers asked to pick their astrologer themselves, and some spoke to several astrologers for a second and third opinion.
- He says business was slow during the day, so Astrotalk added chat, although customers had said chat would not give them the feel.
- He says he felt they had found product-market fit from the day customers began chatting with astrologers during the day, and from then they knew it could scale.

### Product first, then marketing

- He says they first built a good product and then did marketing, and that everybody needs to do marketing.
- He says the business went from 10k a day to 50k a day after one Quora answer was published.
- He says Astrotalk invests in marketing in the US, and 23% of revenue comes from outside India.
- He says that if someone criticises you, do not comment and let it go.

### The astrologer is the product

- He says the astrologer is the product, so Astrotalk interviews each one for communication skills and empathy.
- He says the first astrologers were called from Justdial listings, and going from 1 to 10 took a long time because many could not talk well.
- He says Astrotalk tests astrologers with case studies and hires those who are about 70% accurate on a person's past.
- He says Astrotalk shuts down the profile of an astrologer who takes customers offline.
- He says 90% of revenue comes from repeat customers.

### Who the customer is

- He says the customer is in tier 1 cities and includes senior executives, mostly aged 25 to 35.
- He says 60 to 70% of the business comes from questions about marriage and breakups, and people over 30 ask about career and business.
- He says only the first session is free, and that over 5 crore people have taken a free session.

## Examples and cases used

- Astrotalk: video to audio, fixed sessions to per-minute pricing, then daytime chat, each change coming from what customers did.

## Numbers given

- Astrotalk, as told by Puneet Gupta: revenue of 4 crore rupees a day and 85% market share.
- Astrotalk: 90% of revenue from repeat customers, 23% of revenue from outside India.
- Astrotalk: 20 rupees a minute when per-minute pricing came in.
- Astrotalk: over 5 crore people have taken a free session.
