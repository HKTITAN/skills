# Collectibles, flipping and NFTs

This file collects what he said about garage-sale flipping, sports cards in 2019 to 2021, NFTs from 2021 to 2026, and collectible IP in 2025 and 2026. The garage-sale material is mostly 2017 to 2019 articles and Trash Talk videos; the card and NFT views are dated by month and year, and several are his own holdings or his own products. Prices, predictions and market verdicts here are what he said in that year, not facts about value, and VeeFriends and his card sets are his pitch for his own things.

## Sports cards: what he looks at and what he warns about

- In 2019 he said that in the long term the best sports card investments will be icons, and that too many people would invest in young stars who in 7 to 15 years will not matter culturally and would be better off selling those cards in 24 months than holding them for a decade. (Sports Cards Values Will Explode In Certain Categories. Here's Why, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/sports-cards-value-will-explode/)
- In 2020 he said grading is a currency that could change, with PSA first, BGS second and SGC third that year, and a machine possibly replacing human graders within about seven years. (9 factors to consider before buying sports cards, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/9-factors-to-consider-before-investing-in-sports-cards/)
- In 2020 he listed nine factors to weigh before buying sports cards, starting with raw versus graded. (9 factors to consider before buying sports cards, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/9-factors-to-consider-before-investing-in-sports-cards/)
- In 2020 he said cards are a hobby first and very volatile as a business, and that anyone who expects to buy what he tweets and fund retirement faces a rude awakening. (9 factors to consider before buying sports cards, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/9-factors-to-consider-before-investing-in-sports-cards/)
- In June 2020 he said it is not a gold rush and takes 50 to 200 hours of learning, from blowout forums, Twitter, Discord and YouTube. (11 Tips to Sports Card Investing in 2020 Tea With GaryVee at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pjsf7ImcFDI&t=585s)

- In 2020 he said buying and selling raw cards is a great opportunity and an incredible place for people with time and limited budgets, and that he buys little raw himself because he does not understand how to grade on the eye. - In 2020 he said a 2003 LeBron Topps Chrome he begged his audience to buy at $1,050 went past $6,000 within nine months, his "mother of all shots."
