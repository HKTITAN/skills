<!-- Asked: who said prototyping is thinking, and what was the point? -->
Emil Kowalski, in one article: "Prototyping is thinking." [1] The point around it is that building option A and option B and comparing them beats theorizing, and that it counts only when you then choose one, because nothing stops you shipping both [1].
Source: [1] Friction as a Feature, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/friction-as-a-feature
Not in his writing: whether the phrase is his own coinage; the article does not say where it started.
