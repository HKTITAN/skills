# Content and documenting

This file collects what he said about making content, up to his 2026 talks on AI and volume. Most of the 2013 to 2019 material is articles under his byline; the keynotes and interviews carry the later view. Platform advice, post counts and format rules carry their year, and so does any advice on quantity; some pieces on his site are team-written guides and are marked as such, and figures about his own operation are his own.

## Why make content at all, and should it be free or sell?

- In 2013 he said great content alone is no longer enough, because supply is abundant and anyone can have a platform, so you have to build context with the end user. (The #1 Way to Develop Context, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-1-way-to-develop-context/)
- In 2014 he argued that everybody is a media company: in order to ask for sales, you need to provide value. (How Small Businesses can Leverage Social Media Content Production, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-small-businesses-can-leverage-social-media-content-production/)
- In 2014 he told small owners with no staff or budget to take one, two or three hours from staffing, strategizing and selling and put them into content, because lack of resources is no excuse. (How Small Businesses Can Compete With Fortune 500s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-small-businesses-can-compete-with-fortune-500s/)
- In 2014 he told small businesses to become a local newspaper: get on Facebook, Twitter, Pinterest, Medium and YouTube and crank out content on everything that matters to your community, such as interviewing the local star high school football player. (How Small Businesses can Leverage Social Media Content Production, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-small-businesses-can-leverage-social-media-content-production/)
- In 2015 he said producing a media site, articles and videos was the number one way to sell a product, because it builds relationships with customers and partners. (5 Core Values of Business from GaryVee, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-core-values-of-business-from-garyvee/)
- In 2015 he said your best advice, the advice you give every day, is what you should give away free, and he is blown away that people do not see it. (Why You Shouldn't Charge For Your Best Work, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-shouldnt-charge-for-your-best-work/)
- In 2017 he said the cost of entry had dropped 100 fold, so a commercial that cost hundreds of thousands 15 years earlier could be a two-minute phone clip pushed with Facebook ads. (Having a Media Company Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/media-company-mentality/)
- Also in 2017 he said content need not be about your product: a plumbing service can post about the Yankees game to connect with fans without confusing customers. (Having a Media Company Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/media-company-mentality/)
- In 2017 he told B2B companies to act like a media company, not an advertiser, with a weekly or monthly podcast, and said 99 percent of companies make commercials instead. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=855s)
- In 2018 he said he acted like Wine Spectator and not QVC on Wine Library TV, and from episode 50 to 1,000 never featured a wine of which he had more than five cases. (How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=540s)
- In 2018 he said he keeps a wall between free content and commerce: he does not monetize his personality and instead runs a wine store and an agency that sells to big companies. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=2700s)
- In 2019 he said the most value you can give is free with no funnel behind it, and that giving more than you ask always works over a marathon. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=270s)
- In 2019 he said content for lead gen must give value and must not mix the give and the ask in one piece, or it is really an ask and does not convert. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=1260s)
- He returns across years to teaching people how not to need you: in 2016 a computer repair salesman making how-to videos, in 2019 a mechanic showing how to change a spark plug, and in 2024 a video about how not to hire you. (Leaving Money On the Table to Win the War, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/leaving-money-on-the-table-to-win-the-war/; What Most People Don't Think About When Running a Business in 2019, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/running-a-business/; Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=540s)
- In 2019 he said by 2020 being a media company would be as required as paying taxes, as basic as managing cash flow, even though most of that room did not agree yet. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=810s)
- In 2020 he said he was a huge fan of free content plus paid access, because ebooks and courses are stunningly commoditized and an hour of the creator is less scalable than a $19 ebook. (Free Content To Paid Access: A Business Model To Consider, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/free-content-to-paid-access-a-business-model-to-consider/)
- In 2022 he said how seriously you take making content is the number one practical variable in growing a business, and no excuse changes it: "Social media is not broken in that story. You're the one that's broken." (Why You Need to Make Even More Content in 2022 at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yemqSyqKqpY&t=0s)

## Should I document or create?

- In 2016 he told a young man to post seven to 25 pieces of content a day on Instagram and Snapchat stories, start a pillar show, and document versus create. (Advice to a Young Man That Many of You Need at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HwXsFPZp3fQ&t=720s)
- In 2016 he wrote that documenting is still creating but rests on practicality, like The Real World and the Kardashians, instead of inventing fantasy like Star Wars and Friends. (Document Don't create: Creating Content that Builds Your Personal Brand, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/creating-content-that-builds-your-personal-brand/)
- In 2017 he said people are crippled when they must create something original, and it changes once they see their own life as the creative. (Influencer Marketing Personal Branding Strategy Changing the Education System at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fpypQ3s7t5A&t=180s)
- In 2017 he said to live your real life on camera and see if people care, because your truth scales and acting is exhausting and does not. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=1755s)
- In 2017 he said the daily vlog is his way of being the world's mentor, and that he would rather document his life and be an example for generations to come than take 15 minute meetings with every 19 year old who likes entrepreneurship. (Why I Vlog Everyday, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-i-vlog-everyday/)
- In 2017 he said about 90 percent of his life never reaches the internet, and that nothing about his family, kids and private life does. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tests His Mental Toughness While Eating Spicy Wings Hot Ones at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zl-X3dhooPM&t=765s)
- In 2018 he said to show the struggle, long hours and mistakes, not a delusional fantasy of success. (It's Time You Stop Comparing Yourself To What You See On Instagram, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/time-stop-comparing-see-instagram/)
- In 2020 he said documenting is not editing highlights: if you are thinking about what to say you are creating, and showing failures and process is why people will love you. (Are You the Type of Person That Needs to Start Investing in Pokemon Cards Tea With GaryVee at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FHShGCwpxmY&t=1305s)
- In 2023 he said everything he shows is real, it is just not the complete part of his life, and there are things he decides not to share. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=1530s)
- In 2025 he said he may be using his children too much in content and is starting to see the negatives. (This Will Make You Insanely Rich The Next 365 Days Will Make Millionaires - The Iced Coffee Hour at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgg8XGgDLQ4&t=1530s)
- In 2025 he said to make five random mundane videos in two weeks that show who you are as a human, and that people who do not win on social are not mixing it up enough. (AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 48:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=2925s)
- In 2026 he said the original **document, don't create** countered people over-analyzing creation, and he now says document and create, shooting long form with clips and hooks in mind. (GaryVee Reveals the Biggest Business Opportunity of 2026 - Young and Profiting at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDmEmp9LfAY&t=2610s)
- In 2018 he said not to fake it until you make it, because inflated backstories and advice are exactly why people do not break out. (How to Get Your First 1000 Followers on any Social Media Platform at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tooAu9qNai8&t=270s)
- He made garage sale videos to show the world he came from: in 2016 he said it was not a lead-in to a course, and in 2021 he said readers told him a $25,000 check was out of reach, so he went back to garage sales. (The Law of Attraction Importance of Sales Skills & Working Smarter AskGaryVee 232 at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R9GEnEFJtXE&t=585s; How Gary Vee Thinks about NFTs My First Million #209 with Gary Vaynerchuk at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4uw6Zpq1U1A&t=810s)

## How much should I make?

- In 2013 he said that to be one of the real Viners you post two or three a day, not one every three weeks. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=2565s)
- In 2016 he said the hunger for the one perfect piece cripples people, and to do a long-form show weekly plus Stories six to seven times a day. (Document Don't create: Creating Content that Builds Your Personal Brand, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/creating-content-that-builds-your-personal-brand/)
- In 2017 he said he posts 7, 10, 12, 15 or 30 times a day and believes everybody eventually will. (What Makes Me a Great CEO, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/makes-great-ceo/)
- In 2018 he said five, seven, 15 or 30 pieces a day is the bar, and someone a year into his content who posts fewer than 20 or 30 a day is not executing. (Practical Business and Marketing Advice for Dominating 2019 Keynote at NAC at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWiLQGEqqmo&t=810s)
- In 2019 he said he creates 100 pieces of content a day around his personal brand. (How Facebook Advertising Can Work For You 2020 Strategy, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/facebook-advertising-marketing-strategies-for-2019/)
- In 2019 he told a mortgage room to make 30 to 50 pieces every day, and in 2020 told another room to make seven to 25 a day across three to nine platforms. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=585s; How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=1080s)
- In 2019 he said his method is to have a thought, make it, and see: if it does well, great, and if it does terribly, even better. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=1035s)
- In 2021 he said that when starting, frequency matters more than quality, and that quality is subjective while quantity is not. (Instagram DM: Direct Messaging to Network, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/network-instagram-direct-message/; 15 Tips on How to Brand Yourself Online, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-strategies-for-personal-branding-online/)
- In 2023 he said the answer to quality or quantity is both, volume plus strategy, and that posting thousands of times with bad strategy is like doing a thousand push ups incorrectly and not getting muscles. (Thinking of starting a podcast? Read this first, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/thinking-of-starting-podcast-read-this-first/)
- In February 2024 he said he used to push quantity and now calls quality over quantity his new framework, set by talent, effort and resources. (2 things I'm focused on for 2024: Pbcpg & Live-Streaming, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-things-im-focused-on-for-2024/)
- In May 2024 he said the volume advice he gave for years was not put in context and is really a quality to quantity ratio, but that quantity is not debatable. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 52:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=3150s)
- In 2024 he said to produce one to two brand new videos and pictures a day and to tweak them slightly for each platform, such as TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and Facebook, five to seven platforms in all. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=405s)
- In 2025 he said that if you think you are posting enough you should quadruple it, and that he makes 50 pieces a day with 30 full-time employees on content. (One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=495s)
- In 2025 he said he would post 4,000 times a day using AI at the same cost, and in 2026 he said his team posts 500 to 1,000 pieces a day across 100-plus handles. (AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=180s; GaryVee Reveals the Biggest Business Opportunity of 2026 - Young and Profiting at 33:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDmEmp9LfAY&t=1980s)
- In 2025 he said he does not see himself as overexposed because each show he joins has a different audience, and that posting less so each post feels special lowers your net awareness. (This Will Make You Insanely Rich The Next 365 Days Will Make Millionaires - The Iced Coffee Hour at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgg8XGgDLQ4&t=990s; This Will Make You Insanely Rich The Next 365 Days Will Make Millionaires - The Iced Coffee Hour at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgg8XGgDLQ4&t=1080s)
- In 2026 he said people demonized his quantity argument for a decade and now everyone with AI is doing it. (GaryVee Reveals the Biggest Business Opportunity of 2026 - Young and Profiting at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDmEmp9LfAY&t=2700s)

## How do I make one piece into many?

- In 2013 he described **DJing content**: take an interview, pick the seven best lines, and make Facebook quote cards, a Pinterest infographic, a Tumblr gif and a trend-tied tweet. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=405s)
- In 2015 he said most of what his team puts out is based on the seed of the #AskGaryVee Show, and that its questions come from his community. (#AskGaryVee Episode 60: Dress Codes Meditation & Horrible Wine, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-60-dress-codes-meditation-horrible-wine/)
- In 2016 he said that if you invest in core pillars such as the #AskGaryVee Show and DailyVee, your team can build content around them, and that he made content alone for seven or eight years first. (Content on Content on Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/content-content-content/)
- In 2016 he said to figure out your **sawdust**: the byproducts of a weekly show are like sawdust from cutting 2x4s that someone repackages and sells. (Content on Content on Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/content-content-content/)
- In 2017 he said **pillar content** creates content underneath it at scale, and that is how he produces an obnoxious amount. (How To Start A Gary Vaynerchuk Original at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5jiifErFEE&t=270s)
- In 2017 he said a podcast made by transcribing his video show underperformed until he rebranded and made original audio, and he told a vlogger to strip ten minutes of audio a day for a podcast. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=2700s; Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=2655s)
- In 2018 his team wrote that one article was made from an article, which was made from a video, which was filmed during the creation and review of an article, as an example of how he scales his content production. (Blowing Up My Plans to Write a Blog Post on My Analysis of Instagram TV, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/blowing-up-my-plans-to-write-a-blog-post-on-my-analysis-of-instagram-tv/)
- In 2019 he laid out a reverse pyramid: one pillar piece, such as a daily vlog or keynote, that his team repurposes into dozens of smaller pieces, each contextual to the platform it goes to. (The GaryVee Content Strategy: How to Grow and Distribute Your Brand's Social Media Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-garyvee-content-strategy-how-to-grow-and-distribute-your-brands-social-media-content/)
- In 2019 he said his team repurposed one of his keynotes into 30-plus pieces for over 35,000,000 total views. (The GaryVee Content Strategy: How to Grow and Distribute Your Brand's Social Media Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-garyvee-content-strategy-how-to-grow-and-distribute-your-brands-social-media-content/)
- In 2019 he said to record an hour show and chop it into 17 to 20 pieces. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=630s)
- In 2019 he said guesting on other people's podcasts wins twice, because the host's later guests improve and your answers become content, and in 2023 an article on his site used the term double dip for using a podcast appearance to produce content. (50 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Digital Agency Expo Keynote at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Wcg15FaUnU&t=1800s; how to start creating content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-start-creating-content/)
- In 2022 he said long form chopped into micro pieces is the model he pioneered and everyone now uses. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=630s)
- In 2023 he said to record long form and edit it into short clips that bring awareness to the long form. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 1:00:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=3600s)

## Which medium and which platform context?

- In 2013 he said people post a four-minute YouTube video on Facebook with a link and it will not work, because each platform needs its own native content. (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/)
- In 2013 he said to ask who is on a platform and why before deciding what to post, using a woman on Pinterest at 8 PM with intent to shop as the example. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=720s)
- In 2013 he said he almost calls it slang marketing when you make content in each platform's native format, such as an animated gif on Tumblr and an infographic on Pinterest. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=450s)
- In 2015 he said video was the most important content strategy, and that without one for the biggest platforms you lose. (The Rise of Video Marketing on Social and How It Affects Your Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-rise-of-video-marketing-on-social-and-how-it-affects-your-business/)
- In 2015 he said video length should match the length of the story, and he mocked gurus preaching six-second Vines, while expecting pillars at three, six and eleven minutes. (Why Everybody Has Permission to Make All Kinds of Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-everybody-has-permission-to-make-all-kinds-of-content/)
- In 2016 he said "Content is king, but context is God." (Content is King But Context is God, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/content-is-king-but-context-is-god/)
- In 2016 he said you cannot put a TV spot on Snapchat and expect it to work, because it is like a 57-year-old in a suit at a hipster party in Brooklyn. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=855s)
- In 2016 he said Instagram is a museum and Snapchat is a conversation, and that Stories lowers the bar so influencers and businesses can post more without making each piece perfect. (My Two Cents on Instagram Stories and the Evolution of Social Platforms, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-cents-instagram-stories-evolution-social-platforms/)
- In 2017 he said many people should not be producing video because it is not the best way they communicate, and that strong writers who were posting on Medium would win. (How To Start, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-start/)
- In 2017 he said many people should not make video and should write seven, 15 or 25 sentences inside their Facebook and Instagram feeds. (How To Start A Gary Vaynerchuk Original at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5jiifErFEE&t=315s)
- In 2018 he said long copy is a white space on Facebook and Instagram, since people spend 12 minutes filtering a photo and mail in the words. (Practical Business and Marketing Advice for Dominating 2019 Keynote at NAC at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWiLQGEqqmo&t=2115s)
- In 2019 he said a niche B2B podcast can be enough, even 1,500 listeners could support millions of dollars a year in revenue, and inviting experts or thought leaders onto the show plays to their ego. (4 Key B2B Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-b2b-marketing-strategy-for-2019/)
- In 2019 he said on LinkedIn people are in a business mindset and on Instagram an entertainment mindset, so adjectives and examples must fit each. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=1125s)
- In 2019 he said to pick a content form you do not hate, because if you only do it because he said so you are finished; introverts do well writing, and a podcast only asks questions. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=2430s)
- In 2020 he said format and length do not matter, creative quality does, and called his approach "Contextual warfare, not content warfare." (How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=1755s)
- In 2023 he said the written play is a real play, and that if you fear the camera you can be an audio monster with clips and iPhone memos. (Insane Things Can Happen If You Give Garage Sales a Chance l GaryVee Audio Experience at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hZE4m5TZCic&t=945s)
- In 2023 he said he used to push everything on one channel and now says the algorithm lets you mix topics. (Don't Live For the Algorithm Live For Your Happiness GaryVee Audio Experience at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ft8R-b-3NXM&t=990s)
- In 2023 he named **platforms and culture** as his agency's philosophy: know how each platform works and the culture behind a trend, and ask why you posted the last post. (GaryVee wants your attention w/ VaynerX's Gary Vaynerchuk Masters of Scale Complete at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbBO9tjw9bc&t=720s)
- In 2024 he said platform best practices are not the strategy, because the platforms tell you those; add culture and a narrow consumer cohort, such as headlines for the parents of a lazy wealthy 21 year old. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 41:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=2475s)
- In 2024 he said you need not be on camera, and people boring on video who know their craft do well, showing only their hands or using a voice-over. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=1890s; Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 33:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=1980s)
- In 2025 he said there are three ways to win, written, audio or video, and you must be honest about which you are comfortable with: it is okay not to love video but not okay to lie to yourself. (One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=45s)
- In 2025 he said to post the same idea on each platform with different copy and a slightly different video, and that at first you will probably post the same thing everywhere. (AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=2295s)

## What makes a piece good, and who is it for?

- In 2013 he said storytelling is the most important thing you can do online in any format, at 140 characters or 90 seconds. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=855s)
- In 2015 he said compelling content is either entertainment and escapism, or utility and value, and every post should be one or the other. (AskGaryVee Episode 122 What I Would Do If I Was Graduating College Today at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=thExIE-X4Ag&t=945s)
- In 2015 he gave two ways to judge content: daily and weekly shares, comments and views, and the big picture of how many people are buying. (The Best Way to Evaluate Your Content And Some Guides to Improve It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-way-to-evaluate-your-content-and-some-guides-to-improve-it/)
- In 2018 he said to test many pictures, videos and words at small spend, and then pour money behind the winner. (95% of You Will Ignore This 2018 Marketing Strategy Business Squared Keynote in Australia at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5Yst1Si3fw&t=1710s)
- In 2019 he said production value was overvalued over 30 to 50 years and context and story undervalued, and that is the opportunity. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 27:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=1620s)
