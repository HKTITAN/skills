# Newsletter

The Tidy Ledger, every second Tuesday. Where Ines gives the method in full. Read [[voice]] first; the treatment comes from [[repurpose]] and the opening from [[hooks]].

## Limits

- Limit: 450 words
- This is her own ceiling. Email has no length limit.

## Facts

- Facts checked: 2026-11-10
- Email sets no length limit on a message body. Her ceiling is a choice, taken from the issues she says people finish.

## Confirm before relying on this

- How many characters of a subject line her readers' mail apps show. It differs by app and by screen.

## Form

- The first line of the draft is the subject line, written `Subject: ...`.
- Plain text. No images, no buttons.
- Forms: how-to issue, note ([[content-types]]).
- One thing to do, and the exact wording to copy when there is wording.

## Who reads here

Both segments ([[audience]]). Write for the first-year reader and the settled one will skim.
