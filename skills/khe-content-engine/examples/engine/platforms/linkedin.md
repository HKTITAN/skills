# LinkedIn

Where Ines says how she found something out. Read [[voice]] first; the treatment comes from [[repurpose]] and the opening from [[hooks]].

## Limits

- Limit: 1500 characters
- This is her own ceiling. The platform allows more.

## Facts

- Facts checked: 2026-11-10
- A post holds up to 3,000 characters. Source: LinkedIn Help, "Post and share updates".

## Confirm before relying on this

- How many characters show before the "see more" cut. It differs by device and nobody at LinkedIn publishes it.
- Whether a link in the post body changes its reach.

## Form

- The opening line carries the post alone.
- One idea to a paragraph, a blank line between paragraphs.
- Forms: story post, note ([[content-types]]).
- The caveat is in the post, never in a comment.
