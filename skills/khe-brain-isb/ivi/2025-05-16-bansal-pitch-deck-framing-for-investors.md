# Pitch deck bootcamp on framing for investors, Mohit Bansal

Typed notes from the first pitch deck session with Mohit Bansal of DeckRooster. He argued that a deck exists to close the gap between how the founder sees the business and how an investor sees it, and that the framing has to be fixed before the pitch.

## What was said

### What a pitch deck is for

- Mohit Bansal says a pitch deck bridges the gap between the investor's perspective and the founder's own perspective of the business.
- He says most founders do not think about how investors are thinking about the problem.
- He says visuals and aesthetics are band-aid solutions to this core problem.
- He says to ask what motivates the investor and what return they are looking for. If they want four times their money, show five.
- He defines a pitch as making a case for why someone should invest in your business right now.

### How investors judge

- He uses a car race. To guess the winner you look at the car, meaning fuel, past victories, weight, engine, age and terrain, and at the driver, meaning the driver's history and strategy.
- He says a deck must show both opportunity potential and business capability.
- He says market size is one way investors evaluate potential.
- He says a product with a future product-market fit but no current one does not feel right to an investor.
- He says confidence in future product-market fit can still be high without the product, if there is logic behind it.
- He says every part of a startup that gets built de-risks it, because once that part is solved its value is already proved.
- He says founder-market fit is an advantage in the short term and not in the long term, because the team will execute and more people will come in.

### Insight

- He says founders have unknown knowns, and must tap them to find insights, which raises an investor's confidence in product-market fit.
- He says to go to customers and talk to them until you yourself feel excited about the fit.
- He says not to trust customer inputs on the product, because customers are driven by many other factors and you have to gauge something deeper.

### Fix the framing first

- He says founders put all their attention on the pitch, when the framing must be fixed before the pitch.
- He says the fit between the need gap and the product has to be created first in the mind of the investor.
- His example is a coffee shop, which can be positioned in five different ways, such as a meeting place.
- He says the framing must be proved or backed with data, and the framing layer has to be in alignment with the business layer.
- He says the narrative is crucial. A founder can start from how the company began and add layers to tell it better.
- His reframing example is a regional language product moved from serving Bharat, which reads as cheap and not paying, to serving the mother tongue, which is aspirational.
- He says that when the target group is not coming out right, an introspection exercise uncovers it.

## Examples and cases used

- A car race: judging the car and the driver stands for judging opportunity and capability.
- A coffee shop: one business with five possible positionings.
- A regional language product: reframed from Bharat to mother tongue so the market reads as aspirational.
