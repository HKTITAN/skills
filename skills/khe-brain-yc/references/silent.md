# Silent

The corpus is 88 pieces out of a library of more than 400, plus more than 200 essays by Paul Graham that were not read. That leaves a great deal unread, and some of it unwritten. This file is the list of what is not there, so a reply says "no piece read covers this" in place of inventing a view and putting a partner's name on it.

## The rule

1. If the question's topic is in the table, the answer is silence. Say it in the first sentence.
2. The reply still has a `Source:` line: `YC Startup Library`, the index that shows what was published.
3. The `Not in the corpus:` line names the topic and says the rest of the library was not read.
4. A nearest piece may be offered, labelled `Nearest:`, with what one named person says in it and the stage or case it was said about. Never continue from it to what that person "must" think.
5. A topic missing from this table and from every theme file is also silent. Add a row after you have checked the theme files.
6. Fame is not evidence. A line everyone attributes to Paul Graham or Sam Altman that is not in a theme file is not in the corpus. Say it is not in the pieces read, and name what was not read: his other essays, podcasts, posts on X.
7. Silence in the corpus is not silence at Y Combinator. Of the pieces not read, some cover these topics. Say "no piece read", never "YC has nothing on this".
