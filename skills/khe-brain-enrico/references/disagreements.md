# Disagreements and changes

Open this file before answering any question where he may have said two different things. Give both sides with their videos and dates, and never merge them into one view or pick the one that sounds newer or wiser. The videos run from 2021-08-24 to 2026-11-15 and the technology changed under him, so a line from 2022 and a line from 2026 are often answers to different worlds.

## Disagreements

### Did tech products get worse?

- In May 2025 he said the more popular a product you love becomes, the worse it gets, and he named Google Podcasts, Arc and Facebook as cases. (Why Tech Gets Terrible When It Gets Popular at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQLVgmhgtpQ&t=0s)
- In September 2025 he said the complaint that products keep getting worse is false: "It's called enshittification. And it's not true." (Tech's Weirdest Problem at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQe1r2MOwt8&t=0s)
- In that September 2025 video he said the products were always great and still are, and that only the price people expect to pay was broken by years of free and subsidized services. (Tech's Weirdest Problem at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQe1r2MOwt8&t=540s)
- In November 2025 he said the era when technology was built by nerds who were just having fun is gone, and that the reason Silicon Valley killed fun is the same reason every piece of tech today seems lifeless, boring and almost too perfect. (How Silicon Valley Killed Fun at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYdLq9icgrI&t=0s)
- His own bridge is partial: in May 2025 he said his marginal user law is a different thing from enshittification, which he defined as products getting more expensive as the company monetizes. (Why Tech Gets Terrible When It Gets Popular at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQLVgmhgtpQ&t=45s)
- In June 2025 he said Notion and Notion Mail had so far avoided getting worse as they got popular. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=1260s)
- How to report it: Give the May 2025 line that popular products get worse and the September 2025 line that they did not, each with its date and its own cause. Do not say he believes one or the other, and do not treat the two videos as one theory.

### What makes tech addictive: the people, the build loop, or the scroll?

- In 2021 he said the smartest people of the time work at tech companies trying to send the perfect trigger to the whole world, and he called the result the most powerful habit machine in human history. (How Notifications Are Messing With Your Life at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXqpy_H8onc&t=135s)
- In 2023 he said it is a misconception that people at tech companies have evil agendas, and he counted himself among those ordinary people as a working product manager. (The Real Reason Your Tech Makes You Unhappy at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vbg5TeoLTW0&t=0s)
- In 2023 he put the cause in the objective: things spiralled when companies swapped value for engagement as the number the build loop chases. (The Real Reason Your Tech Makes You Unhappy at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vbg5TeoLTW0&t=315s)
- In May 2024 he said neither smartphones nor social media are really the problem, and that the problem is the loop used to build tech products. (What Happens Now That We All Hate Using Our Phone at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2LpR9zhEl2A&t=45s)
- In March 2026 he said mindless scrolling is not explained by social media being addicting, and pointed to a 100 millisecond input decision on phones instead. (The Most Important Tiny Decision In Tech History at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EVZpW9Rdwk&t=0s)
- In that 2026 video he said swapping taps for scrolls, such as pull to refresh and infinite scroll, is what made products more addicting. (The Most Important Tiny Decision In Tech History at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EVZpW9Rdwk&t=585s)
- In February 2026 he said nobody set out to design something manipulative, and that the designs that exploit psychology simply win the experiments over time. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Short Form Content at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69Gw7aoWMMI&t=765s)
- How to report it: Name the cause he gave in the video being asked about, with its year: the engagement metric in 2023 and 2024, the scroll-first input in 2026. He never blames evil staff, but do not claim he has one single explanation.

### Who should fix it: users, or companies and platforms?

- In 2021 he said the fix should come from people, not companies or governments, and that he does not believe companies should stop what they are doing. (How Notifications Are Messing With Your Life at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXqpy_H8onc&t=450s)
- In September 2024 he said regulation and complaining will not stop manipulation, and that understanding the tricks is the best people can do. (The Internet Wants Your Money Here's How To Outsmart It at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4OLt9pFlRvo&t=630s)
- In 2023 he asked the industry to change: measure something other than engagement, with an operating system level happiness score shown in app stores. (The Real Reason Your Tech Makes You Unhappy at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vbg5TeoLTW0&t=450s)
- In March 2024 he said notification overload has to be solved at system level by Android and iOS as a standard, not by apps and not by users switching things off. (Why You Don't Reply To Your Friends at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVjRPwneLiI&t=765s)
- In January 2025 he said a pattern that only makes social media less addictive does not work because platforms will not adopt it, so a fix must also be good for the company. (Social Media Is Broken But We Can Fix It at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqYIUV7Eqjk&t=720s)
- How to report it: Say that in 2021 and September 2024 he put the job on users, and in 2023, March 2024 and 2025 he designed fixes for platforms and companies. Give the year with each and do not conclude he thinks regulation or company change is the answer.

### Does turning notifications off solve the problem?

- In 2021 he told people to remove the problem at the origin by turning off notifications that bring no value, which he preferred to Do Not Disturb. (How Notifications Are Messing With Your Life at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXqpy_H8onc&t=495s)
- In 2023 he said about 80% of notifications are useless and told people to cut too many and bring some back later. (How I Finally Fixed My Digital Life at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PhEfKCHr7Y&t=135s; How I Finally Fixed My Digital Life at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PhEfKCHr7Y&t=180s)
- In March 2024 he said switching his own off one by one gave him control back but made him miss an important email, an opportunity and a friend in town. (Why You Don't Reply To Your Friends at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NVjRPwneLiI&t=315s)
- In January 2026 he cited research that a fully silent phone makes people check it more, not less. (Why Our Tech Got Silent at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_rFJutK25VU&t=675s)
- In July 2026 he said turning notifications off does not fix it, citing research where people got more done but felt more anxious and lonelier. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notifications at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dx2yPk0FsGM&t=450s)
- How to report it: Give the 2021 and 2023 advice to cut hard, then the 2024 and 2026 lines that cutting is only a partial fix, with dates. Do not present the witch hunt as his current advice without the later doubts, and do not say he withdrew it.

### Is the short-form feed a broken format or the best interface ever built?

- In May 2023 he said the short-form formula is not even working for the companies that built it, because ads perform poorly and TikTok was still losing money. (Short Form Content Is Broken Can We Fix It at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1F85wJmhDkA&t=405s)
- In that 2023 video he called the whole system bad for companies, creators and users alike. (Short Form Content Is Broken Can We Fix It at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1F85wJmhDkA&t=495s)
- In February 2026 he called the short-form feed the most successful user interface in the world, with every button tuned to make people spend more time. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Short Form Content at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69Gw7aoWMMI&t=0s)
- In January 2025 he proposed fixing the feed by adding choice, with diagonal swipes toward depth or toward something different. (Social Media Is Broken But We Can Fix It at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqYIUV7Eqjk&t=495s)
- In February 2026 he said the feed is built for extreme simplicity, and gave that as the reason his own diagonal swipe idea has not been built. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Short Form Content at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69Gw7aoWMMI&t=315s)
- His own bridge: in the 2026 video he noted that he has made videos himself about the effects of short-form videos, and said the lesson of this one is that when something becomes this ubiquitous it is usually because of design, engineering and psychology underneath that we never get to see. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Short Form Content at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=69Gw7aoWMMI&t=945s)
- How to report it: Give the 2023 verdict that the format fails everyone and the 2026 verdict that it is the most successful interface, with dates, and mention that he flagged the difference himself. Do not say he changed his mind about the harm.

### Is chat the right way to use a computer?

- In December 2022 he said ChatGPT feels magical by design because it speaks the language of human conversation. (ChatGPT is Frighteningly Good at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lzQD-7lZJU&t=180s)
- In October 2022 he expected AI to bring a big shift in user interfaces, away from graphical interfaces and back toward typing what you want as a text prompt, and he described "the art of the prompt" as an exploding craft with communities and marketplaces where prompts are bought and sold. (AI Generated Videos Are Scary at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Es01hmaXux0&t=315s)
- In May 2024 he said that for most things a chatbot is the wrong way to interact with a computer and makes tasks worse than before. (AI Has A Huge Problem at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY_b8w8u9NY&t=90s)
- In May 2024 he said chatbots had recreated the command line problem solved 40 years earlier, and that needing a prompting course defeats the point. (AI Has A Huge Problem at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY_b8w8u9NY&t=540s; AI Has A Huge Problem at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY_b8w8u9NY&t=585s)
- In May 2024 he predicted the hype would die down when nobody uses the chatbots. (AI Has A Huge Problem at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CY_b8w8u9NY&t=765s)
- In March 2026 he said AI chat took off because its core input works: a forgiving text box beats clicking through 40 submenus. (The Most Important Tiny Decision In Tech History at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7EVZpW9Rdwk&t=810s)
- His own bridge came in September 2025: graphical interfaces are great at output and bad at input, and chatbots are the reverse. (The Weird Death Of User Interfaces at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KG4ONHqF1qg&t=270s)
- How to report it: Quote the year with every line: praise in 2022, the wrong tool for most tasks in 2024, a winning input in 2026, and the 2025 input and output split as the link. Do not say he is for or against chatbots.

### Are graphical interfaces dead?

- In September 2025 he said the user interfaces of the last 40 years are dead, and that their heavy polish is the sign of the end. (The Weird Death Of User Interfaces at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KG4ONHqF1qg&t=0s)
- In June 2026 he said every attempt to kill the user interface has been a gigantic failure, naming the Humane Pin, the Rabbit and the first vision for voice assistants. (The Weird Future Of User Interfaces at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f32W5BEzWN0&t=45s)
- In June 2026 he also rejected the idea that everything becomes agentic with no interface. (The Weird Future Of User Interfaces at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f32W5BEzWN0&t=315s)
- His 2026 position is that the future is all three together: traditional UI with direct manipulation where it is the best interface, usually for simple jobs; agentic systems for complex jobs; and generative UI wherever it solves the problem best. (The Weird Future Of User Interfaces at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f32W5BEzWN0&t=810s)
- Even in 2025 he admitted he was still building for the old world of UI in his own startup. (The Weird Death Of User Interfaces at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KG4ONHqF1qg&t=675s)
- How to report it: Give the 2025 claim that the old interfaces are dead next to the 2026 claim that killing the interface always failed and that all three types will coexist. Do not report the 2025 title claim on its own.

### Does AI take creative and coding work away?

- In May 2022 he said AI cannot replace the top creatives, who will work hand in hand with it. (The First AI That Really Scared Me at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZdHxkx4i4w&t=360s)
- In that same 2022 video he said AI is the new printing press: as the press made the copyist monks' job basically worthless, some people who adapt or distribute content will become authors but most will be masters of a craft that has lost most of its value, and as someone who makes YouTube videos he found this both exciting and really scary. (The First AI That Really Scared Me at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZdHxkx4i4w&t=315s; The First AI That Really Scared Me at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZdHxkx4i4w&t=405s)
- In October 2022 he said he does not think AI took creativity from humans, and that what followed AI images was the opposite of mass layoffs. (AI Generated Videos Are Scary at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Es01hmaXux0&t=135s; AI Generated Videos Are Scary at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Es01hmaXux0&t=270s)
- In December 2022 he said AI will supercharge many people's work, eliminate the work of many others and put whole sectors in disarray. (ChatGPT is Frighteningly Good at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2lzQD-7lZJU&t=360s)
- In May 2025 he said the people who can code are going through big changes, with graduates struggling to find junior developer work. (Behind The Scenes Of Lovable's Insane Design at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZoVLS-Wxr1s&t=1080s)
- In that May 2025 video he also said that although most Lovable users are non-technical, the people creating the most advanced products with it right now are developers and people who were already building before, who now just do it at a much greater speed. (Behind The Scenes Of Lovable's Insane Design at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZoVLS-Wxr1s&t=585s)
- In September 2025 he said designers will not disappear but will move from drawing screens to writing rules for generated interfaces. (The Weird Death Of User Interfaces at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KG4ONHqF1qg&t=765s)
- How to report it: Give the line for the kind of worker asked about, with its year, and state both halves: tools multiply the best people, and some work loses its value. The 2025 coding lines come from a video made with a vibe coding company, so say that too.

### Do ideas matter, or only execution?

- In 2021 he said "ideas are worthless and execution is what really matters". (Reacting to Innovative Ideas From Reddit at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=82E7VoxCPc0&t=0s)
- In November 2023 he called the Humane AI Pin a case of the same rule: the goal of a product is to turn the idea into meaningful execution, and the pin did not. (We Need To Talk About The AI Pin at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E2-L-Gv0wr0&t=450s)
- In May 2025 he said that being able to build apps and products this way, limits included, is starting to change one of the golden rules of tech, that ideas are nothing and execution is everything. (Behind The Scenes Of Lovable's Insane Design at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZoVLS-Wxr1s&t=720s)
- In January 2026 he said vision alone fails and the levels underneath decide the outcome, using General Magic against the iPhone. (The 7 Levels of Tech Design at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQVRfadJkJk&t=945s)
- How to report it: Give the 2021 and 2023 rule, the May 2025 line that cheap building is changing it, and the January 2026 line that execution under the vision still decides. Do not say he dropped the rule, and do not say he kept it unchanged.

### Should you listen to users, watch them, or build for yourself?

- In 2021 he said you can only win by listening to the people who use your product. (The Spotify Shuffle Button is a Lie at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NhAIA-o7f4M&t=225s)
- In January 2023 he said to validate by talking to the narrow audience you defined, and that asking friends and family is the worst mistake. (Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HABFEs90MZ8&t=225s)
- In November 2024 he called this the most important rule in product management: "Don't listen to users but watch them as carefully as possible." (How Your Tech Tricks You at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7nnyv4yJGJc&t=945s)
- In June 2025 he said that "talk to users" is true advice, but that there looks to be an even better way, building for yourself, as the people who made Notion Mail did when they built it for the two of them. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=540s)
- In February 2026 he said that just working through the list of things people want fixed is the instinct of people who are not chief product officers, and that the Snapchat redesign, which drew a petition with 1.2 million signatures, was not wrong: change itself feels wrong, even when the new thing is objectively better. (The Weird Reason Your Tech Stays Broken at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-51ocAD6zBI&t=315s; The Weird Reason Your Tech Stays Broken at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-51ocAD6zBI&t=810s)
- How to report it: List the four positions in date order with their videos. Do not turn them into a single method, and do not say the 2024 rule cancels the 2021 one, since he never says so.

### Must a product start from one narrow problem and ship early?

- In January 2023 he said to step back from your idea, which is usually a solution, to the problem you are trying to solve, and then narrow your audience to the people who have that problem in its most severe form. (Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HABFEs90MZ8&t=45s)
- In April 2022 he said the basic and sometimes bad first versions of famous apps should give builders permission to start. (Famous Apps Looked Like This at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Sn8Glj5NW4&t=405s)
- In January 2023 he said Notion broke the rule of winning one niche first and it still worked. (Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Notion at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWGviOMQqSw&t=45s)
- In June 2025 he said Notion did not start from a specific user problem but from the building blocks common to all tools. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=135s)
- In March 2025 he said Figma did the opposite of the launch fast playbook by working in private for 3 years, and that this became a secret of its success. (Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Figma at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0jze5RbyoW4&t=540s)
- How to report it: Give his general advice from 2022 and 2023, then the Notion and Figma cases he praises for breaking it, with dates. Say he presents them as exceptions that worked, not as a new rule.

### Is stripping a product down good for it?

- In December 2023 he said WhatsApp won by refusing features while rivals added them, and called staying basic the hardest thing to do. (Why Half The World Uses WhatsApp at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1O1u0UO99NE&t=225s)
- In February 2024 he used Craigslist, which added no features and never redesigned, to show the power of keeping things simple. (The Weird Rise Of Anti-Startups at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xL1MOOD5Ox8&t=135s)
- In May 2025 he said consumer products get dumber because features that give control fail tests against a user with no tolerance for complexity. (Why Tech Gets Terrible When It Gets Popular at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQLVgmhgtpQ&t=585s; Why Tech Gets Terrible When It Gets Popular at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQLVgmhgtpQ&t=630s)
- In June 2025 he said simplicity must in time be balanced against advanced users. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=1080s)
- In June 2026 he said the best products are built by saying no. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Figma at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5t8dh3DSdBk&t=765s)
- His own bridge in June 2026: keep a powerful product simple by letting users reach the complex version step by step, and accept that this will anger some of them. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Figma at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5t8dh3DSdBk&t=945s)
- How to report it: Give the praise for saying no from 2023, 2024 and 2026 and the May 2025 complaint that simplifying for new users ruins products, with dates. Offer the 2026 step by step line as his own way to hold both, not as a settled answer.

### Are metrics and testing how good products get made?

- In 2021 he said the winners in notification design are the ones who design better tests, not the ones with the best idea. (How Notifications Are Messing With Your Life at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXqpy_H8onc&t=315s)
- In 2023 he said the build, measure and test loop is not the problem and is an incredibly effective way to build products. (The Real Reason Your Tech Makes You Unhappy at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Vbg5TeoLTW0&t=225s)
- In July 2024 he said usability testing chipped away, pixel by pixel, at the touches that made technology cooler or weirder. (What Happened To User Interfaces at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HdozqSVF-VM&t=270s)
- In May 2025 he said A/B tests are the mechanism that hands product decisions to people who do not care about the product. (Why Tech Gets Terrible When It Gets Popular at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BQLVgmhgtpQ&t=450s)
- In November 2025 he said that in the 2010s operators took over tech: MBAs, finance and business people came in because tech was where the money was and where they could optimize it further. Boring was profitable, apps ended up all looking the same, and operators kicked the creators out. (How Silicon Valley Killed Fun at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYdLq9icgrI&t=585s)
- In May 2026 he said that as someone who builds products he accepts that the YouTube features people dislike exist because they work. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of YouTube Videos at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=giP1iIbSVh0&t=225s)
- How to report it: Say he defends the testing loop as a method in 2021, 2023 and 2026 and blames its results in 2024 and 2025. Give both with dates and do not call him for or against A/B testing.

### Is Notion a good tool?

(Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Notion at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWGviOMQqSw&t=45s; Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Notion at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aWGviOMQqSw&t=180s)

- In October 2023 he listed Notion among the over-complicated note systems people abandon, while keeping a Notion board for his video ideas. (How I Finally Fixed My Digital Life at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7PhEfKCHr7Y&t=450s)
- In June 2025 he said Notion feels good because of a thousand small details, and used it as his example of successful products that look minimal and simple. (The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=585s; The Ridiculous Engineering Of Notion at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=b00sgRR_Vc0&t=1035s)
- How to report it: Give the 2023 criticisms and the 2025 praise with dates, and note the 2025 video was filmed with Notion's team. Do not report either as his whole verdict on Notion.

### Is Apple's control good?

- In February 2024 he said walled gardens are a good thing because complete freedom blocks mass adoption. (The War To Put Apps In Front Of Your Eyes at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETk5I99d8nc&t=360s)
- In that February 2024 video he said Apple has lost the trust of many developers, and that by milking the App Store it is threatening its own future, because developers will think twice before building on its next platform. (The War To Put Apps In Front Of Your Eyes at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETk5I99d8nc&t=675s)
- In July 2024 he said Apple went from nothing to the best spot to win the AI war by controlling access, and called the move genius. (How Apple Stole AI From Everyone Else at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gV1f-lS0C9g&t=135s)
- In that July 2024 video he said the developers Apple mistreated are the one thing standing in the way. (How Apple Stole AI From Everyone Else at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gV1f-lS0C9g&t=540s)
- In October 2024 he said the next thing ecosystems can offer is actual life improvement, won not by forcing you to choose the ecosystem but by making it a stupid choice not to, which puts people outside the right ecosystem at a disadvantage. (How Tech Secretly Killed Choice at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UdzT1YxeZsY&t=900s)
- His own bridge in February 2024: the truth is in the middle, with the right amount of freedom plus a bit of wall. (The War To Put Apps In Front Of Your Eyes at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETk5I99d8nc&t=495s)
- How to report it: Give the line that fits the question, control as a product strength or control as a risk to Apple, with its 2024 date, and add his middle position. Do not say he is for or against walled gardens.

### Is the Vision Pro a smart move or a loser?

- In June 2023 he called it an insanely good product and said of Apple and its sales: "The reality is that they don't really care how much it sells." (Apple Built The Vision Pro To Fail And It's Genius at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNEni_DP4fY&t=225s)
