# Disagreements and changes

Open this file before answering any question where he may have said two different things. Give both sides with their sources and years, and never merge them into one view or pick the newer one as the true one. In 2017 he said he does not care what people think of him yet reads every comment, and he said he listens to everybody on the micro plane and to nobody on the macro plane. (My 2017 Manifesto, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-2017-manifesto/; Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 1:37:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=5850s)

The corpus runs from 2008 to 2026.

## Changes of mind and positions that moved with the year

### Snapchat, from Stories to Spotlight

- In 2013 he said Snapchat Stories was high-friction and a bad fit for an app built on one-to-one messages. (I Made a Bad Call on Snapchat but Here's Why I'm Totally Cool With It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-made-a-bad-call-on-snapchat-but-heres-why-im-totally-cool-with-it/)
- In 2014 he said he was "100% wrong" about Snapchat Stories and that you learn the most from being wrong. (I Made a Bad Call on Snapchat but Here's Why I'm Totally Cool With It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-made-a-bad-call-on-snapchat-but-heres-why-im-totally-cool-with-it/)
- In January 2016 he said every business should take Snapchat seriously as a customer channel, and that six months earlier it had still been too young. (Deploying Against the Snap: Takeaways from Snapchatting in Vegas & Ces, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/deploying-against-the-snap-takeaways-from-snapchatting-in-vegas-ces/)
- In 2016 he said Snapchat was the place where most of America's 18 to 35 year old attention was, and that Instagram Stories was going to be a big deal. (The Strategy Behind Snapchat's Memories Update, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/strategy-behind-snapchats-memories-update/; My Two Cents on Instagram Stories and the Evolution of Social Platforms, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-cents-instagram-stories-evolution-social-platforms/)
- In February 2017 he said Snapchat had been about to become a monster with 40 and 50 year olds, but Instagram's stories gave them no reason to download another app, so it lost momentum. (Joe Rogan Experience #910 - Gary Vaynerchuk at 1:15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OLHdzLpMg-s&t=4545s)
- In March 2017 he agreed with Tony Robbins that the pick had moved from Snapchat to Instagram Stories in six months, and called it a moving market. (Tony Robbins Unshakeable Gratitude & Focusing on Your Steak AskGaryVee 242 at 36:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9O8haH2tHWY&t=2160s)
- In 2024 he said he was very bullish on Snapchat Spotlight. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=2565s)
- He says he changed: on Stories in 2014, and on Snapchat being too young in January 2016. (I Made a Bad Call on Snapchat but Here's Why I'm Totally Cool With It, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-made-a-bad-call-on-snapchat-but-heres-why-im-totally-cool-with-it/; Deploying Against the Snap: Takeaways from Snapchatting in Vegas & Ces, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/deploying-against-the-snap-takeaways-from-snapchatting-in-vegas-ces/)
- How to report it: Give the call for the year asked about, with its year. Never give the 2013 Stories verdict as his view without the 2014 line above.

(How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=1665s; How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=1710s)

- In 2025 he said the most important platform for him and his team in the last month was Facebook proper, classic Facebook. (AI & Social Media Playbook For The Next 5 Years You Can't Hide From This GaryVee Q&A at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_RiVvFwZ2sw&t=1080s)
- How to report it: He does not say he changed; the dates show it. Name the year with every Facebook line, and keep organic and paid apart. Do not give the 2015 line on organic reach as his verdict for 2023 or 2025.
