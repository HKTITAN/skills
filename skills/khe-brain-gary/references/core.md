# Core

Read this first for a broad question about Gary Vaynerchuk, then open one theme file for the detail. Every line paraphrases what he said in the source it cites. The sources are among 729 pieces read in full, 584 articles from garyvaynerchuk.com, 2013 to 2026, and 145 long videos, 2008 to 2026, most from his own channel and the rest guest appearances, episodes of his podcast and talks; the reference files together cite 575 of them.

Platform calls, figures about his companies and his views on NFTs, cards, AI and hours of work belong to the year he said them, so check the section on what moves with the year before giving any of them as his view. Some articles under his byline read as team-written guides, and his pitches for his own companies, events, books and VeeFriends are his claims, not facts.

## The one sentence

Know yourself and bet on your strengths, go where attention is underpriced, give more than you ask, and play the long game with macro patience and micro speed. (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/; 2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=540s; Giving Without Expectation, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/giving-without-expectation/; How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=855s)

## The load-bearing ideas

1. **Attention is the asset, and he trades it like a day trader.**
   In 2015 he said every person has one job, to grab attention, tell the story and then sell, whether raising money for a school or selling sneakers.
   He calls this the **attention graph**: he got bigger results on Twitter at 100,000 followers in 2007 than at 1.2 million in 2015, because the supply of noise changed.
   By 2016 he said he **day trades attention**, and in 2020 he defined it as watching what people actually do, not chasing the newest app.
   In 2026 he still said a t-shirt, a run for mayor and a nonprofit all depend on attention.
   More in references/attention-and-media-shifts.md (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=945s; 2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=405s; Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=990s; What Is Day Trading Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-day-trading-attention/; If You're Not Posting You're Falling Behind at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TDWFU_uEexc&t=135s)

2. **He goes where attention is underpriced.**
   In 2015 he said one of his biggest skills is marketing in the year you live in, and that he will never be passionate about marketing anywhere other than where he thinks the current consumer's attention is, and more importantly the places underpriced because they are young and unrecognized.
   His line is that **marketers ruin everything**: his 1997 wine email list got 91.1 percent open rates.
   In 2019 he said Amazon was the number one Google AdWords advertiser from 2001 to 2008 while AdWords was the best arbitrage, and he called his split the **79/21 rule**: he spends about 80 percent of his time on the hottest platform and about 20 percent getting a feel for everything else.
   More in references/platforms-year-by-year.md (Stop Marketing Like It's 2012, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-market-your-business/; 2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=450s; How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=405s; The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-79-21-rule-to-build-a-long-term-business/)

3. **Every business is a media company.**
   In 2014 he told small businesses without the resources for content creators and editors to take hours away from staffing, strategizing and selling and put one, two or three hours into becoming a media company.
   In 2017 he said that if you do not start thinking like a media company and put brand along with content first you will lose, because content is the cost of entry and that is where attention is.
   In 2018 he said every business is a media company first and then does what it does, and that eBay needs a real editor in chief, not branded content.
   More in references/content-and-documenting.md (How Small Businesses Can Compete With Fortune 500s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-small-businesses-can-compete-with-fortune-500s/; Having a Media Company Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/media-company-mentality/; Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=2610s)

4. **Content only works in its context.**
   In 2011 he said content is not enough, context is the word for the next decade, and we are in a context war.
   In 2013 he said people forget that a viewer spends a hundredth of a second on a post, so a seven minute YouTube video in a Facebook feed stops no one.
   His line is **content is king, but context is god**: a forty-year-old woman is in a different mindset on Facebook than on Pinterest, and the story has to fit.
   In 2023 he packaged it as **PAC**, platform plus culture.
   More in references/content-and-documenting.md (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=990s; Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=450s; Content is King But Context is God, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/content-is-king-but-context-is-god/; How to make content in 2023, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-make-content-in-2023/)

5. **Document, don't create, and make far more of it.**
   In 2016 he told a would-be creator to put out content on a very regular basis, to document rather than create, and to post seven to 25 pieces of content a day.
   He says **document, don't create** is still creating, but it rests on practicality instead of invented stories, the way The Real World and the Kardashians stand to Star Wars and Friends.
   His **reverse pyramid** starts from one piece of **pillar content**, a daily vlog, keynote, Q&A show or another video, that a team repurposes into dozens of smaller pieces for each platform.
   In 2019 he said that if forced to pick he would choose quantity, because it leads to quality by giving you more opportunity to find out what quality is.
   More in references/content-and-documenting.md (Advice to a Young Man That Many of You Need at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HwXsFPZp3fQ&t=720s; Document Don't create: Creating Content that Builds Your Personal Brand, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/creating-content-that-builds-your-personal-brand/; The GaryVee Content Strategy: How to Grow and Distribute Your Brand's Social Media Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-garyvee-content-strategy-how-to-grow-and-distribute-your-brands-social-media-content/; Quantity Can Be The Gateway Quality – A New Way To Think About Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/quantity-can-be-the-gateway-quality-a-new-way-to-think-about-content/)

6. **Give before you ask.**
   His 2013 framework is **jab, jab, jab, right hook**: give, give, give, then ask.
   He chose a boxing metaphor, and says that if you are always throwing right hooks, people duck because they know what is coming.
   In his world he always tries to go 51/49, giving the other side 51 percent of the value, where he says most marketers try to grab 100 percent.
   In late 2017 he said that if he wrote the book again he would use many more jabs, because as the supply of content and noise grows you have to differentiate with more value, not less.
   In 2026 he told creators to get the ratio right.
   More in references/brand-marketing-and-sales.md (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=90s; The 50/50 of Snapchat Collaborations, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-50-50-of-snapchat-collaborations/; Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=2385s; 1 man VS 100 Billion AI Creators at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHjHVG6mBg0&t=2655s)

7. **Brand beats sales over time, and he expects it to be the only thing left.**
   In 2008 he said legacy over currency is something he believes in very heavily.
   In 2017 he defined brand as how you feel in the moment you interact with a product, service or business, and said the difference between brand and sales is converting against creating an experience, and the experience always wins.
   In 2019 he told an audience that everything is a commodity except brand: anyone can make a sneaker, but the swoosh is worth a trillion.
   In 2025 he said that once bots and agents make buying decisions everything else gets commoditized, so people must set a preference such as Domino's over Pizza Hut.
   More in references/brand-marketing-and-sales.md (Gary Vaynerchuk WineLibrary com Talks at Google at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2c_L60H-cpY&t=1170s; Sales Vs. 8. **Self-awareness comes first, and you bet on your strengths.**
   In 2013 he called self-awareness his biggest gift and said to partner with people naturally good at what you are not; he would never be the CFO, so he hired for it.
   In 2015 he called betting on strengths possibly the most underrated strategy in modern business, because people are obsessed with fixing weaknesses.
   In 2016 he said he would rather be only passable at finance and leave room to be world-class at what comes natural.
   In 2021 he called delusion a self-awareness trap, where people overrate their strengths and underrate their weaknesses.
   More in references/self-awareness.md (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/; When It's Okay to Sweat the Small Stuff, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/when-its-okay-to-sweat-the-small-stuff/; MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=450s; Road to Twelve and a Half: Self-Awareness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-self-awareness/)

9. **Entrepreneurship is a talent, and not everyone is meant for it.**

   In 2019 he called it the greatest era of fake entrepreneurship, with people pushed in by the cool factor, and said a business you like that pays $136,000 a year is a remarkable feat.
   In 2017 he said to build a real business with real revenue instead of raising money, because business is money in against money out and celebrating your fundraise is a joke.
   In 2024 he said entrepreneurship got so cool that people not meant for it try it.

    In 2021 he said people misread this as a contradiction when it is both at once, and in 2023 he said he operates on macro patience and micro speed when he makes decisions.

## His method, in the order someone would live it

1. Know yourself first: take the five to ten people who know you best, split them into two groups, and ask one person from each group to tell you honestly what you are best and worst at. (4 Ways To Find Out What Your Strengths Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-ways-to-find-out-what-your-strengths-are/)
2. Pick the thing by trying many things, and once you find what you are good at, find a way to make money from it. (3 things to do if you don't like your job, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-things-to-do-if-you-dont-like-your-job/)
3. Stay practical about cash, which he calls **cash is oxygen**, and know whether you have a year of rent and overhead before you start. (My Advice for First-Time Entrepreneurs, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advice-first-time-entrepreneurs/)
4. Put about 80 percent of your time on the hottest platform and go as hard as you possibly can on it, and spend about 20 percent of your time getting a feel for everything else. (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-79-21-rule-to-build-a-long-term-business/)
5. Start from one piece of pillar content, such as a daily vlog, keynote or Q&A show, and have your team repurpose it into dozens of smaller pieces for each platform. (The GaryVee Content Strategy: How to Grow and Distribute Your Brand's Social Media Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-garyvee-content-strategy-how-to-grow-and-distribute-your-brands-social-media-content/)
6. Shape each piece for the mindset people are in on that platform. (Content is King But Context is God, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/content-is-king-but-context-is-god/)
7. Add your two cents: in 2019 he described his $1.80 strategy as finding the top performing posts in your space and leaving a meaningful comment on them, 90 times a day. (5 LinkedIn Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/linkedin-marketing-strategies/)
8. Give first, and throw the right hook only after something like 30 pieces of content. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=2700s)
9. Post more content for more audience segments, and refine it on what they respond to, based on actual data, instead of only depending on the subjective opinions of decision makers. (Quantity Can Be The Gateway Quality – A New Way To Think About Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/quantity-can-be-the-gateway-quality-a-new-way-to-think-about-content/)
10. Be patient in years and fast in days, because you only get one at bat. (You're Gonna Die, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/youre-gonna-die/)
11. Take ownership by asking whether you messed up, and if someone you hired messed up, it is still your fault, which means you have the power to fix it. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Accountability, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-accountability/)
12. Keep squeezing today's platform while learning the next one on a second plane. (AI is Ending the Social Media Era and What Comes Next Gary Vee - Sinead Bovell at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUvWVF28CNM&t=2520s)

## What moves with the year

Never state any of these without the year he said it.

- **Which platform is underpriced.** In 2015 he said Facebook dark posts gave him the best conversion funnel he had seen since the beginning of Google AdWords; in 2026 he said anyone can sell products on TikTok through TikTok affiliate, that he has figured out TikTok Shop live shopping, and that attention can be overpriced or underpriced with no general rule.
