# Brand, marketing and sales

This file collects what he said about brand, sales, advertising, agencies and marketing. Most of the early material is articles from 2014 to 2019, the keynote years are 2016 to 2020, and the 2023 to 2026 videos add the agency arguments. Platform advice and price claims carry their year, and figures about VaynerMedia or his own projects are his own.

## Why brand ranks above sales

- In 2017 he wrote that brand versus sales is converting versus creating an experience, and that creating the experience always wins. (Sales Vs. Branding, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/sales-vs-branding/)
- In the same 2017 piece he defines brand as how you feel in the moment you deal with a business, and he wants people to react to his name as the guy who gave more than he asked. (Sales Vs. Branding, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/sales-vs-branding/)
- In 2017 and again in 2021 he says the greatest companies do not sell, they brand. Apple is his case: he says it never runs ads telling you to buy, only ones about sharing an experience. (Sales Vs. Branding, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/sales-vs-branding/; 15 Tips on How to Brand Yourself Online, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-strategies-for-personal-branding-online/)
- He asks whether your next right hook helps you win in 25 years. Anyone can have 15 minutes of fame or one great sale, and few build a relationship that lasts. (Sales Vs. Branding, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/sales-vs-branding/)
- In 2016 he told a room of salespeople that they dismiss social because they work on quant, data and short-term metrics, and that brand and long-term business metrics are a different game. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=585s)
- In 2016 he said math is commoditized and brand is not: no math made him buy his Nikes, Nike's brand did. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=2520s)
- In 2017 he said marketing and branding are just grown-up sales and that both work. Retargeting and affiliate sell directly, while branding explains why you bought what you are wearing. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=1575s)
- In 2019 he told a room he said was almost entirely in the sales business that he always beats salespeople because they look at short-term ROI. He does not care if he is losing 27 to 3 at halftime, because what counts is the score at the end. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=855s)
- In 2019 he told mortgage brokers that everything is a commodity except brand. A sneaker can be made in China by anyone, but the swoosh is worth a trillion. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=1935s)
- In 2020 he said that for people 43 and older, building a business or awareness meant spending a lot of money on direct mail, newspaper, radio and television, a very high cost of entry to build brand, and that brand is the whole game. (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=90s)
- In 2021 he called marketing oxygen and said it is the only thing he knows that can double a business without a significant change in the product or service. (35 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Sage Summit 2021 at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie_pcLsdb5E&t=225s)
- In 2024 he said brand matters more than sales, because sales is what you do when you are not great at brand: brand comes to you and sales goes to them. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1575s)
- In 2023 he said the ultimate form of selling is brand. Apple and Nike do not sell most of their product through AdWords or affiliates, and when people care about you or your business, that is the ultimate form of selling. (The Ultimate Social Media Marketing Strategy at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmA-LrQzcY&t=0s)
- In 2020 he said it is brand and product, not brand over product: the swoosh matters, but so do sneakers that work. (What New Brands Need To Know, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-new-brands-need-to-know/)
- In 2015 he said the product is the variable of your success, and great marketing on a bad product only exposes it faster. (Gary Vaynerchuk Interview on the Startup Grind LA 2015 at 57:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3C4cuCc7jU&t=3465s)
- In 2016 he said that from a business standpoint Instagram's logo change has zero impact on its bottom line, and that a logo or even a company name will always be trumped by the quality of the product. (How Important is a Logo to Your Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-important-is-a-logo/)
- In 2015 he said to use your own name rather than a company name or an expertise tag, because you will evolve and your name keeps the following you built. (Personal Brand or Company Brand: How to Make the Decision, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/personal-brand-or-company-brand-how-to-make-the-decision/)
- In 2016 and 2017 he said a founder does not need a personal brand: if you build the best thing you win, and 99 percent of businesses do not have a personal brand in front of them. He called a personal brand a slang term for your reputation. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 58:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=3510s; Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=1575s)
- In 2024 he predicted that within 15 years most big consumer brands will be built on a human personality. He said the number three cereal and the number seven bottled water will be owned by a person, not just endorsed by one. (2 things I'm focused on for 2024: Pbcpg & Live-Streaming, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-things-im-focused-on-for-2024/)

## Jab, jab, jab, right hook

- **Jab, jab, jab, right hook** means give, give, give, then ask. In 2013 he said people who only throw right hooks get ducked, because the other side knows what is coming. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=90s)
- In 2013 he said that when you give a lot up front with no expectation, a good share of your right hooks land, and you stop worrying when one misses. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=180s)
- In 2013 he said most people promoting on social are in right hook mode, while the roughly 3 percent who understand it are so purist they only jab and are crippled by the ask. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=495s)
- In 2013 he said the ratio depends on how good your product is. Apple at the iPhone's height could be all right hooks, and BP after the Gulf oil spill needed to be in the heavy jab business. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 1:00:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=3645s)
- In 2015 he said he talks a lot about jabs but not enough about the right hook, and that a bad hook can negate all the jabs before it. (To Make the Sale You Need to Go In for the Kill, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/to-make-the-sale-you-need-to-go-in-for-the-kill/)
- In 2015 he said that if he were CEO of Toyota, his Super Bowl ad would say who he is and that he wants you to buy his cars. He calls ponies, eagles and cute dogs distractions from the ask. (To Make the Sale You Need to Go In for the Kill, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/to-make-the-sale-you-need-to-go-in-for-the-kill/; 5 Core Values of Business from GaryVee, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-core-values-of-business-from-garyvee/)
- In 2016 he clarified that jabs do not entitle you to land the right hook. Content and gifts only give you the audacity to ask, and you still have to earn the right. (The One Thing I Didn't Clarify Enough in Jab Jab Jab Right Hook, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-one-thing-i-didnt-clarify-enough-in-jab-jab-jab-right-hook/)
- In 2016 he called a 45-day book push a global right hook after two years of putting out nothing but free content, and told anyone bothered by it to unfollow him, since he would be back to free content in July. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=2340s)
- In 2016 and 2017 he said it means give then ask, not give then take. People misuse it by sending him things and expecting something back, and giving with an expectation is asking twice. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=2025s; Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=1485s)
- In 2017 he told a studio owner it is fine to ask for clients on an arbitrary 3, 6 or 7 percent of posts, as long as the audience does not feel that is your intent. (Tony Robbins Unshakeable Gratitude & Focusing on Your Steak AskGaryVee 242 at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9O8haH2tHWY&t=1800s)
- In 2017 he said that if he rewrote the book he would use many more jabs, because the supply of noise keeps growing and many people think they give value while being selfish. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=2385s)
- In 2019 he said not to mix the give and the ask in one piece of content, because it then converts as an ask and converts poorly. He also called free ebooks and consultation calls manipulation when you expect something back. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=1260s; How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 51:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=3105s)
- In 2023 he said you still need the occasional right hook. He posts sales asks he knows will get 80,000 views instead of his usual 500,000 to a million, because he is in the business to sell. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=990s)
- In 2026 he said the answer to too much selling is to get your ratio right: give good content repeatedly, then occasionally ask. (1 man VS 100 Billion AI Creators at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHjHVG6mBg0&t=2655s)
- In a 2015 fundraising piece he wrote that in sales you never go straight for the kill and you take the time, while a piece about a month earlier said the right hook is when you bring it back around and ask for something in return after the jabs. (Stop Pretending That Fundraising Is Different From Sales, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stop-pretending-that-fundraising-is-different-from-sales/; To Make the Sale You Need to Go In for the Kill, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/to-make-the-sale-you-need-to-go-in-for-the-kill/)
- In 2016 and 2026 he describes **51/49**: give slightly more than you ask for, so you cannot lose. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 50:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=3015s; AI is Ending the Social Media Era and What Comes Next Gary Vee - Sinead Bovell at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUvWVF28CNM&t=2655s)

## Relationships, community and the thank you economy

- In 2011 he said to play offense with a **thank you department** instead of customer service defense: find customers on Twitter and send gestures with no sales ask. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=1440s)
- In 2014 and 2015 he said he preaches community management done by humans, with no scheduled Facebook posts and no automated replies on Twitter, and that community management sells. (Is There Such a Thing As Too Much Community Management, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/is-there-such-a-thing-as-too-much-community-management/; Should Community Management Be A 24/7 Job, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/should-community-management-be-a-247-job/)
- In 2015 he said round-the-clock community management only pays for big global brands such as Pepsi and Toyota. Everyone else should staff when the community is noisiest and listen more than they speak. (Should Community Management Be A 24/7 Job, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/should-community-management-be-a-247-job/)
- In 2017 he said a 13-second personal video replying to a customer who tweets builds more depth than chasing new customers, and that you grow by over-indexing on the ones already in the door. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 1:15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=4500s)
- In 2014 he said one bad tweet does not break a brand, because BP spilled oil and people still buy its gas. Intent trumps all and a sincere apology is powerful. (Gary Vaynerchuk Tells You How to Rock SXSW Full Session Interactive 2014 SXSW at 1:04:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1aSOS9XZL48&t=3870s)
- In 2011 he said **marketers ruin everything**, so you have a window of 36 to 60 months, and that what he teaches will be ruined within five years. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=2205s)
- In 2016 he said that for him ruin is the slang term for being smart and day trading attention, and that the people who create the least friction while promoting their product win, because they do not steal people's time. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=675s)

## Advertising, TV, banners, agencies and where the money goes

- In 2011 he said outdoor, direct mail and TV ads were losing attention: drivers look at phones, and people fast-forward commercials. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=765s)
- In 2015 he called interruptive ads cops and robbers, and said Facebook works because its ads are native to the feed. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 20:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1215s)
- In 2015 he said what matters is how people feel when they see an ad, not how many see it. A hundred million impressions that leave customers angry at your brand mean you lose. (Fat Thumb Clicks: Why I Hate Acura, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/fat-thumb-clicks-why-i-hate-acura/)
- In 2015 he said he still finds value in print and TV, and that "the only thing I care about in marketing is the arbitrage". (Maybe You Should Get Into Print and TV…, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/maybe-you-should-get-into-print-and-tv/)
- In 2016 he said his clients spend 70 to 80 percent on television, print and outdoor, and that 80 percent of the 30 percent that is digital goes to banners or YouTube pre-rolls. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=900s)
- In 2016 he said he dislikes pre-rolls, TV commercials and banner ads because they take his time, and predicted product placement in film and TV would be a much bigger business over 20 to 30 years. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=765s; SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=810s)
- In 2016 he said television advertising was in very bad shape. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=945s)
- In 2017 he called a $6 million Super Bowl spot a steal worth $20 to $40 million on his pricing, and called programmatic banners the worst buy. He said banners and pre-rolls survive because agencies justify a $5 CPM. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=1440s)
- In 2017 he said nothing is dead, things are overpriced: TV and radio still work, but what you pay for is not what you get. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=1440s)
- In 2017 he said most digital money goes to programmatic banners and pre-rolls nobody watches, so this is not traditional against digital. The point is day trading attention. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=765s)
- In 2017 he said WPP, Omnicom and Publicis sell TV and programmatic digital because it earns them the most margin, and that the blame sits with clients, not agencies. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=1800s)
- In 2019 he said companies that do not move ad spend away from billboards and TV would struggle or fail within a decade, and called Toys R Us just the preview. (Effective Ways to Build Trust in the Workplace, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/build-trust-in-the-workplace/; What Most People Don't Think About When Running a Business in 2019, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/running-a-business/)
- In 2020 he said TV commercials are overpriced, yet that the Super Bowl commercial is the most underpriced ad in the world, because "the sport of watching the commercial is real". (What Is Underpriced Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-underpriced-attention/)
- He said that if you asked all 300 million Americans to sell bottled water with a one million dollar budget, he believes 90 percent would make a TV commercial or a billboard. (What Is Underpriced Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-underpriced-attention/)
- In 2024 he said TV agencies still make bad social ads, since a new medium first copies the old one, and YouTube commercials are TV spots run as pre-roll. (How To Build In The Next Era Of The Internet With Chris Dixon - GaryVee Audio Experience at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NwjwfQUEsU0&t=540s)
- In 2023 he predicted the top 45 creative agencies would lose 50 percent market share over a decade, meaning 80 percent of staff let go. (The Main Reason Why Brand is Built on Social Lost in Found Podcast at 27:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eriGPuqRHRo&t=1620s)
- In 2023 he said the Super Bowl is the best ad buy, but 80 percent of its creative is a showcase for the agency, and production and actor fees waste the money. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=2655s)
- In 2023 he said less than 1 percent of large companies take social marketing seriously, and that brands losing on relevance and consideration are obsessed with awareness they already have. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=45s; Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=90s)
- In 2021 he said every agency struggles in his opinion because they are selfish, worry too much about their own margin and sell clients things that are not as good for their business. (The #1 Thing Every Business Needs To Add to Their Strategy This Year at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od-2xtAdaa0&t=1890s)
- In 2025 he told marketing executives to stop wasting money, saying people hold on to billboards, newspapers and television ads while the place where brand and sales actually happen is very underpriced, and that the marketing industry was in crisis. (One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=1710s; One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=1755s)
- In 2020 he said the agency process of strategist research, a hypothesis, selling it to decision makers and then producing the content could take about nine months and depends on one piece of content working out. His alternative is volume: asking whether a hypothesis translates to other groups. (The Definition Of A Post Creative Strategist, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-definition-of-a-post-creative-strategist/)
- In 2021 he described a **post creative strategist** who reads the comments on your creative, sees where attention is going and acts as the brand's ears. He said 99 percent of marketers are not thinking about this role. (Advertising Through The Years: The Evolution of Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advertising-through-the-years-the-evolution-of-attention/)

## Measurement, ROI and budgets

- In 2011 he said the ROI of social cannot be shown in clean data, any more than the ROI of his mother, and that television and print metrics are poor too. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 48:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=2880s)
- In 2013 he said social ROI studies miss because the mechanics are broken, not the tool: IBM's 1 percent study is last attribution to the websites it could track, and the ROI of a piano is very low for most people but large for Elton John. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 1:09:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=4185s)
- In 2015 he said social ROI is calculable and the data is there, and that CMOs still keep multi-million-dollar TV spots because they just work. (Why Social Media ROI is Like Playing the Piano, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-social-media-roi-is-like-playing-the-piano/; What's the ROI of Your Mother, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/social-media-roi-calculate-metrics/)
- In 2015 he said the I in ROI is hustle, and to learn the platforms first: find the mix of creative, copy and strategy, read the insights, iterate, and only then judge the return. (What's the ROI of Your Mother, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/social-media-roi-calculate-metrics/)
- In 2015 he said click-through data can be wrong data, and that chasing a shopper with cookies to the 19th banner can turn them into the Samsung case. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1395s)
- In 2016 he said depth beats width, citing a friend with 297 Instagram followers who may get 150 consuming against one with 11,000 on Twitter who gets four. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 32:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=1935s)
- In 2017 he said that for a business spending $100,000 on conversion, make that spend 20 percent better and put the freed 20 percent into a brand video. His **80/20 budget move** keeps every short-term sale, so there is no pressure from the CFO, the ambassadors or the CEO. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 1:34:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=5670s; Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 1:35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=5715s)
- In 2019 he said the test for any channel is whether it is the best use of your money, not whether it works at all: could the same four thousand dollars get 49 leads somewhere else instead of 18? (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=495s)
- In 2020 he said social is the most trackable channel and that 99.7 percent of businesses do not set it up to measure it, so put a separate Google Voice number on each platform's content to see which leads convert. (How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=720s)
- In 2020 he said to set one fixed brand budget, such as about five points of the marketing mix, agree it with the numbers people and stop debating its ROI. He also said not to blend the brand bucket with the sales bucket. (How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=945s; How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=1575s)
- In 2023 he said a GRP is a proxy and that television reach is potential reach rather than actualized reach. For retail he suggested running media against hold-out clusters to prove it drives incremental growth. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=2205s; Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=1890s)
- In 2023 he said vanity metrics are a disease and that for him it is business over the algorithm. He prefers a higher CPM that reaches likely buyers, like direct mail to wine buyers. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=1305s; How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=2295s)
- In 2023 he named **brand formance**: take a viral organic creative, add an order-now banner and changed copy, and run it as a conversion ad. He adds that viral views do not equal wanting to buy. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=180s; How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=135s)
- In 2024 he said that inside his company he says you cannot measure brand, then measures it by sales: do the brand work in a silo against a consistent baseline, such as three years of the same Black Friday sales, and compare. He gave the 100 days after a Super Bowl ad as an example. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1710s; How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1755s)
- In 2024 he said Google is intent-based sales where you pay a toll for a lead you can measure, while brand comes from social and content, which he says to judge after a year, not in the first week. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 38:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=2295s)

## Influencers, collaborations and endorsements

- In 2014 he said brands should use the influencer model, meaning well-known social users in your space produce content for you, such as a makeup star for a makeup brand. He said any brand not doing well here is losing. (Interested in influencers? Here's what you need to know, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/interested-in-influencers-heres-what-you-need-to-know/)
- In 2015 he said to judge an influencer in three steps, the first being to evaluate very carefully who is following the influencer. (Evaluating the Cost of Using an Influencer in 3 Steps, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/evaluating-the-cost-of-using-an-influencer-in-3-steps/)
- In 2019 he said to build rapport before asking: comment on their last 5 to 15 posts, then make the ask. His outreach math is that if one in 80 says yes and you want 20, you must reach 1,600. (5 Tips for Growing Your Business With Influencer Marketing, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-tips-for-growing-your-business-with-influencer-marketing/)
- In 2019 he said if an influencer outshines your brand, fire them or own the character, since a cartoon does not get drunk or ask for more money. (50 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Digital Agency Expo Keynote at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Wcg15FaUnU&t=2655s)
- In 2020 he said influencers are the new editors in chief: the audience respects the human, who endorses the brand. In 2021 he used the DoggFace208 Ocean Spray video, which got more attention in a minute than Ocean Spray bought in years. (Are Influencers The New Editors In Chief, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/are-influencers-the-new-editors-in-chief/; Advertising Through The Years: The Evolution of Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advertising-through-the-years-the-evolution-of-attention/)
- In 2020 he gave three rules: check the price and whether product can pay, give them full creative control, and run at volume. He said brands that run six influencers and then judge the result are making a mistake. (Are Influencers The New Editors In Chief, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/are-influencers-the-new-editors-in-chief/)
- In 2021 he said micro influencers are a laser and macro influencers a big beam: fans are more dedicated and easier to target with intent, while macro names make reach, cost and access harder. (What is Influencer Marketing? Guide on Growing Your Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-influencer-marketing-guide-on-growing-your-business/)
- In 2021 he said he would spend 30 percent to 50 percent of a marketing budget on influencer marketing. The same article's takeaways say 30 to 40 percent. (What is Influencer Marketing? Guide on Growing Your Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-influencer-marketing-guide-on-growing-your-business/)
- In 2026 he said influencer marketing can be underpriced or a waste depending on skill: a few hundred to 10,000 dollars can bring enormous return, like great ROI for Messi and negative ROI for his hamstring. (If You're Not Posting You're Falling Behind at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TDWFU_uEexc&t=630s)
- In 2022 he said rich is taking the quick bag and wealthy is never compromising your name, and that promoting things you do not understand costs you equity with the two percent who matter. (Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 51:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=3105s; Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 53:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=3195s)
- In 2022 he said no amount of celebrity or influencer support makes a project work if it is not good. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 39:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=2340s)
- In 2023 he said brand collabs and merch multiply value for both brands, and that merch as marketing is open to everyone from a Fortune 500 CMO to a dentist in Denver. He predicted merch collabs would be big over the next decade. (What Businesses Can Learn From The Tiffany & Co. X Nike Collab, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-businesses-can-learn-from-the-tiffany-co-x-nike-collab/)
- In 2020 he said brands that extend where they have permission can win: Tesla tequila, BMW headphones, a Hershey's super premium bar. He warned in 2020 that Supreme could saturate itself with too many partnerships. (Tesla Tequila: Why brands should innovate into different sectors, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/tesla-tequila-why-brands-should-innovate-into-different-sectors/; LinkedIn 5: Hot Takes On Hershey's Levi's Supreme At&T and Medium, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/linkedin-5-hot-takes-on-hersheys-levis-supreme-att-and-medium/)

## B2B, small business and what he tells a marketer

- In 2015 he said every business should think and act like a media company and put out content, and he took Starbucks starting a media property as more proof. (How To Market a Small Business With a Limited Budget, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-market-a-small-business-with-a-limited-budget/; Starbucks Is Starting A Media Company This Is Why It Matters To You, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/starbucks-is-starting-a-media-company-this-is-why-it-matters-to-you/)
- In 2017 he told B2B companies to treat the sector's trade magazine as their competitor and write the white paper or list of things retailers must do next year, instead of buying ads. He cited Michelin's guide and the Guinness Book as past examples. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=1890s; Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=2115s)
- In 2018 he said every business is a media company first and then does what it does. (Watch These 62 Minutes If You Need to Make Money in the Next 24 Months at 43:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9aBtnwK2fs&t=2610s)
- In 2019 he said B2B marketing works like high school popularity: someone who was not quite one of the popular kids could rise by throwing a party, so host a small in-person event for people similar to the clients you are targeting. (4 Key B2B Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-b2b-marketing-strategy-for-2019/; The Best LinkedIn B2B Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/linkedin-b2b-marketing-strategies/)
- In 2025 he said B2B firms that rely on salespeople can escape that by building brand and letting customers come to them. (From Posts to Profits A Step-by-Step Social Media Guide For Businesses at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RJ3ujlMYlus&t=540s)
- In 2018 he told direct-to-consumer sellers to put about 65 percent of spend on Facebook, Instagram and influencers, calling it a blanket statement made without knowing each business. (95% of You Will Ignore This 2018 Marketing Strategy Business Squared Keynote in Australia at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5Yst1Si3fw&t=1665s)
- In 2019 he said a small business should make an anchor video as its Super Bowl commercial and end it with a direct-response ask. He said small businesses must pander more to short-term sales because of cashflow. (Why Every Small Business Should Have a Super Bowl Commercial, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-every-small-business-should-have-a-super-bowl-commercial/)
- In 2019 he said to run narrow, contextual ads and name the audience in the video, because it is "a context battle, not a content battle". (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=2790s)
- In 2023 he said to create 40 to 50 consumer segmentations, not three. In 2024 he said 18 to 35 was a segment television loved for 40 years, and that he likes many narrow targets because the narrower you are, the more relevant you can be. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=1755s; How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=630s)
- In 2019 he said not to hand marketing to a 23-year-old niece, in 2021 that the business owner must do the LinkedIn ad work personally and cannot hire a niece or a one-person agency, and in 2024 that if you do not understand marketing yourself you cannot judge your employees or agency partners. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=1485s; 35 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Sage Summit 2021 at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie_pcLsdb5E&t=585s; How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1530s)
- In 2016 he said to be much more cynical of people selling classes about themselves who never built a business, and to give your best advice away in a structured way. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 1:12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=4365s)
- In 2015 he told a vodka founder in a crowded category to innovate with storytelling, micro-influencers and niche, because going where everyone is would put him out of business. (AskGaryVee Episode 100 The Live Show at 48:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUFbOa-8dPk&t=2880s)

## Brand when voice and AI choose

- In 2019 he said that if you do not brand your Chips Ahoy over cookies, people will say send me chocolate chip cookies and the platform picks the cookie. (The Rise of Audio Branding, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-rise-of-audio-branding/)
- In 2020 he said brand is what survives the move to voice: if people know Carol's Muffins by name, you have done your job. (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=495s)
- In 2024 he said that if you say Alexa, fix my AC you need a name Alexa can match, and that if you do not build brand fast you will be commoditised, as bookstores were. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 44:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=2655s)
- In 2025 he said that when voice and AI agents order for you, brand is the only thing that gets you chosen: Pizza loses to Pizza Hut. (AI & Social Media Playbook For The Next 5 Years You Can't Hide From This GaryVee Q&A at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_RiVvFwZ2sw&t=90s)
- In 2025 he said the business can be famous without the owner, and should put out 20 to 30 pieces of content a day. (What Happens When ChatGPT Replaces Google GaryVee Q&A For ActionCoach UK at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DlNAM0M4WNE&t=135s)

(AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=1395s; AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=1485s)
