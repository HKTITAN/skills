# Patience, hustle and work

This file collects what he said about patience and hustle. It includes 2014 to 2019 articles and keynotes on hustle, and a 2021 article series and 2020s interviews on patience. Read the years, since the file has a section on how his message on hustle changed and what he says he was misread on. Figures about his own hours and businesses are his own statements.

## What does he mean by patience, and how does it sit with speed?

- In 2013 he named patience as the most important asset for a marketer, because social is slow and human like real relationships, so you can ask for more the longer you have been giving. In the same talk hustle is his favorite word and hard work is grossly underestimated. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=1350s)
- In 2017 he told a 22-year-old the answer is macro patience, micro speed: work fast every day from 7 a.m. to 2 a.m., and accept that "nothing good is gonna happen for the next six years". (The Ultimate Advice For Every 20 Year Old at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qRj144Gd7EA&t=45s)
- In 2017 he wrote that micro strategy is grossly underrated, and that you should move insanely fast in the micro and be insanely patient in the macro. People worry about their years while wasting their days. (How Successful People Think, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/successful-people-think/)
- In 2017 he said most people have day to day and macro reversed. They dodge small chances to protect themselves from failure, so at the macro level they stop moving forward and lose. (How To Overcome Failure, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-overcome-failure/)
- In 2017 he said people are slow day to day yet want a huge business tomorrow, and that once you have speed you still need patience, because even seven years is incredibly fast. (The Importance of Speed in Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/importance-speed-business/)
- In 2017 he said the line listen to nobody, listen to everybody is macro versus micro. On the micro plane he listens to everyone, on the macro plane to no one, and these are two different planes. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 1:37:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=5850s)
- In 2019 he described himself as slow in his mind and fast in his actions, and said most people are the reverse: fast and easy in their heads, slow in action. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=360s)
- In 2021 he wrote that patience is the macro and tenacity is the micro: be tenacious in the task at hand and patient in the overall mission. (How to Have Tenacity Without the Burnout: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-have-tenacity-without-the-burnout-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2021 he said people read him as contradicting himself when he praises the long game and also speed and execution, and that it is both, not either-or. (What You're Misunderstanding about Patience: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-youre-misunderstanding-about-patience-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2023 he said he decides on macro patience and micro speed. He makes long-term bets slowly and small decisions fast, using intuition, pattern recognition, clear intent and lots of data. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=855s)
- In 2023 he wrote that patience is never about being lazy or unresponsive, and the advice at any age is "Start now. Move fast. Don't settle." (How My Definition of Patience Is Often Misunderstood, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/whymydefinitionofpatienceisoftenmisunderstood/)
- In 2023 he said he is scared of the word fast. The real way to your first $100,000 is self-awareness about what you are good at, and he asks people to swap fastest for happiest and most likely. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=405s)

## Why does he say people quit, and how long does he say things take?

- In 2015 he said that when people come to him wanting him to validate that they should quit, eight or nine months, even a year, is nothing, because nothing happens overnight. (How to be an Overnight Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-be-an-overnight-success/)
- In 2015 he said unhappy people want everything today and will not pay in time, and that what they give up is usually Netflix or video games, not money. (How to be an Overnight Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-be-an-overnight-success/)
- In 2015 he said so-called overnight successes worked unseen for years, citing LeBron playing basketball for fifteen years before fame. (How to be an Overnight Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-be-an-overnight-success/)
- In 2015 he said four to six months is an insanely short time for a start-up. His check: ask how long they have been at it, and only after two to three years with no traction do you audit. (5 Negative Business Situations and How to Turn Them Around, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-negative-business-situations-and-how-to-turn-them-around/)
- In 2016 he said many people quit a month before it would have worked, and that the lack of patience fascinates him. In 2021 he gave the same warning about podcasts and YouTube shows. (Eric Thomas Motivation Success & Public Speaking AskGaryVee Episode 223 at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h6katuaR5l4&t=1125s; 35 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Sage Summit 2021 at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie_pcLsdb5E&t=1890s)
- In 2019 he said he replied to every email and tweet until 4 a.m. from 2007 to 2011 before anyone knew him, and that people post on TikTok five times and quit. In 2021 he repeated the TikTok example in writing. (ABtalks with Gary Vee - Chapter 12 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxgQRJ1SwZ8&t=810s; What You're Misunderstanding about Patience: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-youre-misunderstanding-about-patience-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2019 he said 99 percent of people lack the patience and stomach to build a following. Wine Library TV ran 35 minutes a day, five days a week, from 2007 to 2010, he spent four or five hours a night on Twitter, and for three years very little happened. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=855s)
- In 2021 he said patience is almost always the missing ingredient in people who do not succeed. Going viral happens to 500 or 5,000 people a year, and he himself was 18 months into five days a week on Wine Library TV with almost nobody watching. (The #1 Thing Every Business Needs To Add to Their Strategy This Year at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od-2xtAdaa0&t=1530s)
- In 2019 he said that for his first two and a half years of Wine Library TV nobody watched, though he posted five days a week. (How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=2520s)
- In 2022 he said a quick bet loses 99 percent of the time. Get dirt under your fingers for a year, and in nine to twelve months you can have $5,000 and much more education. (Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=1035s)
- In 2021 he wrote that impatience comes from insecurity about what others think of your life stage, so people rush for approval and beat themselves up for being behind. (What You're Misunderstanding about Patience: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-youre-misunderstanding-about-patience-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2024 he called patience the number one kryptonite for young winners, who want it fast because they are insecure and play for an audience. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 1:28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=5310s)
- In 2019 he said "The quicker you want it, the more vulnerable you are", and that most people have a poor relationship with time. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=315s)
- In 2026 he said he is patient because he wants to win, and that people who want it fast are often closing the gap of their insecurity, like throwing a 45-yard bomb on every play and getting picked off on the second. (Gary Vee isn't afraid to lose it all betting on himself and reveals one thing - The Pivot Podcast at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ChdO5O6llw&t=135s; GaryVee on Patience Success and Playing the Long Game at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qh3_JJFxDmQ&t=45s)
- In 2025 he said starting at zero leaves no choice but patience, the alternative being the get-rich-quick chase of the next trend. He also called patience the secret weapon of the ambitious, since hungry people cut corners. (I'm Risking Everything To Build The Next Pokémon DailyVee ep 670 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7fgjfuiBF4&t=405s; I'm Risking Everything To Build The Next Pokémon DailyVee ep 670 at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I7fgjfuiBF4&t=720s)

## What does he say about time and how long life is?

- In 2018 he wrote that under 30 you are barely getting started. A 30-year-old has about 60 to 70 years of execution left, and life is a football game where you are still on the national anthem. (Don't Get Tricked By Your Late 20s, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/anxiety-in-your-late-20s/)
- In 2017 he told a 22-year-old not to chase millionaire by 25, because an arbitrary fast goal guarantees you never get there, while a marathon can guarantee millionaire by 36. Missing a sprint goal at 31 damages self-esteem. (The Ultimate Advice For Every 20 Year Old at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qRj144Gd7EA&t=495s)
- In 2019 he said people have not contextualized time: someone under 40 will live two more full lives, so not getting results tomorrow is easier to bear. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=495s)
- In 2019 he told a 19-year-old you are not wrong until you are dead: he could make wrong decisions for 40 years, figure it out at 59 and have a ten-year run. (Unpopular Opinion Losing Actually Turns You into a Winner Brisbane 2019 Keynote at 1:03:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w8v4jhe_EME&t=3825s)
- In 2021 and 2022 he said society's rules on when to marry and what you should have achieved come from a world where people died at 45 to 50, while life expectancy is now 75 to 95. (The Ultimate Life Advice For Success and Happiness Edmonton Canada Keynote 2021 at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz679oTO7Oo&t=180s; How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=675s)
- In 2019 he said people in their 50s and 60s are at halftime, not wrapping up, and he wants them playing triple offense. (13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=2790s)
- In 2023 he wrote that having more time than you think is the heart of his life philosophy, and said a 37-year-old with a mortgage and kids still has two more 30-year chapters. (How My Definition of Patience Is Often Misunderstood, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/whymydefinitionofpatienceisoftenmisunderstood/)
- In 2018 he said you need to be willing to get laughed at, judged, bet against and misunderstood, and to act like life is long, because it is going to be painful before it is amazing. (10 of My Top Instagram Quotes That Have Over-Indexed On The Platform, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/garyvee-instagram-quotes/)
- In 2017 he gave a caller a 24-month plan: clear debt in year one, save in year two, use the savings as the down payment on a first house flip. Going extreme beats stretching it over eight years, and you are never promised tomorrow. (Eating Shit for 24 Months Doing What You Love & Monetizing Your Strengths AskGaryVee 237 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o34pWhhzrZM&t=405s; Eating Shit for 24 Months Doing What You Love & Monetizing Your Strengths AskGaryVee 237 at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o34pWhhzrZM&t=450s)

## What does he mean by hustle and hard work?

- In 2014 he defined hustling as putting every minute and all your effort into the goal at hand, and said many people who say they hustle do not, like wanting to lose weight while sneaking a Big Mac. (A Hustler's Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-hustle-a-hustlers-mentality/)
- In 2014 he asked readers whether they would work 15 plus hours a day, wake up before everyone and stop being romantic, going all in for their legacy. (A Hustler's Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-hustle-a-hustlers-mentality/)
- In 2015 he said that if you do not work 15 to 17 hours a day you are not an entrepreneur. He admitted this may be short-sighted but said he believes it with his whole soul. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=1080s)
- In 2015 he said how hard you work is the number one factor. Success comes from skill, resources and time, but for him the top variable is how hard he works, not money or time. (This Is the Straightest Road to Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-straightest-road-to-success/)
- In 2015 he said hard work was his only gear, because he grew up poor and did badly at school. His line was to work harder, and when you think you have worked your hardest, go deeper. (This Is the Straightest Road to Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-straightest-road-to-success/)
- In 2015 he wrote that intensity beats hours: "Work five insane hours over sixteen soft ones any day." His straightest road is to optimize your time and work as hard as you can, and do both. (This Is the Straightest Road to Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-straightest-road-to-success/)
- In 2015 he said to work harder and also faster. The variable he says he does not talk about enough is being much faster inside the hours you already have. His method is to add a little to each hour, like marathon training, until you do much more per day. (5 Core Values of Business from GaryVee, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-core-values-of-business-from-garyvee/)
- In 2016 he said hard work is the first, second and third part of the formula, and that work is the one non-debatable variable.
