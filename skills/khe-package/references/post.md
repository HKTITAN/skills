# The post

Building has become easy and common; how the work is shown and talked about is what sets it apart. A good package can open doors: people and companies reach out to the person whose work they keep seeing.

## Nobody cares, yet

Assume the stranger scrolling past does not care about your business, your numbers or that you made something. They care about what it does for them. That is why brand accounts and customer stories get little attention.

The first-line test: read it and ask why a stranger would care. If the answer is "because I made it", rewrite it until it says what is in it for them:

- **How it was made**, so they can make one: "A system that suggests a new interaction every day from a few rules. Here is the setup:"
- **What they can take from it:** "Bars that regrow from the baseline read as new data. The whole trick is one transform-origin:"
- **The problem it solves:** "Same menu, same 400 ms. Only the curve changed, and one feels twice as fast:"

Never open with "I made", "We shipped", "Introducing", "Excited to share" or "Proud to announce". The gate's `about-me` line catches these.

At work the bar is lower but the rule is the same: get to the point. Show the thing you want reviewed first; do not talk for minutes before it.

## Proof of work

People need a reason to listen to you over everyone else posting. The strongest is proof of work: what you built, where you worked, what people use. Put it where it supports the post (a line, a link), never as the opening. Without much of it yet, post anyway and lean on the value in each post.
