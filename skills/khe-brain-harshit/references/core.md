# Core

Read this for a broad question, or before a draft, then open one theme file for the detail. Every line paraphrases the posts cited. All of it is from June 2025 on, when his current voice and subjects begin; [disagreements](disagreements.md) has what changed before that.

## The thesis

- The ability to build has been commoditized and is not coming back; what has not been commoditized is taste, knowing what is worth making, what feels right and what to cut. He says that in a reply defending Tim Sweeney's plan for SI at Epic, and the same idea runs through his post on 50 hours with one model: AI is not replacing taste, it is making taste more valuable. (2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829; 2026-06-11-50-hours-fable-5, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065110652935659695)

Everything else here is that idea applied to a product, a game, an agent or a career.

## The twelve ideas

- A product that needs explaining has already failed at its on-ramp. With Smash&Clash he dropped a tutorial he and the players disliked and made the board zoom in on each capture, so the game teaches itself. (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- The best interfaces disappear into everyday life, and the next interface is based on intent, learning you instead of asking you to learn it, a line he posted over a link to Ryo Lu. (2026-07-12-naveens459-swiggy-interaction-best, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2076285742779871609; 2025-12-24-next-interface-wont-ask, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2003887211587231907)
- A product will not live in one place. Agents will compose its interface wherever the user already is, so design systems and component contracts matter more, not less. (2026-10-20-your-product-wont-live, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112453994643870174)
- He endorsed a take he credits partly to Bezos: AI upgrades the builder instead of replacing them, and work moves up to judgment and leverage. Coding was a translation layer between intent and the computer, and engineering, knowing what to build and why, is still the skill. (2026-05-21-cleanest-ai-take-heard, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2057340873587990841; 2026-07-21-2-decades-ago-if, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2079631736724697271)
- Working with agents is closer to managing people than to prompting: describe the destination, the taste and the constraints, not the steps. He calls it manager engineering, and tells people to write skills instead of better prompts. (2026-06-11-50-hours-fable-5, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065110652935659695; 2026-06-01-gap-between-people-who, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2061415603920044197)
- Reviewing Omi's CLI in May 2026, he set out which layer does what: APIs build products, MCP embeds assistants, CLIs orchestrate workflows and agents. (2026-05-18-most-ai-infra-today, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2056242005664297426)
- Clarity comes after action. The best startups come from trying things, putting them in front of reality and letting it teach you, and he would rather take 100 shots and fail in public than keep ideas he never ships. (2026-05-14-best-startups-often-built, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2054933799977595056; 2026-03-23-d-rather-take-100, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7441934265883324416)
- An MVP is not a broken draft but a small, complete, usable thing that tests whether anyone will use it. (2025-07-07-most-people-misunderstand-mvps, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7347971016347873281)
- Talking to users works only when you ask about the past, stay quiet, go one degree out, find people inside the problem and then count; ten real conversations beat a thousand form responses. (2026-07-30-talk-your-users-most, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7488632637612302336)
- Games within games, connected through interoperability, will shape the next decade of gaming, which is why he opened Smash&Clash through an SDK so anyone can build a client on one shared network. (2026-11-09-games-within-games-connected, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2119696322651673030; 2026-10-08-true-s-why-introduced, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2108136746676592883)
- Treat games like products and products like games. (2026-10-21-treat-games-like-products, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112882020636594542)
- Computing is leaving the rectangle. He grew up on the internet and wants to help build a world where you do not have to choose between digital and physical, through glasses and ambient devices. (2026-09-16-ve-been-using-computers, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2100228344478666780; 2026-04-13-pity-moth-confusing-lamp, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7449536835426443264)

## How he builds, in the order he lives it

- Build the smallest complete thing and test it with people who are inside the problem. (2025-07-07-most-people-misunderstand-mvps, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7347971016347873281; 2026-07-30-talk-your-users-most, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7488632637612302336)
- Build with agents: a plan first, one function per ask, read the diff and not the summary, keep rules short and move the rest into skills. (2026-06-01-gap-between-people-who, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2061415603920044197)
- Ship it in public and early, then keep shipping where people already are. (2026-03-23-d-rather-take-100, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7441934265883324416; 2026-10-20-gaming-also-going-get, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112457428868776278)
- Watch real players and real users, and change the product rather than explain it. (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- Open it up so others can build on it, as he did with the Smash&Clash SDK. (2026-10-04-shipped-smash-clash-sdk, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2106659181919154419)
