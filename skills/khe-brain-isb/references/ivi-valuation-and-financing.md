# What a business is worth and how it is financed, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Customer lifetime value and customer management, Siddharth Shekar, 28 May 2025

## SAFE discounts and Series A term sheets, Bhagwan Chowdhry

- Bhagwan Chowdhry says a startup carries two separate risks that finance calls by one word: market risk, which is being paid more in good times and less in bad times, and the probability that the business fails altogether. (ivi: SAFE discounts and Series A term sheets, Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-safe-discounts-and-series-a-term-sheets.md)
- Bhagwan Chowdhry builds a discount rate from a real risk free rate, inflation and a risk premium. His board example is 2% plus 4% plus 5%, giving 11%. (ivi: SAFE discounts and Series A term sheets, Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-safe-discounts-and-series-a-term-sheets.md)
- Bhagwan Chowdhry says the cash flow forecast is the number one thing in a valuation, and that no professor can teach it because it comes from understanding the business and its sales and marketing strategy. (ivi: SAFE discounts and Series A term sheets, Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-safe-discounts-and-series-a-term-sheets.md)
- Bhagwan Chowdhry gives three principles behind discounted cash flow: cash is king and is counted when it comes in, opportunity cost matters, and two things with identical cash flows must have the same price. (ivi: SAFE discounts and Series A term sheets, Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-safe-discounts-and-series-a-term-sheets.md)

## Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry

- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says a cash flow C that grows at rate g for ever and is discounted at rate i is worth C divided by i minus g today. He calls this the Mona Lisa of finance and says every valuation multiple rests on it. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says the discount rate cannot equal the growth rate for ever. A firm can grow faster than the economy for two or three years, but if growth stayed that high interest rates would rise. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says that if earnings are roughly equal to cash flows, the price to earnings ratio is an estimate of 1 divided by the discount rate minus the growth rate. His example with a 2% risk-free rate, a 5% risk premium and 2% growth gives a multiple of 20. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says the price to earnings ratio means little for an early firm, because its earnings are tiny and growing fast and not at steady state. He gives Amazon's early ratio as around 700 to 800. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says a quick first answer to what a firm is worth is its steady-state earnings times the typical multiple for its industry. He says this is a benchmark to start from and not the value. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry says valuation discounts cash flows and not profits because profit is in the book and not in the hand. What counts is cash that can be taken out and used elsewhere. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class says enterprise value is the value of the entire business and belongs to equity and debt holders together. Equity value is enterprise value less debt, plus cash and non-operating assets. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class says the venture approach is to estimate the exit value, when and how much, and discount it back to get today's post-money valuation. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class gives 30 to 50% as the discount rate applied in venture capital. She says it combines an industry risk premium with an extra premium for the chance the firm never reaches its exit. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class values Swiggy from Zomato's enterprise value of 27.6 times revenue at IPO. She says a revenue multiple was used because Zomato was not profitable when it listed. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class says a multiple transfers cleanly only if the two firms match on discount rate, expected growth and the share of EBITDA that becomes free cash flow. These tell you the direction of an adjustment and not its size. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class says two firms compared on a revenue multiple must record revenue the same way. In her example Swiggy reported gross merchandise value while Zomato reported its net take. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)
- The co-instructor in Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry's finance class says a discounted cash flow valuation is better than a multiple when it is possible, because it fits the specific business like tailored clothes against borrowed ones. (ivi: Valuation multiples and the Swiggy example, Prof Bhagwan Chowdhry, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-chowdhry-valuation-multiples-and-swiggy-example.md)

## Customer lifetime value, break-even on a promotion and valuing a startup by its customers

- The instructor says a founder can value a startup by estimating the lifetime value of an average customer, multiplying by the number of customers to value today's cohort, and adding future cohorts from the planned acquisition rate to reach customer equity. (ivi: Customer lifetime value, break-even on a promotion and valuing a startup by its customers, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-customer-lifetime-value-and-break-even.md)
- The instructor says tying valuation to customer behaviour lets a founder justify the number asked for and show how a given investment raises the company's value. (ivi: Customer lifetime value, break-even on a promotion and valuing a startup by its customers, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-customer-lifetime-value-and-break-even.md)
- The instructor says a founder without their own customer data can use category or industry data to get a reasonable range for lifetime value. (ivi: Customer lifetime value, break-even on a promotion and valuing a startup by its customers, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-customer-lifetime-value-and-break-even.md)

## NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2

- The instructor says discounted cash flow is still the fundamental way to understand valuation, but other methods are used for VC valuation because startups stay cash flow negative for long and their estimates are uncertain. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
- The instructor says part of the 40 to 50% a year return expected of early stage startups reflects the risk that the startup does not survive. (ivi: NPV and IRR for investment decisions, part 2, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/undated-instructor-npv-and-irr.md)
