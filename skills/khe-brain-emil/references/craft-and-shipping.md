# Craft and shipping

He has written about how he ships his own projects. He has not written career advice: jobs, pay, interviews and portfolios are in [silent](silent.md).

## Naming and standing out

- The toast category was already crowded when he started; craft and developer experience are what set Sonner apart. (Building a Toast Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)
- He avoided a name that describes the function, such as react-toast, and chose a French word meaning "to ring". (Building a Toast Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)
- He accepts that the name gives up discoverability and clarity in return for being distinct. (Building a Toast Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)
- One interaction, the stacking animation, carried the launch; the announcement videos showed only that. (Building a Toast Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)

## Open source

- Vaul exists because an existing drawer lacked drag to dismiss and had input problems; he built a new one and opened it. (Building a Drawer Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-drawer-component)
- He opens his components because the feedback improves them, and he finds it rewarding. (Building a Drawer Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-drawer-component)
- He published the drawer article with the library unfinished and said so. (Building a Drawer Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-drawer-component)

## Finishing

- Teasing a project in public cost him motivation, because talking about it felt like progress. That project shipped about a year after its teaser. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- Selling a presale before any content existed forced the work, because people had already paid. He calls it useful if you can take the pressure. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- He aimed for 1,000 students and passed 10,000. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)

## Packaging

- Packaging is the first impression and part of the product. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- His examples: a logo with a story behind it that changes as a deadline nears, illustrations that react to hover and click, and a personal welcome page. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)

## Teaching

- Motion has to be felt, so each lesson is built from interactive demos, never a wall of text. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- Every exercise has a video solution and a written one, and the code editor runs in the browser and saves by itself. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- He rewrote his theory material after learning to teach it better, and keeps a changelog. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)

## Getting it seen

- His marketing is word of mouth: make something excellent, and people who like it share it. Sonner, Vaul and his teaching spread that way. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
- He sees no point in making something mediocre. (Building an Animation Course, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-an-animation-course)
