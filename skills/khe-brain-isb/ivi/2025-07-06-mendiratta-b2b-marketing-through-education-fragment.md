# B2B marketing through education and content, Chandan Mendiratta

A short recording from the marketing bootcamp with Chandan Mendiratta of Zepto. The transcript holds only a few minutes, in which he answers a question on how marketing works for a B2B product, drawing on his time at a B2B software company for educators. It ends with remarks to individual founders, which are left out.

## What was said

### B2B selling is education

- Mendiratta says content can play a very large role in B2B.
- He says B2B selling is about problem solving: educate the user on the answer to the problem they are trying to solve, show what can be done about it, and then show that your product fits there.
- He sums up the sequence as addressing the problem, education, solution, then telling.
- He says never to assume customers know what is best for them. A B2B customer never wants a product, so the seller has to teach the customer what the product brings.
- He says a B2B product is a cost to the buyer, so the seller must establish that the buyer will make more money than the product costs. Only then is there a fair chance of a sale.
- He says there will always be scepticism in B2B.
- He says the best way is to talk to customers, understand their problem and then run a webinar.
- He says B2B is about building content, then funnelling people down to the product, and then putting a sales team on it. It is not just branding.

### An example funnel

- Mendiratta's example is the B2B software company he worked at, which sells educators and content creators a platform to turn their audience into buyers of a course or other digital product.
- He describes the funnel there: awareness comes from the creator's own content and a link in their bio, and consideration, purchase and delivery all happen on the platform.
- He says the first thing to tell an educator is that they can earn more money.

### Tools and discovery

- Mendiratta recommends a tool called eWebinar, a plugin for a website that offers the visitor a webinar starting in a few minutes, as a way to deliver the education a B2B product needs.
- He says many B2B product sales now start with a buyer asking ChatGPT how to solve a problem and being given three or four products.
- He says the traditional route of search engine optimisation on Google followed by click campaigns will therefore not work, and that content now has to be made with language models in mind.

### The buyer has to look good

- Mendiratta says a product has to make its consumer look good.
- He says the product should mean less friction for the consumer.

## Examples and cases used

- A B2B software company for educators and creators, his former employer: the funnel from a creator's content to a sale on the platform.
- eWebinar: automated webinars as B2B education.
- ChatGPT: where B2B buyers now ask for product recommendations.
