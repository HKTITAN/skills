# Run: a tax return in one evening

Engine: ../engine
Idea: a client who kept the Tuesday habit filed her tax return in one evening
Platforms: linkedin, newsletter

## Brief

From Ines, 2026-11-10.

- The client is a freelance translator. She agreed to be quoted without her name.
- Her words, from an email: "I filed in one evening and went to bed before midnight."
- She had kept the Tuesday habit for 11 months before the return was due: a short pass each week matching her bank statement to her invoices and receipts.
- Ines does not have a figure for how long the return took the year before. The client said it was "weekends". Ask before using a number.
- Ines's view: a return is slow when it is the first time all year anyone has looked at the paper.

Claim: a tax return is quick when the year has already been looked at, a week at a time.

## Draft: newsletter

- Hook: The correction ([[hooks]])

```text
Subject: Your tax return is slow for a boring reason

You do not hate your tax return. You hate meeting a whole year of paper in a single weekend.

A client of mine, a translator, kept one habit for 11 months. Each Tuesday she matched her bank statement to her invoices and receipts. It is a short job when the week is fresh.

This year the return took her an evening. Her words: "I filed in one evening and went to bed before midnight."

You cannot fix last year. You can start this Tuesday. Open the statement, match what you can, and ask about what you cannot while you still remember.

Do that every week and the return becomes the last step of a job you have already done.

Ines
```
