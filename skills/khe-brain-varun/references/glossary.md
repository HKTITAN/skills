# Glossary

These are his own terms and named methods, defined the way he uses them in the videos. Where a term is borrowed, the line names the person he credits, and where two videos use the same term both are cited.

- **80 to 90 / 10 portfolio**: Keep 80 to 90% of what you have ultra safe and use about 10% for crazy bets, tested small and in stages. (Kata 9 at 53:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=3199s)
- **A, B, C players test**: Imagine the person says they quit today: panic means an A player, three months to replace means B, relief means C. He takes it from Brad Jacobs's book. (Kata 8 at 36:55, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=2215s)
- **Aeos**: The group's name, which he says was picked at random with no full form; someone inside later read it as Attention Economy Operating System. In another telling it came from a Dragon Ball character. (Masters Union at 7:10, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=430s; KK Create at 1:39:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=5993s)
- **Aeos chain**: The video editing school produces editors, the services company consumes them to do YouTube end-to-end for customers, and Labs, a separate engineering-focused company, automates parts of the business to lift its margins. (Masters Union at 9:31, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=571s)
- **Aeos city**: His dream of one owned place where the whole team lives and works. (KK Create at 20:21, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=1221s)
- **Aeos Elites**: People who have been at Aeos three years, which earns access to the office lounge. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 26:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=1590s)
- **Agents as a game loop**: An AI agent is a model on a loop like a game tick: every few seconds it thinks, adds the reply to its context and responds. (PGX Ideas 2 at 33:29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=2009s)
- **AI test**: After AI gives you numbers, phone real people and replace each figure with a checked one. (Kata 3 at 20:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=1253s)
- **AI-designed double incentive**: Asking a model to design a simple incentive that rewards sales and is forfeited if waste or cost crosses a line. (Kata 3 at 24:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=1446s)
- **Alpha, beta, gold**: Alphas are for experiments and redrawing the team, beta is when the shape and conversion rate are found, gold is the bug-free release. (Kata 12 at 38:35, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=2315s)
- **Anti-attempt nation**: His label for India, where fear of ridicule and of finding out the truth about yourself stops people from trying. (Kata 4 at 29:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=1784s)
- **Anti-confusion playbook**: Sort decisions by reversibility, hold one objective with three key results, run 60-day curiosity sprints, default to build and ship, and decide after three serious tries. (Kata 7 at 28:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1730s)
- **Attribution**: Working out what was really responsible for a success or a failure. (Kata 6 at 0:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEnxvZXzo0E&t=47s)
- **Avengers hiring**: Building a team where each person has one superpower that is hard to find and people cover each other's weaknesses. (Kata 8 at 4:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=290s)
- **Belief ladder**: Pick a belief slightly crazier than your social circle, accomplish it, then raise it step by step. (Why Belief Matters at 14:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=864s)
- **Belief meter**: Your level of self-belief, which never starts at 100 and fills as you solve harder problems. (Why Belief Matters at 24:43, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=1483s)
- **Belief to behaviour**: Do not stop at a pure intention; make a plan, start meeting the relevant people tomorrow, expect a thousand rejections and repeat for years. (Why Belief Matters at 11:11, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=671s)
- **Bento box**: His picture of Aeos as separate compartments in one box: talent, services, distribution, apps and emerging bets, each feeding the others. (Kata 1 at 7:20, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=440s)
- **Blinking**: A Dota term for a short teleport, used for jumping into another person's mind to see what they want before you sell to them. (Kata 13 at 2:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=147s)
- **Bounded game**: Any contest with a score and rules that can ban tools such as AI, from Counter-Strike to office promotions; he says future jobs are all bounded games. (PGX Ideas 2 at 41:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=2487s)
- **Branded search share**: The share of unpaid Google clicks that are searches for your brand, tracked quarterly as a sign of brand visibility. (Kata 10 at 29:11, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=1751s)
- **Brass: Birmingham model**: His business lesson from the board game: build connected industries of your own so you depend on nobody, and stay close to anyone you must depend on. (Masters Union at 7:58, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=478s)
- **Breaking point**: The point in a fight where a co-founder walks away, which he says family does not have. (Masters Union at 35:41, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=2141s)
- **Build one feature, then sell**: Find the simplest single feature people use, put your energy into marketing and sales, and stop disturbing it while it works. (Kata 11 at 23:14, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=1394s)
- **Burn the bridges**: Pick one thing, get it to some scale, and treat your other options as not even options for now. (Why Belief Matters at 33:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=2004s)
- **Buyer's journey**: Trigger, reviews, alternatives, comparison, waiting for money, a second comparison, then a gut decision on status and feeling. (Kata 6 at 22:13, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jEnxvZXzo0E&t=1333s)
- **Capacity math**: Measure output per person on work the audience accepted, scale it to the full project, add a 30% buffer, then decide how many to hire. (Kata 12 at 23:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=1397s; Kata 12 at 24:07, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=1447s)
- **Category saturation**: Once a user has one or two apps in a category, the category is filled and nobody downloads another. (Kata 1 at 17:49, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1069s)
- **C-free zone**: An organisation with nobody whose exit would be a relief; his goal in place of an all-A team. (Kata 8 at 38:32, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=2312s)
- **CHAMP-R**: His hiring frame: cultural fit, hard skills, attitude, motivation, potential, and whether you can trust their read of a situation. (Kata 8 at 27:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=1639s)
- **Cheap experiment rule**: Start every new business as cheaply as possible and spend more only after it works, to avoid sunk cost. (KK Create at 1:18:54, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=4734s)
- **Check everyone else's incentives**: Before a decision, ask what each other party wants, such as why a founder is hiring a freelancer and not an employee. (Kata 9 at 32:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=1964s)
- **Chinese MCN model**: A network that makes the influencer from day one, as K-pop does, where American networks sign stars who already exist. (PGX Ideas 2 at 2:01:57, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=7317s)
- **Chronic rank pressure**: Living in a society that keeps ranking you and pushing you down, which he says is India. (Secret Kata 1 at 6:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wM7jyLJBi_g&t=390s)
- **Clean attempt**: A high-integrity try that, if it fails, is owned and shut down openly with the reasons. (Kata 4 at 16:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=966s)
- **Clipping network**: Tens of thousands of small pages run by clippers that repost clips on a topic to shape the feed. (Masters Union at 23:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1425s)
- **CMF**: Content market fit, used next to PMF in Aeos lingo. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 22:31, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=1351s)
- **Confusion land**: Wanting to do five things with none of them working. (Kata 7 at 23:13, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1393s)
- **Confusion versus diversification test**: Ask whether one thing already works and whether the second thing costs you the first; money in things that need none of your time passes. (Kata 7 at 16:04, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=964s; Kata 9 at 47:48, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2868s)
- **Content meta timeline**: Vlogs, then shows, then shorts, then shorts plus extra pages, then AI avatars, now clipping networks and lectures. (Masters Union at 25:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1519s)
- **Context repair technicians**: His name for senior engineers, who fix what an underbaked brief left out. (Kata 3 at 14:33, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=873s)
- **Coordination cost**: The friction each person adds to a team's output, and the people and time a company spends only on coordinating; his main lens on a company. (Kata 2 at 12:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=726s; Masters Union at 46:01, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=2761s)
- **Costly signaling**: A theory he cites: spending all of a small amount on a risky bet is more credible than spending a little of a large amount. (Masters Union at 21:21, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1281s)
- **Creativity recipe**: From his 2017 book Pajama Profit: learn how something was made, break it into components and rearrange them. (PGX Ideas 2 at 24:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=1486s)
- **Credential collection**: Gathering degrees and big-name resume lines so you are ready if luck arrives. (Kata 7 at 4:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=293s)
- **Cringe then clean**: Launch anything new with something different and a little cringe so it travels, take the feedback, then sober it up. (Masters Union at 18:11, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=1091s)
- **Curiosity sprint**: Give a new field 60 days, then commit or reject it, because every field is exciting for two weeks. (Kata 7 at 28:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1730s)
- **Current**: The energy a leader generates to make people care about a task. (Kata 2 at 15:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=917s)
- **Deal flow to business**: Build relationships through content, notice many of them want the same thing, then build a team to sell it. (Kata 10 at 12:14, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=734s)
- **Deep optionality**: Being able to respond to any disruption through transferable skill; he credits a Substack writer for the idea. (Kata 9 at 35:55, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2155s)
- **Deferred life plan**: Treating the current job as a stop on the way to some other final destination. (Masters Union at 38:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=2286s)
- **Delusion bubble**: The closed system or suspended reality that he says every company is. (Kata 2 at 50:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=3046s)
- **Demis's test for AGI**: As he relays it from Demis: train a model only on knowledge up to about 1910 and see if it rediscovers relativity. (PGX Ideas 2 at 19:09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=1149s)
- **Density**: The roles found in the gaps between departments as a thin team becomes a full one. (Kata 12 at 15:16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=916s)
- **Departments, then roles, then density**: Find departments by calling people in the industry, then find roles, then fill gaps until the org chart is dense. (Kata 12 at 15:16, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=916s)
- **Devil's advocate prompting**: After an AI answer, say you doubt it, ask where you are wrong and ask for four or five alternatives. (PGX Ideas 2 at 53:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=3202s)
- **Digital undervaluing**: Advertisers paying too little for digital attention compared with outdoor. (KK Create at 54:51, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=3291s)
- **Distanced self-talk**: Talking to yourself in the third person under stress to move from a threat state to a challenge state. (Secret Kata 1 at 25:47, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wM7jyLJBi_g&t=1547s)
- **Distribution**: The people who follow you and trust you enough that it shapes their decisions; more than views. (Kata 10 at 14:37, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=877s)
- **Distribution efficiency**: Engagement compared with views; 10% is good and 20% or more is excellent. (Kata 10 at 27:31, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=1651s)
- **Distribution founders and operational founders**: The two kinds of Aeos founders, each dependent on the other for leads or delivery. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 33:42, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2022s)
- **Dota dynamic**: A game you think is five against five but every player feels is nine against one. (Kata 2 at 9:42, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=582s)
- **Double win**: Producing a resource and using it yourself, as with school editors placed in his own services company. (Masters Union at 11:51, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=711s)
- **Drip-feed recruiting**: Building a relationship with a candidate and sending them company updates for a year, in place of relying on brand. (Kata 4 at 37:48, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=2268s)
- **Earning into the family**: Trust given for loyalty over time: lounge access after three years, equity handed to people who show commitment without asking. (Masters Union at 39:41, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=2381s)
- **Easy to understand, maintain and fix**: The three tests everything Aeos builds should pass, a rule taken from his earlier company Scenes. (Kata 11 at 16:50, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0tbeJZYvsg4&t=1010s)
- **Emotional tax**: The commitment to someone's career and growth that comes with a full-time hire. (Kata 8 at 34:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=2070s)
- **Energy bending**: Treating money as a force you redirect to buy speed, other people's time and your own sharpness. (Kata 13 at 44:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=2670s)
- **Engineer cope**: Engineers in his comments insisting AI will not automate software and that he is not a real engineer. (PGX Ideas 2 at 1:34:09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=5649s)
- **Engineering luck**: Raising your surface area so that more opportunities can find you. (Kata 10 at 6:37, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=397s)
- **Exit penalties**: What you lose when you leave a world, which he says every strong brand needs. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 52:59, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=3179s)
- **Exponential train**: A rapidly scaling company that you should not leave. (Kata 7 at 34:25, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=2065s)
- **Exposition versus interpretation**: Telling everything versus leaving things unsaid; he says show a slice in public and let people imagine the rest. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 53:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=3226s)
- **Fake optionality**: Interviewing at 10 places to feel wanted without having quit. (Kata 9 at 35:07, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=2107s)
- **Fast belief testing**: Building a way to test beliefs quickly, as the Wright brothers did with a wind tunnel, with separate certainty about problem and solution. (Why Belief Matters at 27:08, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jreQIik5jlE&t=1628s)
- **Fiction platforms**: X and Reddit, because they treat real people as all good or all bad. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 1:02:35, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=3755s)
- **First principles thinking**: Question every known idea, break the goal into components, solve each one, and ignore what others think. (Kata 1 at 14:34, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=874s)
- **Five choices per role**: Keep it in-house, invest in technology, outsource for a while, hire permanently, or train internally with mentors. (Kata 12 at 28:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=1686s)
- **Fixed, flexible, legend**: Three layers of lore: what is always true, what is interpretation, and the stories people tell about themselves. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 37:40, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2260s)
- **Fixer mindset**: Expect everything to break and assume you will fix it. (KK Create at 44:29, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A37ca5F9oG8&t=2669s)
- **Fork**: A split that happens when people disagree on what is canon; he says every Aeos company is built as one. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 40:04, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2404s; The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 44:08, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=2648s)
- **Four acts**: His telling of Aeos: the editing school, then channels for customers, then Labs and AI avatars, then 100x Engineers, with games as a future act. (Kata 1 at 16:59, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1019s; Kata 1 at 27:27, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9NjA41wShTI&t=1647s)
- **Four horsemen**: What a closed system needs: direction, celebration of milestones, rules with enforcement, and outsiders who take it seriously. (Kata 2 at 50:46, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=3046s)
- **Four liquid wealth zones**: 1 crore for security, 10 crore for freedom, 100 crore for influence, 1,000 crore for legacy, counted in cash and assets on hand. (Kata 13 at 14:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=862s)
- **Four traits of people who are good at everything**: Curious, unafraid of mistakes, willing to try many times, and learning from each failure. (Kata 13 at 8:48, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=528s)
- **Frame of reference**: To show something is big or strong, put something small or weak beside it. (The Most Valuable Skill After AI at 16:06, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4N8WadMsdmI&t=966s)
- **Frame shifting**: A technique taught in the first class of the Aeos content school, which he does not explain in the video. (Kata 10 at 38:09, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=2289s)
- **Free market participant**: Someone who can enter any industry without running the work themselves, by hiring skills, making media and owning assets. (Kata 13 at 37:22, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=2242s; Kata 13 at 1:02:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=3720s)
- **Freelancer's trap**: Taking one well-paid project and finding you carry all the payment and pipeline risk. (Kata 7 at 12:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=725s)
- **Frogs**: Executives in big companies who will not make a decision and need all 15 people to agree. (Kata 7 at 17:40, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=1060s)
- **Funding by shape certainty**: Seed is for learning and redrawing the team, series A is when you roughly know the shape, series B is when you know it. (Kata 12 at 35:19, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mwQhnZ32CeE&t=2119s)
- **Fungibility fallacy**: The false belief that one employee can be quickly swapped for another. (Kata 8 at 5:37, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=337s)
- **Gap thinking**: Ask whether there is a gap in supply and demand, not whether the thing is hard; seal it, then look for the next gap beside it. (Masters Union at 4:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NIQwPD6a-d4&t=240s)
- **Give context by example**: State the output, format, folder and naming rule, and add one worked example. (Kata 3 at 12:07, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=727s)
- **Golden rules for a high serotonin team**: Praise in public, correct in private, critique the work, lead with alignment, define evaluation criteria, run blameless postmortems, protect status. (Secret Kata 1 at 20:59, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wM7jyLJBi_g&t=1259s)
- **Good boy, good girl energy**: Niceness that he says Indians overvalue in place of the ability to move things. (Kata 4 at 45:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=2700s)
- **Google 7-11-4 rule**: Seven hours of content, 11 interactions, across 4 platforms or places to build real trust. (Kata 10 at 23:32, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TGKgCLVxRCk&t=1412s)

- **Growth rate versus absolute scale**: When choosing where to work, ask whether the company is growing fast, not how big it is. (Kata 7 at 33:38, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=2018s)
- **GTO in real life**: Borrowed from poker: look for a very cheap, ideally free experiment with a high payoff. (Kata 9 at 16:53, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWFn-RgxZ5M&t=1013s)
- **Gun, not bullet**: His earlier companies were each a bullet; Aeos is the gun that gives any product velocity. (PGX Ideas 2 at 58:56, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=3536s)
- **High Twitter and Reddit, no P&L**: His description of commentators, people with strong opinions and no real attempts. (Kata 4 at 21:44, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mPjj7-eHw1s&t=1304s)

- **Hiring mistakes list**: Rushing, not vetting, ignoring culture fit, not choosing between today and potential, loose interviews, maybe roles, no week-one instructions. (Kata 8 at 32:05, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=1925s)
- **Hiring stack**: His order: attitude, cultural fit, learning agility, understanding instructions, implementing, leadership, diligence, customer first, technical skills, taste. (Kata 8 at 7:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oQd7Dw47VdU&t=435s)
- **How to work with me document**: A sheet some people write on what it is like to work with them, sent to new team members to set context. (Kata 3 at 14:33, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yJFsZLgUa40&t=873s)
- **Human blockages**: His list of what blocks a team: ego, low skill, poor attitude, poor communication, wrong details, low trust, low accountability, passive aggression, being transactional, victimhood. (Kata 2 at 35:24, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=2124s)
- **Humans hallucinating**: People reading agency into AI agents that are posting what humans made them post. (PGX Ideas 2 at 38:17, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ObXOm9YqnBk&t=2297s)
- **Hyper gambling**: Young people taking long-shot risks such as betting apps and crypto because the normal path takes decades; he credits an article. (Kata 7 at 0:55, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ia_bqazMeIw&t=55s; Kata 13 at 50:52, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8WgipmPSPKk&t=3052s)

- **Incentive ladder**: Cash until you earn enough among peers, then respect, status, purpose and mastery. (Kata 2 at 27:20, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uojkYJz7d-k&t=1640s)
