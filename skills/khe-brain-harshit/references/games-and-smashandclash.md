# Games and Smash&Clash

Games are where he tests most of his product ideas, through his own game Smash&Clash, a 2-player strategy card game on a 3×5 grid. Since mid-2025 his settled view is that games are becoming things you open on any screen, that interoperability will shape the next decade ("games within games"), and that as AI lowers the cost of making games, taste and discovery decide who wins, which favors small teams. The lessons he draws from Smash&Clash are about first contact, watching real players, cheap iteration and keeping players once they arrive. Older posts (2022-24) are small asks to game makers and one event writeup.

## What is Smash&Clash, in his words?

- He describes it as chess with cards: high impulse fun with deep strategic play. (2026-03-28-building-2-player-strategy, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2037888710059163656)
- He calls it a 2-player competitive card battle on a 3×5 grid designed around "micro-wins". (2026-05-08-askokara-www-smashandclash-smash, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2052667386160206186)
- He frames the physical version as a shared in-room experience, not just a game; at an AI summit he said watching people connect over it felt like magic. "a shared experience." (2026-02-21-last-3-days, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7430999181579808768; 2026-02-17-wrapped-up-last-2, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7429595604034584576)
- He traces it to a Triple Triad (Final Fantasy VIII) adaptation that started at his first hackathon in March 2025; he later called it a 20-month journey refined with player feedback, using familiar mobile-game visuals and Uno-inspired colors. (2026-08-27-haha-thank-has-actually, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2092770926597407221; 2026-10-18-zaiphirion-appreciate-giving-shot, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2111843155314454871)

## What did building it teach him about first contact?

- He used to explain the mechanics before people played, and the first minutes always had friction; once players got it they called it extremely addictive, so the on-ramp, not the game, was the problem. (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- He tried a tutorial, he and players hated it, and he replaced it with a zoom-in that shows each capture on the board, which also added drama to every move. "i changed the game so it could teach itself." (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- He says whenever he made the game too complex, it showed immediately in how people played. (2026-03-23-d-rather-take-100, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7441934265883324416)

## How does he use feedback and iteration?

- He says watching people play the physical game gave him the fastest truth, so he focused on it before going digital. "gave me the fastest truth." (2026-03-23-d-rather-take-100, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7441934265883324416)
- He says the online version peaked at 9k+ players and retention was weak, but the signal was that people wanted to play, so he kept going; he named consistency and retention as the next focus. "retention wasn’t great, but that wasn’t the signal." (2026-03-23-d-rather-take-100, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7441934265883324416)
- He says agents made iteration cheap: he builds a scrappy version, lives with it, notes what feels awkward, throws most away and rebuilds. Smash&Clash went through 15+ months of different mechanics, boards, cards and rules this way. "you don't read your way into intuition." (2026-07-22-know-how-6-y, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7485749343468011520)
- He says that past month 600+ people played daily (July 2026), and a month after launch he reported 17K MAU with many balance changes shaped by the community. (2026-07-22-know-how-6-y, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7485749343468011520; 2026-07-20-one-month-ago-launched, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2079268447654187262)
- He brought the game to Cafe Cursor Delhi to get mechanics feedback from players. (2026-08-29-cursor-has-become-such, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2093631467050119364)
- He says AI helped him rewrite the game from scratch in about 50 hours, and that the current version was first one-shot with Fable 5 and then refined daily. (2026-06-13-same-helped-rewrite-board, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065780501370306909; 2026-08-27-haha-thank-has-actually, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2092770926597407221)

## Why did he open the game through an SDK?

- He says chess has many clients but none are interoperable; the Smash&Clash SDK lets anyone build a client on one shared network. "none of them are interoperable" (2026-10-08-true-s-why-introduced, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2108136746676592883; 2026-10-13-agent-oli-exactly-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109903986518319295)
- He says if you can draw on a surface you can make a client for it, and he intentionally wants other people to copy his game and build games inside it. (2026-10-04-harshitlog-tldraw-if-can, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2106672928201048321; 2026-11-04-addyosmani-intentionally-want-other, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2117849299333914863)
- He wants the game tied to no single platform, device or interface: games become worlds that travel with you. "they become worlds that can travel with you across interfaces" (2026-09-12-genuinely-excited-future-gaming, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2098624673311568047)
- He says cross-platform always mattered but interoperability matters more now, and walled gardens will not hold. (2026-10-15-sorry-but-gaming-wont, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2110643118832017873)
- He repeats the thesis that games within games, connected through interoperability, will shape the next decade of gaming. (2026-11-09-games-within-games-connected, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2119696322651673030)
- He says every virtual world could become an arcade and every game a distribution channel for other experiences; by a Netflix for gaming he means a connected ecosystem, not necessarily a subscription. (2026-10-15-big-fan-arcade-battle, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7516361321047605249)
- He says gaming needs a shadcn-like layer, meaning interoperable game assets and mechanics (not UI components) that anyone can mash up; he says these are not composable yet. "we need a shadcn for gaming." (2026-10-14-need-shadcn-gaming, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2110228800760082693; 2026-10-14-cool-but-ui-meant, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2110358273547444677)
- He treats agents as players, not enemies: they challenge you and results are verified on the same network. (2026-10-13-youll-notice-smash-clash, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7515683325471113216)
- He says the client stays free to play, core features stay on official clients, and money is meant to come from a layer underneath such as cosmetics and upgrades. (2026-10-13-thanks-honestly-client-stays, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109908599950692465)

## Does AI change who can make good games?

- He says building games is getting as easy as posting a short, but making a game is not making a good one: the floor and the ceiling have both risen, as with video. "the floor has risen, but so has the ceiling." (2026-10-18-building-games-getting-easy, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2111884410010431569)
- He openly says he used SI (super intelligence) for almost everything in Smash&Clash, while still making the decisions, talking to users and iterating, and he does not think tool choice should be gatekept. (2026-10-18-building-games-getting-easy, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2111884410010431569)
- He says taste matters more now: the person with taste and SI beats both the tool alone and the person who refuses it, and indies win on taste, not headcount or budget. "taste still matters, and it matters more now." (2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)
- He compares being against SI to being against the wheel because walking still works, and says games too costly to make before will now exist. (2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)
- He says, replying to a player, that people care whether a game is fun and worth their time, not how it was made. (2026-10-13-daquackers-really-dont-care, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109876231823110402)
- He says if games become something you open on any screen instead of download, discovery and taste matter more than budget and small teams can compete. (2026-10-22-watched-waiting4-asis-top, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2113309428887998492)
- His short rule: treat games like products, and products like games. (2026-10-21-treat-games-like-products, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112882020636594542)

## What keeps a game alive?

- Writing about Pixel Worlds, a game he played almost daily for years until it shut down and relaunched, he says internet products die when their systems break, not when attention drops. "they die when systems break." (2026-04-03-may-not-street-smart, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7445883249387749377)
- From the same post: relaunch spikes come from nostalgia, but bringing people back is easier than making them stay, and trust cannot be relaunched like a product. (2026-04-03-may-not-street-smart, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7445883249387749377)
- He says a broken game economy cannot be fixed halfway, so a full reset made product sense, even though it also reset players' time. (2026-04-03-may-not-street-smart, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7445883249387749377)

## Where does he think gaming is heading?

- He reads Epic's Rocket League UE6 teaser as being about Verse more than graphics: Fortnite becoming the front door and hub for many worlds. "fortnite is becoming the front door, not the product." (2026-05-25-epic-s-rocket-league, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2058783043406696814)
- In the same post he keeps the bear case (missed timelines, Roblox UGC volume) and calls this the first no-VR metaverse shape that makes sense, since it starts on screens people own. (2026-05-25-epic-s-rocket-league, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2058783043406696814)
- He calls Fortnite and Verse the Netflix of gaming, giving creators distribution and players more experiences, and asks how publishing fees would work for UE6 games. (2026-06-17-dont-get-why-people, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2067295748581151010; 2026-06-18-kinda-confused-about-publishing, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2067651970807336994)
- He calls cloud gaming a livestream you control and asks what if games worked like videos: run locally and stream assets in and out, which is the idea behind his rangeplay experiment. (2026-10-06-someone-got-gta-v, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7513290368948465666; 2026-10-26-cloud-gaming-relies-dedicated, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2114737613102055768)
- He wants AI-dubbed localization and says Rockstar could, with voice actors' consent, generate new dialogue during play. (2026-11-09-top5gamingx-dubbed-localization-elevenlabs, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2119729684493799542; 2026-11-09-like-current-dialogue-system, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2119734577325576476)
- Smaller views: dynamic live commentary (The Finals) makes a game far more immersive, the PS5 DualSense is next-level immersion, yearly FC releases look the same to him, and disc licenses should be upgradable to digital for free if Sony stops making discs. (2026-10-19-always-fascinated-by-how, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112035743422177593; 2026-07-28-ps5s-dualsense-immersion-truly, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2082006325169619341; 2026-07-24-dont-see-major-difference, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2080564136120086769; 2026-07-24-if-sony-stopping-disc, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2080571041659904417)
- (2024) After an esports summit in Delhi he backed the panel's call for a locally built distribution service for Indian esports, since Google and Apple dominate game distribution. (2024-12-16-attending-esports-community-industry, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7274452846031335425)
