# Leading and negotiating, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025

- The programme director says to reply to emails within 24 hours, because a later reply gives the impression that you do not care and thinking about it later doubles the energy spent. (ivi: Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-03-sanghvi-orientation-day-1.md)
- The programme director says to aim for in-person meetings even when that means a flight or a long journey, and to hold the meeting at your own place when possible because you get the person's full attention. (ivi: Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-03-sanghvi-orientation-day-1.md)
- The programme director says to follow up at once after a meeting, for example with a handwritten thank-you note written in the car straight afterwards. (ivi: Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-03-sanghvi-orientation-day-1.md)
- The programme director says to turn networking around by offering help and focusing on giving, since the help comes back though not always from the same person. (ivi: Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-03-sanghvi-orientation-day-1.md)
- The programme director says weak links matter, so keep a note of people you meet indirectly even though an ask to them is not strong. (ivi: Orientation day 1 on networking and cold outreach, Shreyas Sanghvi and the programme director, 3 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-03-sanghvi-orientation-day-1.md)

## Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025

- Anil Advani says that in his experience three founders who split equity equally always end up in a conflict of two against one. (ivi: Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-10-advani-startup-legal-essentials.md)
- Anil Advani says all founders should hear the same information but one person, the CEO, should be the face and decision maker, and that requiring every founder to agree on every decision does not work. (ivi: Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-10-advani-startup-legal-essentials.md)
- Anil Advani says co-founders who cannot agree on day one, when the shares are worth little, will not agree later when the company is valuable, and should walk away and stay friends. (ivi: Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-10-advani-startup-legal-essentials.md)
- Anil Advani says nobody should do actual work for the company without a written agreement on scope and compensation, because without one they can later claim whatever they want. (ivi: Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-10-advani-startup-legal-essentials.md)
- Anil Advani's sequence for bringing in a collaborator is to check personal fit, discuss roles and time, check for conflicts with their employer, agree equity and vesting, have a lawyer write it up, and only then let them start work. (ivi: Startup legal essentials, Anil Advani, 10 Apr 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-04-10-advani-startup-legal-essentials.md)

## Building Pickrr and advice on B2B and D2C, Gaurav Mangla and Ankit Kaushik, 22 May 2025

## Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram says she could not afford star doctors at about 12 lakh rupees a month, so she hired three or four young doctors for the same money, all from government hospitals where they had seen 100 to 150 outpatients a day. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says she put her surgeon and medical oncologists on equal pay and made them one team, even though the market pays a medical oncologist about twice as much, so that they would refer patients to each other and not compete. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says a founder has to pitch to talent the way a company pitches at campus placements, with a pay structure and a career path, and that she pays 10 to 15% above market plus an incentive and equity. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says founders must be hard on themselves about deadlines and money, and that successful founders are the ones who did well with limited resources, not the ones who had unlimited resources. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)

## Cash flow, advisers, employees and operational readiness, Sonali Srungaram, 30 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram says once 50 to 300 people's livelihoods depend on the company's direction, creativity alone is not enough and the founder has to become a chief executive. (ivi: Cash flow, advisers, employees and operational readiness, Sonali Srungaram, 30 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-30-srungaram-cash-flow-advisers-employees-and-operational-readiness.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says not to rush into finding a co-founder, because a founder spends 5 to 10 hours a day close to that person and needs to know they truly work well together.
