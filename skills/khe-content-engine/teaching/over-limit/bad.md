# Run: the due date at the top of the invoice

Engine: ../../examples/engine
Idea: moving the due date to the top of the invoice, written as a date, got clients paid sooner
Platforms: x, linkedin

## Brief

From Ines, 2026-11-09, in her words and from her spreadsheet.

- In June 2026 she changed the invoice template that 14 of her clients use. The due date moved from the bottom, where it read "Net 30", to the first line, in bold, written out as a date.
- On the old template the median time to payment was 38 days.
- From July to September those clients sent 212 invoices. The median time to payment was 24 days.
- There was no control group, and summer is a slow season for some of them.
- One client, an illustrator, agreed to be quoted without her name: "I stopped chasing in week two."
- For years Ines told clients that late payment was a client problem.

For years I told clients that late payment was a client problem. I was wrong about where it starts.

In June I moved one line on the invoice template that 14 of my clients use. The due date went from the bottom to the first line, written out as a date.

Then I counted. Across 212 invoices sent from July to September, the median time to payment was 24 days. On the old template it had been 38.

There was no control group, and summer is a slow season for some of them. I am keeping the change anyway.
