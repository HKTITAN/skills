# Glossary

These are the terms he names or uses in his own sense, plus the names of his shows, projects and books as he describes them, each defined from what he says. Where he uses a term differently over the years, the entry gives the years. A citation is the source and a time in h:mm:ss form in a video or a paragraph number such as p12 in an article.

(The 50/50 of Snapchat Collaborations, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-50-50-of-snapchat-collaborations/; Why 1+1 Equals 11, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-11-equals-11/)

(The $1.80 Instagram Strategy To Grow Your Business or Brand, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagram-for-business-180-strategy-grow-business-brand/; Practical Business and Marketing Advice for Dominating 2019 Keynote at NAC at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWiLQGEqqmo&t=2205s; 5 LinkedIn Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/linkedin-marketing-strategies/)
