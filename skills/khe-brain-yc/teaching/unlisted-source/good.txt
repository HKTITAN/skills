Paul Graham's 2012 essay puts a good growth rate during the Y Combinator batch at 5 to 7 percent a week (Startup = Growth). Divya Bhat repeated the figure in a talk the library dates to 2023 (Setting KPIs and goals).
Source: Startup = Growth, https://paulgraham.com/growth.html
Source: Setting KPIs and goals, https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Iu-setting-kpis-and-goals
Not in the corpus: a growth rate for a company outside the batch.
