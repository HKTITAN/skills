Paul Graham's answer moved across three essays, and the latest one read says no (A Student's Guide to Startups; Why to Not Not Start a Startup; Before the Startup). His 2014 essay, written for college students, says not to start a startup in college, because a real startup takes over your life and you stop being a student (Before the Startup).
Changed: in 2006 Graham wrote that Y Combinator had at first encouraged people to start while still in college, and had since decided the line should be to wait until graduation (A Student's Guide to Startups).
Changed: in 2007 Graham withdrew his earlier advice that founders be at least 23 and work at another company for a few years first, and advised people to go ahead and start right out of college (Why to Not Not Start a Startup).
Changed: in 2014 Graham answered plainly that you should not start a startup in college (Before the Startup).
