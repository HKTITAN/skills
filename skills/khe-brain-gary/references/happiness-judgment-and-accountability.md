# Happiness, judgment and accountability

This file collects what he said about happiness, fear of other people's judgment, regret, and accountability in place of complaining. It draws on articles from 2013 to 2021 and keynotes and interviews from 2016 to 2026. Read the dates on each bullet. Figures about money and happiness are his own observations from people he knows, not studies.

## What does he say makes people happy or unhappy, and is money part of it?

- In 2016 he says happiness drives everything, so stick to what makes you happy and the goals you want to execute instead of limiting yourself to what others think. (Nobody Can Define Who You Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nobody-can-define-who-you-are/)
- In 2016 he asks whether you would rather make 70K running your own thing and be happy, or 100K at a job you hate. (Reframing the Narrative on Entrepreneurship, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/reframing-the-narrative-on-entrepreneurship/)
- In 2017 he says he knows tens of thousands of people and sees no link between income and happiness, citing friends on 47,000 dollars a year who are happy and rich people in Silicon Valley who are miserable and lonely. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=405s)
- In 2022 he calls the idea that you can buy your way to happiness the biggest falsity in human society and says the data shows no correlation between wealth and happiness. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=225s)
- In 2023 he says people trade happiness for money believing money will buy it, and that he himself was pumped at 40,000 dollars a year helping his dad, hitting garage sales and watching the Jets. (The Fastest Way To Make Your First $100000 l Erika Taught Me Podcast at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wf3-floJA1c&t=1665s)
- In 2018 he says buying things is makeup and band-aids over unhappiness for 95 percent of people, comparing friends with 40,000 a year who love life to people with 40 million who hate it. (Gary Vee on 6ix9ine's Trolling Millennials Fortnite and Dressing The Part - The Cruz Show at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqvIbndsoxw&t=405s)
- In 2018 he says do not buy a status brand if it makes you feel better for an hour and worse when the card bill arrives, but that it is fine if you have the money and love fashion. (Gary Vee on 6ix9ine's Trolling Millennials Fortnite and Dressing The Part - The Cruz Show at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqvIbndsoxw&t=45s)
- In 2018 he says people take jobs they hate to buy things that impress people they do not care about, and that happiness is the true return, not a mansion, a car or an Instagram look. (Top 10 DailyVees From 2018, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/top-10-dailyvees-in-2018/)
- In 2019 he says success is waking up happy and able to do what you want, that freedom is about how much you spend rather than how much you make, and that keeping up with the Joneses is the poison of society. (The Entrepreneur's Mindset Gary Vaynerchuk USC Talk 2019 at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LVc0Qw1LSm4&t=1350s)
- In 2020 he said rent and the fear of how it looks to live with parents are jails people build for themselves, and that people willing to live in an even worse apartment or take a low-paid job for years can taste more things. (The Solo Show Is Back AskGaryVee 332 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yI047XWUh7Y&t=630s)
- In 2022 he says to redefine success away from money and stuff, naming a stay-at-home parent, a cop, a firefighter and a teacher, and to quit a 180,000 dollar job you hate and rebuild toward real happiness. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 1:06:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=4005s)
- In 2026 he says materialism is a proxy for self-worth that creates envy and a high-school dynamic among adults, and he hopes it declines. (GaryVee Reveals the Biggest Business Opportunity of 2026 - Young and Profiting at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qDmEmp9LfAY&t=1035s)
- In 2026 he says the ability to forgive people because you realize they were hurt makes you strong and changes your life, and that you should forgive yourself right away because everyone else is flawed too. (Inside a Warehouse Where Real Money Is Made at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiJvQBWJwvQ&t=1530s)

## How does he define winning, the climb and legacy?

- In 2014 he says a huge north star means he never has to reset his goals, and that the day he buys the Jets would upset him because the climb would be over. (I Don't Care if I End Up Buying the New York Jets, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/i-dont-care-if-i-end-up-buying-the-new-york-jets/)
- In 2016 he says the item on his bucket list is the pursuit of buying the Jets and not the purchase, and that people confuse the two. (Clarity On My Bucket List, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/clarity-bucket-list/)
- In 2016 he says that because he is in pursuit he has already achieved it, which is why he is at peace: "It's like I've won before I even started." (Clarity On My Bucket List, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/clarity-bucket-list/)
- In 2017 he says the process, the struggle and the failures give him energy, not awards or money. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 55:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=3330s)
- In 2013 he says do not over-celebrate a win such as raising money, and do not be devastated by a loss, because reality sits in the middle. (Treat Your Business Like Your Second Child, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/treat-your-business-like-your-second-child/)
- In 2015 he admits his habit of not celebrating wins is a flaw, though he often reads it as a winner's mentality, and says he wants to change. (When You Don't Celebrate Your Wins You Hold Your Company Back, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/when-you-dont-celebrate-your-wins-you-hold-your-company-back/)
- In 2019 he says he cannot smell the roses and does not like winning, only the game. (ABtalks with Gary Vee - Chapter 12 at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxgQRJ1SwZ8&t=2205s)
- In 2024 he says that when VaynerMedia lands a 20 million dollar client his energy goes straight to problems, and he calls his not celebrating a weakness. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=1170s)
- In 2016 he says he would take 200 million dollars and a reputation for changing how an industry thought about attention over 500 million and being known only as someone who did well for himself. (Two Secrets to Staying Motivated & Hungry, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-secrets-staying-motivated-hungry/)
- In 2016 he says if you live for your funeral the bank account follows by accident. (Advice to a Young Man That Many of You Need at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HwXsFPZp3fQ&t=45s)
- In 2022 he says he would rather have many people at his funeral than own the Jets. (Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=1530s)
- In 2024 he said he is happy his professional goal is so big because it lets him keep playing. (57 Minutes of Business Tactics & Lessons l Podcast With Friends Ep 15 at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tQV_G4SfdKY&t=1395s)
- In 2026 he says success to him is building the biggest sand castle all day and knocking it down, because it is about the day at the beach and not the castle. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=945s)
- In 2013 he says he builds the biggest building by building and not by tearing others down, and he wants others to win as long as he wins more. In 2026 he repeats it as a choice between being talented and admiring others or spending your time tearing the rest down. (Gary Vaynerchuk The Future of Social Media Marketing - Marie Forleo at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Qy9aLqhxcc&t=990s; This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=495s)
- In 2021 he says happiness has to be the ambition, whether the project is an NFT, a business or a YouTube channel, and he asks whether you are acting for you or for them. (Ambition Patience and the Insecurity Trap: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/ambition-patience-and-the-insecurity-trap-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)

## What does he say about fear of other people's opinions, praise and criticism?

- In 2015 he says you cannot give a damn what other people think, not even your parents, and in the same piece tells you to sit down with that person and ask that their reaction to a failed step not crush you. (The Best Way to Deal with Fear of Failure, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-way-to-deal-with-fear-of-failure/)
- In 2016 he says you will care less about others' opinions as you age, and that haters are fuel because nothing is more interesting to him than proving people wrong. (My Thesis on How to Handle Feedback, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/thesis-handle-feedback/)
- In 2016 he says negative comments still sting despite his confidence, and that he will never get 100 percent positive feedback because he is "only as good as your last at bat". (My Thesis on How to Handle Feedback, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/thesis-handle-feedback/)
- In 2018 he says valuing someone else's opinion over your own is the biggest reason people are unhappy, and that you should ask what the agenda and intent behind a piece of feedback is. (How to stop caring what others think, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-stop-caring-what-others-think/)
- In 2018 he says he cannot hear a hundred replies calling him Jesus or a hundred calling him a snake oil salesman, and that if you cannot hear the cheering you cannot hear the booing. (NOW is your time - Speech to Alabama Football Team Gary Vaynerchuk 2018 Keynote at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqlz3Lngh1U&t=1395s; Gary Vee on 6ix9ine's Trolling Millennials Fortnite and Dressing The Part - The Cruz Show at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqvIbndsoxw&t=1260s)
- In 2019 he says to weigh an opinion by who delivers it, since your mother's view, a co-worker's and an anonymous comment cannot carry the same weight. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 12:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=765s)
- In 2019 his steps for negative feedback are to feel sympathy for the writer, look for any truth in the comment, and take the good from it. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=675s)
- In 2019 he says he reads negative comments so he does not become a caricature of himself, and that he does not see trolls, only feedback to calibrate on. (How to Build Confidence and Self-esteem Aubrey Marcus Podcast w/ GaryVee at 33:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzEkg9aM4Hk&t=1980s)
- In 2022 he said a person who is hurting writes a nasty comment to make themselves feel better, which he calls a circle of insecurity, and that the target can have a day, a week or even a month ruined by it. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=855s)
- In 2022 he says that if your happiness depends on people thinking you are awesome, you are vulnerable. (Gary Vee Explains How He Made $90 Million on NFTs & Why They'll Change the World - Full Send Podcast at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UbGVM-Usy8M&t=1665s)
- In 2023 he says the fear that stops most people is fear of other people's judgment, and that it matters more than fear of not having money because judgment is a boogeyman. (Don't Live For the Algorithm Live For Your Happiness GaryVee Audio Experience at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ft8R-b-3NXM&t=2025s)
- In 2024 he says he does not take criticism personally and cares only about the judgment of his inner circles of family, friends and employees. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 1:06:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=3960s)
- In 2024 he said that after learning it the hard way he can take nasty online comments as neutral information and not take them personally, but that it took him a decade. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 51:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=3105s)
- In 2026 he said everyone is stuck in sixth grade, living their lives based on the opinions of people they often do not even like and definitely do not want to be like. (Inside a Warehouse Where Real Money Is Made at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiJvQBWJwvQ&t=2565s)
- In 2026 he says to be comfortable being misunderstood, because no one's opinion of you, even your closest person's, can fully know you. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=315s)

## How does he talk about regret, and what does he say old people teach?

- In 2015 he says he does not allow himself to regret, calling regret the opposite of entrepreneurship, even as he lists passing on Uber's angel round. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=1755s)
- In 2016 he said he passed twice on an angel investment in Uber at 4 million dollars because he had just bought an apartment and was not liquid. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=1305s)
- In 2019 he says he is not crippled by passing on Uber twice, and in the same year that he does not count it a loss because the other path could have had its own bad outcomes. (57 Minutes of Advice For People Early In Their Career Chase Fireside Chat 2019 at 53:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0doo_v0EPOQ&t=3195s; 13 Core Business Principles to Start Immediately Auckland Keynote 2019 at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFKYqyVVet0&t=3060s)
- In 2016 he named one of the biggest regrets of his life as not taking 2 to 3 extra weeks of vacation a year with his wife in the first five years of marriage, and said he now takes seven weeks of vacation a year. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=2205s)
- In 2019 he says he wishes he had five to ten more memories in his twenties on trips with his high school and college friends, and that the dumbest things he bought were the things he did not buy. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 1:05:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=3915s)
- In 2022 he says never reaching out to people he admired was one of the biggest mistakes of his life, and that after Kobe Bryant died he wished he had reached out to tell him what he had done over the years. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=3060s)
- In 2024 he says his main business regret is not pushing harder where the attention was: more TikTok six years earlier, more Google ads in 2001, more email in 1996, more tweets in 2007. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 45:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=2745s)
- In 2016 he says regret is scarier than judgment because you can fix people thinking you are a jerk but not being 96 and wishing you were 64. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 1:36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=5805s)
- In 2016 he says regret is the biggest poison and that you should choose the decision you will regret least at 90. (Inside My Heart Brain And Soul In 13 Minutes DailyVee 101 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnfyyABag3s&t=630s)
- In 2023 he recommends spending a lot more time with 80 to 100 year olds who are not family, such as a neighbor or someone at an airport gate, because a grandparent relationship carries baggage and a neutral third party does not. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=1035s)
- In 2023 he says people aged 75 to 90 talk about what they skipped, and that for a workaholic like him the hardest one was more time with his kids, which shifted him in his thirties and forties. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=1170s)
- In 2024 he said that people who have lived it never regret their moments of jumping and always regret the moments they did not jump, so you will never regret your acts of courage. (These 5 Traits Are The Secret To Success In Business & Life l GaryVee's VeeCon 2024 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0lw3YiniDc&t=990s)
- In 2025 he said that regret is the great thing we should fear, and that if you do not chase your dream because you are scared of what other people think, you will wake up at 70, 80, 90 or 100 and regret the way you lived your only life. (GaryVee Your AI & Social Media Must-Knows + 7 Life-Changing Keys to Success Pt 1 - Jamie Kern Lima at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Df8QmoT6G8&t=720s; GaryVee Your AI & Social Media Must-Knows + 7 Life-Changing Keys to Success Pt 1 - Jamie Kern Lima at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Df8QmoT6G8&t=585s)
- In 2026 he calls the worst sentence the three words "I wish I", and says whatever you fear is far less scary than telling a stranger in 30 years what you did not do. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=1845s)

## Why does he say complaining is worthless, and what is his case for accountability?

- In 2014 he says complaining has no value because apart from health, the loss of a loved one or a terminal illness, everything feels controllable, and if you control it you can fix it. (The One Thing You'll Never Get Any Value From, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-one-thing-youll-never-get-any-value-from/)
- In 2015 he gives his replacement for complaining as three steps: assess the problem, find the solution and get on offense, calling complaining the defense. (3 Things That Will Always Stand in the Way of Your Goals, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/3-things-that-will-always-stand-in-the-way-of-your-goals-2/)
- In 2016 he says that if you are happy you have won and if you complain you have not won yet, and that he is not mad at people who play video games or watch Netflix, only at those who do so while baffled they are not living their dream. (One of the Few Things I Complain About: Complaining, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/one-thing-i-complain-about/)
- In 2016 he says he does not complain, then exempts the Jets, which he admits he complains about a lot. (One of the Few Things I Complain About: Complaining, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/one-thing-i-complain-about/)
- In 2017 he says the number one thing he thanks his parents for is that he never saw them complain, so he is incapable of it. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=360s)
- In 2018 he says whoever listens to your complaints is a tell, and a friend or parent who complains with you means you are in a bad system. (#1 Thing for an Intern or College Student to Do 2018 Summer Interns Fireside Chat at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BJiPvnfdbg&t=1350s)
- In 2017 he says most questions people bring him are answered by asking **What's the alternative**, because each thing you resist has a worse opposite you are choosing instead. (What's Your Alternative, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/whats-your-alternative/)
- In 2017 he says he is happy because everything is his fault, since if you do not like something you can change it or move. (Haste & Hustle Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Ontario 2017 at 41:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NZWPsqiVJ9Y&t=2475s)
- In 2017 he says his default is to believe every negative thing is his fault, knowing it is not literally true, and that he is not talking about extreme cases such as assault. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=450s)
- In 2017 he says that when you are 100 percent accountable and know your craft you cannot lose, and that blaming country, school, boss or parents is almost always a mindset for 95 percent of readers. (Confidence & Humility, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/confidence-humility/)
- In 2018 he says blaming yourself is not judging yourself, and that people fear accountability because they confuse the two. (5 Reminders That Accountability Leads to Happiness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-reminders-that-accountability-leads-to-happiness/)
- In 2019 he says to feel disappointment when you mess up but not to dwell, because dwelling makes the next thing less likely to work. (Be Accountable But Don't Judge Yourself, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/be-accountable-but-dont-judge-yourself/)
- In 2021 he says accountability is the secret potion for happiness, because it leads to solutions, keeps you in control instead of being a victim and diffuses the tension in arguments. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Accountability, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-accountability/)
- In 2021 he said most people hate accountability and love to point fingers at others instead of pointing thumbs back at themselves, listing self-judgement as the first reason, and he gave a habit of asking did I mess up, and if not, did someone I hired mess up, because either way it is his fault. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Accountability, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-accountability/)
- In 2023 he admits accountability can backfire because some people hear that it is all their fault and beat themselves up, and says he is still trying to communicate it better. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=720s)
- In 2024 he says a thumb pointed at yourself leads to happiness and a finger at others compounds unhappiness, and that every problem in his companies is 100 percent his fault because he hired the people who hired the people. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 1:08:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=4095s)
- In 2025 he said that to get closer to your dreams you can post a video saying something you are going to accomplish and ask the world to hold you accountable, and call the person closest to you and apologize for blaming them. (Stop Fearing AI Start Using It Like This GaryVee w/ The Liz Moody Podcast at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCdX4pebCb4&t=2790s)
- In 2026 he says systemic racism, sexism and the rich-poor divide exist and excuses should not define you, and asks where the thumb is while society is addicted to fingers. (Gary Vee isn't afraid to lose it all betting on himself and reveals one thing - The Pivot Podcast at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ChdO5O6llw&t=1710s)
- In 2026 he says everything is your fault and that this is liberating, because blaming others admits someone else is in control. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=450s)

## What does he mean by optimism, perspective and gratitude?

- In 2014 he says to be grateful with one drop in the glass because you know exactly what to do with it, and not to be glass half empty when it is four-fifths full. (A Note on Being Grateful, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/a-note-on-being-grateful/)
- In 2021 he says his productivity is predicated on gratitude for what he was gifted, and that he works hard because he is grateful rather than being grateful because he worked hard. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Gratitude, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-gratitude/)
- In 2021 he said one reason people lack gratitude is that they look up at those who rank higher and do not look down at the billions ranked lower, and that they do not quantify their luck or realise how hard it is to become a human being. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Gratitude, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-gratitude/)
- In 2019 he cites the odds of being born a human as 400 trillion to one, and says those odds mean you have no standing to complain. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 1:09:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=4140s)
- In 2016 he says he wants optimism loaded with practicality, since so many people have optimism without it. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 1:11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=4275s)
- In 2017 he says life is binary: optimistic and on offense, or pessimistic and on defense. (My Content is a Mindset, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/mindset/)
- In 2017 he said to make sure your intuition is speaking and not your delusion, and that optimism is blind when it does not have practicality wired into it. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=2205s)
- In 2017 he says it has never been better to be a human, and that people complain about extra cream in a six dollar coffee because they lack perspective. (Gary Vaynerchuk on Why Perspective Will Make or Break You Impact Theory at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lITalyctN4&t=225s)
- In 2018 and 2019 he says that when you look for bad you will find it and when you look for good positivity becomes the norm, and he asks people to spend 24 hours looking for the good. (Cheering for Optimism and The Internet, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/optimism-and-the-internet/; 10 Examples of Positivity on Social Media, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/positivity-on-social-media/)
- In 2021 he says optimism is often confused with delusion but is extremely practical, and that people dismiss it as fluffy because they fear the disappointment of being wrong. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Optimism, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-optimism/)
- In 2021 he describes **GAP**, gratitude and perspective, and says he pretends a family member has died every few days so that news of lost revenue becomes easy to receive. (Stress In The Workplace: Three takes for you to consider, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stress-in-the-workplace-three-takes-for-you-to-consider/)
- In 2021 he names the **Three Ps** as practicality, positivity and perspective, meaning optimistic but not delusional. (Stress In The Workplace: Three takes for you to consider, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/stress-in-the-workplace-three-takes-for-you-to-consider/)
