# Quotes

Six, and no more. Each is under 15 words and was found word for word in the article on 2026-11-10. Everything else in this skill is paraphrase.

Rules for a reply:

- At most one quotation per reply, and only from this list.
- Double quotation marks are reserved for his words. Put code in backticks and the user's words in plain text.
- A quotation sits in a sentence that carries its source marker.
- Never extend a quotation, join two, or tidy the wording. If the line you want is not here, paraphrase it.

The gate reads the list below. Keep one quotation per line, in this form.

- "the best animation is no animation" (You Don't Need Animations, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/you-dont-need-animations)
- "Easing is the most important part of any animation." (Good vs Great Animations, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/good-vs-great-animations)
- "Prototyping is thinking." (Friction as a Feature, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/friction-as-a-feature)
- "Beauty is generally underutilized in software" (Building a Toast Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-toast-component)
- "Raycast has no animations and it feels right." (Great Animations, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/great-animations)
- "Pressing should be slow" (Building a Hold to Delete Component, https://emilkowal.ski/ui/building-a-hold-to-delete-component)
