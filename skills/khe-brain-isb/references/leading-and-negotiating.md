# How do I lead people, decide and negotiate?

One claim per line, each with its piece. Every line is the repository author's summary: the Leadership files cited here name Goleman, Maslow, Herzberg, McClelland, Tuckman, Schein, Quinn, Kotter, Lewin, Prosci, Janis, John Boyd, Vroom and Yetton in their text, and Deci and Ryan, Thomas and Kilmann and Cialdini in their titles only. They name no professor, no course and no source for any figure. A number inside a worked example is that example's number, never a benchmark. These files disagree on whether to reveal a BATNA, on what a reservation price is, on which rewards are hygiene factors and on who sets strategy; see [disagreements](disagreements.md) before you merge them.

## Leadership styles and emotional intelligence

- The file defines emotional intelligence as the ability to recognise, understand and manage emotions in yourself and others, and calls it a stronger predictor of leadership success than IQ without citing a study. (Leadership: Emotional Intelligence, EI, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/styles-emotional-intelligence-ei.md)
- It has five dimensions: self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy and social skills; unlike IQ it can be developed throughout life, which takes self-awareness, commitment to change, practice and coaching. (Leadership: Emotional Intelligence, EI, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/styles-emotional-intelligence-ei.md)
- Daniel Goleman's six styles are directive, visionary, affiliative, participative, pacesetting and coaching; the most effective leaders flex between them by situation and person, and poor leaders overuse one dominant style. (Leadership: Goleman's Six Leadership Styles, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/styles-golemans-six-leadership-styles.md)
- Servant leadership has the leader serve the team's needs; the file says it works with high-autonomy teams, knowledge work and values-driven organisations and is less effective in crisis or with teams requiring heavy structure. (Leadership: Servant Leadership, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/styles-servant-leadership.md)

## Motivation and performance

- Maslow's five levels are physiological, safety, belonging, esteem and self-actualisation; as lower needs are met higher ones become motivators, though the file adds that the hierarchy is not always linear. (Leadership: Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/motivation-maslows-hierarchy-of-needs.md)
- The Herzberg file puts compensation and benefits among hygiene factors, whose improvement removes dissatisfaction without creating satisfaction, and puts recognition and growth and advancement among motivators. (Leadership: Herzberg's Two-Factor Theory, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/motivation-herzbergs-two-factor-theory.md)
- The key-principles file calls money and title hygiene factors, not motivators, and says real motivation comes from autonomy, competence and relatedness. (Leadership: Key Principles for Effective Leadership, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/styles-key-principles-for-effective-leadership.md)
- McClelland's three drives are achievement, affiliation and power, and they vary by person. The file says to diagnose which drives are strongest in each team member, and that the same reward will not motivate everyone. (Leadership: McClelland's Three Needs Theory, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/motivation-mcclellands-three-needs-theory.md)
- Intrinsic motivation comes from autonomy, competence and relatedness; excessive monitoring, unclear feedback or team isolation kills it no matter how much you pay. (Leadership: Self-Determination Theory, Deci & Ryan, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/motivation-self-determination-theory-deci-ryan.md)
- Specific, measurable goals motivate more than vague expectations and regular feedback beats annual reviews; individual incentives can reduce cooperation, and piece-rate pay raises quantity but often lowers quality. (Leadership: Performance Management Systems, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/motivation-performance-management-systems.md)

## Teams, culture and change

- Tuckman's stages are forming, storming, norming and performing; not all teams reach performing, and regression occurs under stress. (Leadership: Team Dynamics and High-Performance Teams, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/teams-team-dynamics-and-high-performance-teams.md)
- Psychological safety is members trusting they can speak up, admit mistakes and ask questions without punishment or humiliation; demographic diversity brings its benefits only where that safety exists. (Leadership: Team Dynamics and High-Performance Teams, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/teams-team-dynamics-and-high-performance-teams.md)
- Culture is the actual operating norms, not the stated values, and it reinforces itself through hiring and promotion, systems and processes, stories and symbols, informal networks and leader behaviour; the file gives 2 to 3 years as the minimum for culture change. (Leadership: Organizational Culture and Design, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/teams-organizational-culture-and-design.md)
- Kotter's model has eight steps, from establishing urgency to anchoring in culture; failures occur when steps are skipped, often urgency or wins, or when leaders declare victory too early. (Leadership: Change Management, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/teams-change-management.md)
- The ADKAR model, credited to Prosci, has five stages for an individual: awareness, desire, knowledge, ability and reinforcement; the file says change fails most at desire and at reinforcement. (Leadership: Change Management, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/teams-change-management.md)

## Decisions, biases and groupthink

- The file lists nine biases: anchoring, availability, confirmation, overconfidence, sunk cost, framing effects, hindsight, status quo and the Dunning-Kruger effect, each with a debiasing practice. (Leadership: Cognitive Biases Catalog and Debiasing Strategies, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/decisions-cognitive-biases-catalog-and-debiasing-strategies.md)
- The sunk cost remedy is to separate past costs, which are irrelevant, from future costs and benefits and to ask "would I make this decision today?". (Leadership: Cognitive Biases Catalog and Debiasing Strategies, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/decisions-cognitive-biases-catalog-and-debiasing-strategies.md)
- Under loss aversion losses feel about twice as painful as equivalent gains feel good, so people are risk-averse over potential gains and risk-seeking when facing losses. (Leadership: Decision-Making Frameworks, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/decisions-decision-making-frameworks.md)
- Janis's groupthink model gives four preconditions: isolation, a directive leader, high stress and no norms for reviewing assumptions; the file's examples are the Bay of Pigs invasion, Space Shuttle Challenger and Enron. (Leadership: Groupthink: Symptoms and Prevention, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/decisions-groupthink-symptoms-and-prevention.md)

## Negotiation, conflict and influence

- A BATNA is your best course of action if the negotiation fails; a stronger one means a stronger position, and the BATNA file says to use it to set your reservation price. (Leadership: BATNA: Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-batna-best-alternative-to-negotiated-agreement.md)
- The ZOPA file defines the reservation price as the worst deal you would accept and calls it equivalent to the BATNA; the zone is the range between the two sides' reservation prices. (Leadership: ZOPA: Zone of Possible Agreement, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-zopa-zone-of-possible-agreement.md)
- The BATNA file says to know your BATNA precisely but not disclose it, and to reference it indirectly to increase credibility, as in "I have other options". (Leadership: BATNA: Best Alternative to Negotiated Agreement, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-batna-best-alternative-to-negotiated-agreement.md)
- The ZOPA file says revealing your true BATNA may lower your power but credible signals of a good BATNA strengthen it. (Leadership: ZOPA: Zone of Possible Agreement, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-zopa-zone-of-possible-agreement.md)
- The negotiation-fundamentals file lists revealing your BATNA among common mistakes and says it weakens your position. (Leadership: Negotiation Fundamentals Beyond BATNA, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/conflict-negotiation-fundamentals-beyond-batna.md)
- Preparation rests on three foundations: interests against positions, information gathering and strategy formulation; the file states that adequate preparation determines 80% of negotiation success. (Leadership: Negotiation Preparation: The Foundation of Success, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-negotiation-preparation-the-foundation-of-success.md)
- The pie is expanded before it is claimed, through bundled issues and differences in time, risk tolerance and constraints; the file's example turns a $10M asking price into $6M cash now and a $4M earnout over 3 years. (Leadership: Deal Structuring and Creative Problem-Solving, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/negotiation-deal-structuring-and-creative-problem-solving.md)
- The five conflict modes are competing, collaborating, compromising, avoiding and accommodating; each fits different contexts, and most people have one dominant mode and overuse it. (Leadership: Thomas-Kilmann Five Modes, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/conflict-thomas-kilmann-five-modes.md)
- The six principles are reciprocity, commitment and consistency, social proof, authority, liking and scarcity; the file says manipulation uses scarcity and authority to short-circuit rational thinking. (Leadership: Cialdini's Six Principles of Influence, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/influence-cialdinis-six-principles-of-influence.md)
- Expert and referent power are called the most sustainable and effective, coercive power creates resentment and resistance, and formal authority works well only when combined with expertise or referent power. (Leadership: Power, Politics, and Influence in Organizations, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/decisions-power-politics-and-influence-in-organizations.md)

## Governance, boards, crisis and succession

- The corporate governance file says the board represents shareholders, oversees the CEO and sets strategic direction, and it lists approving strategy and major decisions first among six board responsibilities. (Leadership: Corporate Governance, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/governance-corporate-governance.md)
- The CEO-challenges file lists strategy first of five CEO priorities, given in order, and says the board provides governance, oversight and counsel and has authority to remove the CEO. (Leadership: CEO Challenges and Specific Leadership Contexts, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/strategic-ceo-challenges-and-specific-leadership-contexts.md)
- Board size is given as typically 5 to 15 directors, with larger boards less effective, and a majority should be independent from management. (Leadership: Board Composition and Dynamics, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/communication-board-composition-and-dynamics.md)
- A crisis is led in three phases: immediate response in the first 24 to 48 hours, response over days 1 to 30 and recovery from month 2 onward; transparent communication goes internal first, then external. (Leadership: Crisis Leadership, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/strategic-crisis-leadership.md)
- Succession is planned while the CEO is secure, not when leaving; an internal choice preserves culture continuity, an external one brings fresh perspective, and the grooming model identifies a successor 2 to 3 years ahead. (Leadership: Succession Planning, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/leadership/rules/strategic-succession-planning.md)
