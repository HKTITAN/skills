# Attention and media shifts

This file collects what he said about where attention sits and how it moves. The material comes from articles of 2013 to 2021 and keynotes and interviews of 2011 to 2026, and it covers Snapchat and Instagram in 2015 and 2016, audio in 2017, TikTok in 2019 and what he calls interest media in 2025 and after. Platform lines carry their year and were his reading then; some articles on his site are partly team-written guides, and the 2018 Auckland lines in one article are reported by staff.

## Why attention is the asset

- In 2015 he said attention is what he cares about, and that everyone has one job: grab attention, tell your story, then sell. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=945s)
- In 2017 he said that before you can tell anyone how great you are you need their attention, and without it you lost. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=495s)
- In 2019 he said attention is the singular asset and it sits on mobile devices on a few platforms. (Quantity Can Be The Gateway Quality – A New Way To Think About Content, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/quantity-can-be-the-gateway-quality-a-new-way-to-think-about-content/)
- In 2023 he said attention is the most important commodity, but it only buys the chance to tell people about your product, and people err by expecting a purchase after they earn it. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=2520s)
- In 2026 he said you cannot sell trading cards, energy drinks or hats without attention, and a pastor does nothing if the church is empty. (Gary Vee isn't afraid to lose it all betting on himself and reveals one thing - The Pivot Podcast at 54:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0ChdO5O6llw&t=3240s)
- In 2019 he said it has always been one game, where are the eyes and ears and can you put content there, and that the phone in everyone's hand makes this shift unbelievably underestimated. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=540s)
- He says he is in the attention business, not the social media business, and that social media interests him only because it captured so much attention. (Why Super Bowl Commercials Should Cost More Than They Do, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/super-bowl-commercials-should-cost-more-than-they-do/)
- He says he learned attention as a child by watching where people's eyes go, at lemonade stands and then at baseball card shows where the table position decided the foot traffic. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=270s; Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=2430s)
- In 2013 he said absolute follower counts are not the end game, since one tweet noticed by one right person is enough whether you have 100 or 1,000,000 followers. (It's Not About the Numbers, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/its-not-about-the-numbers/)
- In 2021 he said you do not need to target accounts with 100,000 followers, because people with smaller followings but tremendous engagement who live nearby are enough to win with. (Instagram DM: Direct Messaging to Network, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/network-instagram-direct-message/)
- In 2022 he said the audience is the actual asset, and that content at scale with real community building is how you win the attention game. (Opinion: Why MrBeast Passed on a $1 Billion Offer for his YouTube Channel, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-mrbeast-passed-on-a-1-billion-offer-for-his-youtube-channel/)

## What day trading attention and underpriced attention mean

- In 2016 he said he day trades attention, that it is the one thing he does for a living, that he bought into e-commerce when the market did not believe in it, and that he bought into email marketing when nobody else was doing it. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=990s; Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=1080s)
- In 2016 he said that when something is grossly underpriced by the market because people are not practitioners, it changes his behavior, and that many people in the room with strong public opinions on Facebook ads had never run any. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=1170s)
- In 2017 he said he moved from "marketing in the year that we live in" to day trading attention because even inside a year, influencer marketing, Facebook, YouTube and Twitter shift, and small feature updates are often the marginal opportunity. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=540s)
- In 2017 he said to understand the day trading of attention, asking what attention is underpriced and overpriced. If you have $10,000 to sell jet engines, shipping supplies or a SaaS business, you need to know where the cheap attention is. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=810s)
- In 2017 he called himself an "attentionist" and said nothing dies, it is only overpriced. His test looks at price, not medium, and his 2017 examples were iHeartRadio drive-time live reads and Facebook as a top-of-funnel platform, both underpriced. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=1395s)
- In 2017 he said it is not about what works but about what is underpriced and overpriced. Facebook ads can do what direct mail does and convert four times more for the same spend, and TV still works but is overpriced compared to 1974. (Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=675s)
- In 2020 an article on his site defined underpriced attention as platforms and areas that most people who make advertising decisions do not yet fully understand. (What Is Underpriced Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-underpriced-attention/)
- In 2023 he said the framework covers a Super Bowl ad, a carousel ad, an emerging TikTok influencer for a car wash or a LinkedIn post for a SaaS firm, and that you can waste money on digital as easily as on a billboard. (GaryVee wants your attention w/ VaynerX's Gary Vaynerchuk Masters of Scale Complete at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbBO9tjw9bc&t=270s; GaryVee wants your attention w/ VaynerX's Gary Vaynerchuk Masters of Scale Complete at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gbBO9tjw9bc&t=225s)
- In 2024 he said every action a business takes to get customers is underpriced, properly priced or overpriced, so be great at underpriced and stay away from overpriced. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 22:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1350s)
- In 2024 he said the strategy changes day to day and week to week, so a good strategy today will not work for the next year. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=1125s)
- In 2026 he said attention can be very overpriced and very underpriced, that there is no general rule, and that what matters is attention per behavior in the world we live in today. (If You're Not Posting You're Falling Behind at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TDWFU_uEexc&t=585s)

## The cycle of first movers, land grabs and saturation

- In 2015 he said marketers ruin everything: something new arrives for people, marketers see where attention is and move in, which ruins it, and then they move on. He includes himself among them. (Marketers are About to Ruin These 3 Ultra-Effective Tactics, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/marketers-are-about-to-ruin-these-3-ultra-effective-tactics/)
- In 2015 he said that is why being a first mover matters, since early movers get a bigger return just by being there before others catch on. (Being a First Mover is Easy. Here's How, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/being-a-first-mover-is-easy-heres-how/)
- In 2015 he said open rates on his email list fell from 91 percent to 35 percent as attention got scarcer. In another 2015 piece he said they fell from the high eighties to about twenty percent because the channel filled with sellers. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1665s; What Two Decades of First-Mover Advantage Have Taught Me, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-two-decades-of-first-mover-advantage-have-taught-me/)
- In 2019 he said his biggest career regret is not spending more on wine terms when AdWords was underpriced, and that Amazon was the biggest AdWords advertiser then. (How Facebook Advertising Can Work For You 2020 Strategy, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/facebook-advertising-marketing-strategies-for-2019/)
- In 2016 he said the pattern repeats, underpriced then a land grab then noise, and people debating whether Snapchat is a fad will arrive in 24 months when it is harder and noisier. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 53:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=3195s)
- In 2021 he said the cycle is the same on every platform: early movers extract the biggest audiences, big brands wait about three years and then throw real money at it, and the free attention gets scarcer for everyone else. (35 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Sage Summit 2021 at 10:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie_pcLsdb5E&t=630s)
- In 2021 he said that when an attention arbitrage exists the world changes, with P&G the top television advertiser in TV's first 10 years and Amazon the top AdWords advertiser from 2001 to 2005. (35 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Sage Summit 2021 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ie_pcLsdb5E&t=675s)
- In 2017 he said he gets loud when he sees an arbitrage at scale and quiet when it closes, loud in 2009 and 2010, quiet in 2011 to 2013 and louder again from 2014. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=855s)
- In 2019 he said "The host always wins the land grab", and that owning the medium where your clients hang out is an unbelievable arbitrage for B2B. (4 Key B2B Marketing Strategies, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-b2b-marketing-strategy-for-2019/)
- In 2016 he said too much noise makes people stop paying attention, which is why Facebook won, Gmail added a priority tab and Twitter struggled, and it all hinges on supply and demand of attention. (Instagram Change Sends Everyone Into a Frenzy But It's Not That Serious, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagram-change-sends-everyone-into-a-frenzy-but-its-not-that-serious/)

## How he reads old media against new

- In 2011 he said marketing for 150 years was push and for the first time it is pull, because customers now have a voice and a reaction. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=495s)
- In 2013 he said online video was in the first inning of a 16-inning game and that Google wiring cities would lead to the ultimate destruction of traditional TV viewing. (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/)
- In 2013 he said being covered by the New York Times or the Today show is worth less than 15 years earlier, and will keep diluting. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 55:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=3330s)
- In 2014 he said the phone is the first screen and TV is the second, since people pick up their phones when an ad comes on. (The Phone isn't the Second Screen. It's the First One, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-phone-isnt-the-second-screen-its-the-first-one/)
- In 2015 he said marketers count impressions, not attention, so 40,000 views of a video is untrue when people clicked to another tab. He said Nielsen counts viewers against ads even though people reach for their phones at the break. (When Will Marketers Talk About Attention Not Impressions, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/when-will-marketers-talk-about-attention-not-impressions/)
- In 2015 he said he follows the attention graph, not a media type, which is why he also said Super Bowl ads were underpriced. (#AskGaryVee Episode 78: Marketing for Musicians Urinals & Facebook Video, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-78-marketing-for-musicians-urinals-facebook-video/)
- In 2015 he said that what matters in print is the message, meaning the words, the articles, the writing and the images, and not the vehicle. In 2017 he listed drive-time radio becoming podcasts and movie stars becoming Instagram influencers. (If You're Trying to Save Print You're Missing the Point, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/if-youre-trying-to-save-print-youre-missing-the-point/; It's The Message Not The Medium, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/message-not-medium/)
- In 2015 he also argued for print and TV in certain cases, despite being known for preaching Facebook, Instagram and Twitter. (Maybe You Should Get Into Print and TV…, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/maybe-you-should-get-into-print-and-tv/)
- In 2016 he said the telephone is becoming the television and the television is becoming the radio, that it is the first platform shift in half a century, and that he has spent the last five years trying to be the best storyteller for that platform. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 6:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=360s)
- In 2017 he told Joe Rogan his thesis that the phone is the television and TV is the radio, and that going back to 1948 to 1957 to see which brands, media companies and personalities won in the move from radio to TV lets you make bets on the phone. (Joe Rogan Experience #910 - Gary Vaynerchuk at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OLHdzLpMg-s&t=1170s)
- In 2016 he said big brands spend 80 billion dollars making and distributing commercials that nobody in America watches because people fast forward them, and that people care about health, money, family and, fastest growing in value, time. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=2025s)
- In 2017 he said ABC, CBS and NBC are finished because their infrastructure costs do not match consumer behavior, and viewers will go to Netflix, Hulu and Amazon. (Gary Vaynerchuk on Why Perspective Will Make or Break You Impact Theory at 42:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-lITalyctN4&t=2565s)
- In 2017 he said advertisers who spend millions assume they are getting attention, and "When you assume, you lose." He said he knows the actual consumption of a 30-second spot in a show break is quite low. (Don't Take Attention For Granted, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/taking-attention-granted/)
- In 2018 he said that as TV was a good deal for Procter and Gamble and radio was a good deal in 1937, the good deal now in his opinion is the seven platforms. (How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=180s)
- In 2019 he said a Super Bowl commercial is worth $10 million or more, and that the return is there even at $4 million or $5 million for a short spot. (Why Super Bowl Commercials Should Cost More Than They Do, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/super-bowl-commercials-should-cost-more-than-they-do/)
- In 2019 he said Super Bowl commercials are the most underpriced ad buy in all of advertising because everyone's attention is on them. (Why Every Small Business Should Have a Super Bowl Commercial, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-every-small-business-should-have-a-super-bowl-commercial/; What Is Underpriced Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-underpriced-attention/)
- In 2019 he said print, direct mail, radio and TV might become a deal when the market collapses, such as remnant billboards at $2,000 instead of $34,000, and that he is unemotional about it. (50 Minutes of Marketing Strategy You Can Start to Use Today Digital Agency Expo Keynote at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Wcg15FaUnU&t=900s)
- In 2019 he said underpriced attention at scale sat in YouTube pre-roll, after arbitrage in outdoor, direct mail and TV. In 2020 he said Facebook ads, YouTube pre-rolls and Spotify host-read ads were underpriced and Twitter appropriately priced. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=855s; How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=45s)
- In 2021 he said the one strength of traditional advertising for 70 years was stealing attention, and that TV commercials now sit in friction with the market. He said he had not done the research himself and works from common sense and culture. (Advertising Through The Years: The Evolution of Attention, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/advertising-through-the-years-the-evolution-of-attention/)
- In 2023 he said television is not efficient reach and is a waste of money. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=2430s)
- In 2024 he said big companies still spend heavily on TV, outdoor, print and banners because their internal reports say attention is there, which he called boardroom reporting from people who do not practice social. (How To Win At Social Media l Modern Wisdom Podcast at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w5_fVxGLzjw&t=90s)

## Where he said attention sat, year by year

- In 2015 he said Snapchat Discover was handing partners the under-25 demo, with figures he gave as 71 percent under 25 and 100 million monthly users. (Here's Why You're Not Excited Enough About Snapchat's Discover, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/heres-why-youre-not-excited-enough-about-snapchats-discover/)
- In 2015 he said platforms age up with their users, as Facebook went from college kids to moms and grandparents, and that people do not leave forms of communication behind. (The Snapchat Ghost Is Growing Up: Why You Need To Pay Attention to Snapchat, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-snapchat-ghost-is-growing-up-why-you-need-to-pay-attention-to-snapchat/)
- In 2016 he said Snapchat held the most consumer attention he had seen since Twitter in 2007 and 2008, with 900 of 1,000 followers watching a story against email opens of at best 25 percent. (Deploying Against the Snap: Takeaways from Snapchatting in Vegas & Ces, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/deploying-against-the-snap-takeaways-from-snapchatting-in-vegas-ces/)
- In 2016 he said what matters is not whether these platforms will go away but how much attention a platform has and what that means for your business right now. (Will Snapchat and Musical.ly be Around in 6 Months? Who Cares, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/will-snapchat-and-musical-ly-be-around-in-6-months-who-cares/)
- In 2016 he called Mark Zuckerberg the "greatest trader in consumer attention of all time" for trying to buy Snapchat after buying Instagram. (The Snap Generation: A Guide to Snapchat's History, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-snap-generation-a-guide-to-snapchats-history/)
- In 2017 he said audio is the next major shift in consumer attention, ahead of AR, VR and AI, and that 16 to 30 year olds listen to podcasts instead of music on commutes. (The Rise of Audio & Voice, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/rise-audio-voice/)
- In 2017 he said audio wins because it is passive consumption, which respects time, and that he ranks time with health, money and religion as what people care about most. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=1080s; Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=1170s)
- In 2017 he predicted that in 2018 and 2019 technology would move people to consume more radio-like audio, like the 1930s to 60s. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=1170s)
- In 2021 he said podcasting is a must, not a maybe, and that Clubhouse continued the audio resurgence he had talked about for years. (How to Start a Podcast: Complete Step-by-Step Guide, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-start-a-podcast/; What is Clubhouse app and why does it matter, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-clubhouse-app-and-why-does-it-matter/)
- In 2019 he said he does not care if TikTok is gone in 6, 9, 12 or 24 months, and that he is thankful TikTok and LinkedIn arrived, because he would have been irrelevant if the place where people spend attention never moved. (Strike While It's Hot: It Doesn't Matter If A Platform Is Around In A Year, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/strike-while-its-hot-it-doesnt-matter-if-a-platform-is-around-in-a-year/; How to Take Full Advantage of the Internet in 2020 Aime Fuse Keynote 2019 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wavJwpJA3VU&t=270s)
- In 2020 he said that if TikTok were banned its minutes would go to TV, audio, Instagram Reels and other apps, and that attention builds on whatever is there while it is there. (What You Should Think About if TikTok Gets Banned, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-you-should-think-about-if-tiktok-gets-banned/)
- In 2023 he said people now search on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube much more than they did five or seven years ago, and that used to be Google's. (Don't Live For the Algorithm Live For Your Happiness GaryVee Audio Experience at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ft8R-b-3NXM&t=1800s)
- In 2024 he said live-streaming is an arbitrage for exposure, community and sales, in the same underestimated position social media held in 2010 to 2015. (2 things I'm focused on for 2024: Pbcpg & Live-Streaming, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-things-im-focused-on-for-2024/)
- In 2025 he said social media stopped being social about three years earlier and became **interest media**, where content comes to you without following anyone. (One Of The Biggest Opportunities On Social Media & AI In 2025 GaryVee Keynote Exma Mexico at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=inv3cuQEIJc&t=180s)
- In 2025 he said the feed shows what you are currently into, not who you follow, and that the interest graph always interested him more than the social graph. (AI + Social Media = The Biggest Opportunity of Our Lifetime at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OQwHXDgKaT8&t=585s)
- In 2026 he said social media died four years earlier and became interest media, and that before 1990 attention needed a big advertising budget while now an individual can build a very large business. (Social Media Is Dead at 0:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWqpfx2iM2g&t=0s; Social Media Is Dead at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWqpfx2iM2g&t=135s)
- In 2026 he said that if 99 percent of views are bots, people keep posting because results still come from the human 1 percent, and that platforms compete with sleep, the gym and Netflix. (AI is Ending the Social Media Era and What Comes Next Gary Vee - Sinead Bovell at 3:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUvWVF28CNM&t=225s; AI is Ending the Social Media Era and What Comes Next Gary Vee - Sinead Bovell at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sUvWVF28CNM&t=495s)

## What he tells businesses to do

- In 2023 he said to go where the consumer's attention is, even if you dislike the platform, because disliking TikTok or Instagram only costs you customers. (The Ultimate Social Media Marketing Strategy at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmA-LrQzcY&t=315s)
- In 2017 he said "The Market is The Market is The Market", and that you do not get to dictate where the market places its attention, you just have to observe and react. (What Makes Me a Great CEO, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/makes-great-ceo/)
- In 2023 he said a marketer must be agnostic and unemotional, and that people fail from holding an ideology about where people should be, such as kids should not be on phones. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=1710s)
- In 2016 he said to ask what you do as a consumer, since nobody clicks a mobile banner on purpose, and that living one way and marketing another leads out of business. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=1800s)
- In 2019 he said to follow what human beings are doing right now, and that he built his dad's liquor store from three to sixty million dollars in five years by spending properly even though the data showed direct mail, newspapers and radio would work better than email and Google search. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=405s)
- In 2019 he gave the **79/21 rule**: about 79 percent of time on the platform bringing the most value, about 21 percent tasting others, so you are ready when one moves up. (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-79-21-rule-to-build-a-long-term-business/; Strike While It's Hot: It Doesn't Matter If A Platform Is Around In A Year, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/strike-while-its-hot-it-doesnt-matter-if-a-platform-is-around-in-a-year/)
- In 2019 he said diversifying attention is the only way to a long-term business, since people on only the biggest platform disappear when attention shifts, as MySpace bands and Vine stars did. (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-79-21-rule-to-build-a-long-term-business/)
- In 2019 he warned against getting high on your own supply, since if one search term or ad buy is 63 percent of your business it eventually breaks, as it did for the lawyer who dominated the Yellow Pages. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=1530s)
- In 2017 he said there are only two ways to be seen, buying ads and hacking culture, and that distribution is what people underestimate. (Having a Media Company Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/media-company-mentality/)
- In 2017 he said one shout-out from one influencer could be just as valuable as, or more valuable than, all the money you spend in ads, because it is just distribution. (Having a Media Company Mentality, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/media-company-mentality/)
- In 2015 he said micro celebrities on Instagram had a 24 to 36 month window. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1845s)
- In 2017 he said that when influencers and KOLs start charging the proper price for product placement and endorsement, it will not be as fun of an opportunity. (Marketing In 2018 Brand Minds Keynote Singapore 2017 DailyVee 337 at 13:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WdTscOvoBnY&t=810s)
