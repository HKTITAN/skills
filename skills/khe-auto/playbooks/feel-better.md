# "Make the app feel better"

**You own this from a vague complaint to a list of changes made.** Subtract, then retune, then add. Retuning a motion you are about to delete wastes the pass, and additions are judged fairly only once the noise is gone.

1. **Remove** with `remove-animations`. Exit: a list of what went, and a list of what stayed with the purpose that saved each.
2. **Retune what stayed** with `improve-animations`. Exit: plans with exact values, a file and a line each.
3. **Add, sparingly** with `find-animation-opportunities`. Five to seven at most, with the rejected candidates listed.
4. **Finish** with `khe-ui`, polish-and-ship lane: the missing states and the optical details.

If the complaint is "feels off on my phone", run `mobile-native` before any of this. A tap delay or a stuck hover reads as bad motion and is not.

**Reply:** removed, retuned, added and kept, each with its reason, and what needs a real device to feel-check.
