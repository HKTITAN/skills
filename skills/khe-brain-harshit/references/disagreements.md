# Disagreements

Most of his changes of mind are small: a word he first rejected and then used, a tool he praised and then doubted, a plan stated firmly and then reopened. On larger views, such as AI as an extension of the mind, he stays consistent across 2025-26. Several entries are not reversals but two posts telling the same Smash&Clash fact with different numbers. In each case the later post is the one to use unless the line says otherwise.
