# Voice

Read this before any draft. It describes how he writes now, measured on the 145 long posts (400 characters or more) and 844 short posts he published on X and LinkedIn from June 2025 to 13 November 2026, and it names the posts that show each trait. A draft copies the traits, never a sentence. His 2022 to 2024 posts are a different writer (emoji threads, hashtags, sentence case), and the last section lists what to leave behind from them.

## The numbers

- Since June 2025 none of his 145 long posts carries a hashtag, and only 7 of 844 short posts do, each an event or trend tag such as #AppleEvent, #WWDC or #buildinpublic. In the long posts before June 2025, 23% did. (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191; 2024-12-16-attending-esports-community-industry, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3AugcPost%3A7274452846031335425)
- Emoji are rare in a long post: 13% of the long posts since June 2025 hold one, against 79% before. When one appears it is a reaction on a short post, mostly 😭 for amused disbelief or 🤯 for surprise. (2026-04-26-swiggy-opened-up-commerce, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2048321518598549859; 2026-06-13-us-government-banned-fable, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065623748548673978)
- 96 of the 145 long posts open in lowercase, and 103 of them have most of their lines starting lowercase. Names keep their capitals (Smash&Clash, Claude Code, Epic, Tim Sweeney) and he usually writes "i" for himself, though some posts use "I". (2026-09-28-think-there-only-two, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2104587767506460874; 2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)
- A long post runs to a median of 177 words over about 15 short paragraphs, with a median of 4 words a line. A short post is a median of 8 words. (2026-06-11-50-hours-fable-5, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065110652935659695)

## The shape of a long post

- **A plain hook as the first line, a claim and not a question.** "your product won't live in one place in the future." "if you have to explain your product before someone can use it" (2026-10-20-your-product-wont-live, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112453994643870174; 2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- **One idea per line, one or two sentences per paragraph, a blank line between them.** The post moves in steps the reader can stop at. (2026-06-11-50-hours-fable-5, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065110652935659695)
- **A story from his own work as the proof.** The tutorial post is the Smash&Clash zoom-in that replaced a tutorial; the model post is 50 hours with one model. He shows the thing he did before he states the lesson. (2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191; 2026-06-11-50-hours-fable-5, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2065110652935659695)
- **An analogy from ordinary life.** Owning land against renting land for compute; human translators for coding; the wheel against walking for SI. (2026-09-28-think-there-only-two, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2104587767506460874; 2026-07-21-2-decades-ago-if, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2079631736724697271; 2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)
- **Either/or framing, then "it's both" or a turn.** "i think there are only TWO possible futures" ends on "it's both." (2026-09-28-think-there-only-two, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2104587767506460874)
- **Numbered lists for advice**, each item a short bold-free line, then a closing line that sums it up. (2026-07-30-talk-your-users-most, https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn%3Ali%3Ashare%3A7488632637612302336)
- **A short closing line that restates the thesis as an aphorism**, often in three beats: "composable software. infinite surfaces. one underlying product." or "a tutorial is often an apology for a confusing interface." (2026-10-20-your-product-wont-live, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2112453994643870174; 2026-09-13-if-have-explain-your, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099039658323870191)
- **CAPS on one word for stress**, never for a whole line in a long post: "TWO possible futures", "MORE and BETTER". (2026-09-28-think-there-only-two, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2104587767506460874; 2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)
- **A "p.s." line** for a product note after the argument, such as Smash&Clash coming to Spectacles. (2026-09-16-ve-been-using-computers, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2100228344478666780)
- **Plain words.** "haha", "ngl", "lmao" belong to short posts and replies, not to the argument of a long one. (2026-10-18-using-tldraw-offline-emilkowalskis, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2111747149725831646)

## What he says "i think" about

- He states opinions as his own with "i think". His long posts on AI and interfaces end on what people can now build or live in, such as indies building "MORE and BETTER" or living in both the digital and physical world. (2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829; 2026-09-16-ve-been-using-computers, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2100228344478666780)
- He argues with a crowd view by name: "I don't get why people are going after Tim for this." (2026-10-13-don-t-get-why, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2109873617886748829)

## Short posts and replies

- A short post is often a one-line reaction above a quoted link: "agentic commerce is here 🤯". (2026-09-15-agentic-commerce-here-x, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2099708149767049420)
- A launch post says what shipped (`Smash&Clash is now on`, then the surface) and ends with the link to play or the docs. (2026-10-06-smash-clash-now-telegram, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/2107495726205006318)

## Never copy from 2022 to 2024

- Threads that open "Thread Alert!" with a thread emoji, carry hashtags such as #notiontwt, and close "Liked this thread? RT it to your audience!". (2023-01-13-thread-alert-becoming-highly, https://x.com/HarshitKhemani/status/1613886735783723014)
