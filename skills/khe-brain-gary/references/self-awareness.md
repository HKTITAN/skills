# Self-awareness

This file collects what he said about self-awareness, betting on your strengths, and the weaknesses he admits. It draws on articles and keynotes from 2015 onward and, from 2021, a series of long articles and talks on insecurity, candor and self-esteem. Read his advice as one person's point of view; his figures about his own companies are his own.

## What he means by self-awareness and why he ranks it first

- In late 2015 he called self-awareness the greatest gift a person can have, said he had pushed it publicly for 18 months, and promised that making it your 2016 priority would make you happier and more successful. (4 New Year's Resolutions You Should Make to Improve Your Career, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/new-years-resolutions-career/)
- In 2013 he called it his biggest gift and told people who are struggling to know themselves, keep climbing, and partner with people who are naturally good at what they are not. (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/)
- In 2016 he said that if you do not know what you are good at you spin your wheels, that you must be honest when nobody is watching, and that you must be your own number one fan. (Inside My Heart Brain And Soul In 13 Minutes DailyVee 101 at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnfyyABag3s&t=495s)
- In 2016 he said the main thing is to know what you are good at and put your energy there, and that this applies to a fundraiser, a stay-at-home dad or a radio host as much as to an entrepreneur. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=270s)
- In 2016, asked whether EQ or IQ matters more, he said the answer is self-awareness: a CEO, leader or operator must know their strength and double down on it, and for him that strength is EQ. (My In-Depth Look at Emotional Intelligence, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/depth-look-emotional-intelligence/)
- In 2017 he told a Dutch Bros audience that self-awareness drives the biggest success, noted that many ambitious people talk big while their content shows Netflix and Friday concerts, and asked whether you know who you are and whether your words match your actions. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=1710s)
- In 2017 he said his career has been one long game of self-awareness: at 22 he saw himself as a great salesman, not an operator, leader or content creator. (How Successful People Think, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/successful-people-think/)
- In 2018 he called it the coolest thing in the game, ahead of sneakers, a hoodie, money or being a crypto winner, and defined it as knowing yourself and putting yourself in the best position to succeed. (42 Years of My Life in 42 Minutes Interview with Drama at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MQ-4mr6q4Ak&t=315s)
- In 2014 he said he did not think empathy, gratitude or self-awareness can be taught, ranked self-awareness a close third behind the other two, and said it has to be discovered. (This is the One Thing I Would Teach the World, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-one-thing-i-would-teach-the-world/)
- In 2017 he said there is no single unlock for self-awareness, EQ, IQ or gratitude; after seven, eight, ten years of looking he thinks there are 371 of them and he will use all of them, including telling someone they are lazy. (Business is My Vehicle, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/business-is-my-vehicle/)
- In 2023 he called success a self-awareness game between two myths, that it is all grind or all luck, and said the answer is talent and work ethic deployed with self-awareness. (Discovering New Passions When Dreams Fade l AskGaryVee 336 at 0:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qJzCLL0jv0Q&t=45s)
- In 2026 he said success correlates most with self-awareness and self-acceptance, not strategy: strategy is school, and most people fail to execute because they are not good with themselves and run out of energy. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 58:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=3510s)
- In 2024 he said he does not put entrepreneurs above franchisees, employees or police officers, and does not put mom over dad; he puts self-awareness on the pedestal, and many people who think they must start their own thing are built to excel as a franchisee. (57 Minutes of Business Tactics & Lessons l Podcast With Friends Ep 15 at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tQV_G4SfdKY&t=315s)

## Betting on strengths instead of fixing weaknesses

- In 2013 he said he is weak at grammar, reading and being a student, talks only about what he is good at, and gets a better long-term result that way. (My Biggest Weakness: Q&A, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/my-biggest-weakness-qa/)
- In 2015 he said people waste 30 to 40 years checking boxes for things they are not good at, and that once you believe who you are you should push all your chips in. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=270s)
- In 2015 he called betting on strengths possibly the most underrated strategy in modern business, because we are obsessed with correcting weaknesses; he limits it to business and says weaknesses in personal and family life deserve care. (5 Things You Need To Do When Hiring Someone, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-things-you-need-to-do-when-hiring-someone/)
- In 2016 he said to triple down on strengths and would rather be "dangerous" in weak areas like finance than world-class, because that leaves room to be world-class at what comes naturally, which has more upside. (MastermindTalks Keynote 2016 Gary Vaynerchuk at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O6SeKH5Rdl4&t=450s)
- In 2016 he said running a business is a talent like singing or basketball; he called himself an NBA All-Star as an entrepreneur and a below-average rec-league basketball player, and said you can only become the best version of what you are naturally good at. (Gary Vaynerchuk 92Y Talk With Stephanie Ruhle at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPKzoah6A4c&t=180s)
- In 2017 he said he will never be the Jets quarterback however hard he works, so you should not care about what you are bad at and should put yourself where your strengths win. (The Straightest Road to Success: Work Hard and Smart, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/straightest-road-success/)
- In 2017 he said to fix a weakness only enough that it is not fatal, citing that he cannot read well yet speaks to 800,000 people, and that he sold baseball cards at 10 for 3,000 dollars a weekend while failing spelling. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=1800s)
- In 2017 he described failing a 4th-grade science exam, hiding the test under his bed and deciding to accept the punishment and teasing four times a year to find his zone, which he calls the most important decision of his life. (What It's Like to Do What You Love, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-its-like-to-do-what-you-love/)
- In 2018 he called going all in on your one or two strengths his core thesis on self-awareness, as long as your intent is pure. (The Cream Rises To The Top in Coffee and in Personal Branding Veecap, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/cream-rises-top-coffee-personal-branding-veecap/)
- In 2019 he said nobody is broken; everyone is bad at a lot and good at a couple, and if one of the things you are good at is something you like, you are off to the races. (How to Build Confidence and Self-esteem Aubrey Marcus Podcast w/ GaryVee at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzEkg9aM4Hk&t=1035s)
- In 2022 he said most people fixate on getting decent at what they are naturally bad at; he punted blogging in 2002 to 2005 because he cannot write, even though he was right about ecommerce, Google AdWords and email. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 56:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=3375s)
- In 2026 he said one or two or three things you are not as good at eat up all the oxygen, but every one of you is great at something. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=2385s)

## How to find out what you are good at

- In 2015 he said everybody lies to themselves, and that you have to audit who you actually are. (Entrepreneurship Keynote Gary Vaynerchuk at USC 2015 at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z5tugxy70MY&t=90s)
- In 2015 he gave four steps to find your strengths, the first being to take the five to ten people who know you best, split them into two categories and ask one from each what you are best and worst at. (4 Ways To Find Out What Your Strengths Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-ways-to-find-out-what-your-strengths-are/)
- In 2015 he said to take the five to ten people who know you best, split them into those you love deeply and those you are close to but differ from, and ask one from each what you are best and worst at. (4 Ways To Find Out What Your Strengths Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-ways-to-find-out-what-your-strengths-are/)
- In 2016 he said to ask your mom, dad, spouse, friend or coworker what you are good and bad at, and spend a month or two getting them comfortable, because people who love you do not want to tell you. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=270s)
- In 2017 he suggested a dinner where the five people closest to you spend three hours saying what you are good and bad at, because most people are too kind to say it. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 33:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=1980s)
- In 2024 he called the same idea a self intervention: invite two or three close people, ask for the full truth about you, invite follow-up one on one the next day, and he guessed only 3 to 11 listeners would do it. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 1:00:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=3645s)
- In 2021 he said one of the best ways to build self-awareness is to make the people closest to you feel safe enough to tell you the truth about their observations of you, and that another is to pay attention to what you like. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Self-Awareness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-self-awareness/)
- In 2021 he said to ask what comes naturally, what you love, what is in your DNA and what you would do if no one was looking, and not to force yourself to like things. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Self-Awareness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-self-awareness/)
- In 2018 he said you figure yourself out by trying things, and that lack of self-awareness is enormous. (Practical Business and Marketing Advice for Dominating 2019 Keynote at NAC at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lWiLQGEqqmo&t=1125s)
- In 2026 he said to ask two questions, are you good at it and do you like it, and that too many chase what they wish they were or where the money is. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=1305s)

## Insecurity, delusion and other people's voices

- In 2017 he said the opinions that hold people back come from parents, siblings and spouses more than from trolls, that there is a fine line between self-esteem and delusion, and that he documents so much to be an "injection of audacity". (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=990s)
- In 2016 he said not to let anyone define who you are and to "execute against you", and that self-awareness is how you define yourself: play to your strengths and respect your weaknesses. (Nobody Can Define Who You Are, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/nobody-can-define-who-you-are/)
- In 2018 he said the voice telling you that you suck is often your mom or dad putting it in your head, not your own. (Gary Vee on 6ix9ine's Trolling Millennials Fortnite and Dressing The Part - The Cruz Show at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EqvIbndsoxw&t=1530s)
- In 2021 he named delusion as the first self-awareness trap: delusional people critique everyone else instead of looking inward, so they overestimate strengths and underestimate weaknesses. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Self-Awareness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-self-awareness/)
- In 2021 he named overcompensation as the second trap: people who know they are weak somewhere prop themselves up, for example with CEO in an Instagram bio, and someone obsessed with titles inside their own company is likely covering insecurity. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Self-Awareness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-self-awareness/)
- In 2021 he said not to base ambitions on other people's goals or on insecurity, and that if you do not like risk, managing people or the barrage of no's you should rethink wanting to be CEO. (Ambition Patience and the Insecurity Trap: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/ambition-patience-and-the-insecurity-trap-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2020 he said self-esteem and self-awareness need to be important words in society, because you can work hard all you want but if you are insecure you are vulnerable, and the second there is pushback you will not go there. (How to Get Over Your Fear of Judgment on the Internet Senior Bowl Summit Keynote 2020 at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHHl35TBHQc&t=945s)
- In 2024 he told NYU graduates the most important move is listening to no one but yourself, and that there are two ways to the top, extreme insecurity and extreme confidence, and only confidence lasts. (The Ultimate College Graduation Speech 2024 l NYU Stern Business School at 8:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGEScShDdvs&t=495s; The Ultimate College Graduation Speech 2024 l NYU Stern Business School at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jGEScShDdvs&t=675s)
- In 2023 he said you practice self-confidence by not valuing others' opinions over your own and by putting yourself out there, for example saying hello to more people, and that the voice saying you suck was put there by someone else. (How To Deal With The Fear of Making The Wrong Decisions l VeeCon 2023 Closing Keynote at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09Pf5sIBNqk&t=1305s; How To Deal With The Fear of Making The Wrong Decisions l VeeCon 2023 Closing Keynote at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=09Pf5sIBNqk&t=1395s)
- In 2019 he said ego is insecurity and that he thinks he has enormous confidence with no ego, crediting parents and being gifted things that are not him. (ABtalks with Gary Vee - Chapter 12 at 30:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mxgQRJ1SwZ8&t=1845s)
- In 2025 he said 99 percent of people are "uncomfortably trickable" because of insecurity, and that he prefers to win with the 1 percent who are not, since the difference is security, not intelligence. (This Will Make You Insanely Rich The Next 365 Days Will Make Millionaires - The Iced Coffee Hour at 34:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dgg8XGgDLQ4&t=2070s)
- In 2026 he said humility ends insecurity because nobody is thinking about you, and that you should learn not to react to either compliments or insults. (40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=720s; 40 Things You Need To Hear Before You Turn 40 at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7oFoc-8n-KQ&t=180s)

## Self-awareness for founders, leaders and employees

- In 2016 he said to do what you are best at and surround yourself with the other three or four pillars, asking whether you are a salesman, an accountant, an operations or HR person.
