Benji Taylor wrote "pointing beats describing." as the closing thesis of an essay on giving visual feedback to coding agents, published 16 January 2026 (Annotating for agents). The argument behind it: precision drops as feedback moves from seeing a thing to describing it, so the harder a thing is to describe, the more it is worth pointing at (Annotating for agents). Five days later the tool built on that idea was released as Agentation (Agentation).
