# Kindness and empathy

This file collects what he said about kindness, empathy and gratitude in his business. Most of the file comes from his own articles of 2015 to 2023, including the Road to Twelve and a Half series of 2021, and from keynotes and long interviews of 2017 to 2026. Check the year on each bullet. Figures and stories about his companies are his own account.

## Why does he call kindness and empathy a strength in business?

- In 2013 he said vulnerability, empathy, self-awareness and kindness are attractive on social media, and that people on Twitter try to be witty when they should be human. (Get Off Your Ass & Get Noticed with Gary Vaynerchuk Chase Jarvis Live ChaseJarvis at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BT_vv5moEm8&t=1755s)
- In 2016 he said empathy is more than caring: it is understanding people at a higher level, and he credits it for much of his success and for making him a great salesperson. (Empathy: One of the Keys to My Business Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/empathy-one-of-the-keys-to-my-business-success/)
- In 2016 he said his empathy is both genuine care and leverage, and that he is fine with both because being good is always the best option. (Empathy: One of the Keys to My Business Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/empathy-one-of-the-keys-to-my-business-success/)
- In 2016 he said empathy is a sales tool as well as a kindness: if you know what the other person is thinking and what their KPI is, you can work back from it. (SXSW GaryVee Keynote Austin 2016 at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h67-oxooaoM&t=450s)
- In 2017 he called empathy the secret drug of success, for a boss and for a consumer, and said he took it from his mother and his competitiveness from his father. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 40:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=2430s)
- In 2017 he said being nice is ROI positive and wins the marathon, while politicking wins only the sprint. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=1170s)
- In 2018 he said he realized only in the previous ten days that kindness is probably a real big reason it all worked out, beyond hustle, sales or empathy. (Why Kindness is the Key to Winning in Business at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7j8SRRqQxss&t=900s)
- In 2018 he said entrepreneurship rewards decent people more than corporate life does, because the more entrepreneurial the game, the fairer it is. He says a big, lasting, admired business needs kind and talented people. (Why Kindness is the Key to Winning in Business at 17:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7j8SRRqQxss&t=1035s)
- In 2018 he predicted that kindness would be the theme of his content that year and said he would keep tapping into it. (Why Kindness is the Key to Winning in Business at 15:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7j8SRRqQxss&t=945s)
- In 2018 he wrote that people who see kindness as negative are insecure, that you can only be kind from a place of leverage, and that it is a big advantage for naturally tough people. (The Best Business Strategy No One Talks About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-business-strategy-no-one-talks-about/)
- In 2019 he wrote that the best leadership qualities are maternal, not paternal, and that a caring, empathetic, understanding personality suits a leader better than something stern, paternal or aggressive. (Management Strategies from the CEO of a $200 Million Company, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/management-strategies-and-tips/)
- In 2021 he said we have the hard things down in business and the soft things are harder to master, and that kindness is the foundation for why soft skills matter. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)
- In 2021 he said we think of kindness as a nice to have when really it is a must, because kindness can impact your business and career as a whole. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)
- In 2021 he said there is not a statement on earth he hates more than the idea that nice guys finish last. (The best way to be successful in 2022 Compass Real Estate Keynote at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjNq7jMUyWc&t=2115s)
- In 2021 he said his ability to call things early, from up-and-coming social media platforms to good investments, is not about magic or luck but about empathy. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Empathy, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-empathy/)
- In 2020 he described VaynerX as a **honey empire**, said he is uncomfortable with deep negativity, and said you do not have to be mean to succeed. (Why You Should Put Your Employees First: A Conversation With Arthur Blank, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-should-put-your-employees-first-a-conversation-with-arthur-blank/)
- In 2023 he said his work ethic came from 12-hour days in his father's liquor store from age 14, but over time he came to feel that a lot of his success comes from the soft skills he was gifted predominantly by his mother. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 1:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=90s)
- In 2023 he said talented people who were not nice looked like winners at halftime over the last 15 years but lost overall, and that he is nice and plans to finish first. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=135s)
- In 2026 he said he is wildly unimpressed by business accomplishments, his own included, and that he views people based on their humanity. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 27:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=1620s)
- In 2026 he told a father whose son bought a Tesla at 17 that he wants to know how kind the son is, and said he knows plenty of millionaires who are awful. (Nobody Knows What This Is And That's the Point at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZoLFRqwT4vU&t=2385s)

## Is kindness the same as being a pushover?

- In 2019 he wrote that kindness is not being a pushover: it is a business strategy that he pairs with radical candor, always sharing his honest opinions even when not politically correct. (Why Nice Guys Finish Last is Just Not True, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-nice-guys-finish-last-is-just-not-true/)
- In 2019 he wrote that kindness with an ask attached loses, because it is a disguise to get something. (20 Kindness Quotes To Help You Win in Life, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/kindness-quotes/)
- In 2019 he wrote that if you give with no expectation you cannot be taken advantage of, because you are happy whatever happens. (Why Nice Guys Finish Last is Just Not True, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-nice-guys-finish-last-is-just-not-true/)
- In 2021 he quoted his book that you can be kind, candid and hold your ground at the same time, and that being cutthroat is not cool while kindness is the real flex. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)
- In 2021 he said you can be kind, respected, stand up for yourself and crush it in business, and called himself living proof. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)
- In 2021 he told a keynote room there is no good reason not to be kind, that a leader should stop pressure and not pass it down, and that people who say kindness gets you walked over are often manipulating. (The Ultimate Life Advice For Success and Happiness Edmonton Canada Keynote 2021 at 4:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz679oTO7Oo&t=270s)
- In 2021 he said kindness is not coddling: he hates eighth place trophies and entitlement, wants to dominate, and would start a competing brokerage in your town if you bothered him. (The best way to be successful in 2022 Compass Real Estate Keynote at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjNq7jMUyWc&t=2205s)
- In 2020 he said kindness and competitiveness can co-exist, and that his competitive heat can look aggressive in the moment. He credits his mother for the honey empire idea. (Why You Should Put Your Employees First: A Conversation With Arthur Blank, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-you-should-put-your-employees-first-a-conversation-with-arthur-blank/)
- In 2022 he said real kindness means giving without expectation, and that kindness with a hope of return is strategy. He told a reader who says everyone walks all over her that she may be manipulating to create leverage. (Why You Need to Make Even More Content in 2022 at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yemqSyqKqpY&t=405s)
- In 2026 he gave a test: when people say they get walked all over, ask whether you did it for someone or to get something in return. If you expected something back, he says, you were manipulating, not being nice. (Inside a Warehouse Where Real Money Is Made at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NiJvQBWJwvQ&t=1890s)
- In 2017 he said giving with expectation is double selfishness and the biggest mistake, because it kills everything. (Being Selfless by Being Selfish, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/being-selfless-by-being-selfish/)
- In 2022 he said the extremely selfless create subconscious resentment in the people they help, so the selfish should be a little more selfless and the selfless a little selfish with themselves. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=1260s)
- In 2023 he said he balances empathy with not caring about opinions easily: empathy is caring about feelings and not caring about opinions is being secure in your skin. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=2790s)
- In 2026 he said empathy is a gift and that he is a good salesman because he knows what you are thinking and feeling, and that you can do a lot of damage with that gift or a lot of good. (Social Media Is Dead at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RWqpfx2iM2g&t=1755s)

## What does he mean by giving first and the 51/49 split?

- In 2016 he said that if he could leave one line behind it would be 51/49: he wants to give everybody who comes across him 51 percent of the value, and that is how he lives his life. (The Ultimate Advice Every Entrepreneur Needs To Hear Gary Vee & Lewis Howes - School of Greatness at 50:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0NC2PBIWQr0&t=3015s)
- In 2016 he wrote that he prefers to give 51 percent of a relationship because he likes the leverage, and admitted it "might sound dark". (Giving Without Expectation, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/giving-without-expectation/)
- In 2016 he said his **51/49** approach is also his natural state, and told of buying his sister a Cabbage Patch doll at age 10 because it would make her happier than any toy for himself. (The Law of Attraction Importance of Sales Skills & Working Smarter AskGaryVee 232 at 7:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=R9GEnEFJtXE&t=450s)
- In 2016 he wrote that giving up front before the ask does not always work, and that being a good person first is the best option whatever the payout. (Giving Without Expectation, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/giving-without-expectation/)
- In 2017 he said bringing the most value gets value back, and that 51/49 is the entire game. (Don't Take Attention For Granted, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/taking-attention-granted/)
- In 2022 he said to go into every relationship aiming to give at least 51 percent of the value, and that most people think they have to get 80 percent, and that after the stimulus checks many think everybody owes them. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 52:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=3150s)
- In 2017 he told a keynote room that many people do not do the right thing because they expect something back, and that he is fine when someone does not deliver. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 2:20:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=8415s)
- In 2018 he wrote that he can keep giving because he does not expect anyone to reciprocate, which brings him more opportunities than he can handle. (The Best Business Strategy No One Talks About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-best-business-strategy-no-one-talks-about/)
- In 2019 he said that on the **jab, jab, jab, right hook** line, a gift followed by a request for his time is not his favorite, but he grants 20 minutes anyway. (Top Marketing Strategies That Will Help Your Business Get Attention RD Summit 2019 at 57:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6vCDlmhRmBo&t=3420s)
- In 2021 he said giving is different from manipulating: when he helps someone ten times bigger he starts from how he can help, and he can smell fake givers who want to ask within a week. (The #1 Thing Every Business Needs To Add to Their Strategy This Year at 39:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od-2xtAdaa0&t=2385s)
- In 2026 he said to volunteer 5 hours at a retirement home, because it helps lonely people and does more for you. (This One Episode Will Motivate You to Change Your Entire Life Gary Vee - Mel Robbins at 29:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g_wMpEGgc1Q&t=1755s)

## What does he say about kind candor, and where did he fail at it?

- In 2017 he admitted he is not good at radical candor and that a too-warm culture leaves money on the table. (Optimism Or Pessimism You Choose Dutch Bros Keynote In Portland Oregon 2017 DailyVee 316 at 2:12:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0mXH7TX80aY&t=7920s)
- In 2021 he said people assume candor comes easily to him because of his public style, but as a manager and operator he has struggled with it, and it has been his core weakness in 25 years of business. (Kind Candor and Why How You Deliver the Medicine Matters: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/kind-candor-and-why-how-you-deliver-the-medicine-matters-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2021 he said he hated candor in real life and avoided firing people for 25 years, even though his content is candid. (The Ultimate Life Advice For Success and Happiness Edmonton Canada Keynote 2021 at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cz679oTO7Oo&t=2115s)
- In 2021 he compared a good leader to a doctor with good bedside manner, and said to use more kindness when you are most upset. (Kind Candor and Why How You Deliver the Medicine Matters: Road to Twelve and a Half series, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/kind-candor-and-why-how-you-deliver-the-medicine-matters-road-to-twelve-and-a-half-series/)
- In 2022 he said he used to think candor created fear, so he avoided telling employees hard things for the first 20 years of his career, and now calls that a weakness. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 51:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=3060s)
- In 2022 he said that for a long time he saw candor as an excuse to be mean, the biggest weakness of his career. (How To Win The Mindset Game Greator Festival Keynote at 36:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eWFsQLtry08&t=2205s)
- In 2022 he described a **compassionate explanatory no**, like kind candor, which lands better than a bare no. His example was offering episode 101 after a fan reaches 100. (How To Say No Communication Techniques & Branding AskGaryVee 335 at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xo1mGiM86JU&t=135s)
- In 2023 he said he struggled with candor as an executive because he hates negativity, so he now tries to be empathetic and mentions something he has struggled with when giving hard feedback. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=1080s)
- In 2024 he said he thought candor was his strength and that he had to wake up in his mid-40s to realize it was his Kryptonite. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 25:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=1530s)
- In 2026 he said candor, which he once thought was his strength, was in private his Kryptonite, and that he is still working on it. (GaryVee's Most Honest Take on Ambition & Success at 37:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KVg3nJbDvh0&t=2250s)
- In 2018 he said his public persona is combative while his real life is full of kindness, and in 2021 he acknowledged that his aggressive style can hide how central kindness is to him. (Why Kindness is the Key to Winning in Business at 16:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7j8SRRqQxss&t=990s; Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)

## How does he say to treat customers, employees and colleagues?

- In 2015 he said that with a customer who is right you come with nothing but empathy, asking questions and listening before fixing: "Nobody ever talked their way out of a problem." (The Surefire Steps for Dealing With a Pissed-Off Customer, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-surefire-steps-for-dealing-with-a-pissed-off-customer/)
- In 2015 he told salespeople to spend a month or two not selling, listening and asking how they can help, because customers respect thoughtfulness. (Want to be a Better Salesperson? Take a Month Off, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-be-a-better-salesperson/)
- In 2017 he said empathy always wins and doing the right thing is always right, and that he is driven by impact, not dollars. (Put Yourself in Your Customers Shoes, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/put-customers-shoes/)
- In 2019 he said to give influencers personal value before asking, such as a custom jersey for a Jets fan, because personal touches and kindness improve your odds. (5 Tips for Growing Your Business With Influencer Marketing, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/5-tips-for-growing-your-business-with-influencer-marketing/)
- In 2024 he said lack of focus in marketing is a lack of consumer empathy, and that caring means respecting the consumer's time. (How To Build a Successful Brand In The Social Media World GaryVee Q&A Fireside Chat at 3:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EWk3_D1y-KA&t=180s)
- In 2024 he told a conference audience to leave gracious comments from the business account on local posts with no pitch, and to call or gift every customer from the last five years, such as a $50 dartboard for someone who loves darts. (Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 21:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=1305s; Complete Social Media Marketing Strategy For 2025 GaryVee Keynote at 27:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WyW_8kMQmJM&t=1665s)
- In 2021 he said that in a **Thank You Economy** you show your audience they are valued, which is good business and more importantly being a good human, and that thank you should be a main priority for any launch. (The First 30 Days of VeeFriends, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-first-30-days-of-veefriends/)
- In 2015 he asked whether he could help someone get another position outside the company over sixty days rather than firing them in one day, and said that costs a lot more money but does much more for company culture. (4 Easy Ways to Improve Company Culture, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/4-easy-ways-to-improve-company-culture/)
- In 2019 he said he cares more about how he feels about himself, and what people say of him as a man, than about company profit, so he helps people out and keeps relationships after they leave. (How to Fire Employees With Empathy and Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-fire-employees-with-empathy-and-kindness/)
- In 2016 he said he would say kudos if a former staff member left and beat VaynerMedia, and that he wants to be known for building a huge empire with honey on how he treated people. (The Single Best Episode in AskGaryVee History AskGaryVee Episode 229 at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7LomXO4Snqw&t=675s)
- In 2021 he said a client of nearly a decade fired VaynerMedia the day before and he did not make it hard on her, respecting that she chose her employee. (The #1 Thing Every Business Needs To Add to Their Strategy This Year at 30:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Od-2xtAdaa0&t=1800s)

## How does he say to handle critics, haters and negative people?

- In 2015 he wrote that he knows his haters think he is brash, too busy for anyone and loud, and that if he were them he would think those things too. (Why We All Have Haters and Why I Respect Mine, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/why-we-all-have-haters-and-why-i-respect-mine/)
- In 2017 he said to have empathy for why people say things and then not care what they think, and that he replies to critics because the other 80 or 93 percent respect that. (Gary V Talks Mindset K-Swiss + Why He Will Never Do Business With Michael - The Breakfast Club at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I-EjUI-lkrs&t=2025s)
- In 2019 he said he treats negative comments with sympathy for a writer who is not in a good place, then looks for any truth in them. (Gary Vee & Jay Shetty On How To Stop Caring What People Think Of You - On Purpose at 11:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wlQql_Fo5T0&t=675s)
- In 2020 he said the top way to handle family negativity at Thanksgiving is to turn it back to them with kindness and ask why they are dumping on you. (How to deal with negativity this Thanksgiving: The ultimate clapback, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-deal-with-negativity-this-thanksgiving-the-ultimate-clapback/)
- In 2021 he said to meet a critic with compassion, not anger, by saying you appreciate the interest and wishing them well, because overreaction feeds the behavior. (13 Ways to Build a Brand on Social Media in 2021 Clubhouse Q&A at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fYilnGQ7Ub0&t=2790s)
- In 2021 he said everyone deserves empathy, even haters, and that instead of throwing hate back at them you should have massive amounts of empathy for them because they need it the most. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Empathy, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-empathy/)
- In 2021 he said it is easy to be kind when it is easy, and it counts when you are the bigger person after someone wronged you and will not apologize. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Kindness, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-kindness/)
- In 2022 he said he feels bad for people who send hate in the comments and hopes something comes into their life that changes how they spend their time. (Gary Vee Exposes NFT's Flagrant 2 with Andrew Schulz and Akaash Singh at 1:05:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nrdOPOhRcz8&t=3915s)
- In 2022 he said that three years earlier nobody was saying to have compassion for haters, and that he pounded the idea because it is true that a person hurts others to get a second of escape from their own pain. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=1260s)
- In 2022 he said that spending three minutes a day replying to three people could mean one of them completely changes the framework of how they see the world. (Gary Vee On Discovering Logan Paul Kobe Bryant Regrets $100M+ NFT Collection - Impaulsive at 26:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7OEhqIwJR18&t=1575s)
- In 2023 he said hurt people hurt people, so be double nice to someone who is not nice and do the opposite of what you are tempted to do. (How to be the bigger person, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-be-the-bigger-person/)
- In 2023 he said to answer a nasty DM with an audio reply that says you get it, thanks them for following and wishes them well, because it humanizes you. (Don't Live For the Algorithm Live For Your Happiness GaryVee Audio Experience at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ft8R-b-3NXM&t=1485s)
- In 2024 he said anger and negativity come from weakness, so his default is compassion, and that boundaries are fine but cutting off your mom is not what he recommends. (GaryVee This is the #1 Reason You're Unhappy Do This to Overcome Anxiety - School of Greatness at 1:09:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3C7it0HqXyE&t=4185s)
- In 2026 he said he interacts with people leaving negative comments on a live stream because they are not in a good place, that giving love takes nothing from him, and that his mother taught him this. (The Safe Job Is Officially Dead at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TjdPqUI6Vjs&t=900s)
- In 2019 he gave three steps for friends who hold you back: check that you are not full of yourself, over-communicate your path and try to bring them along, then limit time together rather than cut them off. (How to Build Confidence and Self-esteem Aubrey Marcus Podcast w/ GaryVee at 1:02:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YzEkg9aM4Hk&t=3735s)

## What does he say about gratitude?

- In 2014 he said every ounce of his energy comes from gratitude. (A Note on Being Grateful, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/a-note-on-being-grateful/)
- In 2014 he said the two things he believes in most are empathy and gratitude, that you should always be grateful and kind, and that he does not think either can be taught. (This is the One Thing I Would Teach the World, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-one-thing-i-would-teach-the-world/)
- In 2014 he said nothing is uglier to him than someone spoiled, and that grateful people are attractive. (#AskGaryVee Episode 53: Values Selfies & Cornhole, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/askgaryvee-episode-53-values-selfies-cornhole/)
- In 2016 he said that when people ask him about motivation, 80 percent of the time he attributes it to gratitude, and that if you want real fuel to win, be grateful. (Two Secrets to Staying Motivated & Hungry, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-secrets-staying-motivated-hungry/)
- In 2017 he said perspective is gratitude's cousin and that he does it every day by pretending his mother, dad, sister, brother, wife or children are killed, briefly, and that it is insane what that perspective does for him. (Tony Robbins Unshakeable Gratitude & Focusing on Your Steak AskGaryVee 242 at 20:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9O8haH2tHWY&t=1215s)
- In 2021 he said gratitude is the foundation for everything else, including grinding and ambition, and has to be shown by saying it out loud and often. (Road to Twelve and a Half: Gratitude, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/road-to-twelve-and-a-half-gratitude/)
- In 2022 he said gratitude is why he gives back, and that his selfishness is his selflessness. (Why You Need to Make Even More Content in 2022 at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yemqSyqKqpY&t=1125s)
- In 2023 he said his hero is the unnamed person who endures without complaining, such as a woman leaving the subway at 4:55 am with two bags, and that he imagines her story to train gratitude. (Living Without Regrets The Path to True Happiness l Picatinny Keynote at 50:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tx_WkqalWVw&t=3015s)
- In 2025 he said gratitude is his biggest source of energy, and that his morning routine is seeing no missed calls from siblings or parents. (Stop Fearing AI Start Using It Like This GaryVee w/ The Liz Moody Podcast at 33:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JCdX4pebCb4&t=2025s)
