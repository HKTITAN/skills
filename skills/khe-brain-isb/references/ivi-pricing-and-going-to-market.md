# Pricing and going to market, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Market definition, growth hacking and value, Professor Siddharth, 20 May 2025

- Professor Siddharth defines growth hacking as conscious rapid experimentation for growth after launch, with the elements of the marketing mix that create value for consumers, setting communication aside. ## Building a D2C home care brand, Simran Khara, 26 May 2025

- Simran Khara says all first-time entrepreneurs get pricing wrong by reasoning backwards from a superior product. She advises rapid price experiments to find the most efficient marketing return, then holding that price. (ivi: Building a D2C home care brand, Simran Khara, 26 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-26-khara-building-a-d2c-brand.md)

(ivi: Building a D2C home care brand, Simran Khara, 26 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-26-khara-building-a-d2c-brand.md)

## Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025

- The instructor says a pricing policy has to manage credible reference prices: establish reference prices, encourage favourable comparisons and avoid unfavourable ones. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor gives three ways to manage how cost is perceived: bundle products to obscure individual costs, focus attention on customer value, and focus attention on the fully loaded cost including development and other sunk costs. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor says an LED seller should talk about the energy and power bill saved and not the purchase price, since the LED costs more than an incandescent bulb. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor says raw material cost is about the same for a five star restaurant and an ordinary one while the price gap is huge, so price has to be justified by value. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor uses Tesla as a reference price example: it began with the premium Roadster sports car, which set the segment it would be compared against, and later offered other models. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor says skiers holding four one day passes are more likely to ski on a rain ruined day than skiers holding one four day pass, because each pass is seen as its own transaction, and applies this to per use against subscription pricing. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
- The instructor says repeated discounts have less and less value to the customer over time. (ivi: Behavioural pricing and mental accounting, 18 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-18-instructor-behavioural-pricing-mental-accounting.md)
