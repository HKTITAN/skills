# Platforms, year by year

This file is the dated record of his verdicts on specific apps. Sections are by platform and the bullets inside each run in date order, so a change of mind shows as two bullets side by side. Most 2013 to 2018 material comes from his site articles, most 2017 to 2026 material from keynotes, interviews and solo videos. Every verdict carries its year, so read each bullet as that year's view and not as a standing rule, and prices and reach figures are his own numbers for that year.

## What did he say about Facebook, year by year?

- In 2008 he said he had created a non-scalable Facebook app called Ask Gary the day the platform came out, where people asked a wine question and he answered it, and that it dominated. (Gary Vaynerchuk WineLibrary com Talks at Google at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2c_L60H-cpY&t=1890s)
- In 2011 he said SEO was dying in front of our eyes and that people were going to Twitter to search where they once went to Google. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=2520s)
- In 2014 he called Facebook dark posts the number one way to reach people outside your inner circle. His method was to target people who would care about the project, learn Facebook's targeting, experiment, and put in some of your own money. (Two Things You Need to Do to Make Your Kickstarter a Success, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-things-you-need-to-do-to-make-your-kickstarter-a-success/)
- In 2014 he said that if Facebook disappeared tomorrow he would be fine, and that he could not wait for dark posts to start failing because that would be a new challenge to attack. (This Is the Most Substantial Cultural Shift of Our Time, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/this-is-the-most-substantial-cultural-shift-of-our-time/)
- In 2015 he said Facebook was his number one platform and that, with budget for only one platform, it should be Facebook. He called Facebook dark posts the number one tool and told readers to get in and use them. (Optimize Your Facebook Presence With These 5 Easy Steps, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/optimize-your-facebook-presence-with-these-5-easy-steps/)
- In 2015 he said Facebook dark posts, unpublished posts and retargeting gave the biggest conversion rates he had seen since early Google AdWords, at CPMs of $2 to $4 that he said would be $11 in five minutes. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 9:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=585s)
- In 2015 he said he expected to be mocking Facebook dark post ads in three to four years, when return on investment swings the other way. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=1440s)
- In April 2015 he told people who post videos on YouTube that they need to get into Facebook video now, because Facebook has the potential to become a massive competitor in distributing video, given its enormous amount of data to target against. (If You Produce Video Online You Can't Afford To Miss This, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/if-you-produce-video-online-you-cant-afford-to-miss-this/)
- In June 2015 he wrote that Facebook was becoming TV's competitor in video. (The Rise of Video Marketing on Social and How It Affects Your Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-rise-of-video-marketing-on-social-and-how-it-affects-your-business/)
- In December 2015 he said relying on organic Facebook reach was a mistake, since paying for reach is like every other marketing behaviour and Facebook's paid targeting is one of the most powerful marketing tools ever. (Stop Marketing Like It's 2012, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-market-your-business/)
- In January 2016 he said he would still be linking out to YouTube on Facebook rather than uploading natively if he had not changed his mind, and that platforms and best practices shift quickly so you must keep testing. (How to Win in Business? Be Open to Change, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-win-in-business-be-open-to-change/)
- In 2016 he told a keynote room that people get romantic about how they made their money, such as Google AdWords, and that Google's own data showed AdWords clicking down 19 percent. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 21:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=1260s)
- In 2017 he said Facebook ads were the underpriced tactic for early stage companies and predicted that the 6 to 13 dollar CPMs then being paid would be 50 to 80 dollars in 36 to 48 months. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 15:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=900s)
- In 2017 he said 2012 to 2019 would be called the golden era of Facebook ads, and that in six years people would regret not doing more once CPMs were $48 instead of $7. (Amazon was Google's Biggest Advertiser Oslo Business Forum Keynote DailyVee 352 at 24:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UU1y4DKaoXw&t=1485s)
- In 2018 he told an Australian room to spend the year obsessing over Facebook and Instagram or lose, and said the best converters on Facebook were 50 to 80 year olds because they consume the most of the stream. (95% of You Will Ignore This 2018 Marketing Strategy Business Squared Keynote in Australia at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5Yst1Si3fw&t=1080s)
- In 2018 he said the biggest companies were not spending enough on Facebook, Instagram and Snapchat, and that when brands like Coca-Cola and Mercedes-Benz move their budgets there in the next 12 to 36 months, reaching people will cost far more than the $7 per thousand it did then. (95% of You Will Ignore This 2018 Marketing Strategy Business Squared Keynote in Australia at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W5Yst1Si3fw&t=1440s)
- In 2018 he said not to leave Facebook when results drop but to make far more pieces of content, and that Facebook can be better than three years earlier with better content and copy. He also said to squeeze the most out of Messenger while it is full, and to move to another channel once it is empty. (How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 23:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=1395s; How to Sell Anything on Facebook and Instagram 4Ds Consultation with Gary Vaynerchuk at 18:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vSwIYTKTCgw&t=1125s)
- In 2019 he said the price to get into a Facebook feed would not stay at 12 dollars but would go to 450 dollars, and told the biggest companies to stop spending on television commercials and spend properly. (How to Get Your Business the Most Attention Possible Game Changers Summit Keynote 2019 at 46:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-bG82D4bPWc&t=2790s)
- In 2023 he said Facebook Reels were grossly underpriced and would neutralize once content supply caught up. (Why Every Business Needs To Be Good At Social Media l Meta Speech at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w2glmssonDw&t=315s)
- In 2025 he said the most important platform for him and his team over the last month was Facebook proper, classic Facebook. (AI & Social Media Playbook For The Next 5 Years You Can't Hide From This GaryVee Q&A at 18:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_RiVvFwZ2sw&t=1080s)
- In 2026 he said Facebook ads in a 10-mile radius were his favorite thing in the world for local businesses such as a dry cleaner, barber or restaurant. (The Safe Job Is Officially Dead at 31:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TjdPqUI6Vjs&t=1890s)

## What did he say about Instagram, year by year?

- In 2014 he said Instagram was the platform most in need of being hacked for right hooks. (How to Use Instagram to Drive Sales Yes it's possible, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/how-to-use-instagram-to-drive-sales-yes-its-possible/)
- In 2015 he said Instagram was becoming the leading social network and that most marketers disliked it because it has no link-outs. He said he had only 55 to 60 thousand followers there but drove more awareness. (2015 Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote - Online Marketing Rockstars at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eu7z6U8Jdok&t=855s)
- In 2015 he said he regretted missing early Instagram. He was at 125,000 followers and said he would have had 500,000 to 700,000 had he played his thesis there three years earlier. (Gary Vaynerchuk Interview on the Startup Grind LA 2015 at 24:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=d3C4cuCc7jU&t=1440s)
- In March 2016 he said Instagram's new algorithm takes nothing away and shows you more of what you want first, and that it was the moment to go all in while everyone was talking about Snapchat. (Instagram Change Sends Everyone Into a Frenzy But It's Not That Serious, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagram-change-sends-everyone-into-a-frenzy-but-its-not-that-serious/)
- In 2016 he said the best math arbitrage after Facebook was Instagram influencer product placement, because it is fully trackable with codes or links. (Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Icon 2016 at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wbdYg6X1e-g&t=2520s)
- In August 2016 he said anyone who thought Instagram Stories would kill Snapchat was completely out of their mind. (My Two Cents on Instagram Stories and the Evolution of Social Platforms, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/two-cents-instagram-stories-evolution-social-platforms/)
- In March 2017 he agreed that a platform pick is a moving market: six months earlier it would have been Snapchat, and now it was Instagram Stories. (Tony Robbins Unshakeable Gratitude & Focusing on Your Steak AskGaryVee 242 at 36:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9O8haH2tHWY&t=2160s)
- In 2017 he ranked Instagram influencers just behind Facebook ads among the best arbitrage in marketing for doers. (How To Start A Gary Vaynerchuk Original at 6:45, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5jiifErFEE&t=405s)
- In 2017 he laid out the **$1.80 Instagram strategy**: leave your two cents on the top 9 posts for 10 hashtags relevant to your brand or business every single day. (The $1.80 Instagram Strategy To Grow Your Business or Brand, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagram-for-business-180-strategy-grow-business-brand/)
- In 2018 he said platforms like Vine, Twitter and Myspace lost their competitive advantage when they stopped iterating, and that Vine and Twitter had grown romantic about six seconds and 140 characters. He said Instagram impressed him by adding text only stories and GIFs. (How To Use GIFs in Your Instagram Stories, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagramgifs/)
- In July 2018 he declined to publish a prediction on IGTV, said he was not in the predictions business, and said long-form should have been inside Instagram itself. (Blowing Up My Plans to Write a Blog Post on My Analysis of Instagram TV, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/blowing-up-my-plans-to-write-a-blog-post-on-my-analysis-of-instagram-tv/)
- In 2018 he said Instagram direct messages were a window where strangers still replied, like early Twitter and 1996 to 1998 email, with about a year or two left. (NOW is your time - Speech to Alabama Football Team Gary Vaynerchuk 2018 Keynote at 14:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqlz3Lngh1U&t=855s)
- In 2019 he said most of his own effort went to Instagram, and that Instagram would one day lose attention and influencers with big followings would become irrelevant. (The 79 / 21 Rule to Build a Long Term Business, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-79-21-rule-to-build-a-long-term-business/)
- In 2020 he said Instagram Stories ads were still super underpriced, and put them at about $3 CPMs in Delaware. (How to Make Your Creative Work Harder GaryVee Audio Experience at 28:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=N37JLnpAeqU&t=1710s)
- In 2021 he said Instagram direct messaging was the single biggest networking and business development opportunity of the decade. (Instagram DM: Direct Messaging to Network, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/network-instagram-direct-message/)
- In 2023 he said Instagram organic reach had fallen a lot in six to 12 months because attention hours moved to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. (The Ultimate Social Media Marketing Strategy at 19:30, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lqmA-LrQzcY&t=1170s)
- In 2023 he said Instagram is now really hard because it is so mature, and that for the last 60 days he had posted a two-post carousel with a meme first and the video second because it was what was working at that moment. (How To Do Social Media Marketing The Right Way at 5:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bzwJlSXn2mM&t=315s)

## What did he say about Twitter and X, year by year?

- In 2011 he told a keynote room to try Twitter search instead of Google search the next time something caught their eye, because real-time search brings enormous context and value. (Gary Vaynerchuk - Keynote Speech at Inc 500 Seminar 2011 at 42:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lcqCAqZtedI&t=2520s)
- In 2013 he said he used Twitter for engagement and not information, and was in pure counter-punch mode, replying to people. (The #1 Way to Develop Context, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-1-way-to-develop-context/)
- In 2015 he said it was fine to repeat the same post on Twitter because the speed of the feed meant few people saw repeats, and compared it to a news ticker. (At the Speed of Twitter It's Ok to Repeat Yourself, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/at-the-speed-of-twitter-its-ok-to-repeat-yourself/)
- In March 2015 he said Twitter was extremely noisy and would not survive unless it fixed its noise problem. (The Big Difference Between Twitter and Instagram Will Determine Who Wins, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/the-big-difference-between-twitter-and-instagram-will-determine-who-wins/)
- In February 2016 he gave three reasons Twitter worked for business: disproportionate attention for the effort, word of mouth through the retweet, and a public database of consumer insights. He also said its value fell because it got too noisy. (Flying with a Wounded Wing: Why Twitter Still Has More Than a Chance, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/flying-with-a-wounded-wing-why-twitter-still-has-more-than-a-chance/)
- In March 2016 he called Twitter "the only open cocktail party on the internet" and still the best tip for a new business. (Twitter Still Has a Massive Business Opportunity for Your Company, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/twitter-still-has-a-massive-business-opportunity-for-your-company/)
- In April 2016 he said that after a show he dug deeper to see whether the shift was real and saw an emerging growth of teenage Twitter users. He called his idea that Twitter has momentum with teenagers because it offers natural communication an early thesis. (Don't Look Now But Twitter Has Momentum With The Youth, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/dont-look-now-but-twitter-has-momentum-with-the-youth/)
- In 2016 he said Twitter lost its way because it never made an algorithm, which created a fire hose problem. (Being An Entrepreneur Gary Vaynerchuk With Larry King 2016 at 9:00, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UTy8g93Trsk&t=540s)
- In 2017 he said Twitter's ad product was not good enough to spend on, and in July that Twitter was the only true social site left. (Entrepreneur Q&A Talk With GaryVee SXSW 2017 at 2:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM9NzDITGFc&t=135s; Relentless Gary Vaynerchuk Keynote Los Angeles 2017 at 20:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KQEgD4DCqE8&t=1215s)
- In 2017 he said Twitter lost its place as the number two social platform from 2011 to 2015 by not innovating. (Instagram Poll Stickers: What Marketers Should Be Thinking About, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/instagram-poll-stickers-what-marketers-need-to-know/)
- In 2019 he said Twitter was still an incredible platform to build a brand, and described adding a thoughtful response as the third step, starting with posts that already have engagement. (How to Use Twitter For Business in 2019 And Why It's Not Dead, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/twitter-for-business/)
- In 2021 he said Twitter search, and not Google, was the best place for real-time listening. (What is Social Listening and Why is it Important, https://garyvaynerchuk.com/what-is-social-listening-and-why-is-it-important/)
- In 2023 he guessed that Elon Musk's X signals a WeChat-style full-stack app and said Twitter is not going away. (How a $350000000+ Company's CEO Makes Decisions Decoder Podcast with Nilay Patel at 35:15, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aoQAR83VUuU&t=2115s)
