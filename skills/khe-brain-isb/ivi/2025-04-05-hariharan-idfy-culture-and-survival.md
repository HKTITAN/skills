# Culture and survival at IDfy, Ashok Hariharan

Typed notes from an orientation talk by Ashok Hariharan, founder of IDfy. He spoke about building a company like a college, surviving several near deaths, and how IDfy sells to large accounts.

## What was said

### Company as a college

- Ashok Hariharan says he builds the company like a college, with a collegiate atmosphere where people learn new things.
- He says people make many mistakes in college, so mistakes should not be punished at work.
- He says culture was a priority from the start.
- He says there is no artificial pressure at IDfy, and everyone leaves at 7pm unless there is a bug or a release.
- He says that if someone is stressed after three days of long hours, they are given a break.
- He gives IDfy's attrition rate as 9% with 750 people.

### Surviving

- He says the company nearly died in 2013, in 2015 and during Covid, and the team stayed intact because of culture.
- He says IDfy was eight years too early, and that sometimes you are early and it is okay to persevere.
- He gives IDfy's revenue as 11 crore rupees in 2019 and 200 crore in 2025 with profit, and calls it a survival game.

### Government and data

- He says he got into rooms where the government decides things by getting meetings as an ISB alumnus.
- He says banks pushed IDfy towards the government, and IDfy went with HDFC Bank to promote video KYC.
- He says pricing can be changed using data science models that show who is more likely to default.
- Asked how IDfy could identify people with criminal records for Uber, he says it processed public data, about 330 million court and FIR records.
- He says IDfy can detect tampering, including manipulation done with ChatGPT.

### Advice on selling and starting

- He says he would use the programme to talk to as many ISB alumni as possible.
- On the mistakes of first-time entrepreneurs he says it is about execution, not ideas, so share what you are thinking.
- He says he is against product-led growth.
- On above-the-line marketing he says that someone who sees you on a hoarding thinks you are already big.
- He says that if ticket sizes are large in India, do not use sales development representatives. Give one person 30 accounts from start to finish.
- He says IDfy converts 60 to 65% with one person owning each account, against 15% when it used sales development representatives.

## Examples and cases used

- HDFC Bank: IDfy went with the bank to the government to promote video KYC.
- Uber: IDfy checked drivers against public court and FIR records.

## Numbers given

- IDfy revenue as told by Ashok Hariharan: 11 crore rupees in 2019, 200 crore and profitable in 2025.
- IDfy attrition of 9% across 750 people.
- IDfy enterprise sales conversion: 60 to 65% with one owner per 30 accounts, 15% with sales development representatives.
- IDfy processed 330 million court and FIR records.
