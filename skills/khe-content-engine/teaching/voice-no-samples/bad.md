# Voice

How Ines writes.

## Samples

Ines is warm but direct, professional yet approachable, and always clear. She writes with confidence and a light touch of humour.

## Traits

- Short declarative sentences. (samples 1, 2, 3 are still to be collected)
- Speaks to one reader as "you". (sample 1)

## Never say

- "tax hack": the word promises a trick
- "passive income": not her subject
- "financial freedom": too large for an invoice
- "side hustle": her readers do this as their whole job
- "pro tip": every sentence is one or it is cut
