== Seat: khe-brain-enrico (Enrico Tartarotti) ==
A year of building in private breaks a position he states and meets one exception he praises himself (Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea at 0:00; Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Figma at 9:00).
Breaks: a year of building before any check. In January 2023 he says most startups die after spending years and a lot of cash on something people do not want, and he offers a fake door test and a Wizard of Oz test to check demand without building (Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea at 0:00; Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea at 6:00).
Holds: building in private. In March 2025 he says Figma did the opposite of the launch fast playbook by working in private for 3 years, and that this became a secret of its success (Why Everyone Is Obsessed With Figma at 9:00).
Source: Do You Have a Genius Startup Idea, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HABFEs90MZ8

== Seat: khe-brain-yc (Y Combinator) ==
Michael Seibel, in a talk the library dates to 2023, calls the founder who wants a year and 10 million dollars to build the perfect product a "fake Steve Jobs" (How to build an MVP). Paul Graham's 2013 essay says it usually works best in software to show users something once it has a quantum of utility, and he excepts domains with big penalties for mistakes (Do Things that Don't Scale).
Source: How to build an MVP, https://www.ycombinator.com/library/Io-how-to-build-an-mvp
Source: Do Things that Don't Scale, https://paulgraham.com/ds.html
Not in the corpus: whether this product is in a domain with big penalties for mistakes, and how long a build may run before the first user sees it. The decision is yours.

== Seat: khe-brain-isb (the ISB notes) ==
The notes define an MVP as the simplest product that lets a team test one critical assumption with real customers, and say it is not an early release to the general public or something embarrassing to ship (Entrepreneurship: Build Phase: Creating the MVP).
Source: Entrepreneurship: Build Phase: Creating the MVP, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/entrepreneurship/rules/lean-build-phase-creating-the-mvp.md
Not in the notes: how long a build may run before the first customer test, and whether this product needs more than one assumption tested. No file names a professor or a course, so this is the repository author's summary.
