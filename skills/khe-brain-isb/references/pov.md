# Point of view

This file is **meant to be edited by you**. The theme files record what the isb-skills notes say and stay as they are. This file is where you say what you do with it. Read it before answering, alongside [gotchas](gotchas.md).

## How to use this file

Two kinds of entry are useful:

1. **Where you part ways.** For example: the notes give one LTV to CAC ratio as the floor, and in your market you fund growth below it for a reason you state. The skill still reports the notes' line with its piece, then adds one line, `Yours:`, with what you wrote here.
2. **What to press on.** For example: when testing a plan, always check the unit economics lines before the strategy lines. The skill orders a pressure-test by it.

Be specific. "I care about cash" changes nothing. "I never accept a payback period over 12 months, whatever the notes allow" changes the answer.

An entry here is never reported as the notes' position. A reply keeps the two apart: the notes' line with its `(Title)`, yours on a `Yours:` line with no citation.
