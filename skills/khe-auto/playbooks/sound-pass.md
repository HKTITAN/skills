# Sound in a product

**You own this from "should this make a sound?" to files that are wired and mapped.** Most moments get no sound, and that answer is part of the job.

1. **Settle the motion first.** If the motion a sound lands on is not final, run `animate` on it. A sound placed on motion that changes gets placed twice.
2. **Decide** with `khe-sound`: which moments earn one, by how often they happen and what they tell the user. Exit: a short list of moments, and a longer list of rejected ones.
3. **Design one family** with `khe-sound`. One material for the whole product. Exit: a written palette.
4. **Generate** with `khe-sound`, with ElevenLabs or offline.
5. **Wire it** with `khe-sound`: created inside a user gesture, a mute control, haptics on a phone where they fit.
6. **Sync it** with `khe-sound`. Exit: every transient sits on its contact frame.

**Reply:** the sound map (moment, file, frame), the rejected moments, how to mute it, and what was not tested on a device.
