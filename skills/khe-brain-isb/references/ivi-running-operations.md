# Running operations, as ivi speakers put it

One claim per line, each with its session note. Every line is what one speaker said in one session of the I-Venture Immersive programme in 2025, as written up from a recording or from typed notes. It is that speaker's view and nobody else's: not the school's, not the programme's and not a second speaker's. A figure is the speaker's figure, heard through an automatic transcript, with no source behind it. Sessions are in date order, undated ones last. Search this file for a word; do not read it top to bottom.

## Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram says to outsource only what is commoditised and keep what is special and core in-house. When you must outsource, give each party only what it needs to know so no one outside holds the full picture. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says her core IP is the process for delivering chemotherapy safely far from the city, including drug transport and mixing, a triple check and discharge summaries. She kept it in-house and shares it only on a need-to-know basis. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says the main metric for a hospital is revenue per occupied bed. She gives 40,000 rupees for a good multi-speciality hospital and says Max Healthcare runs at 1 lakh. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)
- Sonali Srungaram says 15% is a good EBITDA margin for a hospital, about half of a surgery bill goes to people, and an average hospital can survive only about three months if patients stop coming. (ivi: Building the MVP of a distributed cancer hospital, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-23-srungaram-distributed-cancer-care-mvp.md)

## Lean startup workshop 1, building an MVP for distributed cancer care, Sonali Srungaram, 23 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram's rule is to outsource what is commoditised and keep in-house whatever makes the product or process special, and to rent when renting gets a prototype to market faster and cheaper. ## Operations, value indicators and process thinking, 29 May 2025

(ivi: Operations, value indicators and process thinking, 29 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-29-instructor-operations-value-indicators-and-process-thinking.md)

## Oncology venture from MVP to scale, Sonali Srungaram, 30 May 2025

- Sonali Srungaram says a process designer should not expect a process to work for more than a year. Her standard is to test processes every six months, or once a year if the team is too busy. (ivi: Oncology venture from MVP to scale, Sonali Srungaram, 30 May 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-05-30-srungaram-oncology-venture-mvp-to-scale.md)

## Operations class on process improvement, value indicators and KPI trees, 5 Jun 2025

- The instructor says Kaizen is continuous improvement and Kaikaku is breakthrough improvement, so cutting a 30 second payment wait to 25 seconds is Kaizen and cutting it to 3 seconds is Kaikaku. (ivi: Operations class on process improvement, value indicators and KPI trees, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-operations-process-improvement-and-kpi-trees.md)
- The instructor says Kaizen meets low resistance, costs little and lets every employee run an improvement in turn, which builds a sense of participation and culture. (ivi: Operations class on process improvement, value indicators and KPI trees, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-operations-process-improvement-and-kpi-trees.md)
- The instructor says Kaikaku makes change management hard, because employees whose comfort zone is broken agree in front of the boss and then show passive resistance. (ivi: Operations class on process improvement, value indicators and KPI trees, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-operations-process-improvement-and-kpi-trees.md)
- The instructor says a young firm with capital for trial and error that wants to disrupt suits Kaikaku, and a firm investing over years in culture suits Kaizen. (ivi: Operations class on process improvement, value indicators and KPI trees, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-operations-process-improvement-and-kpi-trees.md)

## Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025

- The instructor says there are two ways to improve a process. Kaizen means continuous improvement in small steps and kaikaku means a breakthrough improvement, and he recommends neither over the other because both have pros and cons. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says a small kaizen change meets little resistance, costs less and gives staff a sense of participation that builds culture. Its disadvantage is the time it takes to reach a large gain. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says a kaikaku breakthrough is large enough for management to see and is hard to imitate, because by the time competitors copy it you can deliver the next one. Its cost is hard change management, since the people affected know how to spoil the system. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says a readiness assessment must come before any process improvement. It has three parts: people, process and technology. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor tells of two celebrated technology upgrades at the World Bank that did not exchange data, so staff salaries went unpaid the next month. His lesson is that pockets of excellence built in silos are dangerous and process readiness must be checked first. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)
- The instructor says a firm that has already launched a big change and is facing resistance should accept that some talented people will leave, document everyone's working knowledge at once and start hiring replacements. (ivi: Process improvement, value indicators and voice of the customer, 5 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-05-instructor-process-improvement-and-value-indicators.md)

## Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025

- The instructor says that with inappropriate KPIs a dashboard looks green most of the time while the company loses customers and never learns why. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor says a good KPI is easy and clear for a front-line employee to understand, such as make 50 donuts at 9 am, 50 at noon and 50 at 4 pm, and is comparable. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor says asking customers what to improve does not prioritise anything, because a customer given a choice asks for everything, like a Swiggy customer who wants variety, five minute delivery, great taste and no charge. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor defines a dissatisfier as a characteristic that adds no new satisfaction when present but causes dissatisfaction when absent, with an ATM that fails to give cash as his example. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor defines a satisfier as a characteristic with a linear relation to satisfaction, with the speed of a diagnostic lab report as his example. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor defines a delighter as something that makes the customer very happy when given and changes nothing in their impression when not given, with Citibank's birthday call offering five times reward points as his example. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor's priority order for improving KPIs under the Kano model is dissatisfiers first, satisfiers second and delighters third. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
- The instructor says that if any dissatisfier KPI shows red or amber the firm should start a process improvement project at once, because basic customer needs are unmet and the firm has a survival problem. (ivi: Kano model for prioritising KPIs, 9 Jun 2025, https://github.com/HKTITAN/skills/blob/main/skills/khe-brain-isb/ivi/2025-06-09-instructor-kano-model-prioritising-kpis.md)
