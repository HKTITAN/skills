Paul Graham's 2012 essay puts a good growth rate during the Y Combinator batch at 5 to 7 percent a week (Startup = Growth). A footnote limits the figure to startups that are launched or able to launch while in the batch (Startup = Growth).
Source: Startup = Growth, https://paulgraham.com/growth.html
Not in the corpus: a growth rate for a company outside the batch.
