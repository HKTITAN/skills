Draft:
most products don't need a better onboarding flow.

they need fewer reasons to have one.

i learned this with Smash&Clash.

people kept asking how the captures worked, so i wrote a tutorial.

i hated it. so did the players.

so i changed the board instead.

now every move zooms in and shows you what happened to the cards next to it.

nobody reads instructions. they read feedback.

and the same change made the game more fun, because every move got a small moment of drama.

if you're writing a tutorial, look at the screen first.

it usually has the answer.
