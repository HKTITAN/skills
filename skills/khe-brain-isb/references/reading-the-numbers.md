# What do the numbers say?

One claim per line, each with its piece. Every line is the repository author's summary; these files name no author and no source for a threshold or an industry range. The thresholds are the files' own rules of thumb and carry no industry, country or year unless the line says so. One worked example in these files, the coffee shop balance sheet, does not close on its own arithmetic, and one leverage ratio carries thresholds that do not fit its own definition; both are listed in [disagreements](disagreements.md), and a reply that uses either says so.

## The three statements

- The balance sheet is a snapshot of what a company owns and how it is financed; the income statement covers a period and shows profit; the cash flow statement shows cash actually moving. (Finance: The Three Financial Statements, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/statements-the-three-financial-statements.md)
- The cash flow statement is called critical because net income is not cash: depreciation is added back, a rise in receivables is subtracted and a rise in payables is added. (Finance: The Three Financial Statements, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/statements-the-three-financial-statements.md)
- Free cash flow is defined here as operating cash flow less capital expenditure and called the most important metric, which has to be positive over the long term. (Finance: The Three Financial Statements, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/statements-the-three-financial-statements.md)
- The statements connect: net income raises equity on the balance sheet, and balance sheet changes such as receivables and inventory explain why net income differs from cash. (Finance: Key Insight: The Three Statements Connect, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/statements-key-insight-the-three-statements-connect.md)
- On quality of earnings the file says "Never trust net income alone": operating cash flow above net income is good, and below it signals earnings manipulation. (Finance: Key Insight: The Three Statements Connect, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/statements-key-insight-the-three-statements-connect.md)
- The first item in the pitfalls list is focusing only on the bottom line, and the fifth is relying on a single ratio without history, competitors or the industry beside it. (Finance: Pitfalls to Avoid, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-pitfalls-to-avoid.md)

## Returns

- Return on assets is net income over average total assets, with ranges given as 25% or more for software, 8 to 12% for manufacturing and 0.8 to 1.5% for banks. (Finance: Profitability Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-profitability-ratios.md)
- Return on equity is net income over average shareholders' equity and is called the most important return to equity investors. (Finance: Profitability Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-profitability-ratios.md)
- Return on invested capital is after-tax operating profit over debt plus equity, and the file's key point is that ROIC has to exceed WACC to create value. (Finance: Profitability Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-profitability-ratios.md)
- The three-factor DuPont splits ROE into net profit margin, asset turnover and the equity multiplier; the five-factor form adds tax burden and interest burden and uses EBIT margin. (Finance: DuPont Analysis & Performance Decomposition, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/dupont-dupont-analysis-performance-decomposition.md)
- The DuPont file's point is that equal ROEs hide different risks: its two companies both reach 15%, one on a 10% margin and one on a 5% margin with twice the turnover. (Finance: DuPont Analysis & Performance Decomposition, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/dupont-dupont-analysis-performance-decomposition.md)
- Sustainable growth is ROE times the retention ratio, and break-even volume is fixed costs over contribution margin. (Finance: Growth & Sustainability, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/formulas-growth-sustainability.md)

## Liquidity and leverage

- The current ratio is current assets over current liabilities, with 1.5 to 2.0 given as healthy, above 3.0 as idle cash and below 1.0 as distress risk. (Finance: Liquidity Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-liquidity-ratios.md)
- The operating cash flow ratio, operating cash flow over current liabilities, is called a better predictor of solvency than the current ratio because it is cash-based; above 1.0 is given as healthy. (Finance: Liquidity Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-liquidity-ratios.md)
- Debt-to-equity under 1.0 is given as healthy for most industries, with banks at 8 to 10x, tech companies under 0.3 and utilities at 1 to 1.5. (Finance: Leverage, Solvency Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-leverage-solvency-ratios.md)
- Interest coverage, EBIT over interest expense, is graded safe above 5x, adequate at 2 to 5x, risky below 2x and distressed below 1x. (Finance: Leverage, Solvency Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-leverage-solvency-ratios.md)

## Efficiency and working capital

- Days sales outstanding is 365 over receivables turnover; 30 to 60 days is given as typical for B2B, and a rising figure is a red flag for collection problems. (Finance: Efficiency Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-efficiency-ratios.md)
- The cash conversion cycle is days sales outstanding plus days inventory outstanding minus days payable outstanding, the days of working capital a business has to finance. (Finance: Efficiency Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-efficiency-ratios.md)
- A high days payable figure improves cash and damages supplier relationships. (Finance: Efficiency Ratios, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/ratios-efficiency-ratios.md)
- Working capital is released three ways: faster collections, fewer days of inventory and longer payment terms, with the caution that poor payment damages credibility with suppliers. (Finance: Working Capital Management, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/workcap-working-capital-management.md)
- The economic order quantity is the square root of twice annual demand times order cost, over holding cost per unit per year. (Finance: Working Capital Management, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/workcap-working-capital-management.md)

## Risk

- Value-at-Risk is the largest loss expected at a confidence level over a horizon; the file lists three limits: it misses tail risk, assumes a normal distribution and looks backward. (Finance: Value-at-Risk, VaR, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/risk-value-at-risk-var.md)
- Forwards lock a future price with no upfront cost and are binding, futures are standardised and exchange-traded, options give a right for a premium, and swaps exchange cash flows. (Finance: Derivatives for Hedging, https://github.com/HKTITAN/isb-skills/blob/060ee06/skills/finance/rules/risk-derivatives-for-hedging.md)
