# Content types

The five forms Ines writes. A draft names one on its `Type:` line. The skeletons are hers; the lengths come from the platform nodes.

## Short take

Where: [[x]]. One claim, one fact, one instruction. Three short paragraphs at most.

## Short thread

Where: [[x]]. One step per part, in the order the reader does them. The first part stands alone if nobody reads the rest.

## Story post

Where: [[linkedin]]. What she believed, what she changed, what she counted, what it does not prove, what she does now.

## How-to issue

Where: [[newsletter]]. A subject line, the reason in two sentences, numbered steps, the wording to copy, the one thing that goes wrong.

## Note

Where: [[linkedin]] or [[newsletter]]. One client moment, told with permission, and the single thing it shows.

## Not made here

Carousels, video scripts and images. This engine writes text for a person to post.
